US-based artificial intelligence startup Scuba has secured a total of 34 million dollars across its pre-seed and seed funding rounds. The investment was led by Accel and IVP, with participation from funds such as BoxGroup, Conviction, Homebrew, and Scribble Ventures, alongside a prominent group of angel investors from major tech companies.

Founded by Noah Desai Weiss and Dolapo Falola, the company develops a platform designed to gather and organize all essential information required in business workflows. The platform allows users to save internal communications, audio recordings, videos, screenshots, and AI chat outputs via a browser extension and mobile application.

The collected data is organized into topic-based groups called Collections, while a feature named Briefing summarizes daily developments. Scuba's system integrates directly with tools like Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Slack, eliminating the need for employees to manually transfer documents into AI assistants.
