Uber is expanding the deployment of ChatGPT Work to approximately 30,000 employees across its global operations. According to the company, the decision follows a pilot study involving 1,000 participants. The new rollout is not limited to software developers, as non-engineering staff accounted for the vast majority of the initial pilot group.

Finance, operations, and product teams participated in the initial testing phase. Employees utilized the tool for tasks such as examining trends in operational data, localizing training materials for different markets, and preparing interactive dashboards for daily work. These examples are based on statements from Sumanth Sukumar, Uber's vice president of engineering. The company did not disclose specific measured productivity gains for every individual use case.

Additionally, Uber previously paired 30 AI engineers with various teams, giving them two weeks to automate certain workflows. In the new widespread rollout, rather than enforcing a strict task list for all employees, the company encourages teams to explore different use cases. The financial terms and the cost per employee were not made public.

Uber's relationship with OpenAI extends beyond this internal office rollout. Previous collaborations included tools like Uber Assistant to help drivers with work decisions through a conversational interface. However, the ChatGPT Work tool deployed for corporate employees is distinct from the applications offered to drivers and riders.

The evaluation process for the new employee rollout remains ongoing. Sukumar stated that the company will continue to monitor which workflows improve and how employees utilize the tool, noting that granting access to nearly 30,000 people does not mean every employee will use it with the same intensity.
