Modernizing legacy software systems has long been a complex and costly challenge for organizations. Instead of manually translating code written in older languages like Delphi or Visual Basic, new methods are emerging to process entire systems at once.

According to Alejandro D'Andrea, founder and chief technology officer of BotMan, intelligence is no longer the primary bottleneck in artificial intelligence. The real challenge is context, as developers previously had to explain every single detail an agent needed to complete a specific task.

The newly developed pipeline maps an application's dependencies, business logic, and data models using knowledge graphs. This enables AI agents to autonomously interpret the system they are rebuilding and re-architect the entire infrastructure rather than just converting old syntax.

Beyond code migration, these knowledge graphs provide reusable business context for AI agents. When unstructured information or legal documents enter the system, relationships to relevant projects and people are mapped once, allowing any future agent to retrieve necessary context automatically.
