One of the most persistent operational bottlenecks in manufacturing—generating custom price quotes—is being transformed by next-generation artificial intelligence. Startup Forgepoint has raised $9.8 million in seed funding to scale its autonomous AI sales agent, Frank, built specifically to streamline the request for quotation process for manufacturers.

The funding round was co-led by Primary Venture Partners and a16z Speedrun, with participation from angel investors associated with companies like OpenAI and Brex. Frank integrates directly into a manufacturer's existing software systems and pricing rules to analyze inquiries, review attachments, and calculate accurate quotes automatically.

Industry studies show that 78 percent of companies ultimately purchase from the first supplier that responds to their inquiries. Because legacy back-office systems often slow down response times, Frank bridges this gap by instantly evaluating requests, asking for missing details when necessary, and significantly reducing the time it takes to win contracts.
