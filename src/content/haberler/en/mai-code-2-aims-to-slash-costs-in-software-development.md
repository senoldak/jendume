Microsoft has unveiled its new generation of coding models, MAI Code 2 and MAI Code 2 Flash, designed to streamline software development while significantly reducing operational costs. Introduced at a recent industry event, these models address the growing concern among enterprises regarding the high token costs associated with scaling artificial intelligence applications. The company claims these models offer competitive performance compared to current market leaders while operating at a fraction of the price.

MAI Code 2 demonstrates impressive benchmarks. According to the company, it delivers 98% of the performance of GPT-6.1 Sol at only 40% of the cost. When compared against Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5, the model reportedly achieves 96% of the performance at just 5% of the cost. The lighter version, MAI Code 2 Flash, also promises high efficiency, matching the performance of GPT-6 Luna at 65% of the price and Claude Haiku 5.5 at 20%.

This release highlights a critical shift toward cost-efficiency in the AI sector. As organizations struggle with skyrocketing token expenses, Microsoft’s strategy focuses on providing high-performance alternatives that allow for better budget management. Beyond the models themselves, the company is introducing "MAI Licensed Weights," which allows trusted customers to customize models with their own data within their secure Azure environments. This approach aims to offer the flexibility of open-source solutions with the added security and control of a managed enterprise environment.

Alongside the coding models, the company also launched MAI Cyber 2 Flash for security tasks and MAI Image 2.7 for image editing. By expanding its portfolio with these specialized tools, Microsoft is positioning itself to offer a diverse range of AI solutions. This strategy ensures that businesses can select the most cost-effective and performant models tailored to their specific operational needs, marking a new phase in the integration of AI into enterprise workflows.
