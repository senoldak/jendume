OpenAI has launched a new capability for its personal AI agent, Dot, adding support for iMessage and RCS messaging. The feature allows users to send texts, photos, and voice memos directly to their agent without needing to open a separate application. Initially, the preview is rolling out exclusively to a limited group of Pro tier users in the United States.

Unlike traditional chat sessions, Dot is designed to handle ongoing tasks in the background and proactively reach out with updates. When assigned a project or a topic to monitor, the agent sends messages when relevant developments occur. OpenAI notes that context carries over across different channels, meaning users do not need to repeat instructions when switching between interfaces.

To set up the feature, users enable the texting option within the Dot profile and complete a phone number verification. OpenAI partnered with messaging infrastructure provider Linq to handle the message routing, noting that extensive penetration testing was conducted to ensure safety. The company clarifies that interactions remain strictly one-on-one and group messaging is not supported.

The personal agent market has become increasingly competitive with recent rival entries and waitlists. By enabling direct text messaging, OpenAI is embedding its agent deeper into the everyday workflows of users, though broad global availability and expansion to other subscription tiers remain to be seen.
