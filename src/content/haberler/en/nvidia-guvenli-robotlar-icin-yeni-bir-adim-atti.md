While major investments continue to flow into AI data centers, chipmaker Nvidia is also channeling billions into physical AI technologies, including robotics and self-driving systems. The company's primary focus is establishing comprehensive safety frameworks to prevent autonomous machines from harming people nearby.

Originally launched in 2025 with tools for self-driving cars, the Halos safety system expanded in June 2026 to encompass broader robotics applications. This architecture is designed to support autonomous mobile robots in warehouses, humanoid workers in factories, and even delicate surgical robots.

As both AI models and robotic hardware advance rapidly, industry leaders identify safety as the next major hurdle. Initiatives like Halos aim to unlock the true potential of autonomous systems by ensuring they operate reliably and securely in human environments.
