Meta has quietly introduced Style Sketch, an AI-driven fashion application designed to help users curate outfit ideas using clothes they already own. Initially spotted by social media consultants, the app is currently being tested in specific Southeast Asian markets. Style Sketch aims to provide a personalized fashion experience by leveraging Meta's extensive visual data network across platforms like Instagram and Facebook.

The app's core functionality revolves around digitizing a user's existing wardrobe. Users can connect their Instagram accounts to import previously posted photos or manually add items by taking new photos and videos. The AI then analyzes this data to suggest outfit combinations tailored to the user's personal style. Furthermore, users can virtually try on these outfits on a realistic digital model matched to their specific body shape and skin tone.

Beyond daily suggestions, Style Sketch offers specialized solutions for trip planning. By entering a destination, trip duration, and planned activities, users receive a day-by-day outfit itinerary. The AI takes weather forecasts and the user's schedule into account to optimize these suggestions. This feature aims to simplify complex preparation processes through intelligent automation.

This launch is part of Meta's broader strategy to accelerate app development. The company is utilizing AI-driven efficiencies to ship niche applications more frequently than in the past. Style Sketch joins other recent Meta AI projects, such as the virtual pet app Waddle World and the video generation tool Vibes. While currently limited to Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand, it remains unclear when Meta plans a wider global rollout.
