Goldman Sachs CEO David Solomon stated on November 9, 2026, that financing demand for artificial intelligence infrastructure remains robust despite recent market volatility. Solomon noted that his firm has been highly active in AI capital markets deals over recent weeks, a trend he expects to continue. This comes at a time when AI-related stocks, particularly those of chipmakers and cloud providers, have faced downward pressure as investors question whether the hundreds of billions of dollars poured into infrastructure will generate adequate returns.

The shifting sentiment has been influenced by concerns that political opposition following the U.S. midterm elections could slow industry expansion. However, Solomon pushed back against resistance to data center construction, arguing that this infrastructure is essential for the U.S. economy. He warned that if such facilities are not built domestically, the demand for compute power will inevitably shift to locations outside the United States. Solomon reaffirmed his deep belief in the long-term power of AI to drive economic productivity.

The market is also closely watching the potential IPO of AI model lab Anthropic, a process where Goldman Sachs serves as a lead bank. This offering is expected to be a major test of public-market appetite for AI companies. Solomon indicated that the current market environment remains constructive for such offerings. These developments underscore a pivotal moment for the AI ecosystem as it balances massive infrastructure requirements with the need to demonstrate tangible financial returns to investors.
