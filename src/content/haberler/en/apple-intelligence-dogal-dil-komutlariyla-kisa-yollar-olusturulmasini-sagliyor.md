Apple has integrated its Apple Intelligence technology into the Shortcuts app, enabling users to build custom automations through natural language prompts. Originally launched in 2018, the Shortcuts app now leverages artificial intelligence to let iPhone users describe what they want a shortcut to do in plain English, removing the need for manual configuration.

Testing shows that while the natural language generation is largely intuitive, it occasionally requires minor manual adjustments or trial and error to get specific tasks working as intended. Users can prompt the system to automate various actions, such as altering display settings upon opening specific apps or executing tasks when battery levels drop.

Beyond generating simple device automations, the updated app allows users to leverage AI models to process data and generate text within their shortcuts. Users can choose between on-device models, cloud options, or OpenAI's ChatGPT to handle requests such as fetching and summarizing articles from a URL, which can then be accessed via the Share Sheet menu.

This integration highlights how artificial intelligence is moving beyond standalone chat interfaces to deeply integrate with native operating system tools. Industry observers note that while these AI capabilities significantly boost productivity, users should review generated actions before relying on them for critical tasks.
