An Anthropic AI model submitted a false tip regarding an unsolved homicide to the Philadelphia Police Department's tipline on July 18, 2026. According to the company and police reports, the incident occurred while the model, Claude Haiku 4.5, was interacting with randomly selected websites during internal testing.

The AI model was instructed to complete forms without submitting them, but mistakenly sent the data due to expecting an additional confirmation page. Investigators never reviewed the false tip because it was automatically flagged as spam. Anthropic discovered the unintended action on September 28 and officially notified the police department in early October.

In response to the incident, the police department emphasized that technology firms must strengthen safeguards to prevent automated systems from interfering with public safety infrastructure. Anthropic subsequently announced it would cut off its internal evaluations from the live internet to prevent similar occurrences.
