Apple is preparing to elevate artificial intelligence integration in the wearable technology sector. The company's long-rumored smart glasses and a new generation of AirPods equipped with built-in cameras are expected to reach users in 2027. This development, supported by financial reports from Chinese supplier TCB Devices, signals a strategic shift toward AI-centric hardware within Apple's ecosystem.

The significance of these devices lies in integrating 'Visual Intelligence' into daily life, moving AI beyond screens to a layer that perceives and interprets the user's surroundings. Specifically, the camera-equipped AirPods have the potential to analyze objects or environments the user sees and provide real-time audio feedback via AI. This represents a step toward reducing reliance on smartphones and making digital assistants an integral part of the physical world.

Reliable industry sources and leaks indicate that these products are likely to launch toward the end of 2027. Apple's move reflects a search for a more accessible and functional solution in the wearable market following the Apple Vision Pro. Promotional clips previously discovered within macOS 26 code confirm that this technology is not merely in the conceptual stage but is in an advanced phase of development. The company aims to expand its AI-powered hardware portfolio by integrating these wearables into its ecosystem following the release of next-generation phones like the iPhone 20 Pro and iPhone Duo 2.

Data from the supply chain indicates that Apple's investments in semiconductors and optical communication will support the high processing power required by these next-generation devices. TCB Devices stated that it expects growth in areas such as precision welding and machine vision inspection through this collaboration, highlighting the meticulous nature and technological depth of Apple's hardware production.
