A new step has been taken away from the traditional text-generation approach in the artificial intelligence landscape. The vLLM Semantic Router research team has announced Decision 3.0, an open-source family of decision models designed to return probabilities and scores directly instead of long text outputs.

Available in five different sizes ranging from 0.8 billion to 27 billion parameters, these models can process text, JSON, and image inputs simultaneously. Instead of generating verbose answers, they evaluate typed questions and return yes, no, multiple-choice, or scaled probabilities. This pattern drastically speeds up request routing and guardrails in software development.

The largest 27-billion parameter model leads both text and vision evaluation tables, while a mid-sized 9-billion parameter version outperforms the previous generation's largest models. Released under the Apache-2.0 license, these tools aim to significantly reduce inference latency and computational overhead for developers worldwide.
