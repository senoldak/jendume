Belgian startup Ravical, which develops AI software for accounting and tax firms, has successfully closed a seed funding round. The round was led by MMC Ventures, with participation from existing backers Lakestar and Pitchdrive, alongside new investor Shapers.

According to the company, more than one hundred accounting firms in Europe currently use the platform. Ravical plans to allocate the new funds toward expanding its presence in core markets such as Belgium, Germany, the Netherlands, and the UK, while also scaling across Europe and launching in the US.

The core idea behind the product is to ensure that a firm's institutional knowledge does not rely solely on a few individuals. The platform captures how experienced staff evaluate and advise on cases, allowing its AI to apply that same approach across all clients so managers can monitor risks and opportunities.

Company leadership points out that accounting firms can no longer rely purely on hiring to grow. Demand for services is increasing while the number of accounting graduates and skilled professionals is declining. Furthermore, research indicates that many small and medium-sized businesses act on AI-generated advice without consulting their accountants.

Ravical was founded by Joris Van Der Gucht, Ken Bastiaensen, and Vandermarliere. Investors note that unlike point solutions that handle only a single task, Ravical aims to operate across an entire firm, addressing a gap in the accounting technology market.
