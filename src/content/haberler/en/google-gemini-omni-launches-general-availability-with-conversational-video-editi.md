Google has introduced Gemini Omni, a new addition to its generative model family designed to process text, image, audio, and video inputs natively within a single architecture. First announced in May 2026, the model avoids routing requests through disconnected media tools, resulting in more cohesive and consistent outputs across all generated media formats.

The system's standout feature is conversational editing. Rather than rewriting a full prompt to change a single detail in a generated video, users can apply modifications through continuous dialogue while the model preserves characters, physics, and scene continuity throughout the session, completely removing the need to start over from scratch.

Announced by Google DeepMind's Koray Kavukcuoglu, the model supports text-to-video, image-to-video animation, and frame interpolation. The general availability release introduced features such as scene extension, allowing clips to be lengthened in ten-second increments up to a total of forty seconds, alongside a more cost-effective 360p draft mode for rapid iteration before final rendering.

Consumers can access the model via the Gemini app and Google Flow, while developers can integrate it through the Gemini API. It is also available at no cost through YouTube Shorts and YouTube Create. This capability reduces friction in digital media production, lowers operational barriers, and gives creators greater control over their final video projects.
