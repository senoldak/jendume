Google has integrated its Duplex technology, first introduced in 2018, with the Gemini model to significantly enhance autonomous calling capabilities. Originally designed for specific tasks like restaurant reservations, the system now leverages Gemini’s architecture to manage complex daily chores autonomously. By calling businesses on behalf of users, the system handles tasks such as booking appointments, checking product availability, or verifying service hours without requiring human intervention.

This development marks a shift where artificial intelligence moves beyond being a text-generation tool to becoming an agent capable of taking action in the physical world. Previously, separate models had to be trained for every specific task type. With the new Gemini-based architecture, the system can understand complex requests in natural language and adapt to unexpected scenarios. For instance, during a service appointment call, it can evaluate alternative times suggested by the receptionist or accommodate specific requests.

The evolution of this stack reflects three distinct eras in artificial intelligence. In the early days, before modern language models, the system relied on custom-trained models for narrow domains. The introduction of models like LaMDA in 2022 allowed for greater flexibility. The entire process is unified under Gemini. This technology extends beyond simple calls, integrating with features like "Call Screen" and "Hold for Me" on Pixel devices to filter unknown callers and manage hold queues.

The scale of this infrastructure is substantial. As of 2023, business information sourced from these automated calls had been displayed over one trillion times across search and map services. This demonstrates that artificial intelligence is evolving from a mere software tool into a massive data and service bridge that streamlines the flow of daily life.
