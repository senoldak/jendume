US President Donald Trump has initiated a process aimed at completely removing the long-used artificial intelligence expression from official documents. Within the scope of the steps taken, it is argued that the super intelligence definition should henceforth be accepted as the standard. In official statements, it is claimed that the current concept is misleading and that preferring the new expression across all government levels will be made mandatory. This situation moves the terminology and definition discussions within the sector to a different dimension.

Aiming to accelerate the process, the administration continues its criticisms particularly directed at certain press organizations. Shares made through social media channels show that the change in question does not only carry the nature of a suggestion and will become an administrative rule. While statements regarding the possibility of facing negative administrative approaches for those who continue to use the old term are met with concern, it is stated that the concept of super intelligence is chosen to define advanced systems capable of surpassing human intelligence.

Authorities argue that this move is not just a name change, but better reflects the capacity reached by technology. While it is defended that old terms are now insufficient, experts and industry representatives point out that this sudden transition may create confusion in standards. Replacing concepts accepted in international literature may require the reorganization of both academic and commercial documents from scratch.

These decisions closely concern not only structures within the US but also international organizations having commercial ties with US-based technology companies. In the upcoming period, which official documents will be revised and how the new expression will find a response in practice are being closely monitored. While it is stated that the White House wing will not take a step back on this issue and will follow the implementation, how the regulations will resonate in the technology world continues to be a subject of curiosity.
