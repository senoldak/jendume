French AI lab Mistral has officially released the weights for Mistral Large 4 (ML4), its latest flagship model nicknamed "Le Chonk." Following its initial preview earlier in October, the 1-trillion-parameter model is now available for broader use, marking a significant milestone for Western open-source AI. By making the model's weights public, Mistral is positioning itself as a leader in the "Sovereign AI" movement, challenging the dominance of proprietary models from the US.

ML4 distinguishes itself through exceptional performance in agentic coding and defensive cybersecurity. In recent benchmarks, the model has climbed to the top of the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, rivaling the world's most capable models. A key technical achievement is its efficiency; ML4 performs complex coding tasks using 47% fewer tokens on average than its closest open-weight competitors, making it significantly more cost-effective for developers to run at scale.

In the realm of cybersecurity, ML4 is designed to assist in vulnerability detection and patching without breaking existing code. Mistral has implemented robust safety guardrails, achieving a 96.6% refusal rate for malicious cyber requests while maintaining high performance for legitimate defensive tasks. To provide flexibility, the model features three distinct reasoning modes—low, medium, and high—allowing users to balance computational cost with the precision required for specific tasks.

The model was trained from scratch over two months using 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral’s European data centers. This localized training ensures a clear lineage and provides a trusted foundation for governments and enterprises with strict data residency requirements. Mistral’s commitment to open weights aims to democratize AI security tools, allowing researchers and institutions to own and customize their AI infrastructure without relying on external providers.
