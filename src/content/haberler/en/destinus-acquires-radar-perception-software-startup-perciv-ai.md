Destinus has acquired Delft-based radar perception startup Perciv AI to expand its sensing capabilities for autonomous systems. According to the official announcement, Perciv AI will serve as the group's radar perception center, and the founding team will continue to manage operations. The parties did not disclose the financial terms of the agreement.

Perciv AI develops software that detects and tracks objects by processing radar data using neural network models. The company's work covers targets with low radar cross-sections, and the software offers radar-based positioning support when global satellite navigation system signals are weak or unavailable. This technology will be integrated with Destinus's existing optical sensing and navigation efforts.

The startup's product lineup includes EgoTrack, which calculates the vehicle's own movement using radar data, and FreeRoad, which determines usable areas of the environment. Additionally, product families focus on combining radar information with standard or thermal camera imagery. The acquisition announcement does not share completed field test results for each of these solutions.

This acquisition marks Destinus's third buyout in the Netherlands, following previous additions such as OPRA assets and Aerialtronics. Perciv AI previously raised a seed investment led by DayOne Capital and Keen Venture Partners, with participation from Vinci Venture Capital.

Destinus founder Mikhail Kokorich stated that radar perception must be developed as part of the overall system, bringing critical technology under their control. Founded by Andras Palffy, Balazs Szekeres, and Srimannarayana Baratam with backgrounds from Delft University of Technology, the team will directly join Destinus's product development efforts with the goal of expanding the Delft team.
