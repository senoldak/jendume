Türkiye Yapay Zeka İnisiyatifi added 43 new companies to its startup map as part of the October 2026 update. Although 21 companies that ceased operations were removed from the list, the total number of startups reached 504, crossing the 500 threshold for the first time. Prepared since 2017, this list includes companies based in Turkey as well as foreign-based startups with Turkish founders.

Of the 43 newly added startups with known establishment years, 25 started operating in 2025 or 2026. The centers of the new startups span across Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir, Bursa, Antalya, Samsun, Bolu, and Gebze, as well as international locations like New York, Dubai, and Amsterdam.

The October update saw the highest number of new additions in the 'Agentic AI' category, which plans steps to execute tasks. This field welcomed 10 new startups, while conversational tools and legal technologies gained five each, and human resources and predictive tools added four each. New legal tech startups focus on helping lawyers research court decisions and review contract risks, while HR tools manage recruitment processes.

Can Sinemli, General Manager of Türkiye Yapay Zeka İnisiyatifi, pointed out not only the numerical increase but also the diversification in working areas. The map, which aims to help institutions, investors, and entrepreneurs track these companies, reveals the expanding use cases of artificial intelligence in the business world.
