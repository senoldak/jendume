Online narratives and rumors can rapidly spiral into major public controversies for organizations worldwide. Gudea, a startup focusing on internet narrative tracking, is leveraging artificial intelligence to help decision-makers identify brewing trouble before it reaches a tipping point.

Unlike legacy social listening platforms that merely measure post volume and hashtags, the system analyzes behavioral patterns and the pathways through which information travels across hundreds of mainstream and fringe websites. The founders adapted tactics originally inspired by intelligence methodologies to map out digital influence.

Founded in 2023, the startup recently secured $7 million in seed funding, bringing its total backing to approximately $15 million. With nearly 40 clients ranging from major enterprises to government agencies, the company aims to address the surge in inauthentic activity fueled by accessible AI generation tools.
