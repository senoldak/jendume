Pioneering organizations across Europe are deploying NVIDIA Earth-2 open AI models to power real-time weather and climate predictions. Accurate meteorological data is essential for energy producers, insurance providers, and logistics managers to adapt effectively to shifting environmental conditions. NVIDIA Earth-2 provides developers and researchers with a comprehensive suite of open artificial intelligence models and tools that can be customized with proprietary data to support global weather forecasting and detailed regional predictions.

During the Berlin technology conference, NVIDIA showcased an interactive meteorological demonstration featuring a fully operational StormScope EU pipeline developed in partnership with Earthmover. StormScope EU represents the latest regional addition to the Earth-2 family, specifically trained on European climate data. It leverages EUMETSAT satellite observations to forecast changing cloud conditions across 16 distinct Meteosat channels at consistent 10-minute intervals. Earthmover manages and serves these analytical results directly as map tiles, enabling visitors to explore predictive weather patterns across Europe seamlessly without requiring the download of massive datasets.

Various industrial and academic organizations are leveraging these foundational capabilities to address specialized regional challenges. AXA utilizes Earth-2 models to study regional cyclone risks across more than 1,000 simulated historical seasons. Vaisala Xweather integrates Earth-2 architectures with its own proprietary observations to upgrade its weather intelligence platforms. University of Manchester researchers adapted the underlying models for local air-quality modeling, while TotalEnergies evaluates solar-energy forecasting potentials and Space42 developed specialized height-estimation applications.
