Produced by Turkish production studio Ay Yapım, Castle Walls has premiered on a major streaming platform as the first end-to-end AI-created series. Utilizing a blend of generative video tools, the show follows the arrival of Hasan Sabbah in Alamut and his interactions with the warrior Azazil. Across its first two episodes, the series presents heavily stylized visuals generated through complex prompting, drawing immediate attention from critics and viewers alike.

While the underlying technology promises to profoundly alter the motion picture industry, early productions like this often invite sharp commentary. Observers compare the current phase of AI video to early silent cinema—revolutionary in its time but visually primitive compared to what follows. However, public reception tends to lean toward skepticism, with viewers questioning whether synthetic tools threaten to displace human-authored artistry.

Despite being entirely driven by generative video tools, the production did incorporate human elements. The script was penned by human screenwriters Kerem Deren and Çisil Hazal Tenim, and human voice actors were employed. The studio maintains that the project did not bypass human skill but rather used artificial intelligence to amplify it, though the resulting dialogue and aesthetic choices remain subjects of debate.

As studios experiment with automated filmmaking, Castle Walls stands as an early testament to the capabilities and current limitations of generative media. The overarching challenge remains finding a reciprocal collaboration where algorithms enhance rather than replace human creative expression.
