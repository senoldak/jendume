Kaszek Ventures has launched a new $650 million fund aimed at strengthening the artificial intelligence ecosystem in Latin America. This capital raise represents one of the largest early-stage funds ever secured in the region's technology sector. The firm intends to use these resources to support 20 to 30 startups, primarily based in Brazil and Mexico, that are building with AI technologies. The investment focus remains on what the firm terms the "application layer" of AI. This involves using language models to improve business processes in sectors such as finance, healthcare, and online marketplaces. Given that many businesses in the region still rely on manual processes, AI solutions hold significant potential for driving productivity gains. Additionally, infrastructure investments, such as data centers and chips, are on the fund's radar. This fundraising is notable given the slowdown in the global venture capital market. While only 14 venture funds closed in Latin America during the first nine months of this year, compared to 68 in all of 2023, Kaszek's $650 million success proves that AI-focused startups still attract significant investor interest despite challenging market conditions. Founded in 2011 by former MercadoLibre executives, Kaszek Ventures has become one of the most prominent players in the regional tech ecosystem. Having backed giants like Nubank and QuintoAndar, the firm now aims to lead the AI transformation with this new fund. Supported by major institutions such as Washington University in St. Louis and the Wellcome Trust, this fund provides significant capital for the region's technological future.
