Visa and Tencent have launched a strategic partnership to enable AI agents to play a more active role in the digital shopping landscape. The two companies have signed a memorandum of understanding to develop a new infrastructure that will facilitate product discovery, evaluation, and payment processes for users. This collaboration aims to improve the payment experience for both users and businesses, particularly in cross-border trade.

At the heart of this system lies Visa’s "Intelligent Commerce," an AI-driven trade solution. This technology aims to allow smart agents, acting on behalf of users, to discover products during shopping and complete payment processes with the necessary authorizations. However, the companies emphasize that this process is not a fully autonomous and uncontrolled spending mechanism; rather, user control and a governance framework are fundamental parts of this structure.

Within the scope of the partnership, plans are in place to strengthen the integration between Tencent’s "TenPay Global" service and the Visa network. Additionally, there is a goal to adapt "Midas," Tencent’s service used for global payments in the gaming sector, to AI-powered commercial scenarios. This will enable developers to design more seamless payment experiences that work with AI agents.

Currently, this initiative is at the memorandum of understanding stage. This means there is no single product being launched immediately that grants new features to all Visa cards or is available in every country. The implementation of these plans will depend on regulatory requirements in relevant markets and the companies' rollout schedules. By combining cloud and AI services with payment infrastructure, the companies aim to shape the future of digital commerce. Concrete details regarding which countries and when users will be able to access these services will become clearer with future product announcements.
