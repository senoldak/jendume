OpenAI has completely reversed its plans to cut usage limits on ChatGPT Pro subscriptions. Instead, the company has doubled the daily usage capacity across all paid tiers. The daily quota determines how many operations a subscription can execute in a given timeframe, directly impacting productivity for intensive users.

Previously, a 50 percent reduction was planned for the $200 monthly tier, which was scheduled to start on October 29, 2026, without any reduction in the monthly fee. This sparked immediate backlash from creators and developers. In response, OpenAI quickly changed course, rolling out new limits and completely shelving the planned cuts.

The capacity increase applies to the $100, $200, and $500 monthly plans. As a result, heavy users can now work with twice the previous capacity for their daily tasks. This extra capacity is especially useful for heavy artificial intelligence workloads and complex projects.

This boost is most notable for ChatGPT Work and Codex users, as both tools share a pool under a single account. Programmers can run long coding sessions, while creative teams can analyze large data files without hitting daily limits. Additionally, OpenAI announced that usage rights for Dots AI assistants will remain separate and will not consume the main Work or Codex quota.

The company has also kept monthly prices at their current levels, giving users double the power at no extra cost. This reversal shows that listening to user feedback helps retain the most loyal customers who expect high quality from premium features.
