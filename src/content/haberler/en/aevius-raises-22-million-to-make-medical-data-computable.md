Aevius, a clinical AI startup, has secured $22 million in seed funding to make medical data more computable and actionable for healthcare providers. Led by Insight Partners, this investment round will accelerate the company’s model development, product design, and team expansion. Spun out of the Mass General Brigham hospital system, the startup aims to transform how health systems utilize complex patient data. With a mission to make medicine computable, the company builds a refreshable, multimodal representation of a patient’s health status.

By integrating both structured and unstructured medical data, the system creates a dynamic picture that updates as new information becomes available. Unlike traditional AI agents that often piece together patient information from fragmented text, this approach maintains a comprehensive view of a patient’s entire history. Care teams and researchers can forecast outcomes, identify clinically similar patients, and track health trajectories over time without needing to restart their analysis for every new question.

The founding team brings significant experience, having previously established Modella AI, a cancer-focused medical startup that was acquired by AstraZeneca. The team is now focusing on providing solutions for health systems, payers, and life sciences companies. The company’s scientific advisory board is chaired by cardiologist and digital medicine researcher Eric Topol, who emphasizes that the value of medical AI will be measured by its ability to promote accuracy in clinical settings.

Clinical data is widely considered one of the most challenging types of information to leverage effectively. By creating a native, computable representation of patient history, Aevius aims to streamline decision-making processes for healthcare organizations. Following this funding round, the company plans to scale its engineering and clinical staff to support its commercial deployment. The company intends to integrate AI into medical practice to move beyond simple data storage toward active, intelligent clinical support.
