Adobe announced a significant expansion of its Firefly AI Assistant at the MAX conference in Miami, introducing autonomous agent capabilities designed to streamline creative workflows. The updated assistant can now generate a comprehensive plan for multi-step tasks before execution. Users have the option to review and edit these proposed workflows or manually select specific AI models for different parts of the project, providing a balance between automation and control.

A key highlight of the announcement is the preview of "always-on" agents. These agents are designed to handle recurring, time-consuming tasks in the background, such as organizing new footage, checking for audio issues, and preparing content for various distribution channels. For instance, in a video production scenario, an agent can automatically apply color presets and remove duplicate clips as files are uploaded, allowing the editor to start with a clean workspace.

Adobe is also positioning Firefly as an open creative studio by integrating third-party models. In addition to Adobe's proprietary models, the platform now supports tools from Google, OpenAI, ByteDance, and ElevenLabs. This multi-model approach allows creators to leverage the specific strengths of different AI architectures within a single interface. The company emphasizes that this integration maintains the professional standards and security that Creative Cloud users expect.

On the mobile front, Adobe is bringing agentic features to the Premiere mobile app, enabling users to create an initial edit from raw footage using natural language commands. According to Adobe's Creators' Toolkit Report, while 78% of AI-using creators say the technology has accelerated their business growth, 77% still believe the final creative decision should remain with the human. These new agents are built to support this philosophy by acting as assistants that handle complexity while leaving the final say to the creator.
