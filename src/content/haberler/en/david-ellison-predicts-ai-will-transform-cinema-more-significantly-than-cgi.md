Paramount Skydance executive David Ellison shared his views on how artificial intelligence technologies could fundamentally transform the film industry during a meeting where the company's financial results were evaluated. Ellison emphasized that the creative opportunities offered by this new technology are at a level previously unseen in modern filmmaking. He specifically noted that artificial intelligence would be a key tool in reducing film production costs and bringing more original stories to the big screen.

Ellison compared the potential effects of artificial intelligence on the sector with the computer-generated imagery (CGI) technologies that entered cinema in the early 1990s. He recalled how digital image-combining techniques at that time allowed for the filming of scenes that were previously unimaginable. He cited the success of productions like Jurassic Park and Terminator 2 as examples of how decisive these technologies were. According to Ellison, the impact of artificial intelligence on film production processes will not be more limited than the revolution created by CGI technology; on the contrary, he predicts that this impact will be much more comprehensive.

Confirming that Paramount Skydance is developing its own proprietary artificial intelligence tools, Ellison stated that this technology would overhaul the company's workflows and operating models. However, the executive emphasized that this transformation does not mean replacing artists, and that human creativity will continue to maintain its fundamental value in the film industry. The simplification of production processes will enable creative teams to realize visions they could not previously execute due to budget or technical constraints. The acceleration of technological infrastructure investments across the sector indicates that artificial intelligence is becoming not just a tool, but a foundational building block shaping the future of cinema.
