Nvidia Corp. is reportedly in early-stage talks to acquire open-weight AI model developer Reflection AI Inc. or increase its financial stake in the startup. According to reports surfacing in October 2026, an agreement could potentially be reached within weeks, though negotiations remain ongoing and fluid.

Several deal structures are currently being considered, ranging from an outright acquisition or equity increase to an acquihire arrangement where Nvidia would license the startup's technology and hire its staff while avoiding lengthy regulatory hurdles.

Reflection AI recently unveiled its first open-weight model named Beam, a 501-billion-parameter system designed to compete in coding and agentic workflows. Having previously secured significant backing from major chipmakers and venture capital funds, the startup continues to scale its computing power through high-capacity data center partnerships.
