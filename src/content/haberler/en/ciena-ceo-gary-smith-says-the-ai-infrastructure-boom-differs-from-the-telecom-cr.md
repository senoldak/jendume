Ciena Chief Executive Officer Gary Smith stated that the current artificial intelligence build-up represents a distinct cycle compared to the telecom crash that followed the fibre boom. Smith, who has led Ciena since May 2001, noted that while the AI era will involve losing bets, the differences between the two booms outweigh their similarities. He emphasized that the current expansion must be judged on its own terms.

Smith outlined AI demand as a series of waves. The initial phase links data centres, followed by inference where businesses deploy trained models. AI agents and robotics will emerge in subsequent stages. He emphasized that people have grossly underestimated the required infrastructure to sustain these technological advancements.

Ciena has developed 3-nanometre WaveLogic chips capable of transmitting 1.6 terabits per second over a single fibre strand, with a 2-nanometre version currently in development. Unlike the past telecom boom where a vast majority of laid fibre went unused, current buyers include highly profitable cloud giants, according to Smith, who noted strong ongoing demand.

Ciena expects a compound annual growth rate of 30 percent from fiscal 2026 to 2029 and anticipates ending fiscal 2026 with an order backlog of about $10 billion. The company's current market value stands at approximately $45.75 billion, and executives report they have continuously struggled to keep up with incoming market demand.
