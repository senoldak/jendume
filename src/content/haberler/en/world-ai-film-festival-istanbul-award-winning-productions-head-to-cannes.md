The Istanbul leg of the World AI Film Festival, hosted by AI Yapım, has concluded with awards given to seven distinct productions. The festival focused on the costs of AI-supported content production, international distribution opportunities, and cinema support funds.

A total of 124 projects applied to the festival's competition. The entries were evaluated across six categories including animation, advertisement, short film, mini-series, series, and special selections. Following the evaluations, seven productions secured awards and earned the right to participate in the international competition to be held in Cannes.

During the festival, the upcoming project Vartolu Geri Döndü, set to air on Prime Video, was also highlighted. Director Umut Aral discussed the preparation and production process of the project, while its first teaser trailer was screened. AI Yapım Partner Yüce Zerey addressed the economic dimension of AI-driven production through token usage during his presentation.

BetaSeries Sales Director Rémi Tereszkiewicz focused on the international distribution processes of AI-generated content. Additionally, the event featured the screening of the commemorative film İlelebet. The award-winning works included Whales Don't Eat Fish in animation and Doyuyo's Irresisstable Taste Meets AI in advertising.

Other winning titles included Kız Kaçırma and Hail Caesar! in short film, Evolution-Denied in mini-series, The Irrigation System During the Timurid Period in series, and From Catstantinople to Catstanbul in the special category. These productions representing Turkey at the international festival mark a significant step for AI integration in the sector.
