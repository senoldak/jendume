AI company Anthropic is actively fighting against large-scale distillation attacks where companies systematically copy an artificial intelligence model's capabilities to train their own systems. Anthropic announced that its new security measures have successfully cut the extraction of internal reasoning tokens by 90 to 99 percent.

According to the company, attackers leverage networks consisting of hundreds of thousands of fake accounts to siphon model capabilities. Anthropic has pointed to firms like Zhipu and Moonshot as participants in these unauthorized data extraction efforts, raising concerns about where these copied capabilities might end up.

In response, Anthropic has restricted access to Claude's internal reasoning steps, deployed classifiers to detect extraction attempts, and automatically banned tens of thousands of flagged accounts. The company is now urging other AI developers to adopt similar safeguards to protect the integrity of advanced models.
