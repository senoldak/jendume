Reliable Robotics has secured 115 million dollars in new funding to further develop its autonomous flight systems. The package announced by the company extends its previous Series D investment round, pushing the company's valuation past the 1 billion dollar mark. According to the official financial disclosure, the package includes a 25 million dollar term loan provided by Silicon Valley Bank, bringing the total announced in the Series D round to 275 million dollars. The funding round was led by existing investor Nimble Partners.

The company will use the funds to expand its aircraft fleet, production facilities, and engineering team. As part of this expansion, two new offices are opening in Seattle and San Diego, with the Seattle operations focusing heavily on radar programs. Reliable states that its employee count has exceeded 250, and the new financing serves as the primary source for this technical and operational scaling.

The technology aims to equip aircraft with automated taxi, takeoff, flight management, and landing capabilities. This approach relies on adding automation software to current aviation infrastructure rather than manufacturing a completely new aircraft body. The Reliable Autonomy System is undergoing certification for commercial use in the US on the Cessna 208B Grand Caravan, though the company notes that all commercial approvals are not yet fully finalized.

Financial details differentiate between the investment round amount, the loan, and the company valuation. The investment round represents raised capital, the loan carries repayment obligations, and the valuation reflects the total worth assigned during the investment process. This new funding directly supports ongoing hardware and software development efforts.
