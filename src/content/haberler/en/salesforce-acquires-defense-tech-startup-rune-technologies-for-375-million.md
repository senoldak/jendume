Defense technology startup Rune Technologies, which builds artificial intelligence-powered logistics software, is being acquired by Salesforce to expand its capabilities in military and enterprise domains. Under the terms of the agreement, the transaction will be completed for 375 million dollars in cash and stock. Established to help the United States military forecast supply needs and manage field logistics operations, the startup provides software that optimizes material planning for military units. Salesforce conducts its national security and defense operations through a dedicated unit called Missionforce. This unit previously recorded significant growth, bolstered by a ten-year, 5.6 billion dollar contract signed with the United States Army. By integrating field logistics data collected by Rune into the Missionforce ecosystem, Salesforce aims to move beyond back-office planning and directly support battlefield operations. In addition, Rune Technologies had previously raised a total of 30 million dollars from investors including Andreessen Horowitz, Point72 Ventures, and CIA-backed venture capital firm IQT, while reaching a 127 million dollar valuation during its Series A round led by Human Capital. Salesforce has also accelerated its merger and acquisition activities throughout the year, having reached agreements to acquire AI agent platform Fin for 3.6 billion dollars and Qualified for 1.2 billion dollars, alongside an agreement to acquire Listen Labs.
