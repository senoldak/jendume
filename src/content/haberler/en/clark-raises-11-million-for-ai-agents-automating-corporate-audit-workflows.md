New York-based startup Clark has secured $11 million in a seed funding round to advance its AI agents capable of handling complex audit tasks. The round was led by Touring Capital, with participation from Marathon Management Partners, Rerail, and Sofeon Ventures. Formerly known as uiAgent, the company has now reached a total funding milestone of $16 million following this latest injection of capital.

The company's new AI agents, branded as 'Clerks,' operate similarly to junior auditors within a firm. These digital entities are programmed to review financial documents, execute specific audit tests, and prepare necessary workpapers. A key feature of their autonomy is the ability to identify missing information; if a document is absent, the Clerk independently contacts the client to request it and resumes work once the file is received. This level of automation aims to streamline the traditionally labor-intensive auditing process.

One of Clark's most significant market differentiators is its 100% accuracy guarantee. In an industry where even the 'Big Four' accounting firms have faced scrutiny over AI-generated errors, Clark is backing its technology with a formal commitment to precision. Founder and CEO Enes Witwit stated that a promise of accuracy is only meaningful when supported by financial weight. Currently, more than 35 accounting firms, including prominent names like CliftonLarsonAllen and Aprio, have integrated these AI tools into their operations.

While the AI agents handle the heavy lifting of data processing and testing, human auditors remain responsible for high-level judgment calls and signing off on final opinions. This hybrid model ensures that while efficiency is maximized through automation, professional accountability remains with human experts. Clark joins a growing sector of startups, such as Andera and Benford, that are challenging the traditional hourly-billing model of the audit industry by introducing scalable, software-driven solutions.
