With artificial intelligence-enabled fraud increasing across digital platforms, new security measures are emerging to protect sensitive transactions. World has introduced a new line of defense tailored for the banking and finance sector. The newly launched offering bundles an identity wallet, a Zoom integration, and a tool for verifying AI agents into a single package aimed at financial institutions. This marks the first time the company has built a version of its identity verification around a specific industry.

The timing of the release highlights the growing threat of AI-driven financial crimes. According to Deloitte estimates, U.S. losses from fraud could climb significantly over the coming years. In banking and asset management, a single spoofed customer request or impersonated call can lead directly to sensitive data exposure, unauthorized trades, or large financial transfers. World's new solution addresses this problem by helping institutions establish that a real human is present during digital interactions.

Video calls represent an immediate front for these security challenges. The newly available Zoom integration allows meeting participants to confirm in real time that the person they are speaking with is a genuine human rather than a deepfake. Additionally, the platform addresses the fast-growing use of AI agents acting on behalf of customers. The agent verification tool lets users establish proofs about themselves and securely delegate them to AI agents interacting with financial platforms.

The entire verification framework relies on quantum-secure cryptography to keep proofs secure as computing capabilities advance. Design partners such as Bain Capital, VanEck Funds, OpenExchange, and Outtake are actively exploring potential use cases within financial services. Originally conceived by Sam Altman, Max Novendstern, and Alex Blania, World continues to expand its global network of verified humans as enterprise adoption grows.
