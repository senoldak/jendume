Apple has officially opened Siri AI to all app developers through a new Developer Preview. Available in the betas for iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, and macOS 27.3, this update allows any application to integrate its content and actions directly into the Siri ecosystem. Previously, apps were restricted to specific categories defined by Apple. Now, developers can utilize 'App Intents' to expose custom actions, such as tracking a delivery, booking a reservation, or placing an order, making Siri a much more versatile tool for daily tasks.

A key feature of this update is the introduction of 'skills,' which enable developers to teach Siri AI how to combine multiple actions into a single request. Users will no longer be required to explicitly name an app if it is already open on the screen or running a Live Activity; Siri will intelligently infer the context. Apple has already implemented these capabilities across 15 of its native apps, including Mail, Maps, and Photos, allowing users to perform complex searches like finding a specific location visited in the previous week or archiving old files.

In a significant strategic shift, Apple also confirmed that expanded access to server-side AI features will eventually require a fee. Features that rely on Apple’s cloud infrastructure, including Siri AI, advanced photo editing tools, and Image Playground, will be subject to daily usage limits. While the basic service remains part of the ecosystem, 'expanded access' for heavy users will be offered as a paid service in the future. The specific pricing and timeline for these fees have not yet been disclosed.

While the preview is currently for testing, the first integrations are expected to reach the general public in a few months. However, Siri AI remains unavailable on iOS and iPadOS within the European Union as Apple continues to navigate regulatory requirements. Additionally, the features are restricted for users under the age of 13. This rollout represents Apple's broader ambition to turn Siri into a proactive assistant capable of navigating the entire digital landscape of a user's device.
