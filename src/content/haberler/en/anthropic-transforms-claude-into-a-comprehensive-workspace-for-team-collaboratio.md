Anthropic is evolving its Claude platform from a text-generation tool into a comprehensive workspace designed for team collaboration. With the latest updates, users can now create and edit spreadsheets directly within Claude, utilizing AI assistance to manage complex formulas and multiple tabs. Additionally, the platform introduces a whiteboard feature for brainstorming and the Claude Data tool, which connects to services like Snowflake and Salesforce to generate data visualizations.

To further enhance collaborative capabilities, Anthropic has launched Interactive Connectors. This feature allows major enterprise software providers, including Adobe, Atlassian, and Salesforce, to embed their applications directly into the Claude interface. This structure enables users to manage their workflows from a single hub without switching between different applications, following a plugin-style integration model.

Furthermore, Anthropic announced Claude Meetings, a feature designed to join calls, take notes, and update documents in real-time. Currently available in a limited beta for Google Meet, this feature supports live whiteboard interactions during meetings. While support for Microsoft Teams and Zoom is not yet available, the company is prioritizing enterprise needs by introducing advanced security controls, such as audit logs, isolated virtual machines, and a compliance API. These updates represent a significant step in integrating AI deeper into corporate workflows, aiming to reduce the operational burden on employees.
