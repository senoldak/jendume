Alibaba's Qwen research team has unveiled QwenGyre, an elastic reinforcement learning framework built to tackle the challenges of training long-horizon AI agents. Traditional training setups often suffer from idle hardware and slow execution times when agents spend hours running complex tasks, such as generating entire software repositories from specifications.

QwenGyre solves this by dynamically shifting idle GPUs between rollout and training phases without stopping live agents. Using an advanced scheduler and trajectory processor, the system efficiently handles executions running for hours and processing vast amounts of data. This elastic approach eliminates bottlenecks, resulting in end-to-end speedups of up to 1.85x compared to conventional colocate setups.

Tested on large-scale models, the framework improved performance benchmarks significantly while reducing training time. As AI agents increasingly take on multi-step, complex responsibilities like automated software engineering, innovations like QwenGyre pave the way for faster, more capable, and reliable autonomous systems.
