Apple has shared new language updates regarding its artificial intelligence-powered assistant, Siri AI. The company announced that the assistant will receive Turkish language support by the end of the year. According to the official statement, Traditional Chinese, Norwegian, and Vietnamese will also be added to the lineup of supported languages alongside Turkish.

As part of the 27.2 software updates, Siri AI has already been made available in French, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and Portuguese. When the assistant first launched in beta, it exclusively supported the English language.

The company has not yet shared the exact date or the specific software update through which Turkish support will arrive. Industry reports suggest that the new languages could roll out with updates expected in December, though this information has not been officially confirmed by Apple.

Powered by Apple Intelligence, Siri AI helps users find information within messages, emails, and photos by understanding personal context. The assistant can execute actions across applications, such as drafting an email or editing and sharing multiple photos. Thanks to its on-screen awareness, it can also answer questions and perform tasks regarding content currently displayed on the screen.

Siri AI is currently available in beta on version 27.2 across compatible operating systems. Accessing the assistant requires a device where Apple Intelligence is enabled and set to a supported language. While Apple Intelligence already supports Turkish, this announcement sets a timeline specifically for Siri's upcoming Turkish language capabilities.
