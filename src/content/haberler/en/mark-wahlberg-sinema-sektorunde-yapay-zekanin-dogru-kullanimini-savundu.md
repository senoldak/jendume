Actor and director Mark Wahlberg recently shared his views on the role of artificial intelligence in the film industry during a technology event. While noting that Hollywood remains divided over the technology, Wahlberg emphasized that AI has a valid place in filmmaking as long as it is applied in the right way. He stated that tools should not be used to generate automated scripts or replace real actors, but rather to enhance production aspects such as stadium settings and large-scale background details.

Wahlberg revealed plans to potentially utilize visual enhancement technologies for an upcoming biographical film project in which he will star. He stressed that storytelling must remain at the absolute heart of filmmaking, contrasting modern complex visual effects with the importance of genuine narrative structures. Having worked on major effects-driven movies in the past, he noted that focusing on real stories takes precedence over pure technological spectacle.

Addressing the topic of digital replicas captured through body and voice scanning facilities, Wahlberg stated he would be open to the concept. He mentioned that in the future, it could be interesting if his children or grandchildren managed his likeness to keep him in quality films. Furthermore, he noted he would not oppose his ongoing television or streaming projects continuing with an AI-generated version of his likeness if audiences desired it.

Concluding his remarks, the actor highlighted that audiences should be entitled to watch what they wish to see, and that emerging technological formats will open up massive opportunities for content consumption as the entertainment landscape continues to evolve.
