Pinterest has launched a new feature allowing parents to limit the amount of AI-generated content their teen children see. This is a rare move for social media platforms in managing the visibility of AI-generated content within user feeds. The rise of images and videos produced by artificial intelligence on social media has caused concern among parents and child advocacy groups. These controls provide parents with the ability to manage their children's digital experiences, offering a filter for how much AI content appears on their screens. Previously, platforms like Snapchat and Meta implemented measures to restrict teens' interactions with AI chatbots.

However, options for limiting AI-generated content encountered in general feeds remained limited. Child advocacy groups have argued that AI-generated content, especially on video platforms, should not be recommended to children. Pinterest took its first steps toward restricting AI-modified content in 2025. Now, the company is moving this control mechanism into an interface that parents can manage directly, aiming to personalize the user experience.

The new feature is being introduced alongside other parental controls, such as stopping auto-playing videos and adjusting notification settings. The parental controls page has also been redesigned to include settings for personalized ads. This approach reflects that platforms are shifting their focus toward user safety and parental control in the age of artificial intelligence. Parents now have the chance to reduce the uncertainties created by AI-generated content by determining what kind of material their children encounter in the digital environment. This development illustrates how technology companies are adopting a more transparent and user-focused approach to the moderation of AI-generated content.
