TCL has unveiled its flagship 5G AI CPE X5 device at the Network X event. Honored with an IFA Innovation Award, the new product aims to deliver a proactive, AI-driven networking experience for home users and small businesses. Powered by an agent-based AI architecture, the device translates natural language user requests into direct actions.

The significance of the device lies in its ability to transform network management from a manual chore into an autonomous optimization process. Dedicated AI engines analyze 5G signal quality to select optimal antenna configurations, achieving up to 51 percent higher download speeds in lab tests compared to standard designs. It supports 3GPP Release 18, offering theoretical peak download speeds of up to 7.01 Gbps.

The system continuously monitors connectivity across Wi-Fi and mobile networks, proactively detecting anomalies and resolving issues through a closed-loop diagnostic process. Local AI-enabled Quality of Service prioritizes latency-sensitive traffic such as gaming and video streaming. Additional parental controls and guest access options provide further management capabilities for home networks.

TCL envisions the device not just as a router, but as a central AI hub for connected living. Its on-device computing capabilities and unified AI operating system aim to keep personal data secure locally while expanding smart ecosystem integrations. The company also showcased other connectivity solutions including the prplWare-certified TCL LINKHUB HH516 5G FWA CPE and the TCL Tbot smart desktop assistant.
