Magentic, a developer of AI agents designed to streamline procurement processes, has secured 18 million dollars in its Series A funding round led by Felicis. The UK-based startup also attracted investments from Borusan Ventures, Sequoia Capital, The Westly Group, and First Momentum Ventures.

Founded in 2024, the company plans to use the new capital to accelerate product development and expand the use cases of its autonomous AI agents. Established by Robin Van Aeken and Odhran O’Donoghue, Magentic automates complex enterprise tasks such as supplier selection, order management, contract negotiations, and invoice tracking.

The startup's AI systems integrate seamlessly with existing workplace tools like email and Microsoft Teams, allowing businesses to adopt the technology without disrupting their current daily workflows. By targeting global enterprises dealing with high-volume purchases, Magentic aims to reduce manual workloads and operational costs.

According to company data, some clients are already managing over a million orders annually through these AI agents, unlocking millions of dollars in potential savings and operational efficiency.
