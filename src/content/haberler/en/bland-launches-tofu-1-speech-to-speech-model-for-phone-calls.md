Bland has announced the release of Tofu-1, a full-duplex speech-to-speech model built specifically for phone calls. The new model targets challenging audio environments such as moving cars, hospital rooms, and weak-signal lines. Unlike traditional voice agents that convert speech to text, pass it through a language model, and synthesize it back into speech, Tofu-1 processes audio directly while reasoning during conversations.

Thanks to this direct processing capability, Tofu-1 can separate background noises, track different speakers, and detect paralinguistic cues such as hesitation, frustration, and urgency. Its full-duplex architecture allows callers to interrupt or correct the model mid-sentence without breaking the flow of the conversation, delivering results only when the work is fully complete.

The company has demonstrated the model's audio understanding through specific testing scenarios, including managing loud background sounds like television audio. Research preview access is currently being rolled out to a limited number of enterprise customers in sectors including banking, healthcare, insurance, and government, with application forms collecting monthly call volume metrics.

This release marks a notable step in the voice AI industry, aiming to eliminate intermediate text transcription layers for faster and more natural phone interactions. Detailed technical specifications, latency numbers, and model size metrics have not yet been published by the company.
