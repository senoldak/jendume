Techable Ventures founder and managing partner Amulya Vishwanath, who previously spent nearly seven years as a product marketer at Nvidia and held engineering roles at companies later acquired by Intel and Molex, recently discussed the future of physical AI and robotics. Addressing the widely anticipated ChatGPT moment for robotics, Vishwanath explained that the industry is currently more in a LEGO era.

She noted that foundational software and building blocks for robotics are being rethought, which is crucial for scaling innovation. According to Vishwanath, developers are currently building basic components such as robotic hands and actuators that will eventually come together like LEGO bricks to create advanced general and purpose-built robots as people continue building and improving them.

Drawing parallels to the internet era and the Industrial Revolution, she emphasized that it is only a matter of time before these elements fall into place. As foundational blocks are rethought to allow innovation to scale, the process reflects historical technological transitions.

Furthermore, she highlighted that human talent remains a critical defining factor and a competitive moat for the U.S. market. By building a strong network through her years of working in the space, she connects founders with top talent to help them stay competitive against major companies like Anthropic and Nvidia, serving as an initial point of strength and support for founders.
