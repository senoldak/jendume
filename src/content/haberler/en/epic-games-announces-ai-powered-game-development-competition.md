Epic Games is preparing to test the limits of artificial intelligence in game development processes. The company announced the Epic AI MegaJAM event under the slogan "Vibe your way to $50k," inviting developers to build game prototypes using generative artificial intelligence tools. The competition is scheduled to take place in November 2026.

The main goal of the event is to help independent developers and small teams condense extended prototyping phases into a single weekend. Participants can build their projects on Unreal Engine or Unreal Editor for Fortnite. The competition features a concept where artificial intelligence is placed directly at the center of the development cycle rather than just serving as a secondary assistant.

Developers can utilize a broad toolset including software script generation, two-dimensional conceptual art, three-dimensional object creation, character dialogue construction, and AI coding assistants. The competition features a total cash prize pool of 65 thousand dollars, alongside grant support of up to 50 thousand dollars provided to successful projects.

Epic Games is taking a more active role in the debated integration of generative AI within the industry. Despite prior criticisms from stakeholders, the company argues that these tools can significantly reduce production costs and time for independent studios. Developers aged 18 and older with an active Epic Games account can participate either from the physical hub or entirely online.
