Unitree has introduced its new humanoid robot, the H2-X. In the promotional video, the robot is shown subduing an attacker, breaking a car door, and lifting an automobile to rescue someone trapped underneath. It also displays flexible movements and kung-fu skills, though the extent to which it performs these tasks autonomously and its readiness for real-world usage remain unclear. Observing H2-X in actual world conditions is necessary to fully evaluate its capabilities.

The company states that the H2-X can remain upright for approximately eight hours. However, since heavy physical activities can increase energy consumption, it is not yet fully detailed under what conditions this duration was measured. Tasks such as walking, jumping, and lifting heavy objects can cause the robot to consume energy faster. Therefore, it is important to note that the eight-hour working period does not necessarily mean the robot can perform rescue or security duties continuously for eight hours under heavy loads.

Technical specifications such as size, weight, battery capacity, and motor power have not been disclosed yet. The previous H2 model stands about 1.8 meters tall, weighs 70 kilograms, and features 31 degrees of freedom. Degrees of freedom refer to the movements a robot can perform independently, allowing it to walk, maintain balance, and move objects using its limbs and torso. How the H2-X differs from this predecessor will be revealed once technical details are released.

It is also unknown whether the showcased movements were pre-programmed, remotely operated, or driven by AI systems. Completing a preset scenario differs greatly from independently addressing an unforeseen danger. Since details on technical specifications, pricing, and launch schedule are currently missing, the H2-X is viewed as a technology demonstration.
