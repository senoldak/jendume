Microsoft plans to deploy its custom-designed Azure Maia AI chips at multi-gigawatt scale starting in 2027, positioning its internal silicon as the primary inference engine across its product ecosystem, according to executive Scott Guthrie.

The current generation, Maia 200, already powers Microsoft Copilot across data centers in the United States, Europe, and Australia, running OpenAI models alongside open-source systems. According to company figures, Maia 200 delivers up to 1.7 times more tokens per watt than leading commercial GPUs in Teams workloads.

## Next-Generation Efficiency with Maia 300

Described by CEO Satya Nadella as the company’s largest commitment yet to in-house silicon, the upcoming Maia 300 chip is scheduled to ship in 2027. Built specifically for long-context inputs, low-latency chat, and autonomous agent workloads, Maia 300 is projected to generate 40% more tokens per megawatt than comparable GPU systems.

According to Microsoft’s technical report, a single 96-chip Maia 300 rack is designed to produce up to 7 times the throughput of a current Maia 200 rack.
