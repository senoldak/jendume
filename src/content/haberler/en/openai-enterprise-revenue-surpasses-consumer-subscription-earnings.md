OpenAI has shifted to a majority enterprise revenue model, according to company President Greg Brockman. Speaking at a technology conference in Napa, California, Brockman stated that sales directed to business entities have now surpassed consumer revenue, exceeding earlier expectations set for the year.

While ChatGPT continues to reach a vast consumer audience, OpenAI is actively accelerating its commercial growth through Codex and specialized artificial intelligence services tailored for enterprises. In April, the corporation reported that the enterprise share of its total revenue had exceeded 40 percent. However, no detailed breakdown was provided regarding the exact percentage of the newly formed majority.

This distinction highlights the fundamental difference between total user count and actual revenue share. While millions of individuals continue to utilize personal accounts, a larger portion of revenue is now successfully generated from commercial businesses. Enterprise services focus heavily on integrating artificial intelligence into daily operational routines and corporate infrastructures.

Brockman also responded positively when asked if the organization would be ready for an initial public offering in the year 2027. This statement, however, does not indicate a finalized public offering schedule or any official regulatory filing.

These commercial milestones demonstrate that artificial intelligence is rapidly transitioning from a consumer novelty into a foundational pillar of the global corporate economy, reshaping how organizations operate worldwide.
