Orange announced at its OpenTech event in Paris that it is expanding its financial commitment by providing its corporate venture arm, Orange Ventures, with an additional €250m to back emerging startups between 2027 and 2032. This fresh capital injection successfully brings the investment vehicle's total assets under management to nearly €500m, reinforcing its strategic footprint in the technology sector.

Out of the €250m allocation, €200m will be directed towards European projects, while €50m is earmarked for Africa and the Middle East. The fund intends to place a significantly heavier emphasis on artificial intelligence innovations and cybersecurity solutions while maintaining its active investments in cloud computing, business software, fintech, and digital health markets.

Christel Heydemann, CEO of Orange, pointed out that as digital systems evolve and potential threats multiply, the region must maintain the capacity to nurture and expand its independent technology leaders. Furthermore, the organization reported that more than half of its current portfolio companies maintain active business partnership agreements with Orange.

Orange Ventures was originally established in January 2021 with an initial €350m capitalization, taking over the previous digital ventures portfolio. With its substantially expanded financial backing, the fund aims to strengthen its support for technological innovation and market expansion over the upcoming years.
