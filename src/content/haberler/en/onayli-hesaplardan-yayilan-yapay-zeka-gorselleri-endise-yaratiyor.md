A viral social media incident once again exposed the challenge of distinguishing AI-generated imagery from reality. Following news of actress Mia Goth's divorce, a verified Instagram account using her name shared an AI-generated image depicting her wearing a modified t-shirt.

The post garnered hundreds of thousands of likes and was even treated as authentic by several entertainment outlets before the truth came to light. The rapid spread of the image has reignited concerns regarding the reliability of verification badges on social platforms and the ethical challenges posed by synthetic media.

Experts emphasize that the growing realism of AI-generated content increases the risk of widespread misinformation, calling for stronger safeguards and detection tools across major social networks.
