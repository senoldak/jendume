Genesis AI has secured 500 million dollars in a Series A funding round to increase the manufacturing output of its general-purpose robot, Eno, and deploy it across more businesses. The funding round brings the company's post-money valuation to 3.5 billion dollars. Following its introduction in June, the company is now focused on expanding manufacturing capacity and customer deployments for Eno.

The investment round was led by Premji Invest, Khosla Ventures, and Eclipse, with participation from Nvidia, AMD Ventures, Samsung Next, Bpifrance, and Zoom. The company plans to allocate the funds toward increasing training data for the robots, improving object-grasping capabilities, and hiring new researchers and engineers. Genesis AI continues to develop Eno's hands and training systems concurrently.

Eno was initially introduced in June, when the company also announced a 105 million dollar seed funding round, planning to begin production and limited customer use by the end of the year. Management noted that a small number of robots are currently being tested with customers, with broader deployment expected in subsequent periods, distinguishing limited initial installations from future widespread goals.

Genesis AI plans to target the United States in the initial phase, followed by a broader customer base in Europe. This milestone highlights the rapid financial backing and scaling efforts within the autonomous robotics sector, supporting the company's expansion strategy.
