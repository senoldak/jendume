Modal Labs Inc., a provider of artificial intelligence infrastructure, has announced a significant funding milestone by raising $750 million in a Series D round. Led by Accel with participation from Kleiner Perkins and existing investors, the company's valuation has surged to $15.75 billion, more than tripling since its previous funding round in May.

This valuation jump mirrors the company's rapid growth, with annualized revenue now surpassing $1 billion. A substantial portion of this revenue stems from Sandboxes, a cloud service providing hosted containers optimized for AI workloads, model training, and running autonomous agents.

The infrastructure leverages a custom scheduler capable of provisioning tens of thousands of containers per second. In recent internal tests, Modal successfully ran 1 million concurrent containers. The newly acquired capital will fuel product development, particularly focusing on reinforcement learning and post-training tools designed to enhance AI reasoning and cost-efficiency.
