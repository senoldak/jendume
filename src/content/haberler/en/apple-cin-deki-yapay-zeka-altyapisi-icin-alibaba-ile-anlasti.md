Apple is collaborating with Alibaba for its artificial intelligence infrastructure inचीनी market. Instead of Google Gemini, the company will utilize Alibaba's Qwen and Tongyi Wanxiang models to power its localized features. Under this partnership, the Qwen model will handle general-purpose AI capabilities, while Tongyi Wanxiang will support image and video generation tasks, enabling visual editing tools for users. However, the experience in China will face notable limitations due to local regulations rather than technical constraints. Reports indicate that the new AI-powered Siri experience will not be rolled out in China, and text generation features in certain applications will also be disabled. Furthermore, the Image Playground feature will remain unavailable due to local legal requirements. Apple faces similar regional restrictions in other markets, such as the European Union. According to previous company announcements, European Mac and Apple Vision Pro users can access Siri AI features if they select a supported language, but the feature is initially omitted on iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS operating systems. Industry reports indicate that Siri AI is not expected to arrive in China or the European Union anytime soon. Overcoming these regulatory hurdles remains a key challenge for tech giants aiming to deliver a uniform global AI feature set across all regional markets.
