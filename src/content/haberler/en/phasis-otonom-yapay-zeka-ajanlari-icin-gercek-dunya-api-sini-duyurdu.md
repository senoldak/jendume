As artificial intelligence technologies advance rapidly, one of the primary challenges for autonomous agents is accurately grasping real-time context and fast-changing environments. To address this gap, Phasis Inc. has developed a new orientation platform aimed at helping AI agents orient themselves before executing tasks.

According to company co-founder Brandon Farley, the platform utilizes a specialized 'World API' layer that provides agents with verified, evidence-backed states of various domains. This allows AI models to avoid relying on outdated or incomplete information by checking current realities first.

The system's core endpoint, '/orient', enables agents to perform a quick reality check before they begin searching, planning, or strategizing. This foundational step ensures that enterprise AI systems operate with higher reliability and precision in complex scenarios.
