Loops AI is replacing traditional e-commerce search boxes and filters with generative AI. Instead of typing specific product names and applying filters, users can describe what they need just as they would talk to a sales assistant in a physical store. The system interprets these requests and matches them directly with the retailer's product catalog.

The technology goes beyond simple search, supporting virtual shopping assistants, marketplace interactions, and virtual try-ons through a shared knowledge layer. According to the company's metrics, shoppers interacting with this AI-driven discovery tool show an 83 percent higher purchase conversion rate and a 77 percent higher add-to-cart rate.

To fuel its global expansion, product development, and engineering capacity, the startup has secured 3.5 million dollars in funding. The investment round was backed by a16z Speedrun, NTT DOCOMO, Connecticut Innovations, Monday Capital, Alumni Ventures, and angel investors from major tech firms. Every customer brings a new use case and technical problem, and the solutions developed by the engineering team become part of the product.

Following its initial deployment in Turkey, Loops AI is taking its technology international, targeting Japan as its first major overseas market. The company also plans to expand into Europe—led by Germany—and Southeast Asia, while eventually bringing its adaptable AI infrastructure to sectors like financial services and energy.
