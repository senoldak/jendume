Amazon CEO Andy Jassy announced during the company's quarterly earnings call that he believes its cloud computing division, AWS, can become a business generating a trillion dollars in annual revenue. Jassy emphasized that what Amazon is building is unprecedented in scale. To reach this milestone, the company plans to increase capital expenditures to build the necessary infrastructure to support growing AI workloads.

As part of its AI infrastructure expansion, Amazon expects 2026 cash capital expenditures to be approximately 220 billion dollars, with the majority allocated to AWS. The company also anticipates spending significantly more in 2027. While Wall Street investors have expressed ongoing concerns regarding these soaring costs and profit margins, management defends the spending as a necessary commitment to capture historic global cloud demand driven by AI.

According to data shared during the call, AWS revenue increased 39 percent compared to the same quarter of the previous year. With this steady growth, AWS now represents a 184 billion dollar annualized revenue run-rate business. CEO Andy Jassy forecasts that growth could exceed 40 percent in both 2027 and 2028, potentially pushing AWS to trillion-dollar status within the next five to ten years.

Stating that the company is still in the very early days of enterprise AI adoption, Jassy asserted that the current strategy is working. This massive wave of infrastructure spending is not unique to Amazon; other major cloud providers like Microsoft are also investing heavily, highlighting the fierce competition and financial commitment required to power the AI era.
