Law firm Sullivan & Cromwell has collaborated with OpenAI to develop Agreement Analyzer, an artificial intelligence tool aimed at identifying potential issues in draft agreements. The application integrates the firm's curated precedents, negotiating playbooks, and ChatGPT Enterprise into a single review workflow.

While AI was initially deployed for repetitive tasks such as drafting emails or organizing schedules, recent advancements in analytical capabilities have paved the way for deployment in challenging legal assignments. The newly introduced tool helps lawyers compare corresponding provisions across past precedents and track how these clauses interact.

When a draft agreement is uploaded, lawyers can include specific precedents and playbooks reflecting firm and client positions. The tool then performs multiple review cycles from various perspectives, synthesizes the findings, and returns a memorandum along with proposed tracked changes for professional inspection.

Transparency remains a core principle of the system, allowing lawyers to inspect the exact sources behind any claims regarding market practice. Specialized agents evaluate the agreement in adversarial processes, checking for market deviations, risk allocation, and ambiguities that might impact potential litigation.

Firm representatives emphasize that recent improvements in reasoning models have removed previous bottlenecks in legal tech applications. Sullivan & Cromwell continues to explore tailored tools to support comprehensive research and writing processes across the firm.
