Google announced that queries within its AI Mode are on average three times longer compared to classical searches. According to data shared during the quarterly evaluation, one out of every five searches globally utilizes voice commands, images, or documents. This situation demonstrates that the search engine is moving user habits beyond simple keywords.

Officials stated that the annual query growth rate has reached the highest level of the past five years. However, a precise ratio was not provided for the total search volume or per-user query count. Among the shared data, the monthly visual search volume exceeding 50 billion also drew attention.

Different input types such as photographs and voice eliminate the necessity for users to express their requests solely by writing. Showing a product with a camera, uploading a document, or voicing questions creates new interaction formats. According to information in the help documentation, the system breaks complex queries into sub-topics and performs multiple searches simultaneously.

Thanks to long queries, searches containing multiple details like travel plans can be made in a single step. However, experts remind that writing long queries does not always guarantee accurate results, and that asking clear questions and verifying results remain important.

Google notes that generated responses may contain errors and that direct link lists can be provided when reliability is low. Transactional steps such as reservations and appointments continue to be maintained as a separate development area due to security requirements.
