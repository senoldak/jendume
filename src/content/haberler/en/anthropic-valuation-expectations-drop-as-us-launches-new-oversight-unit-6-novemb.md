Anthropic is facing downward revisions in valuation expectations from investors as it approaches its IPO, driven by broader tech sell-offs and concerns over AI spending. While the company recently aimed for a $2 trillion valuation, reaching even $1.5 trillion is now considered a stretch by stakeholders. Some investors suggest a more realistic target lies between $1 trillion and $1.25 trillion. As the company prepares to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, analysts emphasize that careful pricing is essential for a successful market debut. As of May, the company was privately valued at $965 billion.

The US Department of Justice has announced the creation of the Super Intelligence Task Force (DOJ-SITF) to safeguard AI technologies and prevent manipulation by malicious actors. Led by Stanley Woodward and Stephen Weiss, the unit is tasked with monitoring threats from foreign state-sponsored groups, transnational criminal organizations, and corporate fraud. The administration prioritizes ensuring that these critical technologies serve the American public, while the industry watches to see how companies will navigate this new regulatory landscape.

In the technology sector, Zyphra has released ZYFA, an open-source library specifically designed for AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs. By providing FP8 and BF16 attention kernels, the library delivers performance speeds 1.16 to 1.76 times faster than AMD's default AITER library. This optimization is particularly effective for long-context prefill tasks, enhancing the efficiency of the processor architecture and enabling faster AI model execution. This advancement highlights the potential of hardware optimization to significantly reduce computational costs for AI applications. ZYFA reaches 2,672 TFLOP/s FP8 performance at 32K tokens, outperforming AITER's 1,944 TFLOP/s.
