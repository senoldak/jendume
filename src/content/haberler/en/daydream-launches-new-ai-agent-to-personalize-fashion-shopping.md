Daydream, a fashion startup, launched its new AI assistant, Daydream Agent, on November 17, 2026, aiming to bring a personal stylist experience directly to users' pockets. The system goes beyond simple outfit suggestions by managing virtual try-ons, budget tracking, and facilitating direct purchases. Users can connect their personal closets and calendars to the agent, allowing it to build outfits based on items they already own for upcoming events.

This development highlights the shift of AI in e-commerce from a simple search tool to an agentic model that understands user preferences and makes decisions on their behalf. Daydream Agent scans real-time pricing and availability data from over 10,000 brands to recommend pieces tailored to individual tastes and fit preferences. The technology is powered by a combination of OpenAI, Gemini, and Claude models, fine-tuned specifically for fashion, and is supported by professional stylists to ensure the recommendations remain grounded in fashion expertise.

Since its launch in June 2025, the platform has served over 2 million shoppers. Led by a team with deep industry experience, the startup is backed by $50 million in funding from investors including Forerunner Ventures and Index Ventures. To streamline the shopping experience, the agent includes a "clothing rack" feature, allowing users to save and compare items before making a decision. This approach demonstrates how AI is evolving into a personalized shopping partner, capable of handling complex tasks that were previously reserved for human interaction.
