South Korea-based technology producer SK Hynix shared that it reached the highest revenue figures in its corporate history during its latest financial reporting period. According to the shared data, the enterprise generated 104.6 trillion KRW in revenue, surpassing the 100 trillion threshold for the first time, and achieved an operating profit margin of 80 percent. The recorded net profit stood at 39.8 trillion KRW. This financial achievement was primarily propelled by the rapid sales growth of high-bandwidth HBM4 memory units developed specifically for artificial intelligence servers. Management anticipates that the HBM4 series will soon overtake previous models to become the primary product line. Alongside this robust financial performance, the company warned the industry that the global memory shortage issue could extend until 2030. Short-term corporate artificial intelligence applications alongside long-term autonomous driving and robotic systems continue to drive high hardware demand. To address these capacity needs, the M15X factory is scheduled to reach full production capacity by the end of the year, while 321-layer TLC chips and high bandwidth flash technologies are also being developed. In light of prevailing market conditions and financial balances, company leadership announced that the public offering process for Solidigm has been suspended. Additional context provided in financial reports highlights that server DDR5 RAM and LPDDR5 SOCAMM modules continue to strain manufacturing capacities. The enterprise aims to satisfy the rising demand for 1b DRAM chips while actively increasing the production share of advanced 1c DRAM technologies.
