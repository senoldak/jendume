Adobe and The Walt Disney Company announced a multi-year strategic AI alliance on November 9, 2026, making Adobe an Official AI Innovation Partner of Disney. Announced at the Adobe MAX conference, the agreement brings custom Firefly Foundry models and technical collaboration to Disney's theme parks, studios, and sports operations. The agreement also provides the entertainment enterprise with priority access to advanced Adobe creative software, media tools, and generative platforms.

The core of the agreement focuses on building custom AI tools tailored to Disney's brands using Adobe Firefly Foundry. These models will be trained on Disney characters, environments, and visual styles to generate commercially safe, production-ready outputs. Disney's Imagineering research division already utilizes Firefly for theme park development, and the partnership will now expand to Walt Disney Studios and ESPN's innovation hub, ESPN Edge, seeking to elevate supporter involvement across sports programming and expand the utility of media archives.

This collaboration highlights the growing integration of artificial intelligence within major media workflows. Disney previously engaged with OpenAI before walking away from the deal, and was also involved with Character.AI. According to financial reports, the company has also been adjusting its operational costs and planning structural changes across its massive entertainment empire.

Through this deal, Disney gains privileged availability of Adobe's newest generative products, aiming to unlock new uses for its extensive archives and accelerate theme park development. For Adobe, the partnership validates its enterprise-grade custom model capabilities within the global entertainment sector, demonstrating how creative workflows are evolving through customized artificial intelligence.
