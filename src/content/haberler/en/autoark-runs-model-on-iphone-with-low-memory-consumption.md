During the artificial intelligence engineering gathering in Shanghai, the pragmatic approach of Chinese developers toward artificial intelligence and the success of local models on mobile devices took center stage. According to information shared at the event, a 35-billion-parameter model developed by AutoArk was successfully executed on an iPhone 16 Pro using only 2.5 GB of RAM.

Unlike Western discourse often dominated by existential anxieties regarding artificial intelligence, participants in Shanghai focused on practical marketing and product development opportunities. Due to local access conditions, Chinese engineers and practitioners predominantly utilize domestic models such as DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, and Qwen, which are reported to deliver sufficient performance for everyday tasks.

Another notable presentation came from a traditional sector. Bottle cap manufacturer Zhu Shihui emphasized that artificial intelligence should not be viewed as a destructive force, but rather built upon the unwritten experiences and tacit knowledge of organizations. For traditional businesses operating on tight profit margins, modest and practical transformation steps were highlighted as essential.

Small models and robotic systems developed by Chinese laboratories continue to advance through principles of practicality and openness within the local ecosystem. Observers note that this pragmatic approach and reliance on domestic models serve as a significant balancing factor in global artificial intelligence competition. Various sessions were held throughout the event focusing on how artificial intelligence technologies can be utilized more efficiently across different industrial sectors.
