Omen AI, a company that monitors cooling fluids for data centers, has acquired ocean sensor developer Aquatic Labs in a cash-and-stock transaction valued at $16 million. San Francisco-based Omen AI announced the agreement, noting that the payment combines both cash and equity without disclosing exact financial ratios. Following the completion of the transaction, Aquatic Labs founder Allan Adams will join Omen AI as chief scientist to lead research and development operations.

Omen AI builds specialized sensors that continuously monitor cooling fluids in artificial intelligence data centers. These devices measure metal content, biological fouling, wear traces, and fluid degradation in real time, replacing traditional laboratory tests that are typically performed at scheduled intervals. The newly integrated technology from Aquatic Labs measures total alkalinity without adding chemicals, helping operators predict scaling and corrosion risks in closed-loop systems.

Originally created for ocean monitoring applications, this technology is now being directly applied to data center cooling networks. Founded in 2024, Omen AI has raised a total of $46 million in funding to date and operates across large data center facilities with a total capacity exceeding 10 gigawatts.

As advanced artificial intelligence workloads drive the industry rapidly toward liquid cooling solutions, automated fluid monitoring is quickly emerging as a key component of modern data center infrastructure. The ability to monitor fluid health continuously helps operators prevent unexpected system failures and maintain optimal cooling performance.
