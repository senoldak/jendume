Many small and midsized industrial businesses face a growing challenge as veteran workers retire faster than they can be replaced, taking decades of crucial institutional knowledge with them. To help solve this issue, a startup named Evos has pioneered an agentic operations platform designed to capture and apply this hidden expertise.

Called Workhorse, the platform introduces autonomous operators tailored for industries like manufacturing, freight, and logistics. Unlike traditional AI tools that receive tasks and execute them blindly, Evos' agents start by observing human workers behind an approval gate, learning the nuances of specific jobs through continuous oversight and feedback.

The startup recently announced a $9 million pre-seed funding round led by Uncork Capital and Field Ventures. Founder and Chief Executive Urav Shah noted that the system is built to assist humans rather than replace them, enabling companies to scale the expertise of their most valuable personnel and run their operations more efficiently.
