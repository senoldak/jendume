Monitoring workers is nothing new, but artificial intelligence is fundamentally transforming how companies track productivity. By processing vast amounts of data, these modern monitoring tools can generate minute-by-minute accounts of employee activity and output.

Reports from sectors like education reveal that these systems are causing severe stress among staff members. Experts warn that constant algorithmic surveillance erodes workplace trust and can ultimately prove counterproductive, damaging morale and mental health.
