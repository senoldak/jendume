Anthropic has officially declared that it has met its own internal threshold for AI-accelerated research, marking a significant milestone where artificial intelligence systems are dramatically speeding up the development of their successors. The company based this decision on its overall rate of progress and expectations for future models, rather than solely on the performance of current systems. This development highlights the growing capability of AI to contribute to its own evolution.

An independent assessment by an evaluation group confirmed that Anthropic’s research pace has roughly doubled compared to previous periods. The findings suggest that a significant portion of this acceleration is driven by AI assistance. Despite this rapid progress, the company emphasized that it will not allow its models to fully build their successors without meaningful human supervision. Safety policies are being updated to ensure that critical research decisions remain under human review.

This milestone has sparked broader discussions about the emergence of "superhuman" AI researchers. Some experts project that if the industry continues at this pace, AI could fully automate coding and research tasks by 2027. In a letter to investors, Anthropic’s co-founders stated that they are willing to modulate this pace to prioritize safety, even if it means accepting a competitive disadvantage.

As the company pursues an IPO with a target valuation of $1.4 trillion, the balance between rapid innovation and safety remains a central theme. The ability of AI to accelerate its own development represents both a technological breakthrough and a profound responsibility, necessitating careful oversight as the industry moves toward more autonomous research capabilities.
