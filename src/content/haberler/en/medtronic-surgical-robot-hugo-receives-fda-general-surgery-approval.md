The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has expanded the clearance for Medtronic's Hugo robotic-assisted surgery system to include general surgery operations. Previously approved in the United States strictly for urological procedures, the platform is now cleared for upper and lower abdominal operations, including gallbladder removal, Roux-en-Y gastric bypass, ventral hernia repair, small bowel resection, inguinal hernia repair, and appendectomies.

According to company figures, this regulatory clearance covers approximately 3.5 million procedures performed annually in the US, representing more than half of all soft-tissue operations conducted nationwide. Procedures utilizing the Hugo system are growing faster than the broader robotic surgery market, with total procedures expected to surpass 50,000 worldwide by the end of the company's fiscal year.

The Hugo platform incorporates a modular, cart-based design that allows surgical teams to use only the required robotic arms and position them according to specific patient anatomy. Its open console architecture enables surgeons to operate from a comfortable seated position with high-definition 3D visualization while maintaining direct line-of-sight communication with operating room personnel.

Medtronic plans to integrate advanced artificial intelligence capabilities into the Hugo platform across the surgical workflow. These enhancements aim to utilize patient imaging for personalized pre-operative planning, automated anatomical structure recognition during surgery, and post-operative recovery tracking. The company previously obtained FDA clearance for its Touch Surgery Aide software, which utilizes computer vision and AI algorithms to analyze surgical videos in real time.

To address global healthcare access constraints related to cost and training, Medtronic is conducting additional clinical studies for gynecological procedures. The firm also executed a $700 million investment in Cornerstone Robotics to distribute the Sentire surgical system in select international markets.
