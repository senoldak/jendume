Moonshot AI has rolled out an advanced iteration of its reasoning system, expanding computational investment during the post-training phase to elevate overall performance. Reaching 55 points on the intelligence benchmark, the newly introduced architecture secures the fourth position overall while delivering substantial performance gains across multiple evaluation categories.

Operating alongside the standard offering, the developer has also prepared a high-speed variant engineered to execute tasks significantly faster while maintaining core reasoning capabilities. The system demonstrates notable advancements in agentic evaluations, achieving 1,846 Elo on benchmark tests. Furthermore, native integration with standard development environments allows the architecture to interact directly with game engines and computer-aided design software for complex 3D modeling tasks.

From a structural standpoint, the architecture retains the dimensional profile of its predecessor, utilizing 2.8 trillion total parameters alongside 104 billion active parameters. It supports a context window spanning one million tokens and processes both textual and visual inputs. Operational expenses have decreased, with token pricing reduced by 33 percent, lowering input charges to $2 per million tokens and output expenses to $10.

Regarding safety protocols, the developer adjusted its training methodology for the upcoming open-weights distribution by filtering out specific security-sensitive datasets from the training corpus. The organization emphasizes that while open architectures should not advance offensive cyber capabilities beyond established thresholds, they remain valuable for defensive system audits and legacy vulnerability analysis.
