As the business world focuses on 2027 budgets and wage balances in the final quarter of the year, artificial intelligence integration continues to expand its transformative impact on the labor market, even if it ranks lower on immediate corporate agendas. A survey conducted by LinkedIn and Kolay İK among 234 human resources professionals revealed that 42 percent of companies prioritize budget and wage equilibrium.

While performance evaluation followed at 23 percent and talent retention at 21 percent, integrating artificial intelligence into business processes ranked last at 14 percent. However, Kolay İK CEO Çağlar Yalı emphasized that once year-end budget rushes subside, artificial intelligence will return to the top of corporate agendas.

Global data supports this outlook as well. According to PwC's 2026 Global AI Jobs Barometer, positions requiring artificial intelligence skills command an average wage premium of 62 percent compared to similar roles.
