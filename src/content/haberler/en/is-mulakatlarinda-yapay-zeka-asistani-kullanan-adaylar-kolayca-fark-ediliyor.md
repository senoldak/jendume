The unauthorized use of artificial intelligence by job applicants during virtual interviews has reached a record high in the United Kingdom. According to a survey by the body representing leading graduate employers, misconduct during recruitment processes rose to 92 percent, with secret AI usage during interviews topping the list of violations.

Recruitment experts note that candidates relying on real-time AI prompts often give away their strategy through delayed responses, unnatural phrasing, and over-reliance on filler words while waiting for chatbot answers. When detected, these interviews are typically terminated immediately, as employers view the tactic as a form of cheating that could land candidates in roles they are unsuited for.

Following live interview misuse at 66 percent, other common infractions include using AI to complete online assessments, application questions, and crafting cover letters. Industry leaders emphasize that authentic preparation remains essential, noting that modern selection panels are increasingly adept at identifying automated assistance.
