The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence has begun to reshape Wikipedia, the collaborative digital encyclopedia that serves as a cornerstone of human knowledge. Recent studies revealed that a noticeable percentage of newly created articles contained substantial amounts of AI-generated text, sparking intense debates within the community.

To counter the influx of generic and unverified content, community editors decided to ban the use of large language models for writing articles entirely, restricting AI tools strictly to minor translation and proofreading tasks.

Experts warn that an encyclopedia written and consumed by machines threatens to strip away human context, passion, and critical verification methods. Protecting human-driven curation remains essential to maintaining the integrity of shared knowledge.
