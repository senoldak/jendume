Estonian cybersecurity startup Blackwall has announced a 100 million dollar funding round led by AVP, quadrupling its valuation to 2.2 billion dollars. Existing backers including Dawn Capital, MMC Ventures, and Tera Ventures also participated in the round. The financing arrives just weeks after Blackwall achieved unicorn status within Estonia.

Blackwall states that the transaction positions it among Europe's most valuable cybersecurity scaleups. Rather than selling directly to individual websites, the enterprise operates through web hosting, cloud, and managed service providers who bundle its tools into their own service offerings. The platform mitigates automated bots, provides DDoS protection, web application firewalls, and encryption while filtering billions of web requests daily.

Founded by Denis Prochko and Nikita Rozenberg, the firm emerged from a need to handle automated traffic flooding websites. With Imperva data indicating that bots account for 53 percent of web traffic, industry leaders warn that a surge of autonomous software agents will challenge human-optimized infrastructure networks.

European cybersecurity financing continues to expand significantly. The sector accumulated a total of 1.3 billion euros distributed across sixty-four funding cycles during the initial half of 2026. Blackwall intends to utilize the newly acquired capital to scale its operations into twelve distinct geographical territories spanning North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East to meet growing international demand.
