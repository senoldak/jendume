Cloudflare has expanded its decision model family with the launch of Clef-omni, a new tool capable of processing text, images, audio, and video simultaneously. Built on top of the Qwen3 architecture, the model evaluates multiple modalities in a single pass to return typed probabilities.

The 30-billion parameter mixture-of-experts model excels particularly in intent classification and routing tasks. By handling diverse data types at once, it streamlines pipelines for developers building complex AI agents.

Alongside this release, Cloudflare optimized its serving stack, roughly doubling the speed of hosted models and reducing pricing. This update makes advanced multimodal decision-making more accessible for everyday applications.
