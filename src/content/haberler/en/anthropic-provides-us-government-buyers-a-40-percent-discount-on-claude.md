Anthropic will provide US government buyers with a 40 percent reduction in costs for Claude through an enlarged pact with the General Services Administration. Announced publicly, the price reduction encompasses state, local, tribal, and territorial administrations for the very first time. The arrangement is valid for Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the Claude API, allowing public agencies to disburse funds solely for actual consumption without mandatory minimum purchases.

The financial concessions provisioned via the pact are projected to yield government savings reaching $1 billion, according to company disclosures. The fresh arrangement supersedes the initial OneGov pact from August 2025 and extends across 27 months until January 31, 2029. OpenAI previously secured a comparable arrangement featuring a 50 percent markdown on token-based consumption.

Anthropic indicates that approximately 500 US federal, state, and local public bodies currently utilize Claude. Weekly active users for the government-focused tier have expanded roughly eightfold since February, whereas adoption across federal civilian bodies has multiplied nearly 20-fold over a year. Within Utah, 34 software creators across 10 state departments evaluated Claude Code, with 71 percent stating they preserved 20 or more hours during a four-week trial.

A federal defensive security unit similarly employed Claude Mythos to identify and remediate a vulnerability overlooked by manual penetration testing. The accord materializes during the period when the firm submitted paperwork for its public stock offering on Nasdaq, emphasizing the widening commercial footprint of core artificial intelligence models.
