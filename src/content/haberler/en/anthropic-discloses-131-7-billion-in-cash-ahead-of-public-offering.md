Artificial intelligence lab Anthropic has disclosed that it held $112.4 billion in cash alongside $19.3 billion in supplementary liquidity holdings as of June 30, according to an S-1 filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission. Totaling $131.7 billion, the amount outpaces every non-bank American corporation except for Berkshire Hathaway and Alphabet, underscoring the rapid financial growth of the six-year-old firm.

The enterprise's cash reserves reflect a strong fundraising capacity that has accumulated a total of $141 billion in private funding since its inception. However, Anthropic faces massive future obligations for computing power and infrastructure. The organization recorded first-half compute and infrastructure expenditures of $8.95 billion, while estimated future undiscounted lease obligations reached $321.4 billion alongside $265.6 billion in projected future fixed or minimum-guaranteed commitments.

Depending on the outcome of its upcoming initial public offering, which reports suggest could top $100 billion, Anthropic could push its cash hoard past $230 billion. Yet, existing investors will also be selling shares into the IPO, meaning the company itself will not collect the entire fundraise. Final details regarding the offering size are expected to be disclosed in about two weeks.

While Anthropic's cash position provides a formidable foundation, the immense scale of its future compute commitments means the company must continue generating profits or securing fundraising to meet its obligations. The filing offers a rare glimpse into the sheer financial weight driving modern foundational artificial intelligence development.
