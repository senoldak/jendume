Personal AI wearable maker Bee has introduced new products featuring a wearable design for wrists or clothing alongside a magnetic device that attaches to smartphones.

Designed to help users stay present, the devices listen to everyday conversations and convert them into summaries, meeting notes, and calendar events. Focusing on privacy, raw audio is processed in real time and deleted, while conversation transcripts are encrypted and controlled entirely by the user.

Integration with productivity software and digital platforms allows users to manage tasks, draft messages, and make purchases hands-free while leveraging generated insights.
