Tencent has introduced Hy4, an open-source AI model designed to provide advanced visual reasoning, computer control, and software development capabilities. Unlike previous iterations, this model distinguishes itself with the ability to process visual data and directly manage computer interfaces. Hy4 can interpret complex charts and tables, solve mathematical problems based on visual inputs, and execute operations within graphical interfaces using command-line tools.

A significant focus of this release is the improvement in model reliability. Internal evaluations indicate that the hallucination rate has decreased from 12.95% to 7.2%. Furthermore, errors resulting from unsupported assumptions have been reduced from 23.1% to 5.6%. These technical advancements are critical for positioning the model as a reliable assistant within professional business environments.

Trained specifically for financial research, Hy4 demonstrates strong performance in areas such as fund research, securities analysis, and quantitative investment strategies. In blind evaluations involving 213 financial experts and 579 real-world tasks, the model achieved a score of 3.04, outperforming its competitors. Integrated with WeCom through the WorkBuddy platform, the system can analyze a wide range of data, including meeting notes and emails, to track project progress and manage tasks.

In terms of software development, Hy4 is capable of utilizing design software such as Blender and CAD, and it is compatible with platforms including Unreal Engine, Unity, and Godot. During tests, the model achieved an average score of 83.18 and a success rate of 96.15%. Regarding pricing, the cost is set at $0.90 per 1 million input tokens, $2.70 per 1 million output tokens, and $0.045 per 1 million cached input tokens. Tencent plans to release the Hy4 Flash model in the near future to offer lower operational costs.
