The academic peer review process has long relied heavily on human effort and time. Researchers at Sakana AI have published a new study shifting the focus of AI reviewers from simply mimicking humans to actively finding planted mistakes in research papers.

The newly developed Multi-Layered Review system reads papers thoroughly using standard API models, analyzing the main text, figures, and equations together. In benchmarks, the system successfully caught over 73 percent of core-claim errors, significantly outperforming existing baseline AI reviewers.

Operating at a low cost of under fifty cents per review, this agentic approach is framed not as a replacement for human reviewers, but as a complementary perspective that helps validate experimental rigor and scientific reliability.
