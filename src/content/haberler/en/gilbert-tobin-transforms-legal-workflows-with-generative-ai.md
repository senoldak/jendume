Gilbert + Tobin, a prominent Australian corporate law firm, has successfully integrated generative AI into its daily legal operations, moving beyond simple experimentation to organizational productivity. The firm’s journey began by encouraging staff to explore AI tools in a safe, controlled environment, ensuring that no confidential client data was compromised. This initial phase allowed employees to understand the technology's capabilities and limitations before scaling its use across the organization.

The firm adopted ChatGPT Enterprise and other advanced tools to assist with complex workflows, such as document preparation and administrative tasks. Rather than focusing on reducing headcount or cutting costs, the leadership emphasized using AI to free up professionals for the "human side" of their work—such as meeting with clients, mentoring junior staff, and engaging in strategic decision-making. This approach has fostered a culture where technology is viewed as a partner in legal practice, not a replacement for human judgment.

One notable example of this transformation occurred during the firm’s annual review process for its 120 partners. By utilizing AI tools to process data from spreadsheets and generate necessary documentation, the firm completed a task that would have previously required multiple people working over several days. The entire process was completed in minutes at a cost of approximately 62 dollars, demonstrating significant efficiency gains.

Furthermore, the firm’s leadership has been instrumental in driving this adoption. By creating "digital twins"—custom AI models trained on their own writing styles and feedback patterns—partners have been able to provide more consistent guidance to their teams. This allows junior staff to test their thinking against the partner’s established context before finalizing their work. With an 87 percent active usage rate among enabled users, Gilbert + Tobin serves as a practical case study for how professional service firms can responsibly leverage AI to enhance the quality and volume of their output while maintaining high standards of governance and security.
