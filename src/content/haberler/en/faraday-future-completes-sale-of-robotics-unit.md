Faraday Future has announced the completion of the divestment of its robotics operations to FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem (FFR), which operates on Nasdaq. According to filings submitted to United States regulators, the transaction was closed on October 23 via an all-share deal valued at roughly $200 million. Upon conversion of newly issued shares into ordinary stock following a shareholder vote, Faraday Future will retain approximately a 65.7% stake in FFR.

Founder YT Jia has assumed the position of chief executive officer at FFR while continuing his leadership role at Faraday Future. Regulatory disclosures filed in the United States indicate that Jerry Wang serves as executive chairman for both organizations, forming an integrated management structure across the entities.

FFR stated it anticipates shipping in excess of 300 robots during October, marking a monthly milestone and bringing its cumulative total beyond 1,100 units. The enterprise additionally forecasts dispatching around 2,000 robotic units throughout the course of 2026. Previously, Faraday Future had delivered a total of 68 robotic units prior to the conclusion of April, depending on an external manufacturer for the majority of production.

Initially founded to produce luxury electric vehicles with limited commercial traction, Faraday Future is now shifting its strategic emphasis toward embodied artificial intelligence and robotics. Company leadership regards the finalization of this agreement as a significant milestone in establishing a dedicated public enterprise focused on advanced robotics. Alternative robotic manufacturers are similarly engaging public investors to finance their ongoing operations.
