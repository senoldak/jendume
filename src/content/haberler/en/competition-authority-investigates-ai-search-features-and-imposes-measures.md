The Competition Authority has taken a significant regulatory step regarding the generative artificial intelligence tools integrated into a major search engine. The board initiated an official investigation to determine whether the AI preview functionalities and AI mode features within the search service comply with competition regulations. Immediately following the launch of the inquiry, an interim injunction decision was adopted against the company.

During the preliminary assessment phases, authorities observed that AI-driven summary screens fundamentally transformed how individuals access information. It was determined that this transformation negatively impacted web traffic for news publishers and digital content creators. The comprehensive review additionally focused on evaluating unfair clauses within content agreements and potential exclusionary effects directed at competing conversational assistants in the market.

The regulatory board stated that evidence pointing to discriminatory and exploitative practices against publishing organizations had reached a level of strong suspicion. In line with the enacted interim measures, the company was mandated to establish a framework that avoids discrimination among different publishers.

As a result of these rulings, the firm is required to fairly reflect the economic value of utilized content back to the publishers. Furthermore, the company must ensure that publishers can independently decide whether to supply content to AI services without depending on their general visibility status within search engine results pages.
