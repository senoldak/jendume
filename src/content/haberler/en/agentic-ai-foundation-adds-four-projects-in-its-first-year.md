At the AGNTCon + MCPCon North America event in San Jose, the Agentic AI Foundation added four projects, bringing its total to ten in its first year. The October gathering shifted much of its focus from new protocol features to security and identity management. Major tech entities contributed new tools and frameworks to the foundation, marking a significant milestone in collaborative agent development. Foundation leadership opened the event by highlighting the growth from three founding projects to a much broader ecosystem.

Discussions during the event highlighted the connectivity needs of capable AI models and the rising security challenges. Experts pointed out that advanced models can identify vulnerabilities rapidly, making secure-by-construction systems essential. Representatives from leading labs noted that cumulative SDK downloads have surged to 4.17 billion, raising the stakes for robust authorization and addressing security gaps across various deployments.

Key figures from the conference illustrated the rapid scaling of agent technologies. Foundation leadership emphasized the shift from initial founding projects to a broader ecosystem with ten active projects. Industry speakers also addressed workflow bottlenecks, such as code reviews accelerated by coding agents, and the importance of establishing verifiable identity protocols.

As context, the adoption of Model Context Protocol and agentic workflows continues to accelerate across platforms. The broader industry is shifting focus from raw model capabilities to underlying protocols, gateways, and verifiable identity to ensure trustworthy deployment. Future foundation events are already scheduled for upcoming months in major global technology hubs.
