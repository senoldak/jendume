Disney has transformed the Anzellan characters from the Star Wars universe into advanced, fully autonomous animatronics capable of interacting with guests. Unlike traditional theme park robots that operate on pre-recorded, fixed loops, these three characters—Vilo Vele, Kado Bre'a, and Jibu Bre'a—use artificial intelligence systems powered by Amazon Web Services (AWS) to perceive their environment and make their own decisions in real time.

This advancement marks a significant shift in theme park experiences. Equipped with onboard cameras and microphones, the robots can detect the number of people standing in front of them, estimate their ages, and even spot specific items like Star Wars-themed shirts or birthday buttons. Based on this data, the characters respond with personalized dialogue and movements that align with their distinct personalities. For instance, Vilo acts as the boss, while the others exhibit shy or attention-seeking behaviors.

The system runs on Amazon Bedrock, which allows developers to switch between AI models from different providers. Furthermore, the development team utilized Kiro, Amazon’s AI coding agent, to translate creative briefs into software, completing the project in just a few months. Disney has emphasized that the data from these guest encounters is used only for the immediate interaction; it is not stored in the cloud, nor is it used to train AI models.

These next-generation animatronics will make their public debut at the re:Invent conference in Las Vegas, taking place from November 30 to December 4, 2026. Disney plans to eventually integrate this technology into its parks. This move serves as a prime example of how artificial intelligence can personalize the entertainment industry. Replacing traditional, scripted animatronics with autonomous systems that can engage with their surroundings and provide dynamic responses holds the potential to fundamentally redefine the visitor experience.
