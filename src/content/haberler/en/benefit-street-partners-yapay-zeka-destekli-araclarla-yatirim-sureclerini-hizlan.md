At Benefit Street Partners, a credit-focused investment manager, portfolio manager Anant Kumar has integrated AI coding assistants to address follow-up questions from investment committees and analyze complex credit agreements. Tasks that previously required days or weeks of manual effort can now be completed significantly faster, allowing analysts to address inquiries even during meetings.

To compare how different firms value the same loans, Kumar developed a custom tracker using public SEC filings. The initial version of the tracker was built in a day, with total development time reaching approximately ten to twelve hours after subsequent refinements. Furthermore, he created an analyzer for credit agreements that often exceed three hundred pages, designed to help investors spot clauses that could shift value away from lenders.

An uploaded credit agreement is processed into a first-pass analysis in roughly twenty minutes, which is then followed by an hour or more of human review. The company has since refactored the tool and deployed it within its internal portal. Kumar emphasizes that while the artificial intelligence excels at collating and synthesizing data, the ultimate investment decisions remain strictly in human hands.

Rather than pushing for a higher volume of deals, the firm is leveraging these tools to add greater review capacity and underwriting rigor within existing timeframes. Looking ahead, the team aims to build continuous underwriting capabilities to monitor portfolio loans dynamically as market news unfolds.
