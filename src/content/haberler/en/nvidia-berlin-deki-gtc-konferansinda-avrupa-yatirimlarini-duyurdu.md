NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang took center stage at the conference in Berlin to outline the future trajectory of accelerated computing and artificial intelligence. The multi-day event features over one hundred sessions focused on automated systems, automated machinery, digital intelligence frameworks, and high-performance computing. During his opening address, Huang highlighted a growing list of major European enterprises spanning various commercial sectors that are actively collaborating with the technology firm on advanced computing initiatives.

The keynote followed an extensive regional road tour spanning prominent European hubs such as Paris, London, Stockholm, and Dublin. Throughout these engagements, the enterprise committed nearly 10 billion dollars to the local technology ecosystem while engaging with more than twelve thousand strategic collaborators. Huang remarked that after decades of cumulative advancement in the computer industry, society is currently standing at the threshold of a brand-new industrial revolution.

Explaining the architecture of modern technology, Huang described digital intelligence systems as a layered operational framework. Energy powers advanced computers integrated within specialized processing facilities, which subsequently generate digital intelligence from underlying models before integration into end-user applications. Drawing historical parallels, he noted that just as the internet required foundational networking and electricity required a stable grid, comprehensive infrastructure must precede the widespread emergence of advanced models.

Concluding his address, Huang urged European enterprises to actively participate in the emerging economy as producers rather than mere consumers. He emphasized that the groundwork for this transformation is already well underway across the continent, positioning regional industries to build their own independent capabilities.
