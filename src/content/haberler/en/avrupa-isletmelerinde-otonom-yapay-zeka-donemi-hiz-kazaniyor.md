Businesses across the European Union are expanding their use of artificial intelligence by integrating agentic tools that can carry out comprehensive tasks from start to finish. According to data released in October 2026, enterprise customers increasingly rely on these autonomous systems for a substantial share of their daily output.

This shift is not limited to large corporations; small and medium-sized enterprises are also adopting these capabilities rapidly. Weekly active users of enterprise work tools among EU small businesses have grown more than eightfold since May 2026, showing widespread growth across member states.

Despite this progress, the adoption of agentic AI remains uneven across the continent. Among sampled accounts, the share of output generated through autonomous tools ranges significantly from 22 percent in some nations to 85 percent in others. Furthermore, while leading European firms use AI more intensively than typical local businesses, they still trail behind global leaders outside the region.

Experts emphasize that closing this usage gap requires helping businesses move past experimentation. Equipping workers with the right skills and making advanced autonomous capabilities more accessible will be essential for driving productivity and ensuring long-term economic strength across Europe.
