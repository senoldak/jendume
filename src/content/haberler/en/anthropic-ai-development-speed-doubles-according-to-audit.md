Nonprofit organization METR obtained eleven weeks of access to internal research records at Anthropic to conduct an independent evaluation. The findings indicate that the pace at which Anthropic models advance has roughly doubled, and approximately eighty percent of that increased speed stems from artificial intelligence being deployed directly within company research efforts.

Whether artificial intelligence accelerates the creation of superior systems functions as a fundamental warning indicator during safety discussions. The primary worry involves a potential feedback loop where successive models upgrade faster and faster, potentially generating extreme capabilities without adequate oversight. Anthropic officially designates this exact form of acceleration as a formal risk metric inside its internal safety policies.

Since late 2025, model test performance metrics compiled by METR utilizing a composite benchmark known as AECI demonstrated faster upward trajectories. The evaluation incorporated three previously unreleased models that concluded their training cycles between July and October 2026. While earlier training generations gained roughly fifteen points annually, subsequent performance growth accelerated significantly.

Auditors determined this performance shift did not originate from expanded computing power allocations alone. Instead, analytical estimates attribute the vast majority of the speedup directly to automated research assistance provided by artificial intelligence models. Investigators acknowledged that uncertainty ranges remain broad, and company officials redacted several sensitive details prior to final publication.
