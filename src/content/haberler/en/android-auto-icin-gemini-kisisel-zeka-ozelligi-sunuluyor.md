Google is bringing the Gemini Personal Intelligence feature to Android Auto and built-in vehicle systems to make the driving experience smarter. This new capability allows drivers to access personal data from services like Gmail, Google Calendar, Photos, and Maps to make journeys much more practical. Users can now perform navigation or information retrieval tasks using natural language instead of complex commands.

The system can reference data such as flight details in emails or appointments in calendars to answer real-time queries while driving. For example, users can check flight status or navigate to doctor appointments with simple voice commands, while the system also analyzes past preferences to enhance comfort. When searching for places along a route, it takes dietary habits and past choices into consideration.

Google keeps the use of this personal data feature entirely optional. Android Auto users can choose whether to enable Personal Intelligence and can control which Google apps the assistant is allowed to access. The rollout has begun gradually worldwide, though Switzerland, the European Economic Area, the UK, and Nigeria are initially excluded.

In the coming months, the feature is also planned to be integrated into vehicles running built-in Google services. This development accelerates the evolution of in-vehicle infotainment systems from basic screens into personalized digital assistants aware of a driver's daily schedule.
