Mistral has established a new regional headquarters in Singapore, expanding into the Asia-Pacific market. This facility functions as both an office and a dedicated research hub focused on creating AI models tailored to the specific needs of local industries. The company is prioritizing public services, robotics, and high-tech manufacturing sectors for its regional operations. At the core of this move is the concept of "sovereign AI," a strategy the company has championed in Europe. The goal is to empower governments and enterprises in the region to maintain full ownership and control over their own infrastructure and data. By allowing organizations to deploy models within their own environments, the company aims to reduce reliance on external technology providers and ensure data security for its clients. The Singapore hub builds upon existing partnerships with local entities, including public safety agencies and defense research organizations. These collaborations ground AI development in real-world operational needs. Furthermore, the company is leveraging this presence to advance its work in physical AI, particularly in robotics, where models are trained to navigate complex environments using visual data inputs. The company plans to more than double its regional workforce, aiming to reach 200 employees by 2027. Founded in Paris in 2023, the firm has rapidly become one of Europe’s most prominent AI players. Following a €1.7 billion funding round in September 2025, which valued the company at approximately €11.7 billion, the expansion provides a viable alternative to dominant US-based technology providers on a global scale.
