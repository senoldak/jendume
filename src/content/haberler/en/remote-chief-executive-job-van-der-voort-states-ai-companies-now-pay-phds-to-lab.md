Remote chief executive Job van der Voort has shared insights on how artificial intelligence is transforming workforce demands, noting a massive surge in demand for independent contractors and PhDs to label data for AI models. Speaking about internal strategies, van der Voort explained that Remote encourages its staff to solve workplace challenges using AI first rather than immediately expanding headcount. The company even developed a compulsory AI course for its employees, which has subsequently been made free to the public as a resource for actual work.

While most traditional customers are not replacing employees with AI agents yet, van der Voort pointed out that artificial intelligence companies themselves are growing rapidly. This growth has triggered a huge spike in demand for independent contractors, evolving from simple data labelling to complex tasks performed by PhD researchers alongside their regular academic research. Van der Voort expects this specific market to continue expanding over the coming years as models require more sophisticated human feedback.

Remote currently serves tens of thousands of customers ranging from Burger King to Anthropic. The platform integrates with authorised AI agents like Claude or ChatGPT via a server, while the company continues to build automated tools for cross-border payroll tasks. Van der Voort noted that the company has approximately 2,000 employees, with the largest group of about 20% located in the United States. He also noted that European hiring regulations create complex challenges for businesses operating across multiple jurisdictions in the region.
