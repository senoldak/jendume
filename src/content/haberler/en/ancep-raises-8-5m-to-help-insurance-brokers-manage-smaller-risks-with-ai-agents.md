In the insurance industry, brokers often prioritize large policies that promise high commissions. This focus frequently leads to smaller, time-consuming risks being turned away or delayed, as the administrative effort required to process them often outweighs the potential profit. London-based Ancep is addressing this inefficiency with AI agents designed to handle the heavy lifting of smaller deals, recently securing $8.5 million in funding to scale its operations and expand its team.

Ancep’s AI agents are built to streamline the administrative workflow for insurance brokers. They can read client requests, prepare standard documentation, and collect quotes from insurers via email or online portals. Crucially, the system is designed to operate within the software brokers already use, ensuring a smoother implementation. While the AI handles the repetitive tasks, the final decision-making power remains firmly in the hands of authorized brokers, who review the agents' work to ensure accuracy and compliance.

The economic climate in the insurance sector makes this technology particularly relevant. With global commercial insurance prices falling by 6% in the second quarter of 2026, brokers are facing pressure to find new revenue streams. Ancep estimates that these smaller risks represent up to 30% of the business brokers encounter, and that approximately half of these could be placed profitably with the right tools. For large brokerages, capturing this segment could boost revenue by 5% to 10%.

Ancep operates on a value-based model, charging a share of the additional revenue generated by its agents rather than a fixed subscription fee. The software is already being deployed by major firms in Austria and the UK, with one client reporting a 10% increase in revenue within just six weeks of implementation. This approach highlights a shift in how AI is being applied in insurance: rather than replacing human brokers, the technology is being used to augment their capabilities, allowing them to serve a broader range of clients efficiently.
