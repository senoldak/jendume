Anthropic is taking a significant step in its strategy to transform its AI model, Claude, into a comprehensive workspace platform. The company has announced the addition of "Claude Sheets," a spreadsheet tool, alongside a video call assistant and a data dashboard feature. This move marks a shift for Anthropic, moving beyond being a simple chatbot to creating an ecosystem where users can manage their daily workflows directly within the model's interface.

This development expands the competitive landscape between Anthropic and its major backers, Microsoft and Google. While Claude Sheets offers an experience similar to traditional tools like Excel or Google Sheets, Anthropic product leaders have clarified that the tool is currently "fairly basic" and not intended for complex financial modeling. The company suggests that users continue to rely on existing software for advanced enterprise needs while they focus on developing new ways of working within Claude.

The new tools are currently available in beta for paid users. Furthermore, Anthropic has unveiled an infrastructure that allows major companies such as Adobe, Atlassian, and HubSpot to integrate their applications directly into Claude. These integrations position Claude not just as a text generator, but as a central hub for business software, effectively acting as an operating system for productivity.

From a financial perspective, Anthropic is experiencing rapid growth. The company's revenue run rate reached $81 billion as of October 31. This figure represents a significant increase from the $65 billion reported in July, marking a $16 billion rise in just a few months. With these strong financial indicators, the company is preparing to list on the Nasdaq later this month. Following the introduction of Claude Docs and Slides earlier this year, Anthropic’s footprint in the office software market is becoming increasingly prominent.
