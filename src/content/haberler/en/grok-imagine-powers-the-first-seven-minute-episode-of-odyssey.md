The first seven-minute episode of Odyssey, created using Elon Musk's video tool Grok Imagine, was released on X on October 23, 2026, instead of the full-length film previously promised. London-based startup Wonder Studios produced the episode in collaboration with US director Dev Walker. Xavier Collins, co-founder of Wonder, stated that a team of three completed the project in under two weeks. While reports indicated the episode cost tens of thousands of dollars, Christopher Nolan's blockbuster adaptation cost $250 million, took 91 days to shoot, and grossed $1.8 billion at the box office.

Upon release, the episode drew mixed reactions; film critics and online viewers heavily mocked its jarring cuts, plastic-looking characters, and modern English dialogue, while others viewed it as an important step forward for AI video generation. Collins acknowledged the feedback and stated that subsequent episodes would address historical accuracy and pacing issues, with the ultimate goal of continuous improvement where each new release is radically better than the first.

Meanwhile, the broader entertainment landscape is seeing other AI milestones, such as Turkish studio Ay Yapım releasing Castle Walls on Amazon Prime Video as a major streaming platform release utilizing AI generation. Set in the 11th century at Alamut Castle, the series used human voice actors and human screenwriters while integrating AI tools. While critics offered harsh reviews regarding its visual execution, the studio defended its approach by emphasizing that AI was used to amplify human skill rather than bypass it entirely.
