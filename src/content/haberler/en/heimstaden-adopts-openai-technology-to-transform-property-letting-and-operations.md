European residential real estate operator Heimstaden has announced that it is using OpenAI technologies to overhaul home lettings, environmental-risk analysis, and property operations. The company accelerated its artificial intelligence program in June 2025 after evaluating multiple platforms and selecting OpenAI. The strategy combines employee training, tool access, and centralized coordination to reshape its core business processes.

Artificial intelligence now supports key steps in the letting workflow, including pricing recommendations, image styling, apartment descriptions, and market feedback review. For properties located in the Netherlands, expenses associated with generating digital property visuals dropped from approximately 50 euros to less than 0.10 euros, representing a reduction of more than 99 percent. Heimstaden estimates an annual savings potential of 130,000 euros in that market alone.

The impact extends beyond advertising costs. In environmental risk assessment, an employee lacking prior programming experience utilized Codex to help construct a data pipeline. The project assessed 20,112 assets and generated over six million rows of useful data, reducing an estimated delivery requirement of five people working for a year down to two people working for two months.

Heimstaden notes that employees remain responsible for reviewing and approving all decisions while utilizing approved tools within data security guidelines. Following the rollout of ChatGPT Enterprise in September 2025, half of the company's employees adopted the platform within a few months. The firm is now preparing for the next stage of integration involving systems that can coordinate multi-step processes.
