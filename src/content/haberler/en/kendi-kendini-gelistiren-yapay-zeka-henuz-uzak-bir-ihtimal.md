Recent insights from Google Deepmind reveal that while AI is automating a large share of routine coding tasks, the prospect of fully self-improving artificial intelligence remains distant. According to company engineers, AI now generates over 75 percent of production code. However, roughly 88 percent of code modifications still require human review before final submission, showing that complete autonomy is far from being realized.

As tasks grow more complex and open-ended, AI success rates drop sharply without human intervention. Researchers emphasize that human expertise is still critically needed for creative research, novel experiment design, and long-term planning.

Tracking progress through internal benchmarks and capability indices, the team notes that model capabilities are growing at a steady, predictable pace rather than accelerating exponentially. While some prominent scientists warn of a potential intelligence explosion in the near future, current operational metrics suggest that fully autonomous research automation is not imminent.
