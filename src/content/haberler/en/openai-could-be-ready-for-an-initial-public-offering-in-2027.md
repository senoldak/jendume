OpenAI President Greg Brockman announced on November 4, 2026, at a technology conference that the company could be ready for an initial public offering (IPO) by 2027. This statement comes as the company experiences significant momentum in its enterprise-focused business, marking a pivotal shift in its operational strategy. Management noted that they are committed to going public when the timing is right and expressed strong confidence in the current state of the business.

This development highlights that artificial intelligence is no longer just a tool for individual consumers but has become a critical infrastructure for large-scale corporate operations. The potential public offering signals a major shift in the tech landscape, where investor confidence and capital are increasingly flowing toward AI-driven enterprise software solutions. The company emphasizes that building trust and long-term partnerships with clients has become their primary focus.

Despite a challenging start to 2026, with the first and second quarters described as difficult, the company saw a 70% quarterly growth in its annualized revenue by the third quarter. The enterprise segment, which previously accounted for only 30% of total revenue, has now become the primary driver of the company's financial performance. This growth has been bolstered by strategic partnerships with major institutions, such as JPMorgan Chase.

Previously perceived primarily as a consumer-facing entity, the company is now actively competing with rivals like Anthropic in the enterprise market. Management emphasized that their focus has shifted toward helping businesses identify key milestones and building long-term trust with clients. The 2027 target reflects the company's ambition to transition from a research-focused developer into a financially mature corporate entity, solidifying its position as a reliable partner for global industries.
