Google has announced the rollout of Tasks within Gemini, making the feature free of charge in six countries and powered by the company's new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. Formerly known during testing as Spark, the tool transforms the standard chatbot into an active, background helper designed to manage multi-step work for users. The feature allows individuals to switch their home-screen control from Chat to Tasks to handle various everyday duties.

Unlike traditional chat functions that merely provide information, Tasks empowers the application to execute basic secretarial tasks. Users in the United States gain access to advanced capabilities such as letting the AI agent call businesses to compare product pricing, check inventory, complete purchases via Google Pay, and securely sign into accounts supported by Google Password Manager. Users can also interact with the agent by sending emails or text messages.

The initial free rollout targets users aged 18 and older in the United States, Canada, Australia, India, Japan, and Korea, while Pro and Ultra subscribers in selected other regions also retain access. Getting started requires simply flipping a toggle switch inside the application from Chat to Tasks.

This expansion highlights the broader industry trend of moving toward agentic AI that acts on behalf of the user in the physical and digital worlds. By enabling assistants to place phone calls for service appointments or handle financial transactions, technology companies are pushing AI deeper into daily practical workflows.
