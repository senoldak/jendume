NVIDIA has officially rolled out its RTX Spark laptops worldwide, introducing a new category of personal computers designed for creators, gamers, and local AI workflows. Developed in collaboration with top manufacturers including ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft, and MSI, the new systems start at $2,599 with regional configurations varying across different markets. The initial launch lineup features the ASUS ProArt P14 and P16, Dell XPS 16, and HP OmniBook.

The lineup offers powerful hardware capable of running self-contained AI agents, creative applications, and major games. Reviewers have praised the platform for delivering polished performance across gaming and creative workflows right out of the box, noting that the systems represent a significant statement of intent for the future of personal computing and fully autonomous CUDA-accelerated digital processing.

To celebrate the global availability, launch events and hands-on demonstrations were held in major cities including San Francisco at Best Buy, Berlin at Xperion, Taipei at Muse Garden, Beijing at JD MALL, Istanbul at Vatan Bilgisayar Topkapı, and Seoul at the COEX East Lobby. These gatherings allowed enthusiasts to test the new capabilities firsthand, explore local creator programming, and join community discussions.

The release highlights a growing industry trend of integrating dedicated artificial intelligence acceleration into everyday computing devices, allowing users to harness advanced software tools locally on their machines without heavy cloud dependency. More configurations and systems are expected to follow through manufacturers and major retailers worldwide.
