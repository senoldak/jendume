Many businesses around the world have long relied on consumer-facing chat apps like WhatsApp or iMessage for everyday staff coordination, despite IT and security compliance hurdles. Founded by Guy Weiss, Zenzap aims to solve this challenge by offering a private messenger infused with AI capabilities tailored for organizational use. The company recently raised $44 million in funding from prominent investors including Bessemer, Lightspeed, and The Garage.

The platform is designed to serve a wide range of industries, from healthcare and hospitality to construction and fast-food chains. Unlike traditional enterprise communication tools, Zenzap provides mobile-first features such as AI-powered voice chat, smart task management, and workspace-wide AI search that adheres to corporate compliance standards. Major brands like FedEx, Starbucks, and Taco Bell currently utilize the platform for operational coordination and supply discussions.

Operating with a team across the U.S., Tel Aviv, and Europe, the startup plans to use the newly acquired capital to scale its product and R&D departments. Industry experts note that as employees increasingly demand a clear separation between personal and professional lives, purpose-built workplace agents are becoming essential.
