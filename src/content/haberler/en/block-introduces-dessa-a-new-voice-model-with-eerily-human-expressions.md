Jack Dorsey’s Block has released a new voice model from its research lab that runs locally and sounds human with expressions like laughs, pauses, and cries. Called Dessa, the model is trained fully on human speech and a single NVIDIA GPU, offering a remarkably natural vocal cadence.

Dessa is a full-duplex model, meaning it can talk and listen at the same time. While traditional speech models typically rely on large language models for knowledge retrieval, Block stated that Dessa’s knowledge stems directly from the conversational data it has processed.

The company pointed out that current voice systems often sound unnatural because they convert text into speech using intermediate pipelines and tags. Block’s researchers claim that emotion is inherently built into Dessa, allowing the bot to adjust its voice tone dynamically, such as sounding tired when discussing fatigue.

A significant technical achievement of the model is its ability to run locally on consumer-grade hardware, such as a MacBook M4 Max or better. There is currently no public access to Dessa, as Block has invited safety researchers to test the model first to mitigate potential misuse.

As a company focused on financial and small business solutions, Block sees potential in leveraging this on-device voice technology to power enhanced business and consumer experiences at a lower operational cost.
