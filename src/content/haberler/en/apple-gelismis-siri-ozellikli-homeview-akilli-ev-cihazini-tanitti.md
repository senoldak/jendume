Apple announced a new lineup of smart home products in October 2026, headlined by the HomeView device. Designed to serve as a central hub, the gadget features a seven-inch screen and face recognition capabilities.

Priced starting at two hundred ninety-nine dollars, HomeView integrates with doorbells, thermostats, and security locks. The device showcases a revamped version of Siri, which leverages personal context to provide more relevant and conversational responses.

With this release, Apple aims to strengthen its position against established competitors in the smart home ecosystem. However, regulatory hurdles in the European Union mean the Siri-powered device will not be available in that market at launch.
