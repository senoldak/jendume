Dwelly, one of the UK’s top ten largest lettings operators, has revealed significant progress in integrating autonomous AI agents into its core business. Since rolling out its agentic product suite in April 2024, the company has automated approximately 55% of the steps involved in letting a property. Previously, every single stage of the process required manual human action; now, the system only flags about 13 tasks per letting that require real human work.

The AI agents handle end-to-end workflows, including screening applications, communicating with landlords, requesting holding deposits, and chasing payments. Dwelly does not build its own foundation models; instead, it utilizes Anthropic’s Claude and OpenAI’s models via business APIs. The company builds its own proprietary data, operating procedures, and testing frameworks around these models to ensure industry-specific accuracy and security.

The impact on operational efficiency is substantial. The share of applicants receiving a response within an hour has jumped from 35% to 90%. The time taken from a rental offer to a paid holding deposit has been slashed from five days to just 2.4 days. Furthermore, the rate of failed lettings has dropped from 14% to 9%. This efficiency allows a single negotiator to manage 700 properties, up from the previous limit of 350.

Dwelly operates on the principle that 'autonomy is earned,' meaning tasks are only handed over to AI once it consistently outperforms human decision-making. While the AI manages the bulk of administrative tasks, human staff remain responsible for landlord relationships and complex problem-solving. The company aims to automate 85% of the letting steps by the first quarter of 2027, further reducing the manual touchpoints required for each transaction.
