New details have emerged regarding the financial statements of OpenAI, one of the leading figures in the artificial intelligence world. The company's annual revenue is stated to be around 50 billion dollars. It turns out that a previously circulated figure suggesting an annualized revenue approaching 70 billion dollars was based on a different calculation method.

These methods, used to make revenue comparisons between major artificial intelligence firms like OpenAI and Anthropic more aligned, create differences in how earnings are recorded. While Anthropic can record total sales through cloud providers as revenue and later show the provider's share as an expense, OpenAI records only its own remaining share as revenue.

Both approaches comply with generally accepted accounting standards, yet they cause companies' financial sizes to appear differently on paper. Especially in the artificial intelligence sector, which grows with multi-billion-dollar infrastructure investments and high costs, such financial details carry great importance for reading company performance correctly.
