As artificial intelligence agents become increasingly integrated into our daily lives and professional workflows, understanding how these systems operate behind the scenes remains a major challenge. Gurmeet Saran, an engineer at the AI research company Anthropic, is dedicated to solving this exact problem by developing infrastructure that ensures the underlying architecture of AI chatbots produces reliable outputs.

Saran points out that observing large language models is vastly different from monitoring traditional databases or web services, presenting a unique observability challenge for the tech industry. He emphasizes that data privacy and system governance cannot simply be bolted on as an afterthought; they must be embedded into the architecture from the very beginning to prevent logic errors and protect sensitive user information.

Drawing on a career spanning more than twenty years building data infrastructure at companies like Yahoo, Intuit, Meta, and Wealthfront, Saran believes that making AI systems genuinely auditable and transparent is one of the most vital engineering challenges of the coming decade, a factor often overshadowed by discussions about raw model capabilities.
