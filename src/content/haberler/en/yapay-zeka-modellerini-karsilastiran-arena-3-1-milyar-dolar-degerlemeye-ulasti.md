Arena, a platform dedicated to comparing and evaluating artificial intelligence models, has secured $200 million in a funding round co-led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and Khosla Ventures. Following this investment, the company's valuation has climbed to $3.1 billion. Originating in 2023 as a research project at UC Berkeley, the platform allows users to input the same prompt into different AI models and vote on which one performs better. These evaluations are then compiled into a comprehensive performance leaderboard. In September 2025, the company expanded its offerings with enterprise evaluation tools to help businesses choose the most suitable AI systems for their needs. The newly acquired capital will be used to further develop its platform for assessing model performance, safety, and alignment under real-world conditions.
