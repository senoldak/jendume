Defense tech startup Shield AI has reached an agreement to acquire Echodyne, a company manufacturing radars for counter-drone systems, for $5.3 billion. To support and fund the deal, Shield AI is raising $2.5 billion in equity and debt at a valuation of $28.8 billion. This valuation more than doubles the company's recorded market value from earlier in the same year.

The newly raised funding package includes a $1 billion Series H round co-led by Disruptive and Innovation X Capital, $1 billion in structured equity provided by Blackstone funds, and $500 million in debt from JPMorgan. Under the terms of the deal, Shield AI will pay $4.8 billion at closing, with an additional payout of up to $500 million contingent on Echodyne reaching specific performance targets. The transaction is targeted for completion in early 2027.

Based in Kirkland, Washington, Echodyne designs and builds compact radar units used in counter-drone and security deployments across more than 40 countries, with clients including the US military. Shield AI develops Hivemind, an artificial intelligence software engine capable of piloting military aircraft autonomously, alongside its V-BAT surveillance drone and the upcoming AI-piloted X-BAT fighter jet.

Shield AI Chief Executive Gary Steele stated that acquiring Echodyne represents the company's first step into dual-use hardware aimed at expanding beyond government defense contracts. The combined technologies are expected to address growing commercial and public safety demands, including defending venues and public spaces against unauthorized drone activity.
