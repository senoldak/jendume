The open-source Hermes Agent, developed by Nous Research, is now available directly within the Microsoft Teams platform. This integration allows employees to delegate routine tasks such as financial analysis, document drafting, or inbox sorting to the AI, with the ability to review results directly within the chat interface. Additionally, a desktop version of the application is accessible via the Microsoft Store.

The most notable feature of this integration is "Collective Wisdom." This functionality automatically converts successful workflows performed by an individual employee into reusable skills. Once reviewed, these skills can be shared with the entire team, effectively transforming individual expertise into a shared organizational asset. This approach ensures that institutional knowledge is preserved and leveraged across the company rather than remaining siloed.

Regarding security and data privacy, the system operates within Azure Container Apps Sandboxes, utilizing isolated microVMs. This architecture ensures that all collected corporate knowledge remains strictly within the company’s environment and is not utilized as training data for any external AI provider.

Nous Research receives financial backing through M12, which is Microsoft's venture capital investment fund. According to the developers, Hermes Agent operates widely across OpenRouter as an open agent, handling an extensive volume of nearly two trillion tokens daily. Nous Research positions itself as a United States-based open-source organization designed to balance global AI developments, and the company has fine-tuned a variation of Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B model named Arete-27B to reflect specific regional guidelines. The developers stated that AI is evolving from a simple task-automation tool into a collaborative partner that actively builds and protects corporate institutional memory.
