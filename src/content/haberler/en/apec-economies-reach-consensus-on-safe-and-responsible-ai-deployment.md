Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) member economies have reached a consensus on the safe deployment and responsible use of artificial intelligence. Chen Xu, chair of this year’s APEC Senior Officials’ Meeting, announced that member states held in-depth discussions on digital technologies and AI governance.

The consensus comes ahead of the APEC leaders' meeting hosted in Shenzhen on November 18 and 19, 2026. Chen Xu, a former UN ambassador for China, stated that participants engaged in comprehensive talks, though specific details of the agreed framework have not yet been disclosed. China expressed its intent to secure an ambitious outcome document from the summit.

AI safety and governance have remained central to APEC’s agenda throughout 2026. In July, members adopted a joint statement during a digital and AI ministerial meeting in Chengdu, establishing a baseline framework for regional AI development and cooperation. This progress builds on Chinese President Xi Jinping’s proposal at the previous summit in South Korea to establish a global governing body for AI technologies.

Comprising 21 member economies including the United States and China, APEC plays a strategic role in shaping global standards for technology adoption. Bringing major global powers together around shared safety principles provides a vital platform for future international cooperation on AI deployment.
