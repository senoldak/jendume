Agricultural machinery giant John Deere has added a new strategic move to its efforts to integrate artificial intelligence into farming. The company announced the acquisition of Orchard Robotics, a startup that monitors crops using cameras mounted on tractors and advanced AI algorithms. This acquisition highlights the critical role that digitalization and precision agriculture applications are playing in the modern farming sector.

The system developed by Orchard Robotics consists of cameras attached to tractors and other agricultural machinery. As these machines move through fields, the cameras continuously collect visual data. The company's AI models then analyze these images in real-time to generate data on vines, trees, and other crops. This information is used to track plant growth, yield, and overall health. With this technology, farmers can perform tasks such as spraying and harvesting with much higher precision, focusing only on the areas that actually need attention.

This development is significant for producers aiming to increase efficiency in agricultural production and optimize resource usage. Precision agriculture technologies reduce unnecessary chemical and fertilizer use, thereby lowering costs and minimizing environmental impact. By integrating this crop-level intelligence with its own field equipment, John Deere aims to provide end-to-end solutions for farmers.

Since its founding in 2022, the startup has received significant support from investors. Having raised over $25 million in total funding, the company secured $22 million in a Series A round in September 2025. Orchard Robotics, which employs approximately 45 people, had become a notable player in the agricultural technology ecosystem. This move by John Deere follows the company's 2025 acquisition of GUSS Automation, a manufacturer of autonomous spraying machines, and continues its automation strategy focused on high-value crops such as orchards and vineyards.
