Liquid AI has released its new decision-focused models, d1.2 and d1.2-ultra. Unlike traditional generative AI that writes text, these models analyze text, images, or audio inputs to return probability-based answers for specific questions. This approach is critical for business processes where determining the need for human intervention is essential. The new models are capable of processing text, images, and audio in a single API call.

The d1.2-ultra model achieved a score of 75 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1, ranking first in its category. This performance highlights the model's ability to analyze complex decisions accurately. Furthermore, the models have expanded their capabilities with five new answer types: reading values, finding text spans, locating objects, estimating numbers, and multi-label choices. In audio processing tasks, the models reportedly achieve an accuracy rate between 95% and 97% on benchmarks like ESC-50 and FLEURS.

One of the most notable features is the system's calibration success. The d1.2-ultra model reports a calibration error of only 0.8 points across 13 benchmarks, offering high reliability in avoiding incorrect decisions. This allows businesses to make safer, more confident choices in AI-driven automation processes.

Previous versions, d1 and d1.1, are now deprecated. Liquid AI currently offers these models exclusively through its hosted API, with no public weights or parameter counts disclosed yet. Pricing is set at $0.02 per 1 million tokens for d1.2 and $0.042 for d1.2-ultra. The company is also offering $2 in free credit for new organizations to test the platform.
