OpenAI previously announced the ChatGPT Space ecosystem, which brought together productivity tools such as Pages, Slides, and Sheets, noting at the time that presentation and spreadsheet features would be released later. That expected step has now been taken, with ChatGPT Slides officially becoming available to users. The tool enables individuals to transform their existing chat history or uploaded files into professional presentations using a single prompt.

The system allows users to generate content from scratch or choose from ready-made templates available within the platform. According to the official announcement shared on OpenAI's official X account, the feature also supports real-time collaboration, much like the Google Slides environment. Invited team members can join the project to make simultaneous edits and leave comments directly on the slides.

Feedback and comments added by team members are thoroughly analyzed by the system, allowing the presentation to be updated automatically based on those contributions. Completed presentations can be opened directly in popular applications such as PowerPoint or Google Slides, and they can also be downloaded to a device in PDF or PowerPoint format.

ChatGPT Slides is currently available exclusively to users subscribed to Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans. Eligible subscribers can start utilizing the feature immediately by giving prompts through a file or chat interface. Meanwhile, ChatGPT Plus subscribers do not yet have access to the Slides feature within the ChatGPT Space ecosystem.
