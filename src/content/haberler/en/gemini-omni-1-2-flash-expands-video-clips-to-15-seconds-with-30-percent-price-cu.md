Google has released its latest video generation model, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, making it generally available to developers and businesses. This update enhances the model's capabilities, allowing for longer, more consistent video clips while significantly improving cost efficiency. The model is now accessible through Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, and the Google Cloud platform.

The most notable improvement is the extension of video duration from 10 seconds to 15 seconds. This change provides creators with more narrative space for advertising campaigns, product demonstrations, and social media content. Furthermore, the introduction of conversational editing allows users to modify specific elements of a generated clip—such as background, lighting, or aspect ratio—without needing to regenerate the entire sequence. This feature streamlines the production process and reduces the need for trial-and-error iterations.

The update also brings a substantial reduction in costs. Google has lowered the price for 720p video generation to $0.07 per second, representing a 30 percent decrease. This pricing strategy encourages a tiered workflow where users can generate low-resolution drafts to refine details before upscaling the final result to 1080p or 4K, making the process more economically viable.

Technically, the model features improved reference adherence. Inputs such as character photos, product images, and audio cues are preserved more accurately across different camera angles. Additionally, all generated content includes an invisible SynthID watermark to assist in origin verification. Google also claims enhancements in natural speech, lip-sync, and the ability to distinguish between multiple speakers. These advancements position the model as a more robust tool for professional video production workflows.
