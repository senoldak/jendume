OpenAI has announced three new discoveries in mathematics, computer science, and physics concerning harmonic analysis, complexity theory, and quantum field theory. To convert these raw research results into scientific understanding, the company is experimenting with a new approach by collaborating with external academic experts to shepherd and analyze the findings.

The mathematics result addresses the local smoothing conjecture concerning wave focusing, proving it in every dimension and thereby resolving the Kakeya conjecture and Stein's restriction conjecture across all dimensions. In computer science, the system proved that the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a longstanding conjecture about computational complexity, is false. This finding was verified in Lean and is being reviewed by a group of experts from UC San Diego and MIT.

The collaborations follow four core principles: transparency, no academic credit claimed by OpenAI, proper recognition for participating researchers, and complete independence. While the computer science natural language proof and Lean formalization have been published directly to GitHub, the physics collaboration decided to hold off on releasing its AI-produced artifacts.

These developments highlight the growing capability of artificial intelligence to tackle fundamental theoretical questions. Refuting the Exponential Time Hypothesis reopens discussions on algorithmic efficiency and complexity. OpenAI's collaborative framework aims to bridge the gap between AI outputs and rigorous academic peer review.
