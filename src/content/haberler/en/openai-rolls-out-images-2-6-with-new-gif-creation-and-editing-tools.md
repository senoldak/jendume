OpenAI has officially released Images 2.6 for ChatGPT, Codex, and the API, introducing new creative capabilities focused on animated images and custom GIFs. The update enables users to generate custom animated media simply by typing a descriptive text prompt or by uploading an existing photo and minimal sketches. This new feature moves beyond traditional clipped media derived from television shows and movies, allowing individuals to bring their specific ideas to life directly within their messaging and group chats.

The platform also offers extensive editing tools once a GIF is generated. Users can easily adjust the playback speed so that animations loop faster or slower, examine individual frames via a sheet view to add comments and markups, or prompt ChatGPT to apply specific visual edits. According to OpenAI, the new iteration generates images about 50 percent faster than its predecessor, while platform-wide daily image generation averages over 500 million images.

Images 2.6 is available to all users without requiring a paid subscription, though generation limits vary based on account tiers. For developers utilizing the API, the update introduces flexible performance choices, such as Flare and Sunburst options, to balance speed, detail, and consistency in image generation. This release marks another significant step in making advanced visual creation accessible for everyday digital communication and creative expression.
