AI lab Third Moment Research has introduced a new system that enables thousands of autonomous agents to run continuously for days using the latest reasoning models. The system builds a self-improving architecture around agent behavior, identifying recurring weaknesses and automatically generating scenarios to enhance performance.

The company initially applied this technology in quantitative finance. AI agents successfully analyzed market data, identified performance bottlenecks, and optimized codebases to reduce processing times from hours to mere minutes while optimizing token consumption.

The setup also automates security testing within research environments. Specialized cybersecurity tools allow agents to conduct automated penetration testing on their own simulation infrastructure to ensure operational safety and prevent unauthorized access.

Looking ahead, Third Moment plans to expand these capabilities into supply chain optimization, software engineering, and large-scale data analysis. This roadmap aims to scale operations to hundreds of thousands of collaborating subagents tackling industrial challenges.
