Jasjeet Sekhon, who served as the chief strategy officer at Google DeepMind, has announced his departure to join the AI research company Anthropic. Sekhon, who spent approximately eight months at DeepMind, stated that his new focus at Anthropic will be ensuring that the Claude AI model practices and values "epistemic rigor." This involves refining the model's ability to distinguish between what it knows and what it does not, a critical step in improving AI reliability. This transition underscores the ongoing battle for top-tier talent in the artificial intelligence sector.

Before his time at DeepMind, Sekhon held significant roles, including serving as chief scientist and head of AI at the hedge fund Bridgewater Associates, where he helped build the AIA Labs research unit. His academic background includes professorships at Harvard, Yale, and the University of California, Berkeley, with a focus on causal inference, machine learning, and experimental design. These skills are increasingly vital as companies seek to move beyond simple pattern matching toward more robust, logically sound AI systems.

The timing of Sekhon's move is notable, occurring just days before Anthropic's scheduled initial public offering (IPO). By securing a leader with such deep expertise in research methodology and AI strategy, Anthropic is signaling a commitment to model quality and long-term reliability as it prepares to enter the public markets.

Previously, Sekhon has spoken on the importance of "recursive self-improvement"—the concept of AI systems building better versions of themselves—labeling it a key component of the current AI investment thesis. By applying his expertise to Claude, Sekhon aims to address the fundamental challenge of AI accuracy. This hire reflects a broader industry trend where the focus is shifting from merely scaling models to ensuring they operate with greater transparency and logical consistency.
