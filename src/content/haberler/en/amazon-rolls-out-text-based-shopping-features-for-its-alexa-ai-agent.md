Amazon is introducing a new text messaging feature for its AI-powered shopping assistant, Alexa for Shopping. Initially rolling out to select customers in the US by invitation, the feature allows users to chat directly with the AI agent without opening the Amazon app or website. Rajiv Mehta, Amazon’s vice president of conversational shopping, notes that the personal shopping agent is now just a text away.

The new capability lets shoppers send text prompts to build weekly grocery lists, search for gifts, or attach photos to find similar items. Users can also ask the agent to reorder products when running low, such as pet food, check delivery statuses, track price histories for up to a full year, and set up alerts for when products go on sale. Requests made through the text thread seamlessly carry over to the Amazon Shopping app and website if further research is needed.

Amazon launched Alexa for Shopping in May and notes that more than 400 million customers used the service in the past year. Additionally, the company generated more than $68 billion from advertising in 2025. The new text thread also introduces a fresh space for ads, allowing sponsored products to appear among the options sent by the agent.

As competing platforms like OpenAI and DoorDash also expand into messaging-based agents, Amazon's latest update aims to keep users deeply engaged within its ecosystem while offering greater convenience in everyday retail interactions.
