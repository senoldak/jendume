Google is rolling out a fresh iteration of its Gemini artificial intelligence assistant that significantly upgrades its capability to execute multistep assignments. According to the enterprise, the enhancement centers on the Task mode, which permits Gemini to manage complicated operations such as scheduling appointments and evaluating online product inventories.

The search engine developer is transitioning the functionality to Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its latest large language model. While slightly less powerful than the advanced Gemini 4 Carbon model, Hydrogen proves considerably more cost-efficient and processes tasks 2.8 times faster than the preceding entry-level model. It accomplishes this by executing project phases concurrently and permitting user feedback at every developmental stage.

For digital transactions, Task mode currently employs Google Wallet and is collaborating with major financial institutions like American Express, Mastercard, and Visa to integrate payment tokenization support. This mechanism substitutes user card numbers with temporary placeholder tokens to minimize data exposure risks. Furthermore, Google intends to introduce the Universal Commerce Protocol to facilitate agent communications with virtual storefronts.

Gemini's Task mode also securely handles information from alternative Google services, such as Gmail, inside hardware-isolated virtual environments. As part of this deployment, Google has made the Task mode available at no cost within the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, potentially challenging rivals that bill users for comparable functionalities.
