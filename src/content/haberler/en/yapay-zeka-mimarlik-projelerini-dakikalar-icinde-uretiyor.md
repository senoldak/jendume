Combining a background in architecture with a passion for technology, entrepreneurs have turned a long-held vision into reality following the rise of generative artificial intelligence. Santa Monica-based startup Blueprints AI has created software designed to produce architectural construction documents in a fraction of the usual time.

Unveiled in October 2026 at an event in San Francisco, the second-generation software targets architecture, engineering, and design firms as well as real estate developers. Users can start with a project description or sketch to simultaneously generate blueprints for architecture, structural, electrical, and plumbing elements. Any single design modification updates the entire project and checks it against local building codes.

The founders emphasize that the technology does not replace licensed architects. Professional human judgment remains essential as licensed experts review the documents and affix their seals before submission. By taking over routine drafting, the tool aims to give architects more time to focus on creative space design.
