Bengaluru-based startup Scimplify has secured $25 million in an all-equity strategic round led by Hitachi Ventures, bringing its valuation to $250 million. The company focuses on using advanced computational tools and artificial intelligence to revolutionize the production of specialty chemicals and critical materials.

Traditionally, turning complex chemical requirements into commercial products takes anywhere from 12 to 18 months. However, by utilizing its proprietary AI platform named Scinode, the startup coordinates research, manufacturing processes, and quality control through specialized AI agents, reducing delivery times significantly.

With strategic markets expanding into electronics, defense, and energy, the company aims to offer alternative sourcing for critical materials heavily concentrated in a few regions. The newly acquired funds will be channeled toward expanding its manufacturing network into the U.S. and Japan while further developing its core AI platform.
