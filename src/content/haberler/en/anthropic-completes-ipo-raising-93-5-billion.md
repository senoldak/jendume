Anthropic has concluded its initial public offering, raising a total of $93.5 billion. The company offered 1.127 billion shares of Class A common stock at a price of $83 per share. While the initial offering raised $81.3 billion, the exercise of the "greenshoe" overallotment option added $12.2 billion, making the event one of the largest in technology history.

Beyond the public offering, Anthropic finalized a concurrent private placement of Class N common stock, securing $26 billion from a group of major tech companies, including Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, and AMD. When combined with the IPO proceeds, the company's total gross funding reached $119.5 billion. This capital allows the company to scale its Claude AI models and expand its underlying infrastructure.

On its first day of trading, the stock opened at $111.50 but closed at $97.17. By Friday, shares had dipped 12% amid a broader sell-off in the technology sector. Investors are weighing the company's prospects against market concerns, such as leadership changes at Nvidia and the potential impact of rising interest rates on multi-trillion-dollar AI infrastructure projects.

Founded in 2021 by siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei after their departure from OpenAI, Anthropic has risen to prominence through its Claude family of models. The founders view this IPO as a milestone in the company's ongoing mission. With this capital, Anthropic is positioned to accelerate its research and development efforts, aiming to maintain its competitive edge in the field of artificial intelligence.
