As artificial intelligence investments accelerate across the corporate world, businesses face an unexpected cost blind spot at the infrastructure layer. While cloud-based managed services speed up deployment, restricted access to underlying systems makes real-time and task-based cost analysis nearly impossible. Industry projections indicate that enterprises will operate thousands of AI agents in the near future, making uncontrolled architectures a severe threat to operational budgets.

Current standard pricing models based on tokens fall short of measuring complex agentic workflows, as a single task executed by an AI agent can generate unexpectedly high costs. Therefore, experts emphasize that companies must shift toward infrastructure controls that ensure transparency and adopt a cost-per-completed-task strategy instead of token-based metrics.
