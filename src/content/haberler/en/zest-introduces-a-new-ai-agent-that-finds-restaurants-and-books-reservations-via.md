Restaurant discovery app Zest is taking a significant step toward a future where mobile apps are no longer the primary interface by introducing a new AI-powered restaurant concierge. The company has developed a feature that enables users to receive restaurant recommendations and instantly book tables through text messages rather than opening a separate app. Founder and CEO Mario Gomez-Hall explains that the agent itself has become the product, leveraging the user's past habits to provide tailored suggestions without requiring weeks of conversation.

The app imports users' credit card data via financial services company Plaid to track food and drink transactions, building a profile of their tastes. With this new feature, users can text the Zest concierge at (212) 470-6067. The assistant can also be integrated into group chats, blending everyone's tastes, settling indecision with an in-thread voting feature, and completing reservations through connected services like OpenTable, Resy, and SevenRooms.

Originally founded in November 2024, the startup secured $1.8 million in pre-seed backing from Alexis Ohanian's 776 and Steve Jang's Kindred Ventures. With this text-based AI agent, Zest aims to scale beyond thousands of users and generate revenue through booking and food ordering services. During testing, the system successfully provided tailored suggestions matching specific conditions such as cuisine, location, and party size.

Over time, Zest plans to expand its AI-driven recommendation and booking model beyond restaurants into businesses, shopping, and more. As conversational AI agents reshape the traditional app economy, Zest's messaging-first approach promises a practical shift in user habits.
