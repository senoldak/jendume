Ten major technology companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, and Mistral, signed a new declaration in Paris aimed at enhancing the safety of advanced artificial intelligence models. Hosted by French President Emmanuel Macron, the meeting addressed the urgent need to manage the risks that rapidly advancing AI technologies pose to collective security.

This initiative represents a significant effort to establish a common framework for safety between governments and private corporations. The signatory companies committed to developing fail-safe mechanisms capable of shutting down their models during internal use or training. Furthermore, they agreed to provide clearer information regarding system capabilities and to permit authorized external specialists to evaluate the software where appropriate. The declaration outlines a shared approach to assessing risks such as cyberattacks, weapons development, and the potential for automated systems to operate outside human supervision or oversight.

Despite these commitments, the French AI safety organization CeSIA criticized the declaration, arguing that it lacks enforcement power. The organization pointed out that the agreement establishes no shared risk criteria for evaluating whether a model should be deployed or distributed, nor does it guarantee that external reviewers or governments will secure entry to the architectures required for evaluation. CeSIA maintains that public authorities must have the clear authority to suspend models when risks become too high.

During the forum, President Macron also highlighted the effects of AI on children, advocating for a pre-certification process for all AI tools intended for young users, comparing them to safety standards required for children's toys. This declaration builds upon previous safety initiatives launched by Norway and Finland, as well as discussions held during international summits. While the declaration remains open to other signatories, it currently includes six countries and sets no specific deadlines for implementation.
