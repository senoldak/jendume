Google has taken a significant step toward privacy in its AI-powered search experience, known as AI Mode. The new 'Temporary Threads' feature allows users to conduct conversations with the AI that are not recorded in their search history. This ensures that specific inquiries do not influence future personalized recommendations or the user's overall Google profile, providing a cleaner separation between casual queries and long-term data.

While Google Search has long offered an incognito mode for traditional web browsing, chat-based AI interactions were previously linked directly to the user's account and search history. With this update, a dedicated icon in the top-right corner of the AI Mode interface enables temporary sessions. In these sessions, the AI provides responses independent of the user's past preferences, and the dialogue is not utilized for Google's future personalization processes.

This feature is particularly vital for users researching sensitive topics or those seeking a one-time answer to a specific question. For instance, someone consulting the AI about a medical condition or a financial matter may prefer that the interaction does not resurface later as an advertisement or a suggested topic. Temporary threads offer the convenience of AI assistance without compromising personal privacy.

Google is deploying this feature globally across all supported languages for both desktop and mobile devices. Once a user closes a temporary session, they return to the standard search experience, and the data from that specific chat is discarded. This move is seen as part of Google's broader strategy to integrate the privacy options found in tools like Gemini directly into its core search engine functionality.
