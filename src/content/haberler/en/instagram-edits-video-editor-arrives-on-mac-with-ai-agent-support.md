Meta has officially released the Mac version of its Instagram video editing app, Edits, to enable creators to handle more ambitious projects beyond mobile capabilities. Already boasting over 50 million active users on mobile, the desktop experience introduces professional-grade features. The software provides a comprehensive editing environment where users can manage multiple layers of video and audio simultaneously. By utilizing the larger screen space of macOS, the app offers the kind of granular control over visual transitions and audio timing that is often difficult to achieve on a smartphone interface.

A pivotal aspect of this release is the support for AI agents through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Matthew Simari, who leads product management for Edits at Instagram, confirmed that the desktop app includes a specialized interface for all internal tools. This allows AI models to interact with the software's editing features directly, potentially building rough cuts or automating repetitive tasks. This integration signals Meta's commitment to embedding AI deeply into the creative workflow of modern content creators by allowing software to 'see' and use the tools just like a human editor would.

Originally launched to compete with ByteDance’s CapCut, Edits has gained significant momentum within the Meta ecosystem. During a recent earnings call, CEO Mark Zuckerberg highlighted the app's growth, noting its 50 million user milestone. Unlike many competitors, Edits allows users to export finished videos without watermarks and publish them directly to Instagram, streamlining the distribution process for creators. The desktop version also supports 4K exports and dedicated keyboard shortcuts, catering to the needs of high-end video production.

Meta is currently developing additional features to allow seamless project transitions between mobile and desktop devices. This will eventually allow a creator to start a project on their phone and finish it on a Mac with the help of AI-driven automation. By combining professional editing tools with AI accessibility, Meta aims to redefine how digital content is conceptualized and executed across its platforms, moving toward a future where AI can autonomously handle complex production tasks.
