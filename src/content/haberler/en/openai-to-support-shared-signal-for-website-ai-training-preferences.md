OpenAI has announced that it will honor a common signal in robots.txt, allowing web publishers and operators to communicate their preferences regarding foundational generative AI training to multiple providers at once. This voluntary effort is designed to make it significantly easier for content creators and website operators to share the exact same preferences with several participating providers simultaneously. Previously, website operators had to identify individual crawlers and maintain separate rules for every single provider, creating a burdensome administrative workflow. Implementing a shared signal across multiple participating providers reduces this operational workload and offers web owners a simpler way to manage how their content is used. This upcoming signal will not replace current crawler controls provided through robots.txt; existing mechanisms will remain fully active and respected. The company aims to implement this signal in the coming months and share another update with implementation details at that time. The initiative builds upon the ongoing efforts of the Internet Engineering Task Force’s AI Preferences working group, aligning closely with broader industry discussions. OpenAI believes this strengthens its continued participation in IETF standards discussions and will continue to adapt its practices as conversations evolve. By taking this practical step toward an open web where people can freely publish and discover information, the company hopes to encourage broader industry adoption and standards among other AI providers.
