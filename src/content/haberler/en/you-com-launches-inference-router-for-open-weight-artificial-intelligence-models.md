You.com has introduced Inference Router, a new API service designed to help companies easily run open-weight artificial intelligence models in production environments. This OpenAI-compatible service allows developers to access twelve different live models through a single endpoint. The system offers enterprise users a minimum uptime service level agreement of 99.9 percent and a zero data retention policy.

The new service aims to eliminate barriers such as security reviews and data privacy concerns that companies face when testing open-source models. Operating entirely on US-hosted infrastructure, the system routes requests directly through You.com's capacity and fails over to partner data centers during outages. Users pay only for what they use with no platform fees.

The system includes numerous open-weight options such as Kimi K3, Qwen3.8 Max, GLM 5.3, and DeepSeek models. While token costs remain close to market rates, developers can provide up-to-date data to models thanks to a built-in web search tool. Internal tests indicate that this integration delivers performance close to frontier models at a lower cost.

This move supports companies in shifting toward open-source models securely without depending on a single provider during AI development. For organizations with high data privacy and security requirements, managed API solutions like this stand out as an important step accelerating AI adoption.
