As personal AI assistants increasingly make transactions on behalf of users, the nondeterministic nature of large language models raises the possibility of bots making unintended purchases. To address this challenge, American Express has introduced a framework aimed at capturing and authenticating user purchase intent.

Dubbed the 'Intent Intelligence Framework,' the company hopes this solution will become an industry standard for agentic commerce. The system starts by capturing customer instructions, such as maximum budget and specific preferences, through an API before a transaction is finalized.

The customer then authenticates their identity, generating a unique Intent ID and a secure payment credential rather than exposing their actual credit card number. This ensures that the purchase remains secure and strictly aligned with what the user originally authorized the AI agent to do.
