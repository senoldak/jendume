ElevenLabs, known for its advancements in voice technology, has unveiled its new application, ElevenAI, designed to transform how businesses manage meetings and follow-up processes. This platform consolidates essential tasks such as note-taking, voice dictation, and the use of AI agents to automate workflows into a single interface. By doing so, the company aims to help teams capture information discussed during meetings, access this data whenever needed, and accelerate administrative tasks that follow a meeting.

Currently in the testing phase, ElevenAI is capable of recording conversations in real-time and converting them into structured notes. The application prioritizes transparency by informing meeting participants that a recording is taking place. Users can interact with the system after the meeting to ask questions about specific topics discussed, allowing them to retrieve important details quickly. This feature is particularly beneficial for teams with heavy meeting schedules, helping them prevent information loss.

One of the most notable aspects of the platform is its integration with AI agents. Operating through ElevenAgent, the system can analyze meeting notes to perform follow-up tasks, such as drafting emails automatically. Furthermore, the application features advanced voice dictation that supports over 90 languages, enabling users to convert their speech into editable text across various applications.

ElevenLabs is positioning this new tool as a platform tailored for corporate teams rather than just an individual productivity app. The company's core strategy is to enable organizations to manage meeting notes, recordings, and follow-up processes through a single solution, rather than paying for multiple software licenses for different functions. Currently in preview mode and accepting early access applications, ElevenAI offers a new option for companies looking to digitize their corporate workflows.
