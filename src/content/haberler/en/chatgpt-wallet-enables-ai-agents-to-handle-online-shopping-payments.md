OpenAI has launched ChatGPT Wallet, a feature enabling AI agents to make payments on behalf of users. This system replaces the previously scrapped Instant Checkout project, which lacked sufficient flexibility. Currently available only in the United States for ChatGPT Pro subscribers, the feature allows users to authorize their AI agents to handle purchasing tasks.

The system operates with a straightforward process. Users add their card details once. During shopping, the AI searches the web for products, and after the user makes a selection, the payment process begins. For security, OpenAI partners with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa to generate payment tokens, ensuring real card details are never shared with merchants. Every transaction requires user approval via biometric checks like Face ID or fingerprint scanning.

This development marks a critical step in the evolution of AI agents from information providers to active assistants capable of completing tasks. Since many websites and payment systems currently block AI agents as bots, OpenAI has released a Commerce Partner Playbook. This guide assists merchants in adjusting their firewalls and data flows to ensure compatibility with agent-driven purchases.

OpenAI is competing in a crowded space, with rivals like Anthropic, Meta, xAI, Google, and Perplexity already offering similar payment and shopping tools. OpenAI’s ultimate goal is to eventually allow users to fully delegate purchasing tasks to their agents within predefined spending limits and product categories.
