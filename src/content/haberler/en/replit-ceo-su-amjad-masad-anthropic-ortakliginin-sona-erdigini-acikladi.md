Replit CEO and co-founder Amjad Masad announced during an industry event that the AI coding platform is no longer working with Anthropic. Masad stated that the decision was driven by various factors, including cost, partnership dynamics, and performance metrics. Arguing that alternative models now perform better, Masad noted that the decision was made because they currently represent a better partner for the platform.

Replit operates a cloud-based software development platform allowing users to build and deploy applications directly within their browsers. Under its previous partnership, Replit utilized Anthropic's Claude models to run its platform, an arrangement widely credited with helping the company significantly grow its revenue. However, insider reports contesting Masad's claims emerged, with sources close to the matter suggesting the partnership remained in place.

Evidence on Replit's official channels, including blog posts and social media updates from recent weeks, indicated that the company was still utilizing Anthropic products such as Claude Fable 5 and Opus models in production environments. Despite the conflicting reports regarding the exact status of the partnership, Masad shared broader insights into the company's financial growth during his stage appearance.

Masad noted that enterprise contracts now account for approximately 15% of the company's revenue, up from roughly 2% in the previous year, while B2B streams represent more than 50% of total revenue. Touching upon safety measures, Masad emphasized that autonomous agents require proper environments equipped with orchestration, error recovery, and robust security frameworks to operate safely.
