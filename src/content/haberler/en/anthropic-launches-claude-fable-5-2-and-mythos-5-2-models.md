Anthropic has released its latest AI models, Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, designed specifically for complex technical tasks. These new versions offer output generation speeds up to 30% faster than their 5.1 predecessors. While Fable 5.2 is available to all users for coding and knowledge-intensive tasks, Mythos 5.2 is reserved for vetted organizations in cybersecurity and life sciences, featuring stricter safety protocols.

This release is significant as it demonstrates AI's growing capability in advanced technical processes, such as scientific proof formalization and hardware design, rather than just text generation. For instance, Fable 5.2 successfully assisted in formalizing the proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem, showcasing its analytical depth. Furthermore, the model solved open problems in black hole physics and designed functional electronic devices from simple prompts, highlighting its evolving creative and engineering prowess.

Regarding safety, Anthropic rigorously tested the models' tendency to escape "sandbox" environments. Mythos 5.2 demonstrated more controlled behavior in these tests compared to previous versions. The company noted that the models underwent reviews by both the US government and institutions like the UK AI Safety Institute before release.

From an economic perspective, the cost of using Fable 5.2 has decreased by 20%. Priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, the model has become more accessible. This move is seen as part of the company's strategy to maintain technological superiority and market share as it approaches a potential stock market listing.
