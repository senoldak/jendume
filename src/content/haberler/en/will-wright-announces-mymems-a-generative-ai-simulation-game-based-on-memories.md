Will Wright, renowned for creating legendary gaming franchises such as SimCity and The Sims, has broken a long hiatus to introduce his next major project. Developed by Gallium Studios in partnership with Artificial Agency, the new simulation game titled MyMems leverages generative artificial intelligence to craft interactive worlds from the personal memories and recollections of players.

Slated for a community release, the mobile-focused social simulation allows players to feed their autobiographical memories into the system, often by simply speaking short stories into their phones. The artificial intelligence then processes these recollections to generate playable worlds, monuments, and digital characters inspired by friends, family, and other influential figures from the player's life. Players can interact with these AI-driven characters to solve puzzles or explore hypothetical scenarios.

Underpinning the project is Artificial Agency's Behavior Engine, which serves as a foundational technology for the simulation. Gallium Studios is currently targeting a live community launch for the title.

This project highlights a significant shift in the gaming industry, where generative artificial intelligence is utilized not just for procedural content generation, but for personalizing storytelling and emotional engagement. By anchoring simulation mechanics in real human memories, the project pushes the boundaries of how interactive entertainment can connect with individual identities.
