OpenAI announced that an internal artificial intelligence model has produced a claimed refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), a conjecture widely believed to be true in computer science. The company stated that the model found the disproof and verified it using Lean, a machine-verified proof language. The findings have been published on GitHub for independent academic review, sparking intense discussion among theoretical computer scientists.

The development has prompted a group of leading researchers from prominent universities to organize an open and independent examination of the claim. While the researchers emphasized that their involvement is not an endorsement, they secured an agreement from OpenAI to release all raw data and fund conferences for the research community. This collaboration highlights the growing need for rigorous, transparent verification of AI-driven scientific discoveries.

In addition to computer science, OpenAI reported breakthroughs in mathematics and physics. The models allegedly proved the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension and produced a rigorous proof regarding quantum field theories in four dimensions. These achievements touch upon complex mathematical problems and Millennium Prize challenges, though the physics group chose to write up their own material independently.

OpenAI stated it does not seek academic credit for these discoveries, aiming instead to recognize the researchers who analyze and explain the results. The company has also provided financial support to the Simons Foundation for workshops focusing on AI-produced results. As artificial intelligence begins to tackle deep theoretical questions, the scientific community is actively debating how credit, recognition, and validation should be managed in the new era of machine-assisted discovery.
