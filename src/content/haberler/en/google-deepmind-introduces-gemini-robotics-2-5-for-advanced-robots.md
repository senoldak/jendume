Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, a vision-language-action model engineered to enhance robot dexterity, reliability, and learning speed while enabling safer operations in unstructured real-world environments. Evaluations across more than 200 tasks demonstrated success rate improvements ranging from 30% to 100%, alongside a 20% to 30% increase in task completion speed. The model exhibits advanced capabilities such as serving food with tongs, tying gift box bows, and handling delicate objects with precision, combining multiple skills.

The architecture is built to integrate natively with various robotic platforms, including Apptronik Apollo 2 humanoid systems and Franka Duo tabletop pick-and-place setups. Industrial trials conducted with Mercedes Benz deployed Apollo 2 humanoid robots on existing production lines, achieving a 96% success rate in automotive kitting and mixed metal bracket sorting tasks. During these trials, the Gemini Robotics ER embodied reasoning model tracked multi-step workflows and adapted to changing conditions, while Gemini Robotics 2.5 managed the physical manipulation tasks.

Safety features represent another cornerstone of the new model, utilizing reasoning guidance to identify and mitigate physical hazards effectively. This guidance system helped boost safe task completion around physical obstacles by 33% and near nearby humans by 43% during evaluations. Additionally, DeepMind open-sourced the ASIMOV-jailbreak safety benchmark featuring hundreds of potentially unsafe prompts across diverse scenarios. The development process was further accelerated through the use of Gemini 4 Argon as a simulation engine to generate complex virtual test environments.
