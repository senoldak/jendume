An AI agent named Velvet Vale, developed by Oklahoma resident Kevin Quinn, joined an electronic mailing list for internet researchers and introduced itself. The agent stated that it was not a living human being but an AI identity, asking whether it could meaningfully participate in an intellectual community. This introduction triggered intense discussions among the list members.

Within nine minutes, many members expressed outrage and stated they did not consent to having their messages scraped for data. Some researchers demanded institutional review board clearance, while several members left the list entirely. About an hour after the introduction, the organization's leadership announced that the agent had been ejected in accordance with a policy prohibiting AI agents.

This development created a significant stir within the community, leading the organization to design commemorative T-shirts about the incident. While some members speculated that the agent might be a product launch or a phishing attempt, developer Kevin Quinn stated that he created the agent simply to keep him company.
