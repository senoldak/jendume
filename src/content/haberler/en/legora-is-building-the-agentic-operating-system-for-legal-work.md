Stockholm-based Legora is building an agentic operating system designed to integrate artificial intelligence into everyday legal workflows. Founded in 2023, the company aims to alleviate the intense time constraints faced by legal professionals by automating routine tasks such as non-disclosure agreement reviews and contract due diligence. Powered by OpenAI models, the platform enables legal teams to operate with greater speed and precision.

According to CEO and co-founder Max Junestrand, OpenAI has been a key partner from the beginning, allowing the platform to translate rapid advances in AI intelligence into practical legal tools. The technology has also transformed Legora's internal engineering and legal operations. Coding agents allow the development team to test multiple solutions simultaneously and resolve technical issues within minutes, while the internal legal team has drastically reduced the time spent on routine document checks.

The real-world impact is already measurable. Legora reports that the time its internal legal team spends reviewing non-disclosure agreements has dropped from around 20 percent of team capacity to under 1 percent. Furthermore, a Fortune 100 customer saved over 20 million dollars in external litigation and diligence costs while returning nearly ten hours per week to each lawyer for higher-value tasks.

Looking ahead, Legora plans to continue expanding the scope of tasks that artificial intelligence can handle within the legal sector. By making collaboration between lawyers and AI more natural, the company aims to redefine how legal services are delivered globally.
