Chinese artificial intelligence developer MiniMax Group Inc. has released M3.1 Flash, a faster and more affordable update to its M3 large language model. The new release aims to reduce the cost of running AI agents, which often require numerous model calls to complete a single task, while delivering inference that is nearly three times as fast.

According to the company, agentic tasks cost 70 percent less to run compared to the previous model, with output at peak reaching over 200 tokens per second. M3.1 Flash features 428 billion parameters and accepts text, image, and video inputs with a context window of up to 1 million tokens. Developers can choose from five effort levels for reasoning on each request.

In testing, the model scored 61.7 on OSWorld V2 and 76.2 on BankerToolBench. It also achieved an 82 percent score on CyberGym for security-related tasks such as code auditing and penetration testing. The model is priced at a flat 15 cents per million input tokens and 60 cents per million output tokens, competing directly in the market.

M3.1 Flash is now available through the MiniMax API, the M Plan subscription, and the MiniMax Code desktop app. The company also confirmed that an anonymous model previously known as Space Bunny was an early build of this release, processing vast amounts of tokens during its testing phase.
