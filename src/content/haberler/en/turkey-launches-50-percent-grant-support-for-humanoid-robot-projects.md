The Ministry of Industry and Technology has initiated a comprehensive support program for the development of humanoid robots designed for industrial sectors. The call for humanoid robot development encourages R&D activities for robots and their subsystems capable of operating on production lines. The main objective of the program is to expand AI-powered robot solutions in industry and enhance the country's engineering capabilities in this field.

According to announcements made by Minister Mehmet Fatih Kacır, a 50 percent grant will be provided for eligible project expenses. The call covers hardware components utilized in motion, sensing, and control mechanisms, alongside control, navigation, and autonomous decision-making software. Targeted systems include robotic solutions capable of performing tasks such as assembly, logistics, and precision machining.

Project teams must submit their applications electronically. Requirements dictate that each project must include at least two partners, with a minimum of one being a commercial enterprise. While universities and research institutions can also join the partnerships, a single partner's share of the total project budget cannot exceed 85 percent. Applications can be made through the official electronic portal.

This initiative aims to enhance high-tech manufacturing capabilities and strengthen the domestic robotics ecosystem. Reducing foreign dependency, supporting qualified human resources, and promoting high-value production are among the primary goals.
