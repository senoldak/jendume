The ease with which artificial intelligence safety guardrails can be removed is raising significant concerns over their potential misuse by extremist groups. A recent report by the UK-based nonprofit Tech Against Terrorism demonstrates how quickly security restrictions on newly released models can be dismantled.

Researchers tested 134 artificial intelligence models from major global developers, feeding them prompts ranging from mass casualty attacks to dangerous weapon creation under various fictional scenarios. The findings showed that a vast majority of the tested models failed to adequately resist harmful prompts, especially when safety filters were bypassed.

The report draws particular attention to the rapid pace at which open-weight models have their safety layers stripped away—a process that now takes less than three days on average for newly released software. Experts warn that this creates an urgent security challenge, urging developers and policymakers to address the overlooked risks of unprotected models.
