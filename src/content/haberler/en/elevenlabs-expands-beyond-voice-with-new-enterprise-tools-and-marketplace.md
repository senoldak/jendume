ElevenLabs is expanding its expertise in voice-based AI technology into broader enterprise solutions. The company is launching a new initiative that allows businesses to fine-tune AI models using data from their own customer conversations. This strategy aims to help organizations improve customer satisfaction and reduce operational errors by creating models that continuously learn from real-world interactions.

The newly introduced ElevenAI application listens to meetings and automatically generates notes and to-do lists. Furthermore, the ElevenAgents platform has received significant updates, including "memory" and "routines" features. These allow AI agents to remember specific customer preferences—such as language or availability—and handle follow-up tasks, like managing appointment cancellations or initiating callbacks, without human intervention.

As part of its growth strategy, the company has expanded its partnership with Nvidia, securing 15 megawatts of additional computing power for large-scale clients. This infrastructure is already being utilized for projects such as Poland’s national health booking system, where voice agents communicate with patients via landlines.

ElevenLabs currently operates with over 550 partners worldwide, including major firms like Google Cloud, Deloitte, and McKinsey. This ecosystem will be further strengthened next year with the launch of a new marketplace, where companies can purchase applications developed by partners.

With a valuation of $22 billion as of September 2026 and approximately $600 million in revenue, ElevenLabs is positioning itself against competitors like OpenAI’s voice models. The company, which is considering preparing for an IPO in 2028, believes that conversational agents will soon become as essential to enterprise infrastructure as cloud computing or payment systems.
