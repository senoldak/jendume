The capability gap in cybersecurity between open-weight and closed artificial intelligence models has been highlighted in a recent data insight from Epoch AI. The findings show that open-weight models currently trail the closed-weight frontier by approximately six to seven months in cyber performance.

Z.ai’s GLM-5.3 emerged as the highest-scoring open-weight model on the Cyber ECI index, achieving a score of 160. This performance places it above OpenAI’s GPT-5.4 released in March, though it still falls behind newer closed models such as Claude Mythos Preview and GPT-5.5. Meanwhile, the closed-weight frontier continues to be led by Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5 and OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra, both scoring 171. These scores demonstrate that proprietary systems maintain a distinct technological lead in cyber benchmarks.

The updated index expanded its scope to include 76 models and 35 cybersecurity benchmarks, drawing data from institutions such as the UK AI Security Institute, CAISI, and other public sources. A total of 324 model and benchmark results were examined in the latest update, with GLM-5.3's score derived from three CAISI benchmark results and Opus 5.5's score based on four benchmarks reported by its developers.

Analysts note that tracking this timeline helps industry stakeholders understand how quickly open models are closing the gap with proprietary systems while navigating security and safety challenges. The research also highlights that index scores can shift following methodology refits and new model evaluations across the global artificial intelligence ecosystem.
