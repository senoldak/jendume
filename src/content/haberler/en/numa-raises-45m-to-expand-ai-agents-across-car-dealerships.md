San Francisco-based startup Numa announced it has raised a $45 million Series C funding round led by Blue Cloud Ventures. The round saw participation from Threshold Ventures, Costanoa, and Google's Gradient Ventures. This latest influx brings the company's total funding to $96 million, following a $32 million Series B round in 2024.

Numa develops artificial intelligence software designed to handle dealership phone calls, text messages, and web chats, book service appointments, and route callers to appropriate staff. According to the company, more than 1,500 dealerships across the US and Canada currently deploy the system. The platform claims to have processed over 1 billion conversations and booked more than 4 million service appointments to date.

This year, Numa expanded its product capabilities into sales operations. A newly introduced AI agent tracks vehicle inventory down to trim levels and mileage, allowing it to recommend alternative options if a requested vehicle is sold out. Additionally, Numa acquired SocketTime in August, a firm specializing in scheduling and repair tools for service shops. Founder and CEO Tasso Roumeliotis stated that the new capital will support engineering hires, noting that the company tripled its annual recurring revenue this year.

Major automakers are increasingly approving Numa's integration into their retail networks. General Motors approved Numa as a vendor for its network, with over 240 GM stores actively using the platform. Numa reports that customer response times at these locations dropped from 23 hours to 24 minutes. FordDirect and Stellantis have also authorized Numa for their dealer networks, opening access to thousands of additional store locations.
