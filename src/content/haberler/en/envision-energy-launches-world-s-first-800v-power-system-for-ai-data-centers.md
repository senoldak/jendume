Chinese energy company Envision Energy has deployed the world's first commercial integrated 800-volt direct current power system tailored for artificial intelligence data centers. Announced on November 2, the system is actively operating at the company's Galaxy AI data center campus located in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, China. The new infrastructure aims to reduce energy losses and enable more powerful artificial intelligence servers to operate within the same physical space.

In traditional data centers, electricity goes through multiple conversion stages before reaching the servers. This process requires extra equipment, cabling, and space while increasing energy losses. Envision's newly developed architecture converts the medium-voltage input directly into an 800V DC power system. Consequently, the need for intermediate conversion equipment is eliminated, freeing up additional space for server hardware.

The electricity consumption of artificial intelligence servers can fluctuate rapidly depending on the workloads being processed. Envision's system integrates solid-state transformers with battery energy storage units to manage these fluctuations. The batteries can respond to sudden power demands within seconds, while the Dubhe Controller coordinates the components using an energy foundation model.

Having commenced operations in August 2026, the Galaxy AI data center campus has a planned capacity of three gigawatts. Once it reaches full capacity, the facility is expected to support one million artificial intelligence accelerators simultaneously. The company also aims to source a large portion of the campus's electricity directly from renewable energy connections.
