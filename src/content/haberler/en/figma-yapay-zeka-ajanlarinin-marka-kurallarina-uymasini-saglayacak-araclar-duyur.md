Design platform Figma has unveiled new features during its event in India to define how AI agents work on its platform. To address the non-deterministic nature of artificial intelligence and guide it toward more predictable outcomes, the company introduced a Markdown-based editor called Figma Specs.

Through this feature, teams can easily define their design language and guidelines in a simple document. Both internal and third-party AI agents can refer to this centralized system to adhere better to brand rules while creating. Additionally, the company is moving its code layers feature into public beta, allowing users to turn designs into interactive prototypes and ask agents to iterate on code.

Figma points out that while software teams are producing significantly more lines of code, much of it is built to explore and design products. These new features aim to bridge the gap between creative intent and automated execution.
