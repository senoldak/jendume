Cities and local technology partners across the EMEA region are increasingly utilizing advanced accelerated computing to build physical AI agents and digital twins for urban operations. Projects spanning countries such as Belgium, Finland, France, Italy, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom aim to anticipate maintenance needs, guide urban planning, and make public services more responsive to residents' daily needs. These applications help interpret video, identify traffic conditions, and manage energy efficiency across local municipalities.

In France, the Department of Côte-d’Or utilized operations agents across more than 500 public buildings, successfully reducing document search times from weeks to minutes while cutting total operating costs by 10 percent, with a long-term target of 30 percent. Meanwhile, in Italy’s Milan region, locally operated AI factories are being deployed to provide real-time translation, citizen chatbots, and faster access to municipal data, reducing retrieval times from hours to seconds. Additional projects in the United Kingdom, Belgium, and Sweden are exploring mobility, safety, and clean energy applications.

System builders and infrastructure providers are packaging accelerated computing and networking into locally operated AI factories. This allows municipalities to customize and run intelligent applications locally while retaining strict control over their data, models, and operations. Through ongoing pilots and expansions supported by integration partners, these technologies are paving the way for more resilient and energy-efficient urban management across the continent.
