Cognition, the AI-powered software development platform, has entered negotiations for a new funding round that would significantly boost its market valuation. The company is reportedly aiming to raise $2 billion or more at a valuation of $75 billion. While these discussions are ongoing and terms could change, this move marks the company's third major capital raise in 2026.

Cognition specializes in developing autonomous tools capable of managing the entire software development lifecycle. Its flagship product, Devin, can write code, debug, test, and prepare software for deployment with minimal human intervention. This technology aims to drastically increase efficiency in software engineering while intensifying competition in the sector. The company currently competes with major players like Cursor, which was acquired by SpaceX at a $60 billion valuation.

The company's financial growth has been remarkably rapid. Just two months ago, Cognition secured $2 billion in funding at a $48 billion valuation. Having raised over $3 billion throughout 2026, the company announced in September that it expected its annualized revenue to hit $1 billion. This revenue figure represents a doubling of its income compared to just four months prior.

Cognition's client base includes industry giants such as Citigroup and Nvidia. If the current investment talks are successfully concluded, the company will solidify its position as one of the highest-valued startups in the AI ecosystem. However, it remains noted that negotiations are still in progress and the final investment amount and conditions remain subject to change.
