Amazon has officially rebranded its Kindle Unlimited subscription service to Kindle+. The revamped subscription maintains its monthly price of $12 while expanding its audiobook category to include titles narrated by artificial intelligence. The company is offering its Virtual Voice service free of charge to authors participating in the Kindle Direct Publishing program.

This upgrade aims to enhance the reading experience by allowing users to seamlessly transition from reading their ebooks to listening via virtual narration during their daily routines. Authors can now bypass human narrators by utilizing the free AI narration tool, potentially reaching wider audiences without incurring high production costs. Kindle+ subscribers retain the ability to borrow up to twenty titles at a time, and the service remains separate from a standard Prime membership.

The Kindle Direct Publishing program allows authors to self-publish their ebooks and enroll in Kindle+ to reach more readers. Authors enrolled in the program earn money for pages read rather than per download, with earnings depending on the monthly payout rate and total pages read.

At the same time, the shift toward AI-narrated books raises questions regarding the human element in storytelling and potential job displacement for professional voice actors who have built dedicated followings. Amazon notes that readers seeking human-narrated and dramatized audiobooks can still access them through alternative platforms such as Audible or local public libraries.

Ultimately, Kindle+ represents a rebrand rather than an entirely new subscription model. While readers gain expanded listening options, the core reading experience remains largely unchanged, with authors gaining a new tool for self-publishing and audio distribution.
