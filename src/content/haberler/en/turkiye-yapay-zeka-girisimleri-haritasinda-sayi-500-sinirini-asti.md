The artificial intelligence ecosystem in Turkey has reached a significant milestone as the number of active startups surpassed 500 for the first time. According to the updated ecosystem map released in October 2026, the total count rose from 482 to 504.

During the six-month period, 43 new startups joined the directory while 21 companies that ceased operations were removed, resulting in a net increase of 22 enterprises. Demonstrating the youth of the ecosystem, 25 out of 41 newly added companies with known founding dates were established in 2025 or 2026. While Istanbul remains the primary hub, cities such as Ankara, Izmir, Bursa, Antalya, Samsun, Bolu, and Gebze also contributed new entries.

The update was marked by a surge in agentic AI initiatives, which gained 10 new entrants. This category was followed by conversational AI and legal tech with five new startups each, alongside HR tech and analytics with four additions each. Furthermore, infrastructure tools designed to monitor corporate GPU usage and deployment costs were integrated into the map.

Industry leaders noted that crossing the 500 threshold reflects a maturing market where companies are moving beyond general-purpose tools to offer specialized, sector-specific solutions. This diversification strengthens the overall capacity of the local tech landscape to address complex operational needs.
