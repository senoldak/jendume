Liquid AI has introduced LFM2.5-24B-A2B, an open-weight reasoning model optimized for personal computers and laptops. Featuring a total of 24 billion parameters with approximately 2 billion active parameters per token, it employs a Mixture-of-Experts architecture tailored for multi-step agent tasks.

The model offers an expanded 131,000-token context window and supports 16 languages, including English, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic. It is designed to handle complex instruction-following and tool use rather than just open-ended chat.

On the hardware front, it achieves notable speeds on consumer chips such as Apple M5 Max and AMD Ryzen AI processors. The base and post-trained weights are available on Hugging Face, providing developers with powerful local tools for on-device applications.
