Tencent has officially released its advanced language model, Hy4, marking a significant leap in its AI capabilities. Trained on substantially more data than its predecessors, Hy4 is the company's first model capable of understanding images and directly operating computer software. While currently available through Tencent Cloud and the Yuanbao chatbot, the company intends to release the model weights as an open-source contribution at an unspecified future date.

The most prominent feature of Hy4 is its ability to recognize fine details in complex diagrams and operate professional tools autonomously. Tencent specifically trained the model on engineering software like Blender and CAD, as well as standard office applications. In demonstrations, the model successfully identified small components within massive aerial images and collaborated with humans in real-time on Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files. Furthermore, the hallucination rate during web research tasks dropped from nearly 16% to approximately 5% due to a revamped search integration.

In evaluations conducted with over 200 finance professionals, Hy4 outperformed local rivals such as Deepseek, Kimi, and GLM on real-world tasks. Independent assessments by Artificial Analysis placed Hy4 in second place among Chinese lab models with a score of 35 on their Intelligence Index. However, the testing also revealed that without web search, the model correctly answered only about half of factual questions and tended to fabricate answers 89% of the time when faced with knowledge gaps.

Tencent emphasized safety during the development process, stating that hacking-related materials and biological hazard risks were strictly filtered during training. Despite the performance improvements, the company is maintaining its preview pricing, charging approximately $0.83 per million input tokens. Looking ahead, Tencent plans to release a faster and more cost-effective variant named Hy4 Flash to support broader integration across various industries.
