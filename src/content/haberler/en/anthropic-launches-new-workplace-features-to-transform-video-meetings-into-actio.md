Anthropic has introduced a suite of new workplace features designed to enhance productivity by integrating its assistant directly into professional workflows. These tools aim to turn passive discussions into actionable plans in real time. The core addition, Claude Meetings, allows the assistant to join video calls as a visible participant. While team members discuss projects, the assistant tracks the conversation, drafts plans, maps out timelines on a whiteboard, and answers questions within the meeting chat. Once the call concludes, all meeting notes and action items are immediately available.

Alongside meetings, the company launched Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets. Whiteboards provide a collaborative space for teams to brainstorm and visualize plans, while Sheets enables the creation and collaborative editing of spreadsheets, such as budget trackers or project schedules, directly within the interface. These tools are designed to keep workflows seamless by eliminating the need for users to switch between different applications while working with data.

This launch marks a significant step in the ongoing competition to dominate the office software landscape. As major tech giants continue to integrate advanced assistants into their suites, companies are positioning their models as essential tools for enterprise efficiency. Anthropic is aiming to streamline these workflows by bringing existing tools into a single workspace.

These features are currently available in beta for Team and Enterprise plans. While Claude Meetings initially supports Google Meet, further integrations are expected. Additionally, the company has made Claude Data, which connects the assistant to internal systems like CRMs and data warehouses, generally available to users.
