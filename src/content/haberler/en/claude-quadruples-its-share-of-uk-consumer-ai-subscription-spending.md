A comprehensive economic study in the United Kingdom has revealed a rapid shift in the landscape of AI subscription spending. The research, titled "Who Pays for AI?", highlights that the share of consumer spending captured by Anthropic’s Claude model has quadrupled in just seven months. While the model held a 7.4% share of AI subscription spending in December 2025, this figure climbed to 29.4% by July 2026.

During the same period, the market leader, ChatGPT, experienced a decline in its share of consumer spending, dropping from 73.5% to 52.5%. The study, which analyzed anonymized records from 14.2 million UK accounts, demonstrates that AI tools are becoming an integral part of consumer spending habits rather than just a niche interest.

The overall market is expanding at a significant pace. As of June 2026, approximately 5.1% of UK adults—roughly 2.8 million people—held an active paid AI subscription, up from 2.3% the previous year. Claude’s growth is partly attributed to a segment of users opting for higher-priced plans. Among Claude subscribers, 6.2% pay for plans costing £80 or more per month, compared to just 0.3% of ChatGPT subscribers.

Demographic analysis shows that paid subscriptions are most prevalent among young, male, and urban populations. By occupation, IT professionals lead the adoption rate at 20.3%. These findings suggest that AI tools are cementing their role in both professional and daily life, with users increasingly diversifying their choices between competing models.
