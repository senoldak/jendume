The technology and storage company has published its Tokenomics 2030 projection, examining the economic framework shaped by the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence inference. According to the report, global artificial intelligence systems currently generate approximately 5 billion fundamental data units every second. The volume of these units processed by models is projected to surge to 317 billion per second by 2030, reaching roughly 10 quintillion units globally on an annual basis.

As artificial intelligence applications evolve toward more complex workflows and extended context management, the ability to securely store and rapidly access data becomes essential. Enterprise-grade solid-state drives capable of offering higher capacity tiers for specific cached data help eliminate the need to repeatedly regenerate previously computed information. Functioning similarly to energy storage units, these hardware components help maximize the value of fixed infrastructure investments.

Achieving useful output requires close collaboration across all infrastructure layers, including storage systems. The company aims to address these infrastructure demands through NAND flash technologies and enterprise storage solutions as part of the ecosystem transforming data into intelligence.

As the strategic position of the hardware layer continues to grow in the future digital economy, surging data demand accelerates the advancement of next-generation storage technologies. This transformation establishes a fundamental foundation for AI systems to operate sustainably and with high performance.
