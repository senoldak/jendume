As the race for deeply personalized artificial intelligence intensifies, startup Ambi has secured $26 million across multiple funding rounds led by Luminous Ventures and Huaqin. The company is releasing dedicated applications for iPhones, Macs, and Apple Watch designed to capture context throughout the day rather than just summarizing finished meetings.

Co-founder and CEO Peter Mo noted that traditional AI interactions are often limited by screens or processing delays, driving the need for an AI companion that operates anytime and anywhere. The platform's autonomous agent aims to proactively surface relevant knowledge and offer suggestions while users are actively engaged in meetings or daily tasks.

However, continuous ambient listening reignites growing industry concerns regarding user privacy and third-party consent. While some similar tools have faced lawsuits over unconsented recordings, Ambi states that ambient capture is turned off by default, fully encrypted, and never stores raw audio recordings.

Looking ahead, Ambi plans to expand beyond software by launching custom hardware featuring a built-in screen, camera, and speakers. With over 50 staff members across offices in San Francisco, Beijing, Shanghai, and Shenzhen, the startup is actively recruiting AI engineers to advance its speech-recognition models.
