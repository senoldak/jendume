Science Academy member and 2024 Nobel Economics Prize laureate Professor Daron Acemoğlu has warned against leaving the trajectory of artificial intelligence solely to the incentives of major technology companies. In an address at Koç University published by the Science Academy in October 2026, Acemoğlu highlighted two forces reshaping the modern world: the erosion of democratic institutions and the rapid rise of AI.

Drawing on economic history, Acemoğlu noted that technological breakthroughs do not automatically translate into shared prosperity. Past industrial transformations improved living standards for the broader public only when accompanied by strong institutions, public accountability, and technologies designed to complement human skill.

## Technology That Complements People Rather Than Replacing Them

Acemoğlu cautioned that much of current AI development focuses on substituting human labor and centralizing information. Instead, he called for steering research toward human-complementary AI—tools that equip teachers, healthcare workers, technicians, and everyday citizens with better information and decision-making power.

Addressing young scientists and engineers, he emphasized that asking who benefits from a technology—and how it can serve a fairer society—must remain a core responsibility of the scientific community.
