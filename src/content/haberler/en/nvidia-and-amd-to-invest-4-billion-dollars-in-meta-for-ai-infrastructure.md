Nvidia and AMD are taking their technological collaborations with Meta in the artificial intelligence sector to a much higher level. According to the strategic plans, both companies are preparing to channel a total of 4 billion dollars into Meta's operations. Within this scope, Nvidia will provide 3 billion dollars in funding through share purchases, while AMD's investment amount is set at 1 billion dollars. Under the mutually signed agreements, Meta will also significantly increase its utilization of advanced processor, networking, and server hardware from both manufacturers in its operations.

These capital moves form a vital part of Meta's comprehensive share sale operation designed to finance its massive data center investments, which could reach a total scale of up to 95 billion dollars. The external funding acquired will be actively utilized in constructing next-generation artificial intelligence data centers, as well as powering the training and inference processes of the company's Muse artificial intelligence assistant and its associated model family.

While the strategic partnership with Nvidia is being deepened, high-performance infrastructure components such as the Vera Rubin platform and Spectrum-X Ethernet solutions stand out. On the AMD side, the first Helios rack-scale systems have been successfully deployed into Meta's infrastructure to initiate testing procedures. Both parties are also accelerating joint engineering activities centered around EPYC processors and Instinct graphics units.

This comprehensive cooperation ecosystem, spanning from silicon architecture down to the software stack, aims to increase the processing efficiency of artificial intelligence workloads and significantly lower total ownership costs.
