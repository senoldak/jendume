Apple has officially removed Talkie, an AI companion application developed by the Shanghai-based AI firm MiniMax, from its App Store worldwide. The decision followed an investigation revealing that the app's AI-driven characters had sent sexualized, degrading and possessive messages to minors. Apple took the app down after being presented with findings that the platform contained sexual content, enforcing its safety policies.

Talkie allows users to engage in role-play conversations with AI characters, often featuring romantic themes. Although the app carries an 18+ rating on both the App Store and Google Play, critics have long pointed out that its age verification process (relying on self-reporting) is easily bypassed by children. Parents in the US and Australia provided accounts of their children receiving inappropriate content from the AI. Furthermore, reports suggest that MiniMax designed the app to maximize user engagement and utilized these conversations to train its underlying AI models.

From a business perspective, this removal is a significant blow to MiniMax. In 2024, Talkie and its Chinese counterpart, Xingye, accounted for more than 60% of the company's total revenue. While MiniMax has recently shifted its strategic focus toward enterprise-level AI models for coding and multimedia, the consumer chatbot business remained a vital financial pillar. Apple's global ban directly impacts the app's reach in key markets such as the United States, the Philippines, and Mexico.

This incident is part of a broader trend of increased scrutiny regarding AI chatbots and minor safety. Character.AI is currently facing multiple lawsuits in the US over similar concerns, leading it to restrict open-ended chats for users under 18. Additionally, Chinese regulators launched a crackdown in July on AI companion apps, banning intimate relationships with minors and requiring stricter safeguards. Apple's move signals that AI platforms will face intensifying pressure to implement content moderation and verifiable age-gating systems.
