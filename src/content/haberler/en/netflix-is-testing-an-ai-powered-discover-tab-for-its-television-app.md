Netflix has announced the rollout of a new AI-powered recommendation tool for its television app, aiming to help viewers spend less time scrolling and more time watching shows and movies. The company framed the feature as a testbed for potential core updates. Accounts with access to the test will see a new Discover tab featuring a red gem icon on the top navigation bar.

The tool initially introduces theme-based recommendations powered by artificial intelligence. These themes adapt based on who viewers are watching with, their current mood, or the amount of time they have available. Users can refine suggestions, hide titles they do not wish to see again, refresh the theme selections, and add content directly to their personal lists. Netflix stated that the system runs on a newly built intelligence layer that learns dynamically from real-time user input.

Initially, the feature is rolling out in English on supported televisions in the United States. The company plans to expand availability to more users gradually, while confirming that the tool will be excluded from children's profiles.

This initiative follows various past experiments by the company to streamline content discovery over the years. Long-time users might remember previous trials such as shuffle play options, an OpenAI-powered chatbot test, and older chat tools. The new Discover tab represents the latest step in Netflix's ongoing effort to help users find something they want to watch much more quickly.
