LangChain Inc., a startup providing open-source tools for building artificial intelligence agents, has successfully raised $300 million in funding. Following this latest round, the San Francisco-based company has reached a valuation of $3 billion. The funding round was led by Alphabet Inc.’s CapitalG fund, with participation from IVP, Sapphire Ventures, GIC, and DFJ Growth.

Founded in twenty twenty-two, LangChain offers an open-source framework that simplifies the process of connecting AI agents to large language models and integrating them with various tools. Beyond its core framework, the company has developed LangGraph for linking multiple agents into automation workflows and Deep Agents to assist with complex tasks. These components include a virtual file system for data storage, a work planning tool, and a subagent generation feature to help agents handle demanding workloads.

LangChain also generates revenue through LangSmith, a paid product suite that helps development teams maintain, debug, restore earlier versions, and monitor agents in production environments. LangSmith generates traces and logs that describe every step of the workflow, analyzing them to find technical issues and prioritize code fixes based on severity.

Prior to this round, LangChain's open-source tools were downloaded approximately 90 million times per month, with major technology firms such as Autodesk and Cisco Systems utilizing its suite. The startup plans to expand its team significantly, aiming to nearly double its headcount by hiring infrastructure experts, AI engineers, salespeople, and marketers as it continues to grow its engineering capabilities.
