Anthropic has released updated versions of its most powerful artificial intelligence models, Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, as the company prepares for its highly anticipated initial public offering. According to the company, these new models perform up to 30% faster on complex tasks such as coding while reducing operational costs by 20%. This update is designed to bolster Anthropic's technological capabilities and competitive standing in the market.

The models are differentiated by their safety protocols and intended user base. Fable 5.2 includes safeguards that block most cybersecurity and biology-related tasks, making it suitable for general users. Conversely, Mythos 5.2 features fewer restrictions and is exclusively available to vetted organizations engaged in cybersecurity or biomedical research.

This launch serves as a strategic move ahead of the company's IPO, scheduled for the week of November 16. Anthropic aims to raise up to $100 billion through this offering. Market estimates suggest a valuation exceeding $1 trillion, although this figure has adjusted downward from earlier projections of $2 trillion.

Financial performance highlights the company's rapid growth. Revenue reached $17 billion in the third quarter, a significant increase from $1.4 billion during the same period the previous year. Despite this growth, the company reported an operating loss of $567 million in the third quarter, largely driven by increased spending on short-term computing capacity. Anthropic faces intense competition from rivals like OpenAI and various low-cost models originating from China. Furthermore, the company navigates a complex regulatory environment, with potential legislative crackdowns on data center construction and broader tech industry scrutiny following recent U.S. political shifts, which remain key concerns for investors assessing the company's long-term growth trajectory.
