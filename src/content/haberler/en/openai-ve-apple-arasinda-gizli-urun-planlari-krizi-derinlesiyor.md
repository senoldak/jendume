The ongoing trade secret dispute between OpenAI and Apple has escalated with new allegations. According to court documents filed by OpenAI, a former employee who left for Apple allegedly transferred more than 100 files concerning an unreleased product to a personal account.

The documents reportedly include sensitive reports on hardware components, engineering data, and product design standards. OpenAI claims this information was presented to high-ranking Apple executives and links the presentation to the company's recent hardware announcements.

Apple has strongly denied the accusations, stating that internal reviews found no evidence of OpenAI files and emphasizing adherence to strict policies preventing the misuse of former employers' confidential information.

With the trial scheduled for October 2027, the mutual accusations highlight the direct and aggressive competition unfolding between the two companies in the field of AI-powered consumer hardware.
