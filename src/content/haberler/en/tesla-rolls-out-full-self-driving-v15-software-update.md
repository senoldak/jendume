Tesla has begun deploying the long-awaited V15 update for its Full Self-Driving (FSD) driver assistance system to vehicles. The new release overhauls all components of the system's end-to-end driving network, offering drivers technical and visual improvements. As part of this update, a newly introduced visual encoder trained on millions of internet videos aims to enhance generalization capabilities across different driving conditions.

One of the additions is the Tesla Reality View feature, which transforms the driving visualization. This feature renders the vehicle's surroundings in real-time from the perspective of a virtual camera, showing drivers how the system perceives its environment. Additionally, detection for traffic signs, small animals, and road obstacles has been improved, alongside smart features such as pothole avoidance and automatic slowing down when approaching speed cameras.

Tesla states that the need for driver intervention in safety-critical situations has been improved in the new version. The updated camera-based attention tracking now operates under a wider range of conditions, even when wearing hats or sunglasses. Furthermore, new learning models that adapt to drivers' past habits regarding route preferences and parking choices have been integrated into the system.

While FSD V15 has already been in use in the company's robotaxis, a phased rollout is currently underway for broader consumer vehicles. Company officials anticipate that a wider release will take place with the holiday update in December. These developments make autonomous driving technologies more intuitive and secure for everyday use.
