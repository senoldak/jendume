Google is introducing new updates to its AI-powered search features to ensure that publishers and content creators maintain visibility as search habits evolve. While AI-generated summaries provide quick answers by aggregating data from various sources, they often reduce the web traffic directed to the original websites. To address this gap, Google is enhancing the prominence of source links within its AI responses. Under the new arrangements, source links will appear more frequently and closer to the top of the AI-generated summaries, allowing users to identify original sources without scrolling through lengthy text. Furthermore, links at the bottom of responses are now paired with small visuals to draw more attention. For video content, creator profiles within AI modes have been made clickable, enabling users to explore the broader body of work produced by the same creator. Google is also introducing dedicated spaces at the very top of AI responses for official websites and expanding news carousels featuring images to more languages and regions. These adjustments aim to balance the convenience of AI search with the necessary support for digital publishers and independent creators whose work feeds these systems. By connecting users directly to official domains, creators, and visual news carousels, the platform is reinforcing the connection between automated answers and the fundamental web ecosystem that sustains them.
