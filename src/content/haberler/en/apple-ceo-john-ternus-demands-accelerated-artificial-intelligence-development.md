Apple CEO John Ternus is pushing for significant changes in the company's software development processes, which have contributed to the firm falling behind competitors in the artificial intelligence sector. Ternus made it clear during a private meeting with senior vice president of software Craig Federighi that the company's software roadmap needs to be significantly accelerated. Finding it insufficient to limit Apple's AI efforts merely to improvements in Siri, Ternus is demanding more comprehensive projects to be implemented in a shorter timeframe. This new strategic approach could bring a complete overhaul of decision-making processes and long-standing management habits within Apple's software teams.

It is noted that Federighi's direct control over software development has been one of the factors making it difficult for the company to move faster. Regarding internal dynamics, there are no signs that the experienced executive will leave Apple. In fact, it is reported that Federighi does not prioritize grooming a successor for his role. Despite this, Ternus clearly wants noticeable changes in Federighi's approach to software development and artificial intelligence.

Apple has historically taken a more cautious path in its AI strategies. However, rapid changes in the industry are forcing the company to restructure its efforts in this field. After the dismissal of Apple's former AI executive, Federighi took over the responsibility of these operations, and his upcoming steps are being closely watched. Ternus's intervention reflects a desire to shift toward a more agile management style to maintain Apple's position in the tech world. This process indicates that not only the software but also the company's corporate culture will undergo an AI-focused transformation, which is critical for the company's future competitive power.
