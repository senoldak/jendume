Czech startup Kardi Ai has successfully closed a Series A funding round led by health-technology venture fund Callisto Health. The fresh capital is earmarked for expanding operations across Europe and Asia while developing models capable of estimating cardiac arrhythmia risks. The system consists of an ECG-capable fitness chest strap, a mobile application, and an AI engine designed to process recordings.

Traditional short-term Holter monitors often fail to capture intermittent arrhythmias that occur sporadically over time. Kardi Ai's platform addresses this by allowing continuous patient monitoring during daily activities, with artificial intelligence analyzing hours of ECG data within minutes. Internal company data indicates that roughly 37 percent of over 6,700 users exhibited arrhythmias, providing clinicians with detailed data to aid in treatment decisions.

Alongside its technological rollout, Kardi Ai has secured the EU MDR Class IIa certification and recently obtained regulatory approvals in India and the UAE. Company leadership notes that these new regions represent a potential market of approximately two billion people. Cardiovascular diseases remain a leading cause of global mortality, and undetected arrhythmias can lead to severe complications such as strokes.

Previously recognized at the Central European Startup Awards and Global Startup Awards, Kardi Ai aims to establish its technology as a global standard for long-term cardiac monitoring. The company plans to scale its solution through hospitals, cardiologists, and corporate prevention programs, with a future focus on predictive modeling to identify cardiovascular risks before major complications arise.
