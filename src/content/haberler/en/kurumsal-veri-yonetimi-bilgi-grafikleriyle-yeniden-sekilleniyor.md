McKinsey & Company has highlighted a new approach that transforms scattered and unstructured corporate data into meaningful insights for artificial intelligence applications. Known as knowledge graphs, this system provides context to AI by mapping relationships between data points, much like social media networks do. While traditional relational databases struggle with ambiguous or complex information, knowledge graphs offer a more intuitive alternative.

Organizations are increasingly combining AI with these graph structures to automate business processes and streamline data analytics. McKinsey utilizes a semantic data layer through its EcliptOS operating system, connecting executive strategy with everyday execution. This enables unstructured data to be turned into deterministic business rules, empowering companies to drive smarter improvements across customer experiences and productivity.
