Google Cloud is launching a new program to develop a specialized workforce in cloud computing and artificial intelligence in Turkey. Funded by a Google Cloud grant, the Turkey Cloud and Artificial Intelligence Competence Academy aims to issue more than one thousand official certificates within the first twelve months.

The academy will operate through various programs tailored to different areas of expertise. In the first phase, financially supported technical training programs will be offered for software development specialists, cloud architects, and systems engineers. In the second phase, senior executives and public sector representatives will focus on artificial intelligence literacy, strategic governance, and AI-driven business models. In the third phase, customized transformation roadmaps will be created to help companies under the TÜBİSAD umbrella modernize their technology infrastructures and increase their software export volumes.

According to information shared at the event, the collaboration between Turkcell and Google Cloud is also progressing. Turkcell announced a seventy-five percent increase in software development speed thanks to Gemini-powered artificial intelligence tools. Meanwhile, the first year has been left behind in the project to establish a local cloud region in Turkey, which is planned as a two billion dollar investment over ten years.

These developments highlight the tangible results of companies in Turkey integrating artificial intelligence and cloud infrastructures into their business processes. Various enterprises are boosting operational efficiency and autonomously resolving customer demands through AI-driven solutions.
