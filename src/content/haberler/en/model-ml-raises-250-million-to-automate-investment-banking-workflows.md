London-based startup Model ML has secured $250 million in a Series B funding round to automate the repetitive tasks that dominate the lives of junior investment bankers and consultants. The funding round, co-led by General Atlantic and existing backer QED Investors, brings the company’s valuation to $1.5 billion. The startup specializes in developing AI agents that integrate directly into software suites used by financial professionals, such as Excel and PowerPoint, to handle complex tasks like building financial models, preparing pitch decks, and drafting due diligence reports.

The company was co-founded by brothers Chaz and Arnie Englander, who previously launched two other ventures, including the peer-to-peer rental marketplace Fat Llama. That company was acquired by Swedish rival Hygglo for $41.5 million in 2022. With their three-year-old startup, the founders are now focused on streamlining high-stakes corporate environments. Model ML’s agents are designed to understand the full context of ongoing deals, enabling them to automatically update financial models and presentations whenever new developments occur, ensuring that data remains accurate and current without constant manual intervention.

A key differentiator for the company is its reliance on open-source models. The founders emphasize that this approach allows them to operate at approximately 2% of the cost of frontier AI models while delivering faster results. This cost-efficiency is a critical factor in scaling their operations and making their services accessible to a broader range of financial institutions.

The market for AI-driven financial automation is heating up, with competitors like Hebbia and Rogo vying for dominance. Rogo, for instance, raised $160 million at a $2 billion valuation, underscoring the intense investor interest in this sector. Following a $75 million Series A round, Model ML is now positioned to accelerate its product development and expand its footprint in the competitive landscape of financial technology.
