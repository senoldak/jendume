Elon Musk's artificial intelligence venture, xAI, is purchasing homes located adjacent to its power plant in Southaven, Mississippi. According to reports based on company letters, homeowners who agree to sell their properties must also drop any ongoing claims in a noise lawsuit against the company. Real estate sources indicate that the target is 418 homes in the Colonial Hills neighborhood, with 18 owners having agreed to sell so far. The subsidiary operating the plant has also acquired five homes in the area since October.

Local residents filed a federal lawsuit in June, arguing that the plant's noise and vibration severely harm their health and property values, seeking to represent over 10,000 people living near the site. The facility operates 69 temporary gas turbines to power data centers in Southaven and Memphis. While xAI denies the allegations and has introduced noise-dampening measures, it has reached an understanding with state authorities to swap out the units for a permanent gas-fired installation.

The local mayor backs the buyout, noting that city sensors have not registered sound levels surpassing the established neighborhood threshold of 70 decibels. Meanwhile, state regulators continue discussions regarding potential fines and stormwater permits. The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence infrastructure highlights the growing friction between tech scaling and local environmental and residential concerns.
