Sierra, an enterprise AI agent company co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, has raised $1.75 billion in a new funding round led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners, with participation from previous backers including Benchmark, Sequoia, GV, and Tiger Global. Enterprise automation represents one of the sectors where artificial intelligence achieves fast deployment. The fresh capital has doubled the company valuation to $30 billion following a prior round at $15.8 billion.

Sierra develops conversational agents designed to answer customer calls and messages for major corporations. Operating under an outcome-based pricing model, the enterprise charges clients for resolved customer issues rather than per seat or per token. A major insurance firm recently deployed the system to handle 1 million dental inquiries annually, while the platform integrated card payment capabilities through Stripe.

Company figures show that annual recurring revenue surpassed $400 million, while nearly half of the Fortune 50 corporations utilize the service. Major financial institutions and enterprises such as BNY, HSBC, Wells Fargo, JPMorganChase, Pearson, Next, and Cox Automotive form part of the client base. Leadership plans to direct the new funds toward scaling infrastructure, layering service agents, and expanding sales operations.

As enterprise automation accelerates, customer experience startups continue securing multi-billion-dollar valuations across the market. While observers monitor potential workforce impacts, company executives emphasize that automated agents primarily lead toward the reallocation of existing contact center personnel toward new tasks rather than complete replacement.
