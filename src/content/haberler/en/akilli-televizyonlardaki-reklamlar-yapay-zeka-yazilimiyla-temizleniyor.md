Smart TV users increasingly frustrated by sponsored advertisements, sluggish interfaces, and intrusive data trackers are finding new ways to clean up their living room displays. In recent weeks, independent developers have begun leveraging artificial intelligence coding capabilities to spin up practical solutions that strip unwanted cruft and telemetry from modern televisions.

Among the emerging projects are tools like dumbtv and cleanyourtv, designed to eliminate built-in advertisements, tracking experiments, and unnecessary applications from Android TV and Google TV platforms without touching the root directory. Furthermore, individual users are turning directly to AI coding assistants like Claude Code to generate custom instructions for debloating their specific TV models and speeding up laggy user interfaces simply through conversational prompts.

The core shift in this trend lies in the speed and accessibility that artificial intelligence provides. Tasks that previously required days or weeks of dedicated programming can now be accomplished in a matter of hours, leading to a proliferation of targeted workarounds designed to improve user experience and privacy.

This movement coincides with widespread consumer weariness over rising streaming costs and increasingly aggressive ad placement by hardware manufacturers. As awareness of big tech tracking grows, AI-driven personalization and automation are offering everyday users a straightforward path to more transparent and controllable entertainment systems.
