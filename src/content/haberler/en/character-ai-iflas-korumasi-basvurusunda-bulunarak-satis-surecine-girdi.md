AI companion platform Character.AI has officially filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy, initiating a court-supervised sales process. Once valued at a billion dollars, the startup faced mounting financial pressures driven by exorbitant compute costs and continuous difficulties achieving operational profitability.

According to company disclosures, maintaining the platform required massive spending on server infrastructure and employee salaries, far outpacing incoming revenues from subscriptions and ads. The intense user engagement meant long, continuous conversations that ultimately generated unsustainable operational bills and millions of dollars in monthly operating losses. Despite implementing in-app advertising and purchases, the company struggled to cover its expenses.

In an effort to control financial bleeding, the platform introduced usage limits and cheaper models, alongside reducing its employee headcount. However, these measures were insufficient against the backdrop of continuous high operational costs and ongoing legal challenges regarding user safety.

As part of the bankruptcy proceedings, Disney has agreed to a twenty million dollar deal for a non-exclusive license to certain intellectual property, acting as the initial bidder. However, this agreement does not guarantee that the application itself will be preserved, leaving the platform's future uncertain if another buyer cannot be found to keep the service running.

While the platform remains operational for now, users have been advised to export their chat data as a precaution against a potential shutdown. The coming weeks will determine whether the service finds a buyer to keep its community alive.
