The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence is driving a shift toward machine-to-machine communication, creating new challenges in managing distributed workloads. Seismora Inc. is building networking technology designed to coordinate AI tasks across various computing environments and providers.

The new control plane enables applications to intelligently decide whether a specific task should run on a local device, at the edge, or in the cloud. This automated routing eliminates the need for developers to hardcode routing logic into every individual application.

Using what the company calls "cognitive routing," the system makes decisions based on capability, cost, latency, and policy constraints. This allows enterprises to seamlessly integrate multiple cloud providers and models while optimizing overall performance and expenses.
