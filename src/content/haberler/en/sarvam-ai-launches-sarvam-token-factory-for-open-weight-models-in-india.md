Sarvam AI has officially launched Sarvam Token Factory, a managed inference platform designed for developers and enterprises, powered by GPU clusters located in India. Operating through a single OpenAI-compatible API, the service hosts both the company's proprietary models and various third-party open-weight models. At launch, the platform supports eight different models, including Sarvam 105B, GLM 5.3, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, providing India-based teams with low-latency token generation.

The core strength of the platform lies in its cache-aware architecture and optimized inference engine. According to company data, the system already handles over 450 billion DeepSeek V4.1 Flash tokens daily, serving a significant portion of prompt tokens directly from the cache. These architectural optimizations help reduce prefill GPU-hours and enhance overall output throughput without compromising accuracy.

Running on NVIDIA GPU clusters, the infrastructure allows models like Sarvam 105B to fit efficiently onto hardware replicas. Sarvam highlights self-reported benchmark figures showing improved time-to-first-token performance from Mumbai compared to other providers, particularly under heavy agent workloads and on models like Kimi K3.1 Pro.

While introductory pricing runs through the end of the year, this India-hosted inference stack represents a major step forward for regional AI deployment. By combining shared caching layers with specialized execution engines, Sarvam Token Factory offers a scalable and cost-effective alternative for running large open-weight models locally.
