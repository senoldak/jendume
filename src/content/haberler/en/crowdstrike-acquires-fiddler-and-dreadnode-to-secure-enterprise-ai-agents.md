CrowdStrike has signed agreements to acquire Fiddler AI, an AI agent observability platform, and Dreadnode, an AI security testing startup. The company plans to integrate both startups into its Falcon platform, with the transactions expected to close in the fourth quarter of CrowdStrike's fiscal year.

The shared goal of both acquisitions is to secure enterprise AI agents—software that executes tasks autonomously on behalf of users. Fiddler provides observability and explainability, showing what agents do and why they do it, with its product to be sold as Falcon Observe. Dreadnode tests AI models before deployment using over 100 known attack strategies, including jailbreak and prompt injection methods.

While CrowdStrike did not officially disclose the financial terms, stating the payments will be predominantly in cash, media reports indicate that Fiddler is valued at approximately 400 million dollars and Dreadnode at approximately 95 million dollars. Dreadnode's team will join the company's Cyber Superintelligence Lab research unit.

Major cybersecurity companies have been actively acquiring AI security startups over the past two years, with Palo Alto Networks, Cisco, SentinelOne, and Check Point making similar moves. CrowdStrike aims to differentiate itself by combining monitoring and automated testing into a single unified platform.

For international markets, including businesses operating under European Union regulations, the ability to document and explain AI agent decisions is becoming increasingly critical due to upcoming regulatory compliance and transparency requirements for automated systems.
