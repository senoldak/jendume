Music streaming platform Deezer is taking steps to help users avoid AI-generated music by introducing a dedicated filter. The company announced it is working on a new toggle designed to remove AI-created tracks, albums, and artists from search results and user feeds.

According to the platform, more than 50 percent of daily uploads averaged around 90,000 tracks by July, consisting of AI-generated music. A previous survey indicated that roughly 45 percent of Deezer customers wanted an option to completely remove AI music from their listening experience.

Other streaming services are also navigating the rise of AI audio differently. While Bandcamp has banned AI tracks entirely, Tidal halted monetization for them, and Spotify focuses on tagging AI artists more prominently.
