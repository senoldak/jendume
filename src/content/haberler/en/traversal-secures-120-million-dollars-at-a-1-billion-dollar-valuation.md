Traversal, a startup developing AI agents for site reliability engineering, has raised 120 million dollars in a Series B funding round. According to the company, the new round brings its total valuation to 1 billion dollars. The funding was co-led by Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint Ventures, and Khosla Ventures, with participation from Sequoia, Battery Ventures, and Amex Ventures.

The company builds AI agents designed to scan logs, metrics, and trace data to find the root causes of software failures in live environments. Founded by researchers from MIT, Berkeley, Columbia, and Cornell, Traversal introduced its Production World Model and Causal Search Engine to address the limitations of large language models in handling cause-and-effect relationships.

According to company metrics, monthly reviews surged from 513 to approximately 73,000 this year, with two-thirds of usage extending beyond root cause analysis into alert prioritization and release risk management. Major enterprises such as Nubank, American Express, PepsiCo, and DigitalOcean are among the platform's customers.

As AI code-generation tools accelerate software production, the growing complexity of live environments has drawn investor focus toward system maintenance and reliability. Competitors ranging from major cloud providers and observability giants to specialized startups are also entering the space, marking a new phase in automated enterprise operations.
