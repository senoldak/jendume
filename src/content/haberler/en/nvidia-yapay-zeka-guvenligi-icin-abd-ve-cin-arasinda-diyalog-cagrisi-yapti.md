Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated that the chipmaker absolutely must work with Chinese artificial intelligence companies to monitor risks surrounding potentially dangerous technology. Speaking during a media briefing in London, Huang emphasized that both sides should work with each other to advance the technology safely.

Discussions surrounding artificial intelligence safety have intensified as researchers from labs such as OpenAI and Anthropic have called for development slowdowns and warned of existential risks to humanity. While figures like Sam Altman and Dario Amodei have advocated for stricter regulations, Huang has emerged as a prominent voice opposing that view, arguing instead for direct communication and cooperation.

Huang noted that dialogue is the proper method for cooperating with Chinese artificial intelligence companies, adding that he maintains direct communication with numerous researchers in China. He asserted that the United States should enhance this bilateral dialogue and expressed his advocacy for the United Kingdom to adopt a similar convening approach.

Safety has become a central topic between the U.S. and Chinese governments as both nations weigh how to address rapid technological advancements. High-level financial and diplomatic officials from both countries, including U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Chinese counterpart He Lifeng, have previously discussed potential frameworks for bilateral dialogue on artificial intelligence risks, incorporating channels for incidents up to a national security level.

Huang added that the United Kingdom holds a unique position globally to act as a convener for these discussions due to the nation's substantial intellectual capital.
