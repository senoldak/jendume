Bedrock Robotics has closed a $600 million Series C funding round co-led by CapitalG and Emergence Capital. According to an official announcement, the round values the San Francisco startup at $4.1 billion post-money, bringing its total funding to nearly $1 billion. Fresh financial backers taking part in the financing include the US Innovative Technology Fund alongside Bessemer Venture Partners and Temasek, with participation from previous supporters like Nvidia and 8VC.

The startup retrofits existing heavy machinery with its proprietary autonomy system, beginning with excavators. Bedrock announced that since mid-August, excavators running its system with no operators at the controls have been performing paid work on live job sites. The new capital will be used to deploy more machines to customer sites and fund the field teams required to keep them running. Boris Sofman, co-founder and CEO of the company, stated that they have proven fully autonomous excavators can perform real value-driven work alongside existing job site crews.

Founded by former Waymo engineers, the enterprise has successfully expanded its clientele significantly, noting that its customer base has grown to more than double since 2025. Its latest partners span multiple states, utilizing the autonomous system for earthwork and other heavy tasks. In addition, the company has hired seasoned executives from Amazon, Nuro, and Uber to strengthen its operations and legal teams. As other robotics startups also secure substantial funding, the trend toward industrial site autonomy continues to accelerate.
