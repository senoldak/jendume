Anthropic has made three tracking features generally available for Claude Enterprise plans to give business clients clearer visibility into their artificial intelligence return on investment. Initially released in beta, these features aim to help organizations optimize their spending and usage as many businesses continue to seek clarity on AI ROI and productivity gains.

The newly available tools include smart reports, pooled budgets, and in-app surveys. Rather than focusing on individual productivity metrics, smart reports evaluate aggregate data showing which tasks employees use the model for, operational frequencies, and associated costs while identifying workflow friction points. Anthropic explicitly noted in its documentation that these reports are built differently and should never serve as metrics for evaluating individual performance or driving employment decisions.

The pooled budgets feature allows administrators to set a monthly spending cap for a group within an organization, supplementing each seat's individual monthly limit. Admins can configure limits based on previous monthly spending data to ensure budgets are optimized across teams, preventing highly active users from being constrained while others leave funds unspent. Users can belong to multiple pooled budgets and organize them by priority. Meanwhile, in-app surveys let admins create surveys appearing directly in chat to collect employee usage feedback and review aggregate responses in their console.

Claude Enterprise is billed at 20 dollars per seat per month, alongside separate API token rates. In comparison, the Teams account also charges 20 dollars per seat but caps out at 150 users while including tokens within the pricing structure. As Anthropic prepares for its public offering amidst a volatile market, providing these insights aims to incentivize larger customers who want clear data on productivity gains from their AI investments.
