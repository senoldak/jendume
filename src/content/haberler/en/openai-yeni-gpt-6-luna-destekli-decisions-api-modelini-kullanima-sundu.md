OpenAI has officially made its Decisions API generally available, taking the endpoint out of public beta and bringing fast intelligence to developers worldwide. The system leverages the GPT-6 Luna architecture to help applications make near real-time decisions from text, images, and structured inputs. By focusing on bounded answers, it allows systems to handle tasks such as content classification, request routing, and work prioritization significantly faster.

The API allows systems to handle tasks such as content classification, request routing, and work prioritization significantly faster than traditional language models. Developers can ask up to 400 questions per request, receiving structured answers such as probabilities, choices, or rubric scores. Early adopters and internal teams have successfully integrated the endpoint to reduce latency, improve responsiveness, and streamline voice applications and automated workflows.

Pricing is set at $0.10 per million input tokens, with cached input dropping to $0.01 per million tokens. Notably, OpenAI does not charge for output tokens, making high-volume usage more economical. Additionally, regional processing and data controls are available to eligible customers to meet specific operational requirements.

Early adopters across various industries, alongside OpenAI's internal teams, have already integrated the endpoint to reduce latency and improve responsiveness in customer service and voice applications, demonstrating its wide applicability across diverse business workflows.
