Eindhoven-based startup Photon Bridge has raised €10 million to build laser light sources for the optical links used inside artificial intelligence data centres. The financing round was backed by multiple financial partners, with investment support from institutional backers including venture capital and regional development organizations.

The company, formerly known as Photon IP until its rebranding following a €4.75 million seed round, plans to use the new capital to further develop its product, conduct customer testing, and hire local talent in Eindhoven. Photon Bridge aims to ship its first product samples by 2027 and ramp up to volume production starting in 2028.

Modern AI data centres are increasingly relying on light rather than traditional copper wires to move large volumes of data between processors and chips. These optical links require specialized lasers to supply the necessary light. However, existing supply chains were built primarily for telecommunications, which requires far fewer components than the rapidly expanding artificial intelligence sector.

To address this manufacturing challenge, Photon Bridge combines illumination from multiple lower-power lasers, utilizing distinct optical frequencies before transmitting them through a single optical channel. According to the company, weaker lasers have a lower failure rate during manufacturing, leading to higher test-passing yields. The design targets up to 40 milliwatts of power per wavelength and relies on external chipmakers rather than operating an in-house factory.

The broader AI ecosystem is witnessing a massive surge in investments targeting optical networking and photonics. Major players and startups alike are securing significant capital to prevent hardware and optical failures from stalling the scaling of advanced artificial intelligence systems.
