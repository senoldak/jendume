Saudi oil company Aramco has finalized the acquisition of a 49 percent stake in artificial intelligence firm HUMAIN. According to the joint announcement, Aramco will transfer approximately 2.6 billion dollars in cash and its own digital assets to the company. The remaining 51 percent stake will continue to be held by the Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia.

The transaction marks the completion of a binding preliminary agreement signed in October. In addition to the cash contribution, Aramco is transferring its digital assets related to artificial intelligence and processing power to HUMAIN. The state fund is also consolidating its own artificial intelligence portfolio under the same roof, uniting the nation's energy giant and sovereign wealth fund in AI infrastructure.

HUMAIN operates across four main areas: data centers, cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence models, and industry-specific solutions. The company's portfolio includes the Arabic large language model ALLaM. This move reflects the broader regional goal of maintaining sovereign control over artificial intelligence infrastructure and establishing a strong global presence.

Such massive investments in the Gulf region demonstrate the accelerating race for sovereign artificial intelligence infrastructure globally. Similar strategies are being pursued by other regional entities. Experts note that these large-scale infrastructure investments create both new opportunities and competitive pressures for entrepreneurs and AI engineers in the region and beyond.
