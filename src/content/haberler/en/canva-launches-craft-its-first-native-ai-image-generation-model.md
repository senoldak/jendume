Canva has announced Craft, the first foundational image generation model developed entirely by its internal research team. The new model is now available to users through the Dream Lab feature and is also provided to developers via an API. The company aims to strengthen its position in the artificial intelligence market, with contributions from researchers of a platform acquired previously.

The model relies on a two-stage approach. In the first stage, user prompts are analyzed to plan the spatial relationships, scale, visual hierarchy, and embedded text of the elements. In the second stage, this comprehensive plan is translated into the final image, providing greater control over complex layouts and precise object positioning.

Unlike models trained purely on photographs, Craft utilized millions of human-designed templates from Canva's library, including posters, presentations, menus, infographics, and social media designs. This training approach enhances the model's ability to handle typography and structured layouts. The company also involved professional designers during the evaluation process and compared thousands of image pairs.

The system supports outputs up to 2K resolution and offers over thirty aspect ratios. Furthermore, integration with Magic Layers allows generated images to be broken down into editable components for precise adjustments without altering other parts. The model also supports text generation across multiple languages.

Looking ahead, the company plans to introduce faster and more efficient variants named Craft-fast and Craft-ultrafast. Developed using model distillation and a specialized inference engine, these upcoming versions aim to optimize generation speed and reduce costs.
