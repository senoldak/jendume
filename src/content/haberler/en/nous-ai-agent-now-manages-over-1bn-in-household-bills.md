London-built AI agent Nous has announced it now manages more than $1 billion in routine household spending. The milestone was reached less than two years after the product left beta, following a $17 million funding round aimed at expanding operations beyond the UK. Over 250,000 British households currently use the agent to handle tasks such as hunting for better energy, broadband, mobile, and insurance contracts, canceling unwanted subscriptions, and sorting out warranties.

The company states it manages an average of £2,866 ($3,829) in yearly spending per home, saving a typical household £734 ($981) on bills. Growth has reportedly run at 25% month on month since launch. While general-purpose agents attempt to tackle any request, Nous focuses squarely on bills, savings, and household administration. The firm also identifies itself as the first UK agent capable of handling regulated tasks like purchasing insurance, noting it neither sells customer data nor monetizes advertising.

The capital raised in October is earmarked for international expansion, broader insurance remits, additional financial services, and new tools for everyday administration. Nous has raised more than $30 million to date with backing from investors including Heartcore Capital, Mosaic Ventures, and Monzo co-founder Tom Blomfield. Founder and CEO Greg Marsh noted that achieving a billion dollars in household spending proves people will trust AI with important decisions if it works reliably and meets high safety standards.

Marsh argues that most personal AI agents are built for tech enthusiasts, whereas Nous aims to serve everyone else by handling routine chores without constant oversight. The founding team leverages consumer startup experience to bet that the next wave of AI adoption will be driven by households simply looking to automate daily chores.
