Apple is planning an overhaul for its long-standing virtual assistant Siri, aiming to turn it into an autonomous artificial intelligence agent. Recent job postings and industry reports indicate that the company wants to move beyond answering questions, designing a digital worker capable of operating keyboards and mice like a human user. The system is expected to handle simple commands, debug errors, navigate across different screens, and plan and execute complex tasks independently. This move comes as competition in the autonomous AI market intensifies, with rivals like Meta and OpenAI advancing their offerings. To close its technological gap, Apple has formed a dedicated team of hardware and software experts. According to reports, the upcoming assistant will be able to browse the internet freely, invoke various services, and autonomously decide which tools to use at each step of a task. Apple aims to maintain strict privacy standards throughout this process. Unlike some of its competitors, the company is focusing on a sandboxed security infrastructure. The environment in which Siri operates on behalf of the user will be shaped by this approach, and the process will be secured through permission models that transparently define the assistant's capabilities. The system designed to transform Siri from a basic chatbot into a general-purpose computer use agent is projected to launch no earlier than the end of twenty twenty-seven, enabling users to interact with their digital environments through autonomous agents.
