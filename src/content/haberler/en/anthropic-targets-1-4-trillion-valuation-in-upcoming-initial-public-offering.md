Anthropic is preparing to execute one of the largest initial public offerings in the technology sector. The company plans to offer 825 million shares at a price range between $70 and $78, with pricing scheduled for November 18 and trading set to begin the following day. This move could push the company's valuation to as high as $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Additionally, a separate $26 billion private placement involving leading AI companies such as Nvidia and Alphabet is being integrated into the process.

The company's financial data serves as a concrete indicator of the rapid growth within the AI sector. The $33.2 billion in revenue reported for the first nine months of 2026 represents a massive 1,206% increase compared to the same period last year. The primary driver of this growth has been the widespread adoption of AI-based coding tools, such as Claude Code, by enterprise customers. Currently, there are over 11,900 enterprise customers spending more than $100,000 annually, up from approximately 1,500 at the end of 2025. Furthermore, the net revenue retention rate, which reflects the spending habits of existing customers, stands at approximately 900%.

While the growth rate is impressive, the company is facing some challenges. The growth rate slowed to 47% in the third quarter, down from 144% in the second quarter. Industry observers attribute this deceleration to the law of large numbers and increasing competition in the sector. Nevertheless, an annualized revenue run rate of $81 billion as of the end of October suggests the company could approach the $100 billion mark by year-end.

This IPO highlights not only the financial success of a single company but also the impact of AI trade on technology markets. At a time when competitors like OpenAI have delayed their IPO plans, Anthropic's move clearly demonstrates its leadership position and investor appetite. The standardization of AI technologies in the corporate world forms the foundation for such massive valuations.
