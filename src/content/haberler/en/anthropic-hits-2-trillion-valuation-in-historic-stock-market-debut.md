Artificial intelligence leader Anthropic made a blockbuster debut on the stock market, experiencing overwhelming demand from investors on its first day of trading. Priced at $83 per share, the company raised a record $107 billion through its offering, with its market capitalization surging to $2 trillion as trading commenced. Founded just five years ago, the scale of capital raised by the company reflects the extraordinary momentum behind the artificial intelligence sector, backed by cornerstone placements from major technology giants including AMD, Google, Microsoft, and Nvidia.

Despite the celebratory atmosphere on the trading floor, financial analysts have raised questions regarding the long-term sustainability of such lofty valuations. Although Anthropic projects revenues of around $50 billion, skeptics wonder whether the company can maintain its rapid growth trajectory and achieve the ambitious figures projected by tech analysts for the coming years. Corporate demand, pricing power, and the rising threat of cheaper open-weight alternatives from rivals remain key variables for future valuation debates.

Furthermore, the company's prospectus detailed $900 billion in spending commitments for computing power and infrastructure. While the public listing provides necessary visibility and funding, debt financing will also play a major role moving forward. Meanwhile, institutions such as the European Central Bank have cautioned about the vast amounts of capital being funneled into the artificial intelligence revolution, drawing historical parallels to past market bubbles and emphasizing the need for financial transparency.
