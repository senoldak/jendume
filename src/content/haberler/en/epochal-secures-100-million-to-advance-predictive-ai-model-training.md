Epochal, a startup founded by former xAI co-founders Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai, and Manuel Kroiss, has secured $100 million in funding. The company has launched a cloud-based service called the Epochal Time Portal, designed to help developers train AI models capable of predicting future events by leveraging historical web data. The Epochal Time Portal provides access to a massive dataset containing over 1 trillion web pages spanning the last 30 years. This system allows developers to train models on specific time periods, enabling them to generate predictions and then compare those forecasts against actual outcomes to measure accuracy. For instance, a financial institution could use the service to train a model on data from the first three quarters of a year to predict fourth-quarter earnings. A feature of the platform is its ability to mitigate the "time discrepancy" issue often faced by frontier models. Modern AI models can often detect that they are being trained on historical data rather than real-time information, which can reduce training effectiveness. Epochal addresses this by deploying models in sandboxed environments that are synced to the training data. For example, the system can run a model on a Linux version from 2025 if it is being trained on data from that year. This ensures the models operate as if they are in that specific timeframe. The company's team includes researchers from organizations such as Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind. Initially, Epochal is offering a free tier with limited usage. The company plans to share the results of various AI experiments conducted using the service in the near future. This initiative enhances the ability of AI to predict the future more accurately by understanding the patterns of the past.
