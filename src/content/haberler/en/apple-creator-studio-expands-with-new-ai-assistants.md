Apple Creator Studio is expanding its capabilities with MotionVFX content, generative image editing tools, and new AI assistants. According to the company statement, this update will be provided without extra charges to existing subscribers, with Turkey pricing set at 349.99 TL monthly and 3,499 TL annually. The newly introduced assistants do not feature Turkish language support in the initial rollout.

As part of the update, Final Cut Pro for Mac receives built-in MotionVFX and a Content Center equipped with stock video. Timeline images can be edited directly in Pixelmator Pro and automatically returned to the project, while Pixelmator Pro's Generate Fill tool modifies selected image areas based on text descriptions. Logic Pro receives a beta assistant, a renewed sound browser, and a Stem Splitter that isolates drum tracks, whereas Keynote, Pages, and Numbers integrate document context-aware beta assistants.

The assistants utilize both Apple and OpenAI models to process requests. Operating these new assistant features requires specific hardware and software versions. The Logic Pro assistant requires an Apple Intelligence-supported Mac running compatible operating system versions, while other assistants require supporting software on compatible devices.

Apple's subscription model does not offer unlimited generative AI usage. According to support documentation, monthly quotas apply for image and slide generation features. The standard subscription supports sharing with multiple users via Family Sharing, while a dedicated educational plan offers discounted rates for verified students and educators.
