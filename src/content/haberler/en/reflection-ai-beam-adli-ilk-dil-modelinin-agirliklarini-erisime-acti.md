Artificial intelligence startup Reflection AI has released the weights of Beam, its first large language model, on Hugging Face. Designed for agentic coding and complex multi-step workflows, the sparse mixture-of-experts model features 501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active parameters, offering an effective context length of one million tokens to handle extensive data processing requirements efficiently.

The company utilized custom reliability optimization algorithms during the training process to mitigate errors and maintain high cluster uptime, effectively reducing training downtime to near zero. Developers can now access multiple checkpoint variants, including standard editions and hardware-efficient quantized versions, to suit various deployment environments and hardware constraints encountered in production.

Major cloud platforms have also announced support for deploying the model across distributed GPU infrastructures, allowing teams to use managed endpoints or direct serving runtimes. Available under an Apache 2.0 license, the release provides technical resources, training recipes, and code templates that enable developers to fine-tune the algorithm and integrate it seamlessly into their software applications.

This open-weight release marks a significant step in making advanced AI systems accessible to the broader developer community, allowing for greater transparency, tailored deployment options, and future improvements through self-distillation methods as the company continues to share its research and expand open intelligence standards across the industry.
