Berlin-based fintech company Pliant has raised an 80 million dollar Series C round to open its business-to-business transaction frameworks to artificial intelligence systems, according to a corporate statement. The oversubscribed funding round was led by Seaya Nova and Speedinvest, with participation from Illuminate Financial, Motive Partners, and Molten Ventures.

The newly secured capital will fund Pliant's expansion into the United States and Canada, its entry into additional markets, and its broader artificial intelligence roadmap. The investment follows a three-year period during which Pliant doubled its business annually, shortly after the company surpassed 100 million dollars in annual recurring revenue.

Pliant integrates multiple financial operations into a unified platform for corporate clients. Its proprietary automation tools already handle recurring finance operations for customers. Meanwhile, external agents ranging from Claude and ChatGPT to custom enterprise solutions can connect through the Model Context Protocol to execute payment workflows with built-in permissions.

Founded in 2020, Pliant functions as a principal member of Visa and Mastercard. Operating under a European e-money license alongside regulated partners, the organization extends financial products globally. The company currently employs more than 300 people across eight offices, serving clients in travel, insurance, and technology sectors.

Broader payment networks are also exploring agent-driven transactions. Artificial intelligence systems recently began settling supplier invoices in a pilot program with major financial networks, highlighting a growing industry trend toward automated financial operations.
