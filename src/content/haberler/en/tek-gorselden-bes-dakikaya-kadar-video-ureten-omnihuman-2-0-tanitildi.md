TikTok parent company ByteDance has launched OmniHuman 2.0, the new version of its AI-powered video generation model, through its corporate platform BytePlus. The newly developed system can create continuous digital character videos of up to five minutes based on a single image and an audio recording. The model synchronizes character speech, facial expressions, and body movements precisely with the audio content.

One of the most notable aspects of the system is that it goes beyond standard lip-syncing by reflecting the rhythm, tone, and meaning of the voice directly into the character's movements. According to information shared by BytePlus, the model deeply analyzes audio content to generate appropriate gestures and emotional responses, achieving a natural harmony between spoken words and body language.

The technological infrastructure is not limited to human visuals; animals, three-dimensional designs, and characters in various artistic styles can also be animated successfully. Users can additionally add new objects to the scene, trigger specific movements, and direct camera angles via text prompts. Supporting multi-character scenes, the system can produce rhythm-sensitive performances, particularly in music videos.

According to data shared by the company, OmniHuman 2.0 has a video production capacity ranging from thirty seconds to three hundred seconds. With the new version, motion quality has been improved and costs have been reduced by forty percent. The usage fee for the model is set at zero point zero seven dollars per second.

Finding a wide range of applications in advertising, film and television productions, digital music videos, and individual content creation, OmniHuman 2.0 has been made available to developers via API. This move aims to allow companies to easily integrate the AI model into their own marketing solutions and digital character applications.
