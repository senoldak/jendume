A major milestone has been reached in the field of robotics and physical reasoning. A newly introduced physical reasoning model empowers robots to perceive three-dimensional environments, establish visual-semantic context, and generate executable code on the fly.

Achieving top performance on benchmarks designed to test spatial and physical problem-solving in 3D environments, this general-purpose model spans the entire robotics lifecycle. It allows quadrupeds and robotic arms to autonomously perform everyday physical tasks, such as handling household items and organizing workspaces with high dexterity.

The system bridges reality and simulation by creating digital twins of physical objects, enabling robots to practice their movements in a virtual environment before executing them in the real world. This closed-loop visual reasoning ensures precise visual and physical alignment, significantly reducing error rates and marking a giant leap toward physical artificial general intelligence.
