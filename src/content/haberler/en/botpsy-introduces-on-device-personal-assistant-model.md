Tether AI Research has unveiled BotPsy, a new model with 0.8 billion parameters designed to run directly on user devices. Unlike many advanced models that rely on massive cloud infrastructure, BotPsy is built to operate locally, eliminating the need for constant internet connectivity or expensive server resources. Based on the Qwen3.5-0.8B architecture, this model functions as an "agent," capable of using tools to execute specific tasks for the user.

The current landscape of intelligent models is dominated by large systems that raise concerns regarding privacy, data control, and operational costs. BotPsy addresses these challenges by offering a compact, efficient architecture that brings high-level performance to personal devices. It is capable of handling various daily tasks, including web research, travel planning, financial operations, and device management. Despite its small size, it outperforms other models in the 1.2 billion parameter class and even competes with models 2.5 times its size.

The model's performance is particularly notable in multi-step workflows. According to benchmark results, BotPsy roughly doubles the agentic capability of its base model. For instance, on the BFCL v4 benchmark, its score rose from 23.4 to 44.0, while on ACEBench, it improved from 30.1 to 58.7. These metrics demonstrate that the model is not only efficient but also highly capable, proving that smaller models can effectively handle complex instructions.

This development marks a significant shift for the future of personal assistants. By processing data locally rather than in the cloud, BotPsy enhances user privacy and security. Furthermore, its low computational requirements make it suitable for a wide range of applications, from smartphones to vehicle systems, democratizing access to intelligent agents. By reducing reliance on heavy cloud infrastructure, BotPsy paves the way for a more accessible, secure, and private integration of intelligent technology into everyday life.
