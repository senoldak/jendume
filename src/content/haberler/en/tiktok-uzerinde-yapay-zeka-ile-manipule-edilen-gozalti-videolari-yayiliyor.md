Content creators on TikTok have been using artificial intelligence tools to manipulate real arrest footage in order to drive engagement on social media. The manipulated videos originate from actual footage, which appears to have been filmed for reality television shows. AI tools were used to alter the clips to depict law enforcement officers and arrestees in non-consensual sexualized scenarios. Arrestees are frequently shown wearing handcuffs and being held against police cruisers while the fabricated activities take place.

These abusive deepfakes are spliced in front of longer arrest videos as a tactic to hook scrolling users and keep them watching until the end. Research findings indicate that this method is intentionally designed to exploit social media algorithms and capture user attention through shocking manipulations. Company leadership stated that this is a disturbing example of AI being used to create deepfake versions of real content for attention, noting that real people are being depicted in scenarios that never happened without their consent.

In some of the altered videos, individuals are pictured smiling, which falsely implies that they are willing participants in the sexual advances while being arrested. While some of the altered videos include disclaimers claiming they are for entertainment purposes only, experts emphasize that sexualized deepfakes created without consent remain inherently harmful. Following notification and review, platform moderators removed the specific flagged examples of this content and pointed to existing guidelines regarding the clear labeling of realistic AI material.
