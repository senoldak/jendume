Google has introduced a new approach in the artificial intelligence landscape with the launch of Gemini Decision 1, a model designed specifically to classify data rather than generate text. While traditional models often consume significant resources by generating prose for simple tasks, this new model focuses solely on providing precise answers such as "yes/no," multiple-choice selections, or rubric scores. This shift is particularly impactful for workflows like customer support, content moderation, and automated routing, where speed and efficiency are paramount.

Why does this matter? Currently, many organizations rely on large, expensive generative models for basic classification tasks, leading to unnecessary costs and latency. Gemini Decision 1 addresses this by offering a more streamlined and economical alternative. The model processes text, images, video, audio, and PDFs in a single pass, providing a confidence score for every answer. This feature allows systems to automatically escalate uncertain cases to human operators, enhancing reliability.

The pricing model is equally significant. Priced at $0.10 per million input tokens, the model charges nothing for output, as it does not generate prose. This eliminates the cost of discarding unwanted output tokens. With support for up to 262,144 input tokens per request, it can handle substantial data inputs efficiently.

Google positions this model as a "scoring engine" rather than a chatbot. It does not support text generation, function calling, or structured output generation. By focusing exclusively on classification and scoring, it represents a strategic move toward more specialized and efficient AI tools. Developers can now integrate this model into their agent stacks to manage complex workflows with lower overhead and higher precision, marking a shift toward task-specific AI utility.
