Block, the parent company behind widely used financial services like Square and Cash App, has integrated advanced artificial intelligence into its security operations. By utilizing sophisticated AI systems, the security team has managed to compress vulnerability detection, tuning, and incident response processes that used to take weeks down into hours and minutes, all while keeping humans in the loop for critical validation.

The system goes beyond flagging isolated bugs by hypothesizing attack patterns and chaining complex attack paths together, uncovering risks that might have lingered for years. In recent exercises, the time from submitting a high-level vulnerability to receiving an automated proposed patch dropped to just 18 minutes. Furthermore, automation has freed up roughly 600 analyst-hours per quarter previously spent on false-positive triage, allowing teams to focus on deeper investigations.

Security leadership notes that this technology democratizes advanced defensive capabilities, moving them beyond elite tech giants and government agencies to help organizations of all sizes secure their digital networks.
