The UK government is investing 130 million pounds in AI cyber defence, establishing a National Cyber Shield that leverages agentic artificial intelligence to safeguard critical infrastructure. This funding forms part of a broader package exceeding 600 million pounds for artificial intelligence, which Chancellor John Healey is set to introduce in the national Budget.

The plans outline 60 million pounds dedicated to the National Cyber Shield, which includes a 25 million pound challenge fund aimed at UK-based artificial intelligence and cybersecurity firms. Additionally, the government will scope a regulatory sandbox for agentic security tools across industry, the public sector, and critical infrastructure facilities.

A further 70 million pounds will establish a National Exposure and Real-Time Vulnerability Centre. This facility will deploy artificial intelligence tools to detect cyber vulnerabilities across the wider economy and critical infrastructure networks. Meanwhile, the AI Security Institute will receive 120 million pounds, and a new cybersecurity center of excellence in Manchester will train government specialists.

The largest single item within the comprehensive package is 165 million pounds allocated for biosecurity, featuring a 100 million pound AI Biosecurity Lab. Leading artificial intelligence developers OpenAI and Anthropic will contribute up to 200 million dollars in model credits to support this initiative, as officials stress the importance of hardening domestic digital defenses.
