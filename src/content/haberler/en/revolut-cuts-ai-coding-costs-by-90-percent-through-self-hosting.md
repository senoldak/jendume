Fintech company Revolut has successfully reduced its AI coding expenses by 90 percent by optimizing its software development processes. Instead of relying on external providers like Anthropic and OpenAI, the company has transitioned to running open-source models on its own graphics processing units (GPUs). This strategic shift has made the coding workflows of over 2,000 engineers significantly more economical and efficient.

As part of this transformation, Revolut adopted the GLM-5.3 model, developed by Z.ai. David Abitbol, who leads AI operations at Revolut, stated that the model runs on 56 Nvidia B200 chips, bringing the blended cost down to 30 cents per million tokens. The company continues to use more advanced models, such as Claude Code, only for exceptional and complex tasks.

The operational results of this technological shift are notable. According to internal data, coding time has improved by 60 percent, while the lead time from feature development to deployment has accelerated by 70 percent. Revolut emphasizes that this transition has eliminated monthly billing shocks and created a more predictable cost structure for its AI operations.

Financially, Revolut continues to grow, recently completing a secondary share sale that pushed its valuation to $115 billion. This valuation is 53 percent higher than the $75 billion set a year earlier. As the company continues its global expansion with over 80 million retail customers, it aims to maintain both security and cost advantages by keeping its AI infrastructure under its own control.
