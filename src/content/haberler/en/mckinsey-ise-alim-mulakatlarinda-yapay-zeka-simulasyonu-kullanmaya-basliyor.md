Global consulting firm McKinsey & Company is incorporating artificial intelligence into its recruitment strategy by introducing an AI client simulation for entry-level candidates. Applicants for business analyst roles will engage in a ten-minute conversation with a simulated client as part of the evaluation process.

The AI tool is designed to reflect the vast majority of scenarios consultants encounter in their daily work, focusing on realistic problem-solving rather than aggressive questioning. The system does not score the candidates directly; instead, human evaluators observe the interactions to assess how well applicants handle simulated business situations.

This new assessment tool is currently being tested for undergraduate business analyst applicants in select offices. As organizations continue to adopt intelligent automation, recruitment practices are shifting toward practical, tech-driven evaluations.
