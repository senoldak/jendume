Three high-level national security and strategic initiative leaders at OpenAI, namely Sasha Baker, Caroline Zier, and Ben Crampton, fell victim to an embarrassing prank orchestrated by Russian comedians known as Vovan and Lexus. Posing as top Ukrainian government officials, the comedians managed to arrange a video call with the OpenAI staffers that lasted a humiliating seventeen minutes. During the conversation, the company employees openly discussed military artificial intelligence applications and ongoing talks regarding the supply of tools to Ukraine.

During the recorded call, Crampton noted that OpenAI aims to build a deep relationship with Ukraine, though acknowledging that certain leaders within the company might object to their technologies being used in offensive military operations. During the discussion, Crampton explained that national security personnel at the organization often work to reconcile executive hesitations regarding military AI. He also admitted to having contacts within the Ukrainian Ministry of Defense through a Washington-based colleague.

Vovan stated that arranging the meeting was remarkably simple after contacting a senior Ukrainian official in San Francisco who helped facilitate the connection. The release of the recorded meeting online raises profound concerns regarding OpenAI's security protocols, especially given that the AI lab maintains massive Pentagon contracts to deploy advanced systems in classified environments. The breach highlights how easily malicious actors could potentially access high-level personnel.

In response to the stunt, an OpenAI spokesperson stated that the company launched a thorough investigation into the call's authenticity. Working internally and with relevant authorities, OpenAI confirmed the call was a hoax executed by parties known for targeting public figures and officials, and adopted measures to prevent similar situations in the future. Vovan and Lexus have previously targeted numerous public figures, including political leaders like John McCain and Bernie Sanders.
