Social network Bluesky announced an expansion of its AI-powered Attie tool during an industry disruption event in October 2026. Initially launched to help users build custom feeds, the platform now allows creators to generate interactive experiences such as games, quizzes, polls, and small websites using simple text prompts.

The update aims to democratize software creation, allowing individuals without formal coding skills to build custom applications for their communities. These user-generated experiences live on the same underlying decentralized protocol as Bluesky, enabling creators to tap directly into the platform's established distribution network rather than launching standalone projects. The integration ensures that new projects benefit from an existing user base right from the start.

During the announcement, company executives highlighted that the broader network currently reaches 47 million registered users. Additionally, private data support—a top request from developers—is currently undergoing alpha testing with a planned rollout by the end of the year. Leadership noted that these small, fun social experiences will offer fresh ways for friends and communities to interact without needing to broadcast everything to the entire world constantly.

Attie serves as an experimental sandbox for Bluesky to test new AI-driven concepts and engage both developers and creators. Users can bring their own AI models or utilize open-weight alternatives when generating sites and games, marking a strategic effort to reinvigorate developer activity and user engagement on the platform through community-focused tools.
