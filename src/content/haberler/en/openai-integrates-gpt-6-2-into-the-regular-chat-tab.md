OpenAI has rolled out an update bringing its latest generation AI model, GPT-6.2, directly to the regular chat tab. Users no longer need to switch to specialized work tabs to benefit from the company's newest improvements in coding, research, and complex problem-solving. The move aims to make powerful AI features integrated into everyday digital interactions.

According to the official release notes, the updated assistant is better at interpreting images, handling complicated instructions, and conducting web searches. In internal testing, GPT-6.2 scored 71.7 percent in image understanding, a jump from 47.4 percent with the previous model. Additionally, its success rate with challenging instructions climbed to 78.1 percent compared to 62.5 percent previously.

These technical benchmarks translate into tangible everyday improvements, such as more reliable schedule planning, detailed photo analysis without buying new items, and better adherence to editing constraints without rewriting entire texts. OpenAI is focusing on eliminating minor frustrations that often undermine the time-saving benefits of using an AI assistant.

The update began rolling out on Monday to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers. By reducing the need to repeat instructions or correct misunderstood queries, this release represents a practical productivity boost for average users relying on conversational AI.
