AI lab Anthropic has announced a new feature called Smart Reports, which allows managers to scrape and analyze their employees' daily interactions with various AI products. According to the support page, the tool lets bosses generate summaries based on team usage in order to report on ongoing work throughout the day. Management can use these reports to sort worker outputs by overall cost and chatbot output, providing direct oversight over perceived efficiency when using AI tools.

While Anthropic claims the system will anonymize sessions, past privacy approaches have faced scrutiny over how easily data could be de-anonymized using large language models. The introduction of this monitoring tool aligns with the company's broader drive to secure enterprise-level contracts ahead of an anticipated initial public offering. Corporate customers currently account for 80 percent of the total business, making enterprise-focused features crucial for market performance and future growth.

The ultimate utility and reception of such workplace surveillance tools remain to be seen as privacy advocates and office workers evaluate the implications. As corporate adoption continues to grow, balancing enterprise demands with individual worker privacy stands out as a critical challenge for developers of workplace AI technologies, especially as companies look toward public markets and large-scale enterprise integration.
