OpenAI has rolled out a new feature called Intelligent UI, designed to make ChatGPT responses much more visual and interactive. Powered by the GPT-6 architecture, the system goes beyond generating plain text, capable of producing charts, diagrams, forms, maps, and interactive buttons tailored to the user's specific query.

At the core of Intelligent UI is GPT-6's ability to determine the most suitable presentation format. For instance, a travel plan can be displayed directly on a map, while a complex mechanism can be explored through interactive diagrams. Additionally, OpenAI has introduced speed optimizations allowing the model to begin responding to users much earlier, even while the reasoning process is ongoing.

Rolling out globally across user tiers starting October 8, this update aims to fundamentally reshape the conversational AI experience. The company envisions a shift where users no longer adapt to fixed software interfaces, but instead rely on AI that generates the appropriate interface on the fly based on the task at hand.
