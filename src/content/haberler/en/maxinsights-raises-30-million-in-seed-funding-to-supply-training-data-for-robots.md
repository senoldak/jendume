Maxinsights, a startup focused on fueling physical artificial intelligence, collects first-person video footage of people performing everyday chores to build training datasets for robots. The company announced that it has raised $30 million in seed funding in a round led by Peak XV, with participation from Tola Capital, South Park Commons, INCE Capital, and Alumni Ventures.

The startup works with more than 5,000 contractors across six countries who wear head, wrist, and chest cameras while doing chores such as cooking, cleaning, and washing cars. Unlike competitors that stage tasks in controlled mock settings, Maxinsights captures natural speeds and movements in actual homes, hotels, and restaurants. The company reports that it has captured over 2 million hours of footage, with 1.5 million hours ready for training AI models.

Alongside the funding round, Maxinsights launched two new products: MaxLoop, a software tool that automatically reviews incoming footage to cut review costs, and MaxGlove, a sensor glove developed in partnership with Genesis AI to record grip force and object weight. The startup has supplied data to frontier labs and was the sole provider of the 200,000 hours of first-person data behind Genesis AI's model GENE-26.5.

As investments in physical AI surge, obtaining high-quality training data remains a major bottleneck. Maxinsights aims to tackle this shortage by treating quality control as an engineering challenge. Following operations in China and Indonesia, the company plans to expand its presence to Turkey and Mexico in the near future.
