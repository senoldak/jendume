A researcher at Physical Superintelligence (PSI) has successfully solved the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture, a quantum computing problem that had remained unsolved for 18 years. This breakthrough marks a significant milestone, demonstrating that AI systems are capable of tackling complex scientific challenges beyond simple text generation. The solution was achieved using an advanced AI model and was subsequently auto-formalized and verified using Lean, a coding language designed for mathematics.

This development highlights the transformative potential of AI in scientific research. Experts suggest that the ability of AI to accelerate discovery could fundamentally reshape academic publishing and the methodologies used by researchers worldwide. Solving this specific problem, which is central to quantum query complexity, clarifies the theoretical limits of how much faster quantum computers can solve certain problems compared to classical ones.

The startup’s business model operates on a dual-track strategy. While the long-term goal is to convert theoretical physics breakthroughs into tangible technologies, PSI currently funds its research operations by optimizing data centers. The company’s AI systems analyze variables such as heat, power, and water consumption to enhance operational efficiency. Executives describe this approach as a proving ground, where the practical application of AI in data centers helps validate the systems needed for more ambitious scientific endeavors.

The scientific community is currently navigating the implications of this AI-driven era of discovery. Academics are actively discussing the need for new standards to validate and interpret AI-generated solutions, ensuring they remain accessible to human researchers. In disciplines like physics, where models must align with real-world physical constraints, the ability to validate computational results through experiments is crucial. PSI aims to close this loop, positioning itself at the intersection of high-level theoretical physics and practical, AI-driven technological application.
