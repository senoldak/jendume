Square is rolling out a brand-new hardware kiosk designed to let customers order items simply by talking to an artificial intelligence. Named the Square Kiosk, the device features a large pill-shaped design and a 21.5-inch touchscreen.

The system incorporates an agentic, conversational voice layer that fully understands a business's menu. It listens and responds in real time, allowing customers to place orders using natural language just as they would with human staff, as well as request modifications and substitutions.

Equipped with presence detection, the kiosk can even greet customers as they approach. This new release marks a significant step forward in bringing practical, voice-based AI agents into physical retail and dining environments.
