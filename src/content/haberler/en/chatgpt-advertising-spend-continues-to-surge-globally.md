Advertising spend on ChatGPT has seen significant growth, particularly within the US market. According to October 2026 data, US advertisers invested $70.7 million in ChatGPT ads, representing a 127% increase from the $31.1 million recorded in April. This trend highlights that AI models are evolving beyond simple tools into major advertising platforms. Businesses are increasingly utilizing these interactive environments as new digital storefronts to reach their target audiences directly.

Globally, across 24 tracked countries, total ad spend reached $110.6 million. The US accounts for nearly 64% of this total, solidifying its position as the primary market. The largest spending category was programming and developer software, totaling $27.9 million. Other sectors, including graphics, multimedia, job placement, education, and travel, are also actively leveraging the platform to connect with users.

OpenAI transitioned its ad model to a cost-per-click pricing structure in April. Following an expansion into 26 additional countries on October 21, the ad network now operates in 89 countries, serving over 100,000 advertisers. The participation of major corporations like Amazon in pilot programs further validates the growing acceptance of AI-driven advertising in the corporate sector. While investment growth varies by region, the direct engagement capabilities offered by the platform are establishing it as a potent alternative to traditional digital advertising methods.
