As companies adopt artificial intelligence to streamline operations, the tendency of models to generate probabilistic answers rather than factual ones remains a major hurdle. Imago Platform tackled this challenge by integrating graph databases to improve the reliability of business intelligence tools.

Working with its first client in the lighting and electrical sector, the startup ingested extensive PDF documents to resolve complex warranty claims across various manufacturers. However, standard language models fell short of requirements due to their probabilistic nature. By incorporating graph-based technologies through the Neo4j Startup Program, the team mapped relationships between disparate data sources, enabling the model to deliver consistently accurate results.

The resulting system connects customer relationship management and enterprise resource planning tools with emails and documents into a secure hub. Operating across Sweden for retailers and property management teams, the technology powers needs-based product engines that bypass traditional checkbox searches.

Experts emphasize that grounding AI agents in relevant business information is essential for reliable automation. Graph architectures provide the necessary context while allowing developers to trace every step an autonomous agent takes to reach a conclusion.
