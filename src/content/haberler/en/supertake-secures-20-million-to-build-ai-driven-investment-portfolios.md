Supertake has announced a $20 million seed funding round. The platform allows users to express their market insights and predictions about global trends in natural language, which are then converted by AI agents into actionable stock portfolios. This system enables investors to create strategies based on their own beliefs rather than manually picking individual stocks. The company compares this progression to the evolution of self-driving cars, aiming to balance trust and technological innovation within the financial sector.

This development democratizes investing by removing the need for deep technical knowledge or manual stock picking. By leveraging AI agents, the company evolves the traditional "robo-advisor" model into a more dynamic and personalized financial tool. To address trust concerns regarding AI managing money, the platform implements strict guardrails, ensuring the AI cannot deposit or withdraw funds without explicit consent and operates within defined trading limits.

With this funding, the company has appointed former Coinbase AI product and engineering leads as its new CEO and CTO. Led by a team that previously founded and sold the podcast platform Anchor to Spotify, the startup is currently operating in a beta phase via a waitlist. The platform tracks user ideas on a leaderboard, allowing others to invest alongside them. In the future, the company plans to expand its capabilities beyond stocks to include derivatives and various other market strategies. This move demonstrates that the role of AI in financial services is evolving from simple data analysis to autonomous agents capable of executing trades.
