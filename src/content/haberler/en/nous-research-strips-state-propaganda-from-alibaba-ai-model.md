US-based artificial intelligence startup Nous Research has fine-tuned Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B model on fifty-three million tokens to create a new open-weight model named Arete-27B. The process successfully dropped the rate of Chinese state propaganda responses from twenty-two percent to under one percent while preserving the model's general capabilities. The research team tested the models using eight hundred and three prompts spanning categories such as free speech, ideology, and human rights. Test results showed that the original model scored two point thirty-two out of five on factual accuracy, whereas the new model improved to two point ninety-eight. The most dramatic shift occurred in the free speech category, where the propaganda rate plunged from sixty percent to one percent. Nous Research concluded that the bias layer in Chinese-aligned models is shallow and can be removed through carefully chosen supervision without disturbing underlying functions. The startup secured ninety million dollars in Series B funding in October, reaching a one point five billion dollar valuation during the round. Previous studies by other organizations had similarly documented pro-Beijing biases in several leading models. The weights and test datasets for Arete-27B have been released under an Apache two point zero license on Hugging Face, offering researchers full access to the new model.
