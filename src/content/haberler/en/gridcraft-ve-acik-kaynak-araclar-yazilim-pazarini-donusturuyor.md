In a major shift for the software industry, free and open-source alternatives to Adobe's flagship applications such as Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere Pro, alongside Microsoft Office suites, have been introduced. Interestingly, these tools were built not by a large corporate team, but by a single developer running AI agents in parallel. Projects now available on GitHub include GridCraft as an Excel alternative, DeckCraft for PowerPoint, and WordCraft for Word. This development demonstrates how the traditional barriers to software creation, often considered too expensive and difficult, are being dismantled by artificial intelligence. Available for Windows, macOS, and Linux via GitHub Releases, the tools support XLSX files and include SHA-256 checksum verification for security against imitation sites. Key considerations surrounding the shift include the enterprise readiness of the applications, the origin of the 86 percent compatibility rate, the legal implications of clean room approaches, and overall open-source security. The rise of free alternatives forces established software giants to rethink their pricing strategies and business models. Ultimately, this transformation rewrites the rules of price competition in the software market. By lowering costs for consumers and organizations, AI-driven development signals a new era where traditional software monopolies face agile open-source challengers. As development barriers fall, the software landscape transforms significantly.
