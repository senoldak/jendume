Vehicle rental platform Turo is preparing a new system to help users easily find Tesla vehicles equipped with FSD (Full Self-Driving) capabilities. Through an integration with Tesla's API, the platform will verify whether the feature is actively enabled in the car and display this status directly on rental listings, ensuring transparency for consumers seeking advanced driving technology.

As part of the update, eligible vehicle owners on the platform will also receive a one-month free trial of the FSD software. This allows cars that have not previously activated the software to offer the feature during the trial period. Turo aims to rely on direct software verification rather than depending solely on owner descriptions in listing texts.

According to Tesla's recent data, approximately 1.7 million customers worldwide have paid for the FSD feature, while the company's total production and sales exceed 10 million vehicles. Models 3 and Y rank among the most rented vehicles on the platform, and the new labeling system targets these popular models to help users distinguish software capabilities.

The system formally requires continuous driver supervision, and users renting an FSD-equipped Tesla through Turo will be presented with warning texts and tutorial content. While this partnership expands the ways drivers can experience advanced automation technology through short-term rentals, the system remains strictly a supervised driving assistant.
