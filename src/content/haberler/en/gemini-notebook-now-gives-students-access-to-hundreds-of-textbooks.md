Google has rolled out a new update for Gemini Notebook, its platform designed for deep research and study sessions. Formerly known as NotebookLM, the tool allows users to produce custom notebooks by uploading files, linking numerous sites, and attaching purchases from Google Play accounts. The platform's studio features also enable users to transform their research guides into video overviews, audio summaries, slide decks, and flashcards.

The latest update introduces a vast library of textbooks provided by organizations based in the US and India, including Open Textbook Library, S Chand Publishing, and OpenStax. This feature allows students and everyday users to access hundreds of academic textbooks completely free of charge. By opening a notebook and selecting the textbook source option under the add sources menu, users can incorporate verified literature into their research workflows.

Supported texts are categorized across fifteen distinct areas, including business, computer science, economics, engineering, health and medicine, history and philosophy, languages and writing, law and politics, life sciences, mathematics, physical sciences, psychology, sociology and culture, student success, and other topics. Specific guides such as biology and chemistry textbooks cover complex subjects like cell division, metabolic pathways, stoichiometry, and periodic trends.

This update builds upon previous platform enhancements, such as the expert intelligence introduction that lets users utilize select ebooks from Google Play Books and third-party subscriptions. By combining generative AI capabilities with standardized academic textbooks, the platform aims to streamline the study and research process. Users can generate executive summaries, break down step-by-step math and science problems, and review comparative theoretical frameworks effortlessly.
