Sezgin Çetin, based in Antalya, has released a mobile application named BeMatrx on both the App Store and Google Play without having any formal software engineering background. According to the creator, the entire codebase was written using Anthropic's AI coding tool, Claude Code. The development process took approximately seven months, with the founder testing every single stage directly on a mobile phone. The project was entirely self-funded with a total cost of around one thousand two hundred dollars.

BeMatrx combines elements of social networking with life simulation. Users can move to one of ninety real cities across fifty-four countries and choose professions such as a chef, doctor, or pilot. The application features economic mechanics including apartment rentals and monthly salaries, and the simulation continues to run even when the application is closed.

The application prioritizes user privacy by omitting real names and photographs. An AI system generates anonymous character faces that do not resemble the actual users. The platform operates without any advertising or ranking algorithms, offering support in twelve different languages completely free of charge.

Projects of this nature highlight a new era where artificial intelligence tools lower the barriers to software development, allowing solo creators to ship full scale products. The development metrics shared by the creator have not been independently verified.
