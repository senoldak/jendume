Goldman Sachs has announced a 500 million dollar commitment for the next phase of its small business support program, which includes establishing a new AI institute built alongside Anthropic, Google, Meta, and OpenAI. Bank executives noted at the launch event that while frontier models are crucial for fields like cybersecurity, defence, and healthcare, most business operations can thrive using existing base tools.

The newly formed Small Business AI Institute will focus on educating business owners about artificial intelligence, publishing research on its practical use, and leveraging technology to widen access to capital. Each technology partner brings unique contributions to the initiative. Anthropic will lead learning series and workshops, Google will provide professional certificates and software access, Meta will offer training tools, and OpenAI will host community college workshops.

A report released alongside the announcement indicates a steady rise in AI adoption among entrepreneurs. A significant majority of surveyed business owners now incorporate AI into their daily operations. However, most users spend modest amounts each month or rely strictly on free tools, and a large portion reports no changes to their workforce size due to adoption.

Leaders advised business owners to approach the technology thoughtfully rather than rushing into expensive implementations. Productivity gains are expected to materialize across customer experience, sales, and manufacturing. Bank representatives emphasize that AI is increasingly turning developers and workers into managers of their own automated processes.
