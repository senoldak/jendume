Mercedes-Benz is preparing to launch two distinct systems featuring significant innovations in driving technologies. The manufacturer announced MB.Drive Assist Pro, which can largely take over urban driving from a starting point to a destination, alongside Hands-Free Highway Assist solutions that allow travel on highways without steering wheel intervention. Powered by Nvidia's artificial intelligence capabilities, these technologies specifically aim to serve as an alternative to Tesla's Full Self-Driving system. Mercedes-Benz will initially begin tests with a limited user group in California and subsequently aims to roll out these features across the entire United States within 2027. The newly developed systems will find a place in the brand's electric vehicles as well as certain internal combustion engine cars.

MB.Drive Assist Pro provides much more comprehensive driving support compared to traditional adaptive cruise controllers and lane-keeping assistants. Activated when the driver selects their destination via the navigation system, this technology can independently perform many maneuvers throughout the journey. Processes such as intersection crossings, adapting to traffic lights, changing lanes, and using highway connection points are managed by the system. In heavy urban traffic, the movements of pedestrians, cyclists, and other vehicles are continuously monitored to make the safest decisions. On the other hand, unlike Tesla's solution, Mercedes-Benz's city system requires drivers to keep their hands on the steering wheel.

This step, bringing a fresh breath to the autonomous driving race in the automotive market, stands out as a critical milestone in the integration of artificial intelligence solutions into daily transportation. The innovations introduced by the company aim to alleviate the burden on the driver and enhance road safety. The system, which will reach broader audiences following the initial phase in California, is anticipated to fundamentally transform future transportation habits.
