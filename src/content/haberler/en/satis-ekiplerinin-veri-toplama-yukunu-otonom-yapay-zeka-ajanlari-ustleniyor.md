The time-consuming process of prospecting and maintaining clean customer relationship management data is being transformed by autonomous AI agents. Founded by Shivam Mahajan and Zaaheda Islam, the startup emerged after noticing that enterprises spent countless manual hours cleaning and updating customer records. The platform allows users to describe what they are looking for, deploying AI agents to scour various sources and build relevant lists of companies and contacts.

The system enriches account details using more than one hundred third-party data sources and employs a structured approach to verify contact information. By continuously monitoring the web for changes such as job promotions, company relocations, or new funding rounds, the platform ensures customer records remain up to date. According to the company, users have reported cutting their prospecting time by as much as seventy percent.

Having surpassed one thousand customers and over one point two million dollars in annual recurring revenue, the startup plans to open-source its autonomous customer knowledge database by early 2027, pending security reviews. This move will enable organizations to host their own data and build custom applications on top of the technology, highlighting how AI is reshaping everyday enterprise workflows.
