YouTube has rolled out new content labels on YouTube Music to make the role of generative artificial intelligence in music production more transparent to listeners. The labels identify tracks where AI was significantly used in creating vocals or compositions, with plans to expand the feature to the main YouTube app.

Listeners can tap on the labels in the playback screen to view additional details about how AI contributed to the track. Content uploaders classify their AI usage into distinct categories, ranging from fully generated pieces to mixed creations.

While various music streaming platforms adopt different strategies for handling synthetic tracks, YouTube focuses on clarity and informing audiences rather than penalizing creators.
