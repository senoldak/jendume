Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has responded to claims suggesting that his company supports surrendering human will to artificial intelligence systems. In a detailed statement shared on social media, Amodei dismissed these allegations as "absurd." He stated that humanity should not sacrifice its legitimacy or the right to determine its own future for the sake of efficiency or safety. Amodei explicitly stated his opposition to the idea of AI becoming the final decision-making authority in governance processes, noting that his previous articles and interviews consistently support this stance.

This statement arrives during a period of intensifying debate among top AI executives regarding the extent of human control over decision-making. Other industry leaders have voiced similar concerns. Earlier in November, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman stated that he wants humans to continue having a say in their own future. Similarly, Alexandr Wang, who leads AI efforts at Meta, wrote that delegating societal management to AI is unacceptable. Wang noted the uncertainty regarding how humanity can maintain control in the face of AI systems that can improve themselves without any constraints. He further emphasized the necessity of AI safety and ensuring that systems remain aligned with human goals.

Amodei’s remarks have prompted global discussions on AI safety and the alignment of systems with human objectives. The tech industry is balancing the efficiency gains offered by AI with the ethical boundaries of these systems. Experts argue that developing technology in a controlled manner is the only path to preserving human autonomy in the future. These discussions show that AI is a powerful tool that impacts societal structures and decision-making mechanisms.
