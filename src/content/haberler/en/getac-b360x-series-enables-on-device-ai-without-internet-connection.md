Getac has announced the B360X and B360X Pro models, designed for professionals working in demanding field conditions. These next-generation fully rugged laptops stand out with their ability to run artificial intelligence applications even in environments where internet connectivity is limited or non-existent. This feature allows field workers to process data directly on the device without sending it to the cloud, enabling instantaneous critical decision-making.

At the heart of the devices are Intel Core Ultra Series 3 processors. Equipped with Intel Graphics and Intel AI Boost technologies, these processors ensure that AI-powered tasks are executed locally on the device. This local processing capacity increases security by preventing sensitive data from leaving the device while also eliminating latency. Public safety, utilities, manufacturing, and defense sectors constitute the primary target audience for this technology.

On the hardware side, the laptops feature 14-inch LumiBond displays with 1,400 nits of brightness. These screens offer readability under direct sunlight as well as support for use with gloves or under rain. In terms of durability standards, the computers hold MIL-STD-810H and IP66 certifications, showing resistance to drops from 1.8 meters. While the B360X Pro model offers more expanded features than the standard model, the Intel Core Ultra X7 processor option is exclusively available for the B360X model.

This development demonstrates that artificial intelligence is becoming accessible not just in data centers but at the very edge. Getac's move represents a significant milestone in ensuring that autonomous systems and AI-powered analytical tools can operate uninterrupted even in the most challenging physical conditions of the world.
