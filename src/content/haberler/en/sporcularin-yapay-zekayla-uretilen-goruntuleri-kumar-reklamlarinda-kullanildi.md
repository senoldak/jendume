The Australian Football League (AFL) has instructed its official gambling partner, Sportsbet, to stop featuring high-profile athletes and coaches in AI-generated social media content without their consent. The videos, designed to boost engagement, utilized artificial intelligence to manipulate players' images and voices, depicting them reciting lines from movies and speeches. Prominent athletes such as Lachie Neale and Will Ashcroft were among those digitally replicated in the controversial posts. The AFL Players’ Association intervened, arguing that the content breached collective bargaining agreements regarding image rights. Following the backlash, the videos were removed, and discussions began on establishing stricter guidelines for AI content in sports partnerships, underscoring regulatory gaps in managing synthetic media.
