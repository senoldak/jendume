Developed by the Google Deepmind team, AlphaProof Nexus has achieved a milestone in mathematical reasoning by cracking some of the most difficult open problems in the field. According to a study published in the journal Science, the system successfully solved nine of Erdős's long-standing problems and proved 44 conjectures from the OEIS database, all while undergoing traditional peer review.

The system is built around the Gemini 3.1 Pro model, which drafts proofs in a formal mathematical language called Lean. By continuously checking its own work and using error messages to guide subsequent attempts, the AI ensures a high degree of logical rigor and accuracy without relying solely on specialized tools.

This achievement highlights a crucial turning point in how artificial intelligence contributes to pure science. Rather than just processing existing data, AI is now actively participating in expanding the boundaries of human knowledge, paving the way for future discoveries across all scientific disciplines.
