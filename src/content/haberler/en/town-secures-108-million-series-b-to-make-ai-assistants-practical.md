San Francisco-based AI startup Town has announced a $108 million Series B funding round, co-led by Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures, bringing the company's valuation to $1 billion. Founded in late 2024 by Jean-Denis Greze and Tony Vincent, the company aims to bridge the gap between advanced AI capabilities and the practical needs of everyday business owners.

While major language models are widely available, the startup notes that ordinary people often struggle with knowing what prompts to enter into a blank chat box. Rather than serving merely as a search engine replacement, Town focuses on agentic workflows that perform actual tasks for small businesses, such as automating email drafts, managing customer information, and handling routine operational friction.

The company points to real-world use cases where small operations, such as vacation equipment rental firms, successfully reduce administrative tasks from twenty-five minutes down to five minutes. By automating repetitive emails and CRM lookups, these tools allow smaller enterprises to save valuable time without needing dedicated software development teams.

Operating in a rapidly moving AI landscape where competition is fierce, Town targets business customers with a primary subscription plan priced at $50 a month. The company balances consumer-friendly use cases with enterprise-grade utility, prioritizing privacy and practical workflow integrations to carve out its niche against larger tech platforms.
