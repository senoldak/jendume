## Technology

Meta has acquired Conductor, an AI coding startup that enables users to manage software projects by deploying multiple agents into parallel workspaces. This acquisition aims to enhance the company's software development capabilities as it continues to catch up in the AI services sector. While Conductor's cloud service is scheduled to shut down on November 20, the core features will remain operational until early next year. This move follows the launch of the Muse Code agent in August and the success of Muse agents, which have surpassed 10 million users.

OpenAI has launched its Images 2.6 model, introducing the ability for ChatGPT users to generate animated GIFs from text prompts or uploaded photos. Users can now adjust animation speeds and edit individual frames, while developers gain access to new "Flare" and "Sunburst" API models for speed and quality. Following the shutdown of the Sora video app in March, the company continues to iterate on its image tools, reporting that users are now generating over four billion images per week across its platforms.

## Economy

Google plans to invest 15 billion dollars in Malaysia throughout 2027 and 2028 to expand its data center and cloud operations. The project includes building new campuses in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan, while also funding local water supplies, power generation, and training initiatives. Specifically, the company is funding a 160 million dollar water treatment plant to supply 65 million liters of water daily. This massive infrastructure development is expected to support over 37,000 local jobs annually and contribute approximately 1.1 billion dollars to the Malaysian economy each year.
