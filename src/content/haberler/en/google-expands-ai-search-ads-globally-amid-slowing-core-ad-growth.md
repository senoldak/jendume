Google is introducing promotional placements within its artificial intelligence-driven search tools to international regions, expecting generative algorithms to unlock alternative revenue channels while core advertising expansion slows down. The announcement accompanied Alphabet’s quarterly earnings report, which revealed $127.6 billion in revenue, marking a 25% increase year over year. However, search advertising expansion continued to decelerate, with Google Search revenue growing 12% year over year, down from 17% in the second quarter and 19% in the first quarter.

This deceleration places added pressure on the corporation to turn its expanding artificial intelligence features into a meaningful advertising revenue source. Executives noted that high-quality promotional units within AI overviews and conversational modes are being scaled to additional users and queries globally. Early performance indicators show that user engagement metrics for sponsored product listings in these AI modes match standard organic listings. Furthermore, queries processed through conversational AI features average three times the length of conventional queries, and a significant portion of searches worldwide incorporate voice, visual inputs, or uploaded files.

During the same reporting period, the company's core services division brought in $96.3 billion, representing an 11% annual increase, while cloud infrastructure earnings surged 103% to $30.7 billion. These financial results underscore a highly active operational period for the organization, highlighted by the rollout of the Gemini 4 Argon architecture, expansion of the conversational application to one billion monthly active users, and the debut of the Pixel 11 hardware lineup.
