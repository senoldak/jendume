Amazon has introduced a new approach to streamline supply chain management by bridging the gap between fragmented enterprise data and decision-makers. Traditionally, managers spend significant time switching across multiple systems like enterprise resource planning tools, monitoring software, procurement logs, and email to track disruptions and inventory exceptions. The new framework integrates Amazon Quick and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore to unify these processes into a single conversational thread.

The system utilizes Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to expose enterprise data securely. Instead of manually stitching together reports, managers can ask plain-language questions about active disruptions, production halts, and alternative suppliers. For instance, assessing a critical production halt can instantly reveal financial exposure, affected orders, and supply duration without touching legacy ERP interfaces.

The underlying architecture separates the intelligence layer from the interface layer. Amazon Bedrock AgentCore provides serverless hosting, secure gateways, identity management, and deterministic authorization policies to keep automated actions safely within bounds. Meanwhile, the AWS Agent Registry acts as a governed discovery layer for standardized tool connectors.

Crucially, the system maintains human oversight. Every automated recommendation or emergency quote flows through a named human reviewer before execution, ensuring that AI serves as a powerful operational assistant rather than an autonomous decision-maker.
