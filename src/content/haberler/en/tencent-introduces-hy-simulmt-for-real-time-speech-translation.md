Tencent has introduced Hy-SimulMT, a new artificial intelligence model capable of bidirectional, real-time speech translation between Chinese and English. The system can begin translating before the speaker even finishes their sentence, successfully balancing speed and accuracy. According to the results shared by Tencent, the model achieved even fewer errors during translation than human translators.

The core significance of the system lies in its ability to continuously analyze incoming audio streams and generate translations on the fly. Achieving a latency result of approximately 3 seconds in evaluations, the model recorded the lowest latency among the compared systems. Furthermore, it outperformed all evaluated open-source models and four out of five commercial APIs in GEMBA translation quality scores.

Hy-SimulMT is not limited to text translation alone, as it also features real-time voice cloning support. While translating speech into the target language, the system preserves the characteristic features of the original voice, making it easier to distinguish speakers in multi-participant meetings and interviews. This feature enhances fluency and strengthens the user experience in multilingual communications.

Tencent has released the 30-billion-parameter Hy-SimulMT-30B-A3B model and its various editions as open-source via GitHub. While inference and training tools are provided for local deployment, the Hy Translate application has also been launched on Android and iOS for direct use. Currently supporting only Chinese and English, the model is expected to expand to additional languages in the future.
