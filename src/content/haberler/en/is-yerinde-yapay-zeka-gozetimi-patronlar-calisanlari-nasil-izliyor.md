Employee monitoring has evolved from traditional output targets to digital tracking tools. With the integration of artificial intelligence, these systems have gained unprecedented capabilities, processing massive volumes of data to provide minute-by-minute accounts of worker activity.

Certain industries are rapidly adopting these monitoring approaches to measure productivity closely. However, experts note that such intense oversight can ultimately backfire, increasing stress and potentially reducing overall morale among workers.

As organizations navigate this digital transformation, the boundaries of ethical surveillance remain a pressing concern. Balancing accountability with employee well-being is one of the defining challenges of the modern workplace.
