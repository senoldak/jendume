Artificial intelligence is reshaping the enterprise software market as Dreamteam announced a $53 million funding round to scale its AI-native customer relationship management (CRM) platform. The investment was led by Accel, Together Fund, and Premji Invest. The company was founded by former Freshworks executives Anand Venkatraman, Ramesh Parthasarathy, and Senthil Kanthaswamy.

Unlike legacy systems such as Salesforce or HubSpot, which rely heavily on sales representatives manually entering data, Dreamteam automatically extracts signals and context from emails, calls, and meetings. At the core of the platform is the "Company Brain," a living memory architecture that unifies customer interactions into a shared organizational record.

The platform deploys four specialized AI coworkers designed to handle specific tasks. These autonomous agents take over both operational workloads like meeting prep and account research, as well as strategic processes including win-loss analysis and revenue forecasting. Additionally, its "Magic Apps" feature enables users to build custom dashboards and tools using natural language without writing code.

Investors highlight that the $100B+ CRM market is ripe for disruption as software transitions from static systems of record to proactive systems of intelligence. Currently used by hundreds of companies across healthcare, manufacturing, media, and consulting, Dreamteam plans to use the capital to expand its AI capabilities across the entire customer lifecycle.
