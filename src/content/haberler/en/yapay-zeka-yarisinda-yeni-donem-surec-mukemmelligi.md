In 's fast-evolving technological landscape, competition among companies goes far beyond simply accessing advanced AI models. With open-source tools widely available, the defining factor for success has become how businesses manage their operational processes and feed contextual knowledge into AI systems.

Organizations are now mapping their workflows using digital twins, enabling autonomous AI agents to operate safely and accurately. Global giants like Siemens and Legal & General are digitizing their supply chains and financial processes end-to-end to maximize the impact of automation.

Companies lacking process discipline struggle to extract real value from AI, while those with strong integration achieve cost efficiency and sustainable competitive advantages. Ultimately, leaders must view AI not merely as a tech investment, but as a core strategic imperative.
