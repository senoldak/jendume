Interactive chatbot platform Character.AI has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection just five years after its inception. The startup, which allows users to create and converse with custom AI characters, faced a series of regulatory hurdles, lawsuits regarding platform safety, and difficulties in scaling its business model sustainably.

According to court filings, the company reported assets and liabilities between $50 million and $100 million. Amidst the bankruptcy filing, Character.AI has agreed to a court-supervised sale process, establishing a preliminary $20 million deal with Disney as part of the proceedings.

The company stated that the platform will continue to operate while the sale process is underway, expected to conclude in late November or early December. However, leadership noted that if a definitive buyer is not secured through the auction process, the platform may be wound down entirely.
