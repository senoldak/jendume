Itaú, one of Brazil's major financial institutions, has rolled out new artificial intelligence solutions for both its customer base and internal workforce as part of its ongoing digital strategy. Recognizing that millions of people seek better support for their financial decisions, the bank designed a conversational interface aimed at making technology simple, direct, and seamlessly integrated into everyday life.

The newly introduced ia.i platform, which reached approximately 300,000 customers in its initial phase, operates via text or voice to handle checking accounts, credit, cards, and insurance services within a single environment while connecting users to human specialists when needed. To power this broad range of services, the bank utilized a combination of models rather than relying on a single option. OpenAI models were integrated to handle everything from cost-sensitive daily tasks to complex scenarios requiring advanced reasoning and contextual grounding.

Behind these consumer-facing features lies an architectural framework known as IARA, a governance and orchestration platform built to manage multiple AI models securely. The platform enforces token-cost control, response accuracy, and responsible AI policies across the organization. For instance, the bank's legal department utilizes a solution running on this infrastructure that processes a repository of over 90 million documents and executes more than 2 million tasks monthly, achieving efficiency gains between 20 and 80 percent across various process stages.

The bank's AI-first approach extends internally to assist managers and customer service teams with tasks such as meeting preparation, document summarization, and workflow planning. Itaú expects these implementations to significantly increase time dedicated to customer relationships, accelerate service speeds, and improve overall operational effectiveness.
