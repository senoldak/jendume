ByteDance has taken a significant step in the generative AI landscape by launching its new model, Seedream 5.5. One of the most persistent challenges in AI-driven image generation has been the degradation of quality and loss of visual integrity during successive editing rounds. The new model aims to solve this issue with its "pixel-level preservation" technology. Users can edit an image up to 100 times consecutively, and the model ensures that the parts of the image not intended for change remain stable, preventing color shifts or loss of detail.

This development is particularly critical for professional designers, game developers, and animation studios. In traditional generative workflows, changing an item like a character's clothing or an accessory often forced the model to reprocess the entire image, leading to unintended alterations. Seedream 5.5 simplifies this complex process by allowing the image to be separated into up to 100 independent layers. Designers can now edit characters or objects within a scene independently and reuse them across different projects.

Another notable feature of the model is its native RGBA support. This allows for the direct generation of transparent or semi-transparent images without the need for post-processing to remove backgrounds. Furthermore, significant improvements have been made to text generation capabilities. The issue of character distortion or misplacement in text-heavy images, such as posters or product labels, has been minimized. According to the company's data, the heavy text distortion rate has been reduced to 0.4%.

Seedream 5.5 is available through ByteDance's cloud service, Volcengine Ark. While the standard cost is set at $0.02 per image, this can drop to as low as 1 cent in promotional packages. When compared to its predecessor, Seedream 5.0 Pro, the model shows more than double the usability in fields like cinema and office productivity. It also outperforms competitors like GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2.1 in realism and text structure tests. This technology promises to make visual production workflows more efficient and aligned with professional standards.
