Samsung, in collaboration with Google and Qualcomm, has officially opened pre-orders for its new Intelligent Eyewear series, marking a significant step in the era of wearable artificial intelligence. First teased in July, these devices aim to blend traditional eyewear aesthetics with advanced AI capabilities, reducing screen dependency and making digital interactions feel more natural. The product is currently available for pre-order in South Korea and the United States.

At the heart of the device lies Google’s Gemini AI model, powered by the Android XR platform. The glasses utilize integrated cameras to analyze the user's surroundings in real-time. Features such as live translation, voice-guided navigation, and instant note-taking are all accessible hands-free. The hardware includes a 12 MP ultra-wide-angle camera and a four-microphone array with spatial audio support, allowing the device to enhance the user's perception of the world through a digital layer.

Under the hood, the device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 chipset, specifically designed for smart glasses. Supported by 2 GB of RAM and 64 GB of internal storage, the system maintains a lightweight profile, weighing between 40 and 44 grams, ensuring comfort comparable to standard eyewear. Battery performance is optimized for daily use, offering up to 9 hours of continuous operation on a single charge, with a charging case that extends the total battery life to over 72 hours.

This development signals that AI is becoming a standard feature in physical accessories, not just software. Partnerships with fashion brands like Warby Parker and Gentle Monster demonstrate how technology can seamlessly integrate with personal style. Set for general release on December 10, the glasses are compatible with Android 17-updated phones and iOS devices, aiming to reach a broad user base. Starting at $395, these glasses shift our relationship with AI from screens to vision.
