AI-driven biotechnology startup Isomorphic Labs has secured 5 billion dollars in a Series C funding round. The investment was co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with participation from major investors including Alphabet, Andreessen Horowitz, Thrive Capital, GV, and Temasek. With this significant capital injection, the company's total funding has reached 7.7 billion dollars.

Founded in 2021 by Demis Hassabis, Isomorphic Labs operates as a biotechnology company that leverages artificial intelligence to develop novel therapeutics. Operating under the Alphabet umbrella, the company builds upon DeepMind's AlphaFold research to revolutionize the drug discovery timeline. The startup utilizes its proprietary AI-powered drug design engine, known as IsoDDE, to model and analyze novel drug molecules in a digital environment. By simulating these interactions, the company can identify potential drug candidates for various diseases more efficiently than traditional methods.

The company announced that the new funding will be used to further develop the IsoDDE engine, expand its research into new biological domains, and build the operational infrastructure needed to advance its first drug candidates into clinical trials. Alongside developing its own proprietary treatments targeting cancer and immunological diseases, Isomorphic Labs maintains strategic partnerships with major pharmaceutical companies including Novartis, Eli Lilly, and Johnson & Johnson.

By bridging advanced artificial intelligence with practical drug development, Isomorphic Labs is preparing to transition its pipeline into clinical trials. Having previously raised 2.1 billion dollars, the startup's rapid financial growth underscores the intensifying global race in AI-driven biotechnology and healthcare innovation. The company aims to accelerate the drug development process and discover new treatments, marking a significant shift in how modern medicine approaches complex diseases.
