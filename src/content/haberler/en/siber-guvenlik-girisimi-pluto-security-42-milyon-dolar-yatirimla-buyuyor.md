The rapid integration of AI tools into daily workflows by company employees is ushering in a new era for corporate security teams. Unvetted AI extensions and employee-built applications can inadvertently expose sensitive company data to significant security risks.

To address these growing concerns, Pluto Security has announced that it has raised a total of $42 million in funding. The startup has quickly gained traction since launching its commercial operations, securing over thirty enterprise customers.

The company's technology monitors how employees use AI tools and provides real-time risk analysis. The system can actively intervene if autonomous agents attempt to exceed their intended permissions, helping organizations maintain secure environments without hindering productivity.
