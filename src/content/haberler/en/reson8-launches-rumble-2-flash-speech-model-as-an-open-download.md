Amsterdam-based speech recognition startup Reson8 has published its new transcription model, named Rumble-2-Flash, as a free download accompanied by open weights. Engineered specifically for rapid transcription of prerecorded audio files, the five hundred million-parameter architecture provides native support for over forty distinct languages. The enterprise is directing this offering toward software developers and academic researchers seeking robust speech recognition tailored for linguistic groups outside of the English-speaking majority.

Company leadership observed that prevailing speech models rely predominantly on English training data, causing accuracy metrics to drop significantly when processing specialized terminology in other languages. Reson8 applications currently operate in production environments powering automated meeting note takers, artificial intelligence receptionists, and customer support voice agents. Furthermore, the organization emphasizes adherence to strict data privacy standards and regulatory frameworks during audio processing operations.

Positioned among the most compact architectures listed on the Hugging Face Open ASR Leaderboard, Rumble-2-Flash achieved an average word error rate of 7.09 percent across seven different languages including German, French, and Dutch during internal evaluations. The average error rate for English transactions sits near five percent. On separate benchmark tests featuring ten lower-resource languages such as Maltese and Basque, the system recorded a mean word error rate of 17.6 percent.

Distributed freely under an Apache 2.0 software license, the transcription tool remains available via the corporate application programming interface starting at 0.01 euros per audio hour. The emerging firm previously secured five million euros in pre-seed financial backing to accelerate its developmental roadmap.
