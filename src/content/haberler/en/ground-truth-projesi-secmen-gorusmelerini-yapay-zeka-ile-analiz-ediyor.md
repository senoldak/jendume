The Ground Truth project, aimed at improving political engagement and rebuilding trust among voters in the United States, is reshaping grassroots campaigning with artificial intelligence. Volunteers across multiple states record voice recaps of open-ended conversations with everyday citizens, moving beyond traditional door-knocking methods that only ask for votes. Instead of conventional questionnaires, this approach focuses on deep listening and records lengthy, meandering discussions that are stored in a central database. Large language models process these extensive voice notes, allowing organizers to query records in plain English and extract actionable insights for political campaigns. This technological shift enables candidates to uncover hyperlocal community concerns that traditional campaign methods often miss. Although political consultants increasingly rely on AI tools in their weekly routines, public skepticism regarding artificial intelligence, budget shortfalls, and technical glitches remain hurdles. Projects like Ground Truth serve as a foundational test for how artificial intelligence can transform political communication and bridge the gap between voters and political institutions. Furthermore, expanding technological infrastructure allows organizations to share doorstep data securely with broader campaigns and outside groups, enhancing overall outreach strategies. Volunteers on the ground utilize specialized applications to capture detailed feedback, ensuring that even hesitant voters have a voice in the electoral process. Ultimately, integrating advanced language models into grassroots campaigning redefines how political organizations listen to the public and address systemic trust issues across the country.
