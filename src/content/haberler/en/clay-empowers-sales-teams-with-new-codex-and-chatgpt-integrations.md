Clay operates as a self-learning revenue engine designed to help sales teams understand prospect data and develop winning pitches. With new ChatGPT and Codex integrations, the platform empowers users to research deals, build company and people lists, and turn that information into actionable go-to-market plays. These tools bring rich customer context directly into the environments where representatives are already working.

The platform utilizes a range of OpenAI models to match intelligence and cost to specific tasks. Clay employs GPT-6 Sol as its primary model, alongside GPT-6 Astra for more complex reasoning and GPT-6 Luna for cost-sensitive operations. This combination supports background research, copywriting, and interactive agents. Furthermore, the Responses API allows Clay to access different levels of capability through a single unified interface.

The Codex integration lets builders create and run go-to-market workflows while granting sales reps access to vital account research data. Meanwhile, the ChatGPT plugin brings the exact same customer intelligence into another familiar tool, allowing representatives to learn more about prospects without leaving their current workflow. Claygent relies on these underlying models for research, qualification, enrichment, and signal detection.

Beyond sales operations, GPT-6 Astra aids in software development by writing code, building and running tests, and recording features automatically. This closes the engineering loop and simplifies the building process significantly. Looking forward, Clay aims to handle end-to-end go-to-market workflows using self-improving agents that learn continuously from their results.

Ultimately, Clay seeks to bring the same transformative shift experienced in software engineering over to go-to-market strategies, empowering builders and sales professionals to optimize their daily operations through advanced artificial intelligence.
