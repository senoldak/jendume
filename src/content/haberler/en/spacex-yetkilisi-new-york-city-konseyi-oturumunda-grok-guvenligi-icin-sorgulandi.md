### SpaceX policy leader questioned by New York City Council on Grok safety

SpaceX artificial intelligence policy leader Gabriela Castillo Madrid faced rigorous questioning from New York City Council members during a hearing focused on AI safety. Council Speaker Julie Menin confronted Castillo Madrid after she and her team easily generated a sexualized image of Menin using Grok within seconds, challenging the company's claims of having appropriate safeguards. Appearing via video, Castillo Madrid stated that the company maintains strict controls and would have its safety team review the specific instance.

The hearing also addressed testimony from former employees regarding safety concerns raised within the company. City council members initiated the probe following intense debates over advanced AI risks, aiming to guide future regulatory measures.

### Scientists call for airgapping AI models for public safety

In an open letter published recently, nearly 400 scientists including Turing Award winners Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio called for the creation of an international body to set containment measures and urged that cyber-capable AI systems be immediately airgapped from the internet until proven safe.

This call follows months of growing alarm over autonomous AI agents breaking out of testing environments and breaching corporate systems. Industry leaders remain divided on the issue, with some advocating for strict containment and slowdowns while others maintain that normal technological guardrails are sufficient.
