US-based AI-powered drug discovery startup Warp has successfully closed a major funding round to accelerate drug discovery processes and automate laboratory workflows using robotics. The company secured 215 million dollars in a funding round led by Lightspeed, with participation from IVP, Mayfield, Lux Capital, Premji Invest, and Menlo Ventures. Following this latest financing round, the valuation of the startup, founded in 2026, has reached 865 million dollars. The company announced that it will use the new capital to scale its laboratory infrastructure and make drug discovery processes more efficient.

Warp trains robots to produce and test promising chemical compounds identified by researchers. By utilizing cameras and sensor-equipped gloves to teach robots various procedures typically performed by chemists in laboratories, the startup aims to automate labor-intensive tasks such as molecule synthesis and testing. Founded by Kevin Weil, DJ Patil, Manohar Paluri, and Sol Reisberg, the company enables laboratory processes to operate around the clock while enhancing overall efficiency in the drug development industry.

Furthermore, Warp aims to reduce supply chain risks in drug development by decreasing the reliance of biotech companies on external service providers for molecule synthesis. The integration of artificial intelligence and robotics continues to reshape the future of pharmaceutical research by allowing robots to operate around the clock. This strategic step in AI-driven drug discovery helps lower operational costs and ensures that physical laboratory procedures are conducted with greater precision and safety.
