Alibaba has taken a significant step in the AI landscape by introducing its most advanced model to date, Qwen4-Max. Built on the Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture, this new model promises a major leap in both performance and cost-efficiency compared to previous generations. Boasting 2.4 trillion parameters, Qwen4-Max optimizes resource usage by activating only 71 billion parameters per token, significantly reducing computational overhead.

The most striking aspect of the model is its versatility beyond text-based tasks. Qwen4-Max can operate professional 3D design software like Blender to create digital assets suitable for 3D printing. Furthermore, it integrates with game engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine, allowing it to design game scenes, write code, and test visual effects based on natural language instructions. These capabilities aim to drastically reduce human intervention in software development and design workflows.

In the scientific domain, the model has delivered impressive results, autonomously working for 48 hours to discover a new molecule for cooling next-generation semiconductor chips. During this process on Deep Principle's Mira platform, it evaluated 2.7 million candidate molecules, resulting in a new coolant that has been experimentally validated in a laboratory setting. Additionally, in industrial applications such as production line planning, it has demonstrated the ability to reduce manual labor time by 95 percent.

From an economic perspective, Qwen4-Max reduces costs per task by more than 60 percent, making AI-powered operations more sustainable. Alibaba has opened API access to the model via QwenCloud while simultaneously announcing new safety measures that filter out offensive cyber capabilities while retaining defensive strengths. With this release, Alibaba has crossed a major milestone on its roadmap toward the upcoming Qwen5 series.
