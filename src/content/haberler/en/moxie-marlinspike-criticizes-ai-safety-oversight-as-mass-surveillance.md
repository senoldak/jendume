Moxie Marlinspike, the creator of Signal, discussed the state of artificial intelligence safety practices during an interview, arguing that current misuse monitoring methods are turning into an unprecedented mass surveillance system. Marlinspike pointed out that monitoring user interactions for bad behavior creates a machinery of surveillance larger than any seen before in history. He noted that chatbots encourage a false sense of privacy, leading users to share personal confessions that eventually end up in data lakes used for training and analysis.

As an alternative, Marlinspike suggested shifting the monitoring focus toward the AI models themselves rather than the human users. Labs could deploy narrow models to check general models for problematic tendencies. He also highlighted his private chat service, Confer, where messages remain inaccessible to anyone other than the user, emphasizing that major chatbots do not operate private services.

Addressing broader technological risks, Marlinspike discussed the potential impact of AI on cryptography and software security. While acknowledging uncertainties surrounding public-key encryption, he estimated it could take about a year before AI becomes as proficient at patching software vulnerabilities as it is at finding them. He also detailed how Signal protects user data and implements measures to block AI agents from reading private chats.
