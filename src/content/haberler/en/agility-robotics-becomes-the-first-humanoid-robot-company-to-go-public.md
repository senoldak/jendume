Agility Robotics has officially become the first humanoid robot company to list on the Nasdaq stock exchange, marking a significant milestone for the robotics industry. Based in Oregon, the company is known for deploying its "Digit" humanoid robots in commercial warehouse and logistics settings. Through this public listing, the company raised $465 million in new capital. This move serves as a major test of market confidence, signaling that humanoid robots are transitioning from experimental research projects to scalable business models.

Founded in 2015 by researchers from Oregon State University, the company has focused on automating repetitive, physically demanding tasks in industrial environments. Currently, Digit robots are being used by nine different customers in various logistics facilities. The company is now preparing to launch its next-generation model, Digit 5, which is designed to operate safely alongside humans. Agility has already secured $300 million in planned contracts from 30 customers for this new model, which is central to its future growth strategy.

Despite the excitement, the company faces financial hurdles. It reported a loss of $362 million in the first six months of 2026, highlighting the capital-intensive nature of scaling robotics production. The company plans to use the newly raised funds to expand operations and finalize the rollout of Digit 5. The long-term success of the company will depend on the robot's performance metrics, including uptime, task accuracy, and overall reliability. By offering robots as a service, Agility aims to provide a cost-effective alternative to human labor, with plans to demonstrate the new units at CES 2027 and make them widely available by the end of that year.
