Silicon Valley startup Outlit is developing AI-powered surveillance systems aimed at helping companies manage customer relations and prevent churn. Founders Josh Earle and Leo Paz argue that every company will soon assign a dedicated AI agent to watch every customer's move. The Y Combinator-backed venture has not yet been publicly released.

The system gathers real-time information by trawling internal records such as call logs and sales data, alongside external signals like social media posts and corporate websites. This data is compiled into detailed customer profiles and actionable sales guidance. The startup asserts that human workers alone cannot manually process the sheer volume of valuable data available in 's digital landscape.

Outlit primarily focuses on business-to-business software companies and states that it does not monitor individual everyday consumers. The founders believe that context-absorbing agents will ultimately understand and maintain human business relationships better than humans can. However, the automated trawling of public profiles also sweeps in personal life events, sparking valid privacy concerns regarding consent and surveillance.

While Outlit maintains that its data collection practices are entirely legal and secure, the normalization of continuous AI monitoring marks a profound shift in corporate automation. Operating around the clock without the need for sleep, these agents represent a new frontier in how businesses interact with and analyze their clients.
