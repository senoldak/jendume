Apple CEO John Ternus has provided insights into the company's artificial intelligence strategy. Ternus, who took office at the beginning of September, stated that AI is not an end in itself and should only be used as a tool where it provides tangible benefits. Expressing satisfaction with the company's current position in the field of AI, Ternus emphasized that Apple will not add AI to its products just because it is popular or for the sake of appearances.

This approach reflects the product development philosophy that Apple has maintained for many years. According to Ternus, the success of a technology is measured not by its name or the power of the model behind it, but by the experience it provides in the user's daily life. Arguing that users should not have to question whether a feature is powered by AI or traditional software, Apple's management states that the primary priority is functionality. This strategy demonstrates the company's determination to protect its ecosystem and prioritize user experience above all else.

The company has faced criticism in recent years for falling behind in the field of AI. At the center of these criticisms was the development process of a more powerful version of Siri. This version was released during the summer months. Ternus stated that he is aware of these criticisms but that the company has not changed its course, expressing their belief in the accuracy of their current strategy.

Addressing views that AI agents could fundamentally change the app ecosystem in the future, Ternus acknowledges that this technology can make life easier. However, he argues that the product development process is a deeply human and personal endeavor beyond machines. Apple continues to position AI not as a marketing element, but as an assistant that simplifies the user's life. Ternus's statements clearly demonstrate that despite rapid changes in the technology world, the company will not deviate from its unique path and will continue to place user experience above everything else.
