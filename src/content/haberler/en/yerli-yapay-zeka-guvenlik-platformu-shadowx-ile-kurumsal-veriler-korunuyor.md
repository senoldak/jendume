The rapid integration of generative AI tools into corporate workflows has created new security and data privacy challenges for organizations. Founded in 2024, Turkish startup ShadowX has developed a specialized platform designed to help companies use AI tools within the boundaries of their internal security policies.

The platform's FilterX component detects and masks sensitive information in texts, files, and images in real time before they are shared with AI models. Additionally, the Model Defense module protects systems against external threats such as prompt injection and jailbreak attacks, safeguarding corporate environments from malicious use.

Going beyond traditional chatbots, ShadowX also focuses on securing agentic AI systems. It allows organizations to monitor and policy-limit actions taken by autonomous agents, such as accessing files or executing API calls. Operating in sectors like banking, public administration, and technology, the startup offers on-premise deployment options, enabling sensitive data to be processed securely without relying on third-party cloud services.
