NVIDIA has introduced its new series of open-weight models specifically created for continuously active artificial intelligence agents and edge computing tasks. At the core of this rollout is the massive Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model, featuring 120 billion parameters and built to support enterprise intelligence systems. Alongside this large enterprise model, the company also unveiled two lightweight Bolt models.

The Super VL model delivers high accuracy in essential duties such as following instructions and utilizing tools, despite operating with fewer parameters than previous generations. When fine-tuned for specialized tasks like document review, financial accounting, scientific research, and medical insurance processes, it produces rapid results. The ecosystem supporting these capabilities includes prominent organizations such as Skydance, EY, Ramp, Crowdstrike, Perplexity, and Salesforce.

Meanwhile, the Bolt models are engineered to operate locally on next-generation AI computers and workstations rather than relying on centralized data centers. Made available through partnerships with companies like Microsoft and Dell, these systems provide privacy for users who prefer not to send their personal data to cloud services.

The Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB model features 74 billion parameters, handling complex workflows such as multi-step reasoning and web browsing. The smallest member of the family, Bolt E4B, is optimized for chat-based agent applications. While the Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B models are currently accessible, the Bolt E4B model is scheduled for release later in the month.
