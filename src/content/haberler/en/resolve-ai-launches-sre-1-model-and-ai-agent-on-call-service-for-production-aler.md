Resolve AI has launched SRE-1, a new model designed to uncover the root causes of production alerts, and Primary On-call, a service that deploys AI agents to handle engineering pager duties. The company noted that while artificial intelligence has transformed software development, it has largely left software operations untouched. SRE-1 was built to address this gap by analyzing private incident data and telemetry. The 27 billion-parameter model was trained by a 16-person research team using up to 512 graphics processing units over a period of 7.4 days, leveraging Snowflake infrastructure. Testing showed significant improvements in investigation success rates, with the model matching frontier capabilities at lower costs.

The Primary On-call service investigates telemetry, infrastructure, and code, automatically resolving or mitigating alerts where permitted. Early users, such as Salesforce, reported that the AI agents successfully closed a large portion of paging alerts without waking human engineers. The system links related alerts and investigations into a shared view, allowing agents to check fixes against production baselines until full recovery is achieved.

Resolve AI is backed by significant venture capital, having raised over $190 million to date, including a Series A extension that valued the company at $1.5 billion with backing from DST Global and Salesforce Ventures. Notable enterprise customers include Coinbase, DoorDash, Figma, Nasdaq, and SAP. While Primary On-call is now generally available, access to SRE-1 remains restricted to the company's dedicated platform, marking a major step toward autonomous software operations.
