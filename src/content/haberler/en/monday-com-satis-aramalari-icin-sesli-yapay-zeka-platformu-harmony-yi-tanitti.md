Monday.com has officially launched Harmony, a voice AI platform that makes sales calls, qualifies leads, and hands the most promising ones over to human representatives. Aimed at mid-market and large enterprises across the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, and Singapore, the platform is powered by voice technology from OneAI, which Monday.com acquired in May for $14.1 million in cash.

Rather than relying on a single large language model, Harmony utilizes a collection of smaller, task-specific models trained on real customer interactions. These models handle specific tasks such as lead qualification or handling objections. Operating largely on Harmony's own servers, the system achieves response times of about 200 milliseconds. Vitali Margolin, who leads Harmony, stated that these specialized models create conversations and response times that feel human.

The platform integrates with Twilio for telephony to dial numbers, route incoming calls, and record consent. It also automatically logs call notes into CRM systems like Salesforce and HubSpot. According to the company's website, agents explicitly inform callers that they are artificial intelligence. While Monday.com claims Harmony reaches up to 70% of the people it calls, specific pricing details have not been published.

Early adopters are already seeing results. UK car finance broker Bright Car Finance reported closing 9% more deals in less than a year using the platform. The launch aligns with Monday.com's broader strategic pivot toward artificial intelligence, which included a $200 million fund for workplace AI startups established in June and workforce reductions announced in July.

Harmony enters a competitive and fast-growing market for voice AI solutions, where competing startups have secured significant funding this year. By emphasizing low latency and task-specific models, Monday.com aims to capture a larger share of the automated sales market.
