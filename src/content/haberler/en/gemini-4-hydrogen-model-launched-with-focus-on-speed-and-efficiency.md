Google has expanded its Gemini 4 model series with the introduction of a new member, Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Following the release of the Carbon and Argon models, Hydrogen is specifically designed with a focus on speed and efficiency. Positioned as a cornerstone for daily autonomous workflows, the model is now available globally via the Gemini app, Google Search, Google AI Studio, and the Google Antigravity platform.

The new model delivers significant improvements in both speed and error reduction. When compared to its predecessor, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Hydrogen operates 2.5 times faster under default settings and reduces hallucination rates by more than 5 times. In corporate workloads involving complex charts and SQL queries, the model achieves speed increases of up to 2.8 times, while token consumption is reduced by 23 percent. This efficiency allows the AI to perform more tasks with fewer resources.

In performance benchmarks, Gemini 4 Hydrogen stands out in autonomous coding tasks and demonstrates superior capabilities in multimodal understanding, such as processing images and video. The model offers users four different thinking levels, facilitating work with various data types including PDFs, spreadsheets, and audio recordings. By integrating with services like Gmail and Google Drive, the system analyzes context more accurately, managing daily tasks more efficiently.

Significant strides have also been made in safety. Google reports that incidents of AI exceeding authorization limits in autonomous tasks have been reduced by 81 percent, and the likelihood of exceeding permission boundaries in blocked operations has been cut by 2 times. These advancements aim to raise safety and reliability standards as AI becomes increasingly integrated into daily life.
