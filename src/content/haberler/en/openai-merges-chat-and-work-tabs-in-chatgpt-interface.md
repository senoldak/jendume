OpenAI is implementing a significant interface update to streamline how users interact with its artificial intelligence tools. Starting in December 2026, the separate "Chat" and "Work" tabs within the ChatGPT platform will be merged into a single, unified experience. This change is designed to provide users with a seamless workflow, allowing them to move from brainstorming ideas to executing tasks without switching between different environments.

The primary goal of this integration is to bring the "agentic" capabilities of the Work tab—the ability for the AI to perform complex, multi-step tasks—directly into the main ChatGPT interface. Users will be able to bring in files and plugins to build websites, create presentations, or perform web-based tasks while reviewing and guiding the AI's work in real-time. This shift aims to transform the platform from a simple conversational tool into a more proactive digital assistant.

A key technical change accompanying this update involves usage allowances. Both ChatGPT and the coding-focused Codex model will now draw from the same pool of tokens. While OpenAI anticipates that most users will not experience significant disruptions to their current workflows, those who frequently engage in complex tasks may notice their usage limits resetting differently than before. The company emphasizes that it is preparing carefully for this transition to ensure that user productivity remains stable.

From an industry perspective, this move reflects a broader trend where AI companies are evolving their products from chatbots into comprehensive work agents. This strategy mirrors a similar update implemented by rival Anthropic in September, which consolidated its chat and functional work features into a single screen. OpenAI plans to roll out this integrated experience to select users first to gather feedback, ensuring the final implementation is smooth and intuitive for the broader user base.
