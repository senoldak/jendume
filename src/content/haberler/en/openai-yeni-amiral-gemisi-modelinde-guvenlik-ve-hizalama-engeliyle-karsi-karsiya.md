OpenAI is working intensively on its next flagship model, utilizing a looped transformer technique similar to its Astra system. This approach significantly boosts math and coding performance while reducing costs by looping tokens through the same transformer layers multiple times before generating output.

However, this architecture complicates safety monitoring. By shifting some of the model's reasoning into internal numerical representations that human reviewers cannot easily inspect, chain-of-thought monitoring becomes harder. To address these concerns, OpenAI has dedicated a substantial portion of its total compute capacity—roughly ten percent—toward safety and alignment research.

Additionally, under executive guidelines, advanced AI systems must undergo rigorous safety evaluations and provide government access prior to public deployment. As company leadership and researchers point out, maintaining readable reasoning while advancing capabilities remains one of the industry's most pressing challenges.
