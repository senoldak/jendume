US House committee chairs have sent a letter to Nvidia founder and CEO Jensen Huang, asking him to explain reported spy links. The letter was sent by John Moolenaar, Brian Mast, and Rick Crawford, who lead the China, Foreign Affairs, and Intelligence committees, giving Huang a deadline to respond or offer an alternative date.

The inquiry follows reports that US intelligence agencies had monitored Alice Huang over her ties to China, with officials fearing she could learn about Nvidia's plans. Reports noted that officials did not believe Jensen Huang knew about the situation, and the two are not related. Neither has been charged with any wrongdoing.

Meanwhile, Singapore-based firm Megaspeed stated it hired outside lawyers to look into claims regarding restricted Nvidia chips. US officials have also examined whether the firm helped route restricted technology to China. Nvidia spokesman John Rizzo stated they are confident the government would inform them if anyone posed a threat to American industry.

It remains unclear whether Huang will attend, as Nvidia declined to provide details. Huang has previously turned down congressional requests, including declining an invitation from Senator Elizabeth Warren regarding sales to China. The developments underscore the growing regulatory and geopolitical focus on the hardware suppliers driving the artificial intelligence boom.
