OpenAI has released GPT-6.1 Terra, a new AI model built to handle routine computer work, visual reasoning, and automated tasks. Replacing the previous version, Terra aims to lower the cost barrier for companies looking to automate everyday office workflows such as processing incoming invoices, filling in forms, drafting proposals, and interpreting technical drawings.

The model features significantly reduced API prices, cutting previous costs in half with input tokens priced at $1 per million and output tokens at $5 per million. Cached inputs have also dropped to $0.05 per million tokens, providing a much more economical option for developers and enterprise customers running heavy workloads.

According to evaluations, Terra delivers higher intelligence per dollar, improving performance across coding, visual reasoning, and computer control tasks while dramatically reducing error rates. On coding benchmarks, the model solves 50.3 percent of tasks at maximum reasoning effort, demonstrating a massive leap over its predecessor and placing it in a competitive tier for software development tasks.

GPT-6.1 Terra is now available for ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users, as well as in Codex and through the API. The company notes that the model meets its critical cybersecurity preparedness thresholds and includes robust safeguards against prompt injection attacks, ensuring safer deployment across corporate environments.
