Apple has introduced new artificial intelligence-based audio features developed for smartwatch users. Named Live Rewind and Siri Recap, these tools make it easier to track conversation flows and recall important details. Enhancing audio processing capabilities in wearable technologies, these systems aim to make daily life more practical for users.

The Live Rewind tool converts the final 15 seconds of conversations held through the watch into written text. Users can access this text by pressing the side button twice. This makes it easy to remember missed directions or daily menus. If no action is taken, the texts are removed from the system about 30 seconds after the screen dims. Additionally, audio warnings and animations are activated so that people nearby notice when the feature is active.

Siri Recap, a more comprehensive tool, analyzes conversations throughout the day and generates titles and summaries for each. The feature includes special safeguards to exclude sensitive financial information and identification numbers from the summaries. The system does not provide verbatim transcripts of conversations but brings together key points.

Apple states that the audio intelligence features do not store raw voice recordings and that data is processed in hardware-based secure areas. While Live Rewind runs entirely on the device, cloud infrastructure is utilized for the summarization function. To use the new tools, compatible watch and phone models must be used together. Initially available only in English, the features will not be accessible at launch in European Union countries and for users under 13 years of age.
