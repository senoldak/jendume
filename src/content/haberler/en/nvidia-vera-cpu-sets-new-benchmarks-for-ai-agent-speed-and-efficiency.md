In the evolving landscape of artificial intelligence, agents are doing far more than generating text. They are actively searching for information, querying databases, writing and testing code, and executing tasks. These processes require constant movement between GPU reasoning and CPU execution. To address the growing need for both speed and capacity in these workflows, NVIDIA has released performance results for its new Vera CPU.

In a 100-megawatt data center comparison, the Vera CPU demonstrated superior performance over traditional x86 architectures. The data shows that Vera delivers 2x faster agent speed compared to the most energy-efficient x86 CPU, while simultaneously enabling 1.6x more agents per watt than the fastest available x86 CPU. This capability allows data center operators to handle a wide range of agent workloads with a single CPU architecture, eliminating the need to mix and match different hardware components to balance speed and capacity.

Early adopters in the industry are already validating these results in production-style environments. Perplexity reported running agentic coding tasks 1.5x faster with Vera compared to traditional CPUs, while DeepInfra achieved up to 2.2x the speed of the best x86 processor for its orchestration workloads. Regarding operational efficiency, Daytona utilized the Vera architecture to initialize 1,000 agent sandboxes in a span of 10.8 seconds. Furthermore, the system maintained a throughput of 12.7 completed jobs per second while managing 2,000 concurrent sandboxes. Other companies, including ClickHouse and Modal, also reported significant scalability and throughput gains. These results highlight a critical shift in AI infrastructure, where maximizing work done per watt is becoming essential for scaling agent-based systems. Data centers are increasingly power-constrained, making the ability to maximize output per watt a primary competitive advantage for infrastructure providers. By consolidating high-speed performance and high-density capacity into a single unit, Vera simplifies the deployment of complex AI agent ecosystems.
