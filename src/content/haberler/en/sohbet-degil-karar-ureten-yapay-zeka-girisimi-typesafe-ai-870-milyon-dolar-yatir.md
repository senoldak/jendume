Artificial intelligence startup TypeSafe AI has completed an $870 million Series A funding round led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Sequoia Capital and existing investor DCVC. The investment values the company at $7.5 billion and brings Andreessen Horowitz partner Martin Casado onto its board of directors.

TypeSafe AI’s flagship model, Jev—launched on September 15, 2026—takes a different path from conventional large language models. Rather than generating conversational prose, the transformer-based system calculates the likelihood of specific outcomes to produce structured outputs the company calls “calibrated decisions.”

## Structured Outputs for Enterprise Automation

Jev is designed for software automation, classification, and verifying the outputs of other AI systems where predictability is paramount.

By focusing on machine-readable decisions rather than lengthy natural-language responses, TypeSafe AI aims to run faster and consume significantly fewer tokens than general-purpose language models.
