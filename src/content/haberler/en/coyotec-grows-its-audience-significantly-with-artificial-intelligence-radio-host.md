Los Angeles-based broadcasting station José 97.5 FM has achieved notable success with Coyotec, an artificial intelligence radio cohost featured on its Spanish-language broadcasts. After debuting with a weekend show, Coyotec quickly transitioned to cohosting the weekday morning program Al Aire y Sin Permiso alongside human broadcaster Geraldine Guzman. The program focuses on addressing community issues and immigrant rights with a culturally resonant persona.

Coyotec's distinctive voice and culturally relatable persona have resonated strongly with listeners, particularly as it fearlessly addresses immigrant rights, community issues, and daily life. The pairing's success led to Al Aire being syndicated across five new markets in California, Nevada, and Texas, expanding its reach significantly.

Data from the station showed substantial growth in listenership following the syndication, especially among Gen Z and millennial audiences. Cumulative listenership among Hispanic people and younger age brackets experienced substantial increases during the broadcast periods. The station experienced significant increases in its younger demographic brackets, proving that AI-driven personalities can successfully revitalize traditional radio formats.

Behind the scenes, Coyotec's persona is built using a combination of advanced AI tools including Claude Haiku, Eleven Labs, OpenAI, and Midjourney. With fine-tuning from human cohosts and their families to perfect cultural nuances and language delivery, Coyotec has established a unique and engaging presence on the airwaves, supported by workflow software integration.
