Meta’s Superintelligence Lab released two new frontier models on October 12, 2026—Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full—marking the company’s first direct entry into the top tier of large-scale reasoning systems. Unlike the earlier lightweight Muse Spark model built for low latency, the Wonder family targets complex workflows such as software engineering and multi-step planning.

According to the company’s technical evaluations, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full achieve benchmark performance comparable to leading frontier systems including Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra. Both models are engineered to power personal and professional AI agents.

## Pricing and Developer Access

The increased capability is reflected in the API structure: accessed through the Meta Model API, Muse Wonder is priced at $4 per 1 million input tokens and $20 per 1 million output tokens.

Meta described the release as a key step on its scaling roadmap toward personal superintelligence, giving developers stronger reasoning foundations for agentic applications.
