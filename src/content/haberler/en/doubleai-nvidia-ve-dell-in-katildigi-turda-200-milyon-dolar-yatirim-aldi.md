Tel Aviv-based artificial intelligence developer doubleAI has announced a $200 million Series A funding round. The investment was co-led by Lightspeed and Bessemer, with participation from major tech players including Nvidia and Dell Technologies. Reports indicate that the funding values the company at over $1 billion, establishing its presence in the enterprise sector.

The company has developed an AI model named Maxwell, which automatically optimizes software code and improves execution efficiency. Unlike traditional software development methods that require manual code changes, Maxwell applies optimizations directly to a program's binary code. This capability allows applications to run more efficiently while reducing hardware usage. By targeting the binary level rather than the source code, the system enables modifications while programs are actively running in production environments.

doubleAI plans to allocate a portion of the fresh capital to purchase advanced Nvidia graphics cards, bolstering its infrastructure to compete with industry giants like OpenAI and Anthropic. The funding will support ongoing research and development efforts aimed at expanding the model's commercial deployment. As major players increasingly automate research tasks, this financial backing provides doubleAI with the resources needed to scale its operations.

Maxwell has previously demonstrated its capabilities through various technical achievements, including performance benchmarks and mathematical discoveries. By targeting hardware optimization, doubleAI aims to offer enterprises a practical solution for lowering the operational costs associated with heavy AI workloads. The technology's ability to automatically enhance software efficiency represents a notable shift in how companies manage computing resources.
