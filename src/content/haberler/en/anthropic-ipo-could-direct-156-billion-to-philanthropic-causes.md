Anthropic, a leading force in the artificial intelligence sector, is poised to trigger one of the largest philanthropic transfers in history following its initial public offering (IPO). Through a combination of corporate fund allocations and employee share pledges, the company is expected to direct approximately $156 billion toward charitable causes. To put this in perspective, the total amount is equivalent to roughly one-quarter of all charitable donations made in the United States during 2025.

The cornerstone of this initiative is the company’s Public Benefit Fund (PBF), which holds 5% of Anthropic’s total shares, amounting to nearly 850 million units. At the current trading price of $95, this endowment is valued at $80 billion. The PBF is specifically mandated to fund research and activities that maximize the benefits of AI for humanity. Its first major commitment is a $200 million allocation to study the impact of AI on the global workforce, with potential future distributions aimed at universal basic income (UBI) initiatives.

In addition to the corporate fund, Anthropic employees have made significant individual pledges to various philanthropic organizations. The company has committed to a matching program, providing a 3-to-1 match for long-tenured employees and a 1-to-1 match for newer staff. According to regulatory filings, over 550 million shares have been reserved to fulfill these pre-IPO pledges. Estimates suggest that employee donations will account for $23 billion, while the company match could reach $53 billion, depending on future stock performance.

This massive financial commitment reflects the 'effective altruism' roots of Anthropic’s leadership. The funds are expected to support organizations focused on AI safety, global health, and long-term existential risks. As the company transitions to public markets, with tech giants Amazon and Google holding nearly 30% of the shares, the scale of this philanthropic endeavor sets a new benchmark for the responsibility of AI developers toward the public good.
