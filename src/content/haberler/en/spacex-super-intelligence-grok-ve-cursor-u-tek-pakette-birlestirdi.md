In an effort to attract a broader subscriber base, SpaceX Super Intelligence has announced the rollout of a unified subscription plan called "The X Subscription." This new offering merges X Premium, the Grok chatbot, and the AI coding assistant Cursor into one comprehensive package.

Designed to compete with major players like OpenAI and Anthropic, the bundle features five different tiers ranging from a limited free option to monthly plans priced between 8 and 200 dollars. Higher tiers grant access to the most advanced AI models and increased usage caps for voice, image, and video interactions.

The company noted that the pricing remains comparable or more favorable for existing X Premium+ subscribers. The upgrade option is rolling out gradually, allowing current users to switch through their account settings whenever they are ready.
