Chinese electric vehicle manufacturer Xpeng has announced plans to begin mass-production of its AI-driven Iron humanoid robot in January. Speaking at the company's AI event, executives shared that the robot will first undergo training as a store assistant in Xpeng shops. Co-president Brian Gu stated that the robot could reach homes to handle household chores within one or two years. The Iron robot features 76 degrees of freedom and more than 1,500 touch sensors across its body.

It operates on three of Xpeng's proprietary Turing AI chips, the same technology used in the company's vehicles. Xpeng's new production line is engineered to complete one unit every 2.5 minutes at full speed. The company is targeting tens of thousands of sales for the upcoming year, with public availability expected starting in the second quarter.

Pricing is anticipated to align with Xpeng's car lineup, which sells for between 119,800 yuan and 309,800 yuan. Leadership noted that achieving profitability is considerably faster in robotics than in the automotive sector, following a funding round that raised over $900 million in August ahead of production. Industry analysts point out that Chinese firms continue to lead global humanoid manufacturing and scaling efforts, with rival carmaker BYD also preparing a humanoid for its showrooms.
