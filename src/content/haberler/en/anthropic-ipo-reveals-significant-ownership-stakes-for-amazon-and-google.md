Anthropic has disclosed critical details regarding its highly anticipated IPO, shedding light on the ownership stakes held by tech giants Amazon and Google. Following the public offering, Amazon will hold a 14.7% stake in the company, while Google will control 13.7%. Together, these two tech titans will command nearly 30% of the company's total equity.

This development is significant for the future of the artificial intelligence ecosystem. The deep financial ties between major cloud providers and AI model developers are fundamentally reshaping industry power dynamics. Amazon and Google are not merely investors; they are strategic partners providing the essential infrastructure that powers these advanced models. Notably, Amazon retains the option to increase its stake to 23.8% should it choose to convert its remaining promissory notes into equity.

The financial scale of these partnerships is immense. Anthropic has committed to spending over $100 billion on cloud services over the next decade, cementing its reliance on these massive infrastructure providers. Furthermore, as part of the IPO, a consortium of tech leaders—including AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC—has agreed to purchase $26 billion worth of non-voting stock. Within this group, Google and Nvidia have each committed to purchasing $10 billion worth of shares.

This landscape illustrates that the race for artificial intelligence is driven not only by model performance but also by massive infrastructure investments and strategic alliances. Anthropic’s journey from being perceived as a secondary player to a central figure in the industry highlights the unwavering commitment and dependency of cloud giants on this technology. These companies are evolving beyond simple software developers into the cornerstones of global digital infrastructure.
