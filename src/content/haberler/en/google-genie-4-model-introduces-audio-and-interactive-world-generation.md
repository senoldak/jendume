Google is expanding its interactive world generation capabilities with the introduction of the new Genie 4 model. According to the official announcement on October twenty-seventh, the model generates ambient sound, music, and interactive dialogue alongside the visual scene itself. Unlike previous approaches where audio is added separately, sound is integrated directly into the world generation process.

The experience duration for simulations can now extend up to approximately two minutes. Users can navigate through the scenes using a keyboard and modify the simulation using text prompts, such as changing weather conditions or adding new elements to the environment. Potential use cases highlighted include pre-visualizing film scenes, prototyping game ideas, and generating training scenarios for robotics.

Google clearly outlines the current limitations of the system. As simulations run longer or undergo complex modifications, visual details and object persistence can degrade. Complex physical interactions and long-term consistency remain active research topics, meaning the preview is positioned as a research release rather than a fully finished game engine.

All imagery and audio produced by the model will include the SynthID watermark. Initial access begins with a research preview for trusted test users, with future expansion planned for Google AI Ultra subscribers and developer partners. A waiting list has been opened, though a definitive release date for broader availability has not yet been announced.
