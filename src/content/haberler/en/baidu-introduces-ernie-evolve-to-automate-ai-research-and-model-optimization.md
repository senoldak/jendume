Baidu has launched ERNIE Evolve, an automated research system designed to streamline AI model development and code optimization. This system is a practical step toward the goal of "recursive self-improvement," where AI systems autonomously enhance the software that builds and serves them. ERNIE Evolve takes on complex research tasks, enabling models to achieve higher performance in significantly shorter timeframes.

The system operates using two nested loops. The outer loop determines which research direction to pursue, while the inner loop develops and tests improvements within that area. The process consists of a director, selector, executor, evaluator, and reviewer. This structure ensures that the AI does not just write code, but also verifies the accuracy and efficiency of that code, preventing flawed or inefficient updates from being integrated into the model.

The performance of ERNIE Evolve has been validated against public benchmarks, where it surpassed existing records in tests such as NanoChat and NanoGPT. In tests conducted on the ERNIE-5.5-Flash model, the system significantly boosted inference speed and efficiency. On Kunlunxin P800 hardware, the generation speed reached more than six times the baseline performance.

While Baidu keeps the system closed, it shares results and logs with the developer community. Developing AI models typically requires months of intensive human labor. Systems like ERNIE Evolve aim to accelerate this process, reduce development costs, and increase the pace of innovation. This development is viewed as a concrete step toward a future where AI can optimize itself, fundamentally changing how foundational models are built and refined.
