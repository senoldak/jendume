Promise Robotics has secured $40 million CAD in Series B funding to scale its automated homebuilding production network. Led by White Star Capital, the investment round included participation from Horizons Ventures, Radical Ventures, and PSP Investments. The company operates under a "Factory-as-a-Service" model, where it utilizes robotic factories to manufacture structural home components based on existing builder designs, eliminating the need for construction firms to build their own manufacturing facilities.

The construction industry is currently facing significant pressure due to global labor shortages and a persistent housing deficit. Scaling traditional homebuilding methods has become increasingly difficult, making robotic automation a vital solution for increasing efficiency and managing costs. The company currently has over 2,500 homes under contract across three markets. As part of its expansion strategy, it has secured a multi-year agreement with one of America’s largest homebuilders to assist in the production of more than 1,250 homes.

This development highlights how autonomous systems are moving beyond digital environments to reshape physical infrastructure and essential needs like housing. By standardizing production quality and increasing output speed, robotic manufacturing addresses the limitations of manual labor in construction.

Following the success of its existing facilities in Canada, the company is preparing to launch its first United States plant in Phoenix in 2027. This move marks a significant step in its goal to expand its production network across North America. The broader trend of integrating robotics into construction—ranging from self-driving excavators to automated assembly—demonstrates how the physical world is being fundamentally transformed by automation and advanced manufacturing technologies.
