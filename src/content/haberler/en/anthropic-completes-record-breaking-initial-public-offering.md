Anthropic has completed a record-breaking initial public offering on the Nasdaq stock exchange. The company raised approximately $81 billion through the IPO, with the total reaching $107 billion when including private share sales. This development underscores the massive financial scale of the artificial intelligence sector and the intense investor interest in the field. The company's initial valuation was set at approximately $1.5 trillion. Anthropic is the first major generative AI developer to go public as a public benefit corporation.

To support its Claude models and other AI technologies, the company has committed to paying vendors over $900 billion over the next 19 years. This massive infrastructure investment is central to the company's expansion strategy. Financial reports indicate that the company's most recent quarterly sales reached $17 billion, representing an increase of more than 1,100 percent compared to the previous year. The company acknowledges that prioritizing safety and ethical standards may impact growth.

CEO Dario Amodei has noted that developing safeguards is a limiting factor in deploying AI models. Additionally, the company faces challenges such as environmental concerns regarding data centers and opposition from local communities. With 5,300 employees, Anthropic is navigating the balance between shareholder interests and societal impact. Despite reporting over $104 billion in losses through September due to stock-based compensation, investors remain optimistic about the long-term demand for AI models. The number of customers spending six figures annually has increased 12-fold compared to the previous year. This IPO serves as a critical test for how AI companies will manage massive infrastructure costs and balance rapid growth with societal responsibilities.
