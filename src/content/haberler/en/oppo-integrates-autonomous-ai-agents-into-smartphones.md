OPPO is transforming the smartphone experience by partnering with the autonomous AI platform Manus. Through this collaboration, Manus’s autonomous capabilities are being integrated into the "AI Mind Pilot" system within the ColorOS 17 operating system. This integration allows users to complete complex, multi-step tasks with a single command, changing how users interact with their devices. While traditional AI assistants are often limited to simple question-and-answer processes, this "Agent Mode" performs more. The system analyzes the user's goal and breaks it down into manageable sub-steps. For example, when creating a travel plan, the system automatically retrieves and consolidates flight tickets, hotel reservations, and event details from various applications. Instead of manually gathering data, the user can complete the entire process with a single prompt. These capabilities also extend to professional workflows, such as preparing presentations or organizing photo libraries. One of the most notable aspects of this technology is the emphasis on privacy and user control. The system utilizes on-device models to process personal information, keeping a significant portion of data directly on the phone. Local tasks, such as file organization or renaming, are performed without the need for cloud servers. When a cloud-based operation is required, the system requests explicit user consent before sharing any data. This approach ensures that autonomous systems can be integrated into daily life securely. This technology, referred to as "Agentic AI," represents a shift where technology evolves from being an information provider to an assistant that takes action on behalf of the user. Digital assistants are moving from passive responders to proactive partners that handle tasks directly. These features will first be available on the Find X10 Series smartphones. Mobile devices are becoming personal digital managers.
