Taiwanese electronics giant Hon Hai Technology Group, widely known as Foxconn, is actively expanding its footprint in the artificial intelligence server market and scaling up manufacturing in the United States as infrastructure demand remains robust. Company leadership emphasizes that AI hardware serves as a primary growth driver, noting that manufacturing operations maintain solid scheduling commitments spanning beyond eighteen months. Foxconn currently holds around 40 percent of the global AI server market, a share that could rise to approximately 45 percent next year and reach 50 percent within a couple of years. Furthermore, artificial intelligence infrastructure is expected to account for 60 to 65 percent of the company's revenue over the next three to five years, excluding edge AI devices. Demand originates predominantly from creators of machine learning systems and network hosting operators, whereas public sector bodies and corporate institutions are still integrating these solutions into their operations. To better serve the U.S. market, Foxconn plans to move further up the supply chain, initially focusing on high-margin products such as AI server racks and data center equipment due to higher domestic manufacturing costs. While scaling operations, management stressed the importance of disciplined capital allocation to prevent overinvestment, noting that annual spending on AI could exceed the planned 3 to 5 billion dollars depending on vertical integration efforts.
