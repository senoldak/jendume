Google has introduced a powerful new capability to its AI-powered research tool, Gemini Notebook. The update enables users to transform their notes, documents, and other research materials into fully interactive web pages, ranging from educational resources to data dashboards and project tracking pages.

To build a site, users simply type the prompt "make me a site" into the tool's chat interface and describe the desired layout and function. The AI then processes these instructions alongside the source materials to generate a dedicated webpage. These generated sites can be viewed in a new browser tab and easily shared with others.

Demonstrations provided by Google highlight the versatility of the new feature. In one instance, chemistry notes were converted into an interactive periodic table with clickable elements and a five-question quiz. Another demonstration showed five sales files transformed into a filterable revenue dashboard where results can be compared against sales targets. The tool can also generate project tracking pages to manage tasks and deadlines in a single view.

This rollout follows the rebranding of NotebookLM in July, which previously brought cloud execution environments to notebooks. The interactive website creation feature began rolling out globally on November 16 for users aged 18 and older, including Google Workspace subscribers. The feature is expected to arrive on mobile applications in the future.
