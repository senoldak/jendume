New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani and activist Ilana Glazer spoke about artificial intelligence at Webster Hall in Manhattan, addressing the future of AI in society. Mamdani encouraged New Yorkers to resist the feeling that AI's rapid integration into jobs and education is inevitable, urging them to reclaim control. Speaking at an event hosted by the AI Now Institute, Mamdani argued that the federal government has abdicated its responsibility to regulate AI, leaving local municipalities to step in. Mamdani previously issued a ban on AI use in elementary and middle schools and introduced a policy requiring landlords and real estate platforms to disclose when listing photos or videos are generated by AI to protect residents from misleading listings. Mamdani criticized the national framing of AI as a geopolitical arms race with China, which he says sidelines humanistic concerns. He pointed to the U.S. military's strike on a school in Iran, noting that internal investigations highlighted the overreliance on AI targeting as a contributing factor leading to the death of more than 150 people. Mamdani emphasized that AI companies often push technology forward without adequate reckoning with its limitations or accountability, aiming instead to escape oversight while pursuing public market valuations. He concluded that a completely different framework is required to govern artificial intelligence in the future.
