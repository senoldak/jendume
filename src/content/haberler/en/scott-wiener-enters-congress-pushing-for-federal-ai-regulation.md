Scott Wiener, transitioning from California state senator to the US Congress, is immediately pushing for federal action to regulate advanced artificial intelligence technologies. Shortly after winning the congressional seat previously held by Nancy Pelosi, Wiener warned that policymakers are running out of time to establish meaningful guardrails.

Wiener highlighted a recent report by the AI nonprofit METR, which suggested that Anthropic may have achieved recursive self-improvement—a phenomenon where AI contributes to its own advancement. Describing the findings as deeply concerning, Wiener argued that Congress must act swiftly to establish a strong federal regulator, whistleblower protections, and mandatory kill switches for frontier AI systems.

Having previously authored pioneering AI safety legislation at the state level in California, Wiener emphasizes that federal laws should not strip individual states of their ability to regulate. However, he remains cautiously optimistic about Congress's capability to successfully rein in the tech industry, noting historical challenges in tech policy and shifting political landscapes.

Despite facing criticism during his campaign regarding contributions from pro-regulation political groups, Wiener maintains that his focus is entirely on protecting the public interest. He contends that robust oversight and public accountability are essential to ensure humanity maintains control as artificial intelligence advances rapidly.
