No organization in the world operates more robots than Amazon. The company deploys more than one million robots in its warehouses around the clock to ensure fast package deliveries, with plans to significantly expand this robotic fleet in the coming years.

Despite this massive deployment, Amazon remains the world's second-largest corporate employer with over 1.5 million human workers. Company executives argue that the growing robot fleet eases the burden on human staff by taking on high-risk physical tasks. However, labor advocacy groups and independent researchers point out that AI-driven warehouse automation brings complex challenges for human employees.

Experts note that collaborative robots fundamentally change workplace dynamics compared to past automation. Because these robots work in close physical proximity to humans, they now possess the ability to actively dictate the pace of daily work.
