Etsy has announced new rules banning the sale of recipes, knitting patterns, and instruction guides generated by artificial intelligence tools. According to the platform's seller update, product listings must now include at least one unedited real photograph. The new regulation specifically targets functional guides, requiring sellers to prepare the practical information themselves rather than relying on automated AI generation.

Under the updated guidelines, sellers must display a real result achieved by using the instructions provided in the listing. AI-generated or simulated result images are no longer accepted as substitutes. Listings entirely created by bots without human participation also fall under the ban, and Etsy stated that non-compliant stores could face removal from the platform.

However, the company is not enacting a blanket ban on all AI-generated works. Artwork produced with artificial intelligence will remain permitted on the marketplace, provided the seller discloses a clear creative contribution and explains the production method. Sellers are strictly prohibited from marketing these items as entirely handmade. The new framework focuses on preventing hidden human contribution and misleading product presentations rather than prohibiting AI usage outright.

The platform is also implementing distinct conditions for print-on-demand, digital, and personalized goods. Existing listings must be updated to reflect the new photo requirements, and Etsy is investing in tools to detect misleading AI visuals and handle user reports effectively. Buyers will be given reporting options for deceptive AI imagery as the marketplace works to maintain consumer trust.
