Cogent Security has developed cyber defense factories that combine OpenAI models with enterprise context to help companies address escalating security risks. The company notes that the hardest part of security is not finding vulnerabilities, but fixing them across complex organizational structures that span multiple teams. The new system allows AI agents to understand a company's environment contextually and execute verified remediation steps safely from vulnerability disclosure to verified fixes.

The system utilizes GPT models integrated with enterprise knowledge graphs to continuously reconcile information regarding assets, vulnerabilities, identities, ownership, and dependencies. Geng Sng, Co-founder and CTO of Cogent, describes this as an enterprise memory harness, eliminating the need to relearn environment structures for every new task and thereby reducing latency, inference costs, and inconsistent results. Security policies and automated checks govern agent permissions, ensuring that tool calls are thoroughly validated through deterministic checks before execution.

When critical flaws like PHP2Shell emerged in the programming language PHP, enterprises deployed these defense factories to rapidly identify affected systems and orchestrate fixes. Automated workflows have allowed a Fortune 1000 automotive customer to reduce its critical vulnerability remediation time by 75 percent, while a Fortune 500 IT services customer achieved 5x vulnerability-triage throughput. Trading platform MarketAxess notes that the system evaluates environments the way an attacker would, focusing remediation work on the greatest risks and adversary paths.

Cogent reports superior reasoning quality, higher fidelity, reduced hallucination rates on technical codebases, and more reliable execution when utilizing OpenAI models for complex investigations. The company also utilizes Codex for software development to increase engineering productivity. Looking ahead, the company plans to expand its roadmap toward fully autonomous discovery, validation, and closed-loop risk elimination.
