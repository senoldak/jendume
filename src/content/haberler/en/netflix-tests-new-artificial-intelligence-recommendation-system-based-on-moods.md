Netflix has begun testing a new artificial intelligence-powered recommendation system on its television application to enhance the overall viewing experience. Named AI Discover, the new feature appears as an interactive tab at the top of the screen. Currently available to a limited number of users in the United States, the system will roll out to a broader audience in the coming period following comprehensive tests and direct user feedback.

The system is meticulously designed to help users discover content based on their current mood and emotional state. Users can click options like 'Scare Me' to receive tailored suggestions within horror or other genres seamlessly. In addition, specific themes such as Solo Movie Night or romantic stories are provided, displaying relevant movies and series directly on the home screen.

The effectiveness of the recommendations is directly shaped by continuous user feedback. An 'Edit Recommendations' option beneath each title allows viewers to indicate what they like or dislike with ease. When a title is blocked, the system asks for the specific reason to better analyze future preferences. Subcategory options are generated using past choices and advanced artificial intelligence predictions.

This strategic move is not the company's first step in AI-driven content discovery across platforms. Previously, a natural language search feature was tested extensively on the mobile application. AI Discover brings this approach directly to the television screen, helping viewers find their desired content among thousands of options much faster and more intuitively.
