A politician delivering a flawless speech packed with precise statistics in the Senedd by allegedly using smart glasses as a teleprompter has ignited a fresh debate over wearable technology. The incident drew immediate attention as the device transformed the lenses into a tiny screen, allowing the speaker to present complex figures without notes.

In response, lawmakers urged rapid action to establish explicit guidance on smart glasses, with some political figures writing to the speaker to demand a ban across the parliamentary estate. The primary concern centers on the discreet recording capabilities of these devices, which often resemble ordinary eyewear and pose a potential threat to private discussions and secure documents.

A spokesperson for the UK parliament stated that policies regarding camera-equipped smart glasses and other wearable technologies are currently under review. Parliamentary authorities are working together to balance operational needs with physical security, data protection, and privacy considerations.
