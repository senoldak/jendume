As artificial intelligence tools become more accessible, building custom applications for personal use is no longer restricted to software engineers. However, testing and running these AI-generated creations on mobile devices traditionally required complex deployment steps.

The new capability introduced by Google enables users to launch their personally crafted apps directly onto Android smartphones using their standard accounts. These applications remain completely private, hidden from public search catalogs, and accessible only to their creators or a trusted circle of friends.

This shift moves the app ecosystem away from a one-size-fits-all model toward deeply personal software. Users can experiment with custom tools designed for specific daily needs, bridging the gap between creative prompt engineering and native mobile deployment.
