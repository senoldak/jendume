Anthropic has announced Claude Duet, a new personal AI agent released as a research preview to help users automate their daily workflows. Designed to compete with rival tools from Meta and OpenAI, the new agent stands out with its ability to run independently in the background. At the start of each day, the assistant provides users with a short briefing compiled from connected apps, handling tasks ranging from scheduling meetings to building web applications.

The system is powered by Claude Opus 5.5. Users are not required to keep an active Claude session open at all times, as Duet runs on its own virtual machine to complete tasks in the background. Initially, users in the US can join a waitlist to try the tool, and Anthropic has stated that subscribers of its twenty-dollar-per-month Pro plan will gain access once the agent reaches general availability.

To manage rate limits efficiently, Anthropic designed Duet to check connected accounts every few hours rather than continuously. On the security front, the company has incorporated a new classifier and system prompt to prevent sensitive information leaks. Anthropic emphasizes that Duet never directly views passwords or sign-in tokens, and data related to health or personal finances is only shared if explicitly permitted by the user.

This release highlights how proactive AI agents are becoming a major competitive battleground. How much users are willing to trust an AI agent with their entire digital lives remains one of the defining questions moving forward.
