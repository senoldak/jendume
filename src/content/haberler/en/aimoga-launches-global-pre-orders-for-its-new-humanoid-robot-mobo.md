Introduced by AiMOGA Product Director Yvette Gao, Mobo was designed to meet the needs of different age groups living in the same household. Equipped with artificial intelligence-driven communication capabilities, the robot stands 90 centimeters tall, featuring rounded lines and soft exterior materials designed for a user-friendly appearance. The device utilizes a dedicated skeletal setup that enables walking and various body movements.

Mobo offers features such as object recognition, answering questions, storytelling, and interactive language learning for children, while providing daily conversations and task reminders for elderly family members. Capable of communicating in more than 20 languages, the robot stands out with its ability to recognize family members and retain information from previous interactions. Using advanced memory technology, the device connects past conversations and learns user habits and preferences over time to deliver a personalized communication experience.

With its spatial perception system and social navigation technology, Mobo can assess its environment, detect the movements of people around it, slow down when necessary, and safely navigate around obstacles. The dual-brain architecture forming the basis of the MoAgent system combines long-term planning with instant response capabilities. Through this structure, the robot can break down complex tasks into different stages and respond quickly to changing environmental conditions.

AiMOGA stated that it has achieved more than 3,000 robot sales worldwide, with its products operating across numerous countries in diverse usage scenarios. Utilized in fields ranging from public services and healthcare to security, retail, and logistics, these technologies support the company's strategy of adapting its systems to various needs in daily life and business. This step demonstrates the ongoing transformation of robots from simple command-following devices into intelligent household assistants.
