Anthropic has announced a strategic plan to allocate 5 percent of its company shares to a new fund operating for the public good. Named the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, the new initiative is currently awaiting formal approval from shareholders. Arriving at a time when the artificial intelligence enterprise is actively discussing the broader impacts of technology on working life, this announcement does not represent a completed cash donation. Instead, the artificial intelligence firm links the total size of the fund directly to the future value growth of the company itself. According to the outlined plan, whenever the company value increases by 50 percent above its initial baseline level, the board of directors will have the option to allocate an additional 2 percent share to the fund. However, the organization clarifies that this extra contribution will not function as an automatic process; rather, the discretion of the board of directors and future valuation thresholds will dictate the mechanism. Anthropic is establishing this fund as a wholly owned limited liability company structure. Through this approach, the organization will be able to evaluate options such as direct transfers to specific communities alongside large traditional charitable donations. Furthermore, the Anthropic shares held within this fund will not be utilized to finance lobbying activities or political donations in the United States or any other country. This new commitment, when combined with previous share donation pledges made by founders and employees, aligns approximately 20 percent of total company shares with public interest goals. The company notes that further details regarding the governance and operational mechanics of the fund will be shared in the coming months as the project evolves.
