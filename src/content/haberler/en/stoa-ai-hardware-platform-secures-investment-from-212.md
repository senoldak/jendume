AI hardware platform Stoa has announced receiving backing from 212. While financial details of the funding round were not disclosed, the startup stated it will use the funds to strengthen its product infrastructure, expand its engineering and data teams, and grow its buyer and supplier network globally.

Founded by Berat Çelik, Eren Berke Sağlam, and Kaan Yiğit, the San Francisco-based company also participated in the Y Combinator program. Focusing on the trade and pricing of physical AI hardware, particularly graphics processing units, the startup offers two core solutions named Stoa Markets and Stoa Intelligence.

Stoa Markets connects companies looking to source hardware with trusted sellers on the same platform. The system coordinates steps from gathering bids to completing transactions while integrating services such as inspection, shipping, insurance, and financing through partners.

Stoa Intelligence provides indices and valuation data so companies can track the market value of their hardware. Device pricing is based on criteria such as model, configuration, condition, and age, with data derived directly from transactions executed on the platform.

Kıvanç Aydın, Regional Fund Partner at 212, noted that as AI investments increase, the share of hardware on company balance sheets rises. Aydın emphasized that the goal of generating reference prices from marketplace transactions to build a structure enabling hardware financing and risk management was a decisive factor in their investment decision.
