Anthropic is preparing for its initial public offering (IPO), bringing clarity to the company’s valuation and the significant financial gains awaiting its backers. As one of the most prominent players in the AI sector, Anthropic is heading toward a public listing with a private valuation of $965 billion. While venture capital firms are set to see substantial returns, the financial windfall for tech giants like Amazon and Google is particularly striking.

Amazon stands out as one of Anthropic’s largest financial beneficiaries. Since 2023, the company has invested approximately $18 billion into the AI startup. Much of this investment is structured through convertible notes and preferred stock, which are poised to convert into equity upon the IPO. As of June 30, Amazon’s holdings in these convertible notes were valued at $97.9 billion. This underscores Amazon’s dual role as both a major investor and a critical business partner.

This development highlights the shifting power dynamics within the AI industry. Anthropic has committed to spending at least $110 billion on Amazon Web Services (AWS) over the next decade. Similarly, Google is positioned to benefit significantly, having secured cloud computing commitments from Anthropic totaling at least $114 billion through 2033.

The IPO filings reveal how tech giants are dominating the AI landscape not just through capital, but by controlling the cloud infrastructure that these models rely on. While venture capital firms will reap billions from Anthropic’s growth, the primary winners appear to be the tech giants providing the underlying computing power. This dynamic illustrates the symbiotic relationship between model developers and infrastructure providers, highlighting how massive capital flows are shaping the future of the industry.
