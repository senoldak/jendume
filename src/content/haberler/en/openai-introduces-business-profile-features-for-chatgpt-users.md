OpenAI announced the beta launch of "Businesses in ChatGPT" on November 10, 2026. This initiative allows companies to create corporate profiles and manage product feeds directly within the ChatGPT interface. Through the Business Hub, eligible businesses can request profiles and, upon verification, update their business information and product availability. However, the "Business Agent" feature is currently restricted to eligible e-commerce and online service businesses located in the United States.

This development highlights the potential of AI-powered conversational assistants to evolve into commercial discovery and marketing channels. As users discover and compare products within chat interactions, ChatGPT becomes a new visibility space for brands. OpenAI reports that ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion weekly users, creating a significant potential customer pool for businesses.

The system does not share individual user conversations with businesses. Instead, it provides aggregated interaction data from the past 28 days via the Business Hub. Companies can track mentions, website clicks, and discovery topics through this dashboard. OpenAI emphasizes that requesting a profile or purchasing ads does not guarantee higher placement in ChatGPT’s independent recommendations or ensure increased sales.

Regarding functionality, the Business Agent does not autonomously complete purchases or reservations. Instead, it explains products and policies before directing the user to the company's website to finalize the transaction. Furthermore, sponsored placements are clearly labeled and separated from standard ChatGPT responses. While no official launch timeline has been announced for regions outside the U.S., this move brings discussions about trust and transparency in AI-driven shopping to the forefront. Businesses must complete persona identity verification to receive a blue checkmark for their profiles.
