Artificial intelligence lab Anthropic has overhauled the usage policy for its Claude assistant, expanding restrictions on high-risk activities including election interference, coordinated propaganda campaigns, surveillance operations, and weapons development.

Among the most notable additions is an explicit rule addressing how users interact with the model itself: the updated policy prohibits persistent, purposeless aggressive or cruel behavior directed at Claude.

## Distinguishing Everyday Frustration from Abuse

Anthropic clarified that the rule does not apply to users expressing ordinary frustration, challenging the model’s answers, writing dark creative fiction, or conducting legitimate safety evaluations. Instead, it targets extreme cases where individuals repeatedly subject the assistant to purposeless abuse.

The company had previously given Claude the ability to end conversations when faced with sustained abusive prompts; the updated policy makes terminating such interactions the standard enforcement mechanism.
