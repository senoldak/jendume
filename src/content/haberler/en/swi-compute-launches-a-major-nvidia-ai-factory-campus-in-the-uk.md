The Amsterdam-listed investment firm has established a specialized division focused on constructing and operating Nvidia-powered artificial intelligence facilities across Europe. The newly formed enterprise has placed an order valued at approximately one billion dollars for advanced hardware systems, with its inaugural large-scale deployment scheduled for construction in the United Kingdom. Operations at the British facility are slated to begin with a thirty-megawatt capacity, housing thousands of specialized processing units within advanced rack architectures, and the site is scheduled to become operational before the conclusion of 2027.

Company leadership emphasizes that power availability remains the primary structural obstacle constraining European artificial intelligence development. To mitigate this challenge, the corporate group spent an extended period securing massive electrical capacity across twenty-four distinct locations spanning the United States and Europe, maintaining major campuses in Ireland, Britain, Denmark, Spain, and Italy.

The initiative targets substantial operational capacity by the end of the decade, following its selection as a preferred cloud partner. Initial deployments integrate cutting-edge system designs, while subsequent phases will adopt standardized regional reference architectures. Early commercial traction is already established, with a United States-based inference platform securing contracted capacity for upcoming workloads.

Ultimately, the venture is structured to deliver fully integrated computing infrastructure tailored for regional technology startups, academic laboratories, and corporate enterprises operating under local regulatory frameworks, thereby strengthening sovereign technological capabilities across the continent.
