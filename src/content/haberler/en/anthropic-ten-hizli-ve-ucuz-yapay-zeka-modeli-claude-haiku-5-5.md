Anthropic has unveiled Claude Haiku 5.5, the newest addition to its Claude 5.5 model family. Positioned as the company's fastest and most capable small model to date, Haiku 5.5 is specifically engineered for high-volume, cost-sensitive tasks. It focuses on scenarios where speed is crucial, such as summarization, data extraction, live customer support, and in-app AI assistants.

Achieving about a 75 percent reduction in average usage costs compared to the previous generation, the new model is priced at 0.10 dollars per million input tokens and 0.50 dollars per million output tokens for prompts under 100k tokens. Haiku 5.5 also demonstrates significant performance improvements in computer-use and coding benchmarks over its predecessor.

Notably, it is the first Haiku model to feature an adjustable effort level, allowing developers to balance costs and reasoning capacity based on their specific use cases. Available through the Claude Platform and major cloud providers, the new release further strengthens the company's product portfolio.
