AI-powered B2B sourcing platform Everful has secured 48.5 million dollars in a Series A funding round. The investment was co-led by venture capital firms Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India and January Capital. The funding round for the US and Singapore-headquartered company also saw participation from Square Peg and Lightspeed. The newly acquired capital will be deployed to accelerate the company's expansion across international markets. Everful plans to scale its operations in the United States as well as key European countries, specifically the United Kingdom, France, and Italy. In addition to geographic growth, the enterprise aims to improve its AI-driven procurement technologies and build deeper integrations with various e-commerce platforms. Founded in 2024 by Sisi Song and Kevin Han, Everful functions as an intelligent sourcing bridge that directly connects small-scale retailers and brands with manufacturers located in China. By facilitating access to factories across diverse product categories, the platform allows businesses to make wholesale purchases directly from production plants. Beyond connecting buyers and manufacturers, Everful offers comprehensive management of product sourcing, order consolidation, quality control, and end-to-end logistics operations. To streamline these complex trade procedures, Everful utilizes specialized autonomous AI agents. These intelligent agents handle workflows including tracking real-time consumer market trends, matching buyers with suitable suppliers, coordinating order schedules across multiple production facilities, and orchestrating logistics workflows. Consequently, small enterprises lacking dedicated internal procurement departments no longer need to manage isolated communications with separate factories, allowing them to navigate global supply networks efficiently.
