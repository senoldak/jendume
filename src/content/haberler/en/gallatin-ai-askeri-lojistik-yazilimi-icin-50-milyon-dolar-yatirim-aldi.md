California-based Gallatin AI has announced a $50 million Series A funding round, backed by investors including 8VC and Silent Ventures, to expand its military logistics software.

The company's flagship platform, Navigator, is utilized by the U.S. Army and Air Force to manage supply chains. The software aggregates data from various sources such as inventory reports, chat tools, and vehicle sensors into a standardized format.

Using artificial intelligence, Navigator visualizes data on dashboards and predicts when supplies will run out based on historical consumption patterns and current inventory levels. It also automatically flags areas for improvement in resupply plans.

Gallatin plans to use the new capital to enhance the AI capabilities of its platform and hire additional engineering talent to support ongoing development.
