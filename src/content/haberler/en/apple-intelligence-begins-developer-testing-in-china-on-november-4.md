Apple has announced that it will open developer testing for Apple Intelligence in China starting November 4. The company plans to follow this with a general rollout for supported iPhone users in December via the iOS 27.3 update. The initial feature set includes writing tools, live translation, photo cleanup, and integration with Alibaba's Qwen model. However, Apple explicitly stated that the next-generation Siri AI is not included in this initial release, drawing a clear line between regional rollout plans and global Siri AI updates. This first phase focuses strictly on iPhones, while other platforms will receive support pending regulatory approvals. Users will be able to access Qwen through Siri, Writing Tools, and Visual Intelligence to interact with photos and documents. The company notes that access can be achieved through a Qwen account or Sign in with Apple, while the writing tools' creation feature can generate content from scratch using Qwen. Compatible devices include the iPhone 15 Pro, Pro Max, iPhone 16 series, iPhone Air, and iPhone Duo, provided the device language is set to English or Simplified Chinese. Features such as Live Translation, messaging integration, and notification summaries are rolling out as part of this initial wave, remaining separate from the new Siri AI. Previous announcements and the October 31 disclosures highlight that regulatory processes are ongoing and features will be deployed gradually.
