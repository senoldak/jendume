Intuit has introduced the AI-driven Intuit Enterprise Suite to simplify financial management processes for growing mid-market companies. Growing businesses often face high costs and failure risks when migrating from entry-level accounting software to legacy enterprise systems. According to Intuit executive Ashley Still, 50% of mid-market ERP implementations fail, typically costing $60,000 and taking six months. As businesses scale, they encounter success challenges such as managing more customers, more entities, and multiple currencies, requiring advanced technology to support their growth.

Intuit’s new solution allows growing companies to scale on their existing data platform where their data already lives, instead of starting over. Offering industry-specific editions for construction, manufacturing, and nonprofits, the system provides Chief Financial Officers with a complete and consolidated command center view of the business for the first time ever, enabling real-time decision-making.

Accounting firms play a central role in this strategy because a large majority of mid-market organizations rely on professional advisors. Specifically, the vast majority of medium-scale enterprises collaborate with financial consultants, and the platform automates categorization and reconciliation processes. Furthermore, the newly introduced Agent Studio enables companies and advisory firms to build customized agents simply by outlining their specific requirements, and firms such as Citrin Cooperman and Sorren have already integrated these tools into their operations.

The overarching goal is to ensure that tedious financial tasks are handled for advisory firms and corporate teams rather than requiring them to perform every step manually. These innovations alleviate manual burdens, giving financial professionals the technological backing they need to navigate mid-market scaling challenges safely and efficiently.
