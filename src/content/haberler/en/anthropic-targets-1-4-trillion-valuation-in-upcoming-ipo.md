Anthropic is preparing for one of the most significant initial public offerings in the artificial intelligence sector. As the developer of the Claude models, the company is opening its shares to the public, offering investors a direct opportunity to bet on a standalone AI giant. This IPO serves as a crucial test for the industry, specifically regarding the sustainability of the massive capital expenditures currently flowing into AI technology.

The company has set a price range of $70 to $78 per share, planning to sell 825 million shares. At the top of this range, the offering could value the San Francisco-based company at approximately $1.4 trillion. Financial disclosures reveal a sharp surge in revenue, which reached $17 billion in the third quarter, a significant increase from the previous year. The company's annualized run-rate revenue stood at about $81 billion as of late October.

Major tech players, including Google, Nvidia, AMD, and Microsoft, have committed to purchasing $26 billion worth of non-voting shares at the IPO price. Founders Dario and Daniela Amodei have emphasized a safety-first approach, which is reflected in the company's regulatory filings. The documentation explicitly warns that advanced AI systems could pose existential risks to society if not developed and deployed responsibly.

Beyond the financial metrics, the company is making notable commitments to social responsibility. The seven co-founders have pledged to donate 80% of their personal equity to charity, and the company plans to allocate 5% of its shares to a new public benefit fund. These actions highlight a strategic focus on aligning corporate growth with public safety. Trading is expected to commence on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT," providing a benchmark for investor sentiment toward the broader AI boom.
