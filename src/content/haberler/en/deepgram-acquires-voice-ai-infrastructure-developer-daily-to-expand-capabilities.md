Deepgram has taken a significant step in the voice AI sector by acquiring Daily, the developer behind the popular open-source voice agent framework, Pipecat. This acquisition aims to bolster the company's voice AI infrastructure and provide developers with more flexible, vendor-neutral tools. While the Daily team will join Deepgram, the company has committed to ensuring the framework maintains its status as an open-source project, remaining accessible to developers without cost under its current BSD V2 license.

Pipecat serves as a critical framework that manages voice and video streams. The system handles complex pipelines that include converting spoken audio into text, processing information through large language models, and synthesizing speech from text, all while minimizing latency. By acquiring Daily, Deepgram is expanding its capabilities beyond just speech models, gaining a deeper understanding of the real-time infrastructure required to build sophisticated AI-native software. Alongside the acquisition, the company is launching the Real-Time Context Standard (RCS), an open protocol co-developed with the Pipecat team to define metadata for voice agents, such as tone and intent signals.

The voice AI industry is currently experiencing a wave of consolidation as companies race to build the most robust infrastructure. Daily has raised over $62 million in funding to date. Deepgram, meanwhile, achieved significant growth following a $130 million Series C funding round. Interestingly, both organizations share a history, having been part of the same Y Combinator batch in 2016.

This move highlights the growing importance of open-source frameworks in the AI ecosystem. Deepgram’s leadership emphasizes that keeping Pipecat open and vendor-neutral is a strategic advantage, allowing the ecosystem to thrive. By enabling developers to plug in various models into a unified context bus, this acquisition empowers creators to build more expressive and capable voice agents without being locked into a single provider. This shift is crucial for the future of AI, as it moves the industry toward more interoperable and efficient real-time communication systems. Furthermore, the framework allows the company to explore areas beyond voice, as it caters to real-time communication in various formats.
