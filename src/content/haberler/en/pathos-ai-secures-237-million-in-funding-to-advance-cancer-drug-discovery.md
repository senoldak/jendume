Pathos AI Inc., a biotechnology startup leveraging artificial intelligence for drug discovery, has announced a major financial milestone. The company successfully raised $237 million in a funding round led by pharmaceutical giant Bristol Myers Squibb Co., with participation from returning backers such as AstraZeneca plc, NEA, and General Atlantic. Following this round, Pathos reached a valuation of $3 billion.

The company develops cancer treatments using an advanced artificial intelligence platform known as Foundry. According to Pathos, the software provides access to approximately 200 petabytes of oncological data. By processing these massive datasets, Foundry assists researchers in identifying the most promising drug candidates and matching them with the patients who can benefit the most from specific therapies.

Pathos plans to allocate the new capital to advance several key drug development initiatives, most notably its JSKN016 program focused on antibody-drug conjugates for cancer treatment. The funding will also support three additional drug programs, including a collaborative initiative with AstraZeneca to develop novel ER PROTAC therapies. Furthermore, Pathos has entered into a five-year agreement to pay Tempus AI Inc. $55 million for anonymized neurological patient data.

Chief Executive Officer Iker Huerga emphasized that the platform is designed to systematically improve the success probability of drug development. By integrating artificial intelligence into clinical trials and biomarker analysis, Pathos aims to streamline the entire pipeline from laboratory discovery to patient care, marking a significant step forward in tech-driven medicine.
