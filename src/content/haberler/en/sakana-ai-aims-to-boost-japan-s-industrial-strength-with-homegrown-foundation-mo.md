Tokyo-based AI startup Sakana AI is advancing plans to develop its own foundation model named Sawara. Co-founder and CEO David Ha explained that the upcoming model goes beyond national security, aiming to strengthen Japan's industrial and manufacturing sectors while reducing reliance on foreign technologies in sensitive application areas like cybersecurity. Sakana AI plans to begin working on Sawara, targeting a smaller-scale model launch in early twenty twenty-seven followed by a larger version.

The startup previously launched Fugu, an AI orchestration system, and Namazu, a model fine-tuned for Japanese language and cultural needs. However, leadership notes that existing foreign open-weight models often carry creators' biases and may not adequately address specific Japanese political and cultural contexts. The new Sawara model will be particularly useful for on-premises solutions required by government agencies and enterprises. Drawing from its work with Japan's Ministry of Defense and Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications, Sakana AI understands the strict requirements for auditable and locally deployed AI systems.

While future public offering plans in Japan remain a possibility, the startup's primary focus is helping Japanese manufacturers become globally competitive and strengthening industrial capabilities across domestic and international markets. The company emphasizes that while foreign systems will continue to be utilized alongside domestic alternatives, establishing independent capabilities remains essential for national defense and critical infrastructure applications.
