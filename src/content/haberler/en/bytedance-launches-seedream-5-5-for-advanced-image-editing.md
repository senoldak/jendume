ByteDance's research team has released Seedream 5.5, a new artificial intelligence image model designed to keep unchanged parts of an image intact over numerous rounds of edits. The model is currently rolling out slowly through ByteDance's Chinese applications such as Doubao and Jimeng, as well as its Volcengine cloud platform. Conventional AI image editors often degrade an image because each output serves as the input for the next modification, leading to pixel wear and color shifts. Seedream 5.5 addresses this challenge by maintaining parts that should remain untouched down to the exact pixel.

In company demonstrations, the model successfully edited the same portrait repeatedly while swapping clothes and objects without introducing distortion. Furthermore, Seedream 5.5 can generate assets with transparent backgrounds, allowing designers to drop elements directly into layouts without manual cutout work. ByteDance also states that the model renders skin tones, lighting conditions, and dense small text with greater realism. In internal tests, the model showed strong performance, though the company noted that serious text errors appeared in a small fraction of the test images.

Seedream operates alongside ByteDance's Seedance video model, with both tools covered under a copyright agreement signed with Hollywood studios. The company has also announced that a Pro version of Seedream 5.5 will be released in the future. These continuous advancements highlight the rapid evolution of generative media tools and their expanding role in professional creative industries.
