Trajectory, an AI startup, has raised $45 million in funding to expand its platform that allows companies to retrain and improve large language models for their specific needs. The Series A funding round, led by Sequoia Capital, included participation from technology giant Nvidia, as well as Bessemer Venture Partners and Bain Capital Ventures. Founded by Ronak Malde, Michael Elabd, and Arjun Karanam, the startup's market valuation has reached $300 million with this latest investment.

The platform developed by the company targets sectors where the margin for error is low and the requirement for expertise is high, such as law and finance. Currently, general-purpose models, while possessing vast knowledge, may not fully align with a company's internal operations or very specific technical tasks. Trajectory steps in at this point, connecting open-source models to companies' existing business applications and collecting continuous feedback on model performance. This approach allows companies to conduct model training without the need to establish expensive internal research teams or hire costly engineering consultants.

The most striking feature of the platform is its use of a method called 'continual learning.' Thanks to this system, AI models become more competent over time by learning from user feedback and previously completed tasks. Moreover, managing this process does not require deep software or data science knowledge. A lawyer can directly improve the model's contract review capabilities through the system, while a finance expert can guide the model to perform more accurate company valuation analyses.

Trajectory's solution ensures that AI is positioned not just as a tool but as a constantly evolving business partner in the corporate world. Already used by organizations such as Nvidia, Harvey, Rogo, Decagon, and Mercor, the platform aims to make AI models more efficient and experienced in specialized tasks. The new investment is planned to be used to strengthen the platform's technical infrastructure, improve the post-training processes of AI models, and support global growth strategies.
