Isomorphic Labs Ltd., an Alphabet spinoff dedicated to accelerating drug discovery through artificial intelligence, is reportedly raising a new funding round. Sources indicate that the company is eyeing a valuation between $40 billion and $50 billion as discussions remain in the early stages.

The startup's flagship AI platform, IsoDDE, is designed to automate manual research tasks involved in developing new therapies. It specializes in mapping binding pockets and protein-ligand complexes, allowing researchers to study how potential drugs attach to disease-causing cells with high precision.

Building upon advanced protein-folding technologies, Isomorphic has previously established multi-billion dollar research partnerships with major pharmaceutical companies including Eli Lilly, Novartis, and Johnson & Johnson to revolutionize small-molecule drug development.
