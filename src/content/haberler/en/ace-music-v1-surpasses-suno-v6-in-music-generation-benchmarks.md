The AA-Music-Vocal v1.1 list, which evaluates the performance of AI music generation models, relies on blind listening tests to determine quality. In these assessments, two different songs created using identical prompts are presented to listeners without revealing which model produced them. Based on listener feedback, ACE Music v1 has secured the top spot with 1160 Elo points. Previously leading the list, Suno v6 has dropped to second place with 1144 points. This shift shows the rapid evolution of the generative music sector and the emergence of new, highly capable models.

Beyond simple audio generation, ACE Music v1 offers users advanced editing capabilities. The ability to export tracks in MIDI format and separate vocals from instruments provides significant flexibility for professional music producers. Furthermore, the ACE Music v1.1 Preview version introduces a feature that generates a music partition using ABC notation before the final audio is produced. This allows users to modify melodies and chord structures directly within the partition, enabling more tailored and precise vocal performances.

Currently, the tool is accessible exclusively through the company's web application and does not offer API support. Free users are provided with 20 credits per day, while those requiring more extensive access can opt for paid subscriptions starting at $7.90 per month, billed annually. Looking toward the future, the development team plans to release a lighter version, ACE Music v1 Flash, as an open-source project in late 2026. This initiative aims to allow users to run the model locally on their own hardware, thereby democratizing access to high-quality AI music production tools. These advancements are transforming how artists and content creators interact with AI, shifting the focus from simple generation to a more controlled and iterative creative process.
