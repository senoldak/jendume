## Economy

Microsoft and Alphabet have encountered hardware hurdles in their race to build out artificial intelligence infrastructure due to delays from third-party providers. Microsoft lowered its capital expenditure forecast for the year to $170 billion from $175 billion, while Alphabet maintained its guidance between $195 billion and $205 billion. Microsoft CFO Amy Hood noted that delays in graphics processing unit (GPU) capacity would push some availability into the first half of 2027. These constraints impact the growth trajectories of major AI players like Anthropic and OpenAI, which rely heavily on these hyperscale infrastructures for their computing needs.

## Technology

AssemblyAI has released AssemblyAI-TTS-1, its first streaming text-to-speech model designed specifically for live voice agents and phone calls. The company announced a pricing of $15 per 1 million characters, claiming a median time to first byte of 47 milliseconds, which significantly undercuts competitors in cost and speed. Supporting six languages and over 4,000 voices, the model utilizes continuous audio representations rather than discrete tokens to improve realism. This API integration allows developers to create more responsive voice assistants that can handle real-time interruptions and natural conversational flows effectively.
