Google launched the Gemini Decisions API in November 2026, an artificial intelligence service engineered to answer user queries in a cost-efficient manner. Powered by a new AI model named Gemini Decision 1, the service targets a much narrower focus compared to traditional large language models. While conventional models process a broad range of open-ended prompts, Gemini Decision 1 is built to handle yes-no questions, multiple-choice items, and specific rating scales.

The primary advantage of the new model is its cost-efficiency. According to Google, the model operates at nearly five times lower cost than its entry-level Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite LLM. The system can process datasets comprising up to 256,000 tokens of text, audio, images, and video, returning concise outputs such as "yes" or "no" alongside a confidence score that helps applications evaluate the response accuracy.

Decision models provide faster responses for targeted questions and simplify application integration by avoiding redundant details. Google DeepMind representatives noted that the model maintains low median latency across multimodal inputs and varying query volumes. In comparative evaluations against competing market offerings, Google stated that its model performed strongly across benchmark tests.

Gemini Decision 1 is accessible through the Gemini Decisions API at a rate of 10 cents per 1 million input tokens, with Google omitting charges for output tokens. Developers can also explore the algorithm through a limited free tier in Google AI Studio, marking a strategic push into efficient task-specific AI deployment.
