Navigating the world of public relations and media outreach has long been a challenge for startups and entrepreneurs. To address this, industry veterans Michelle Masek and Nadia Jamshidi created Honeyjar, an AI-powered platform designed to bring expert-level communication judgment to a broader audience.

The startup's technology automates routine tasks such as drafting announcements, finding relevant reporters, and tracking media coverage. However, the founders emphasize that human oversight remains central to the process, ensuring that users retain full control over what gets published or sent out.

Gaining significant traction with thousands of signups upon opening its waitlist, the tool is currently being tested by prominent tech companies and venture firms. The overarching vision is to combine strategic context, timing, and execution into a cohesive workflow that makes professional communication accessible to everyone.
