Security and intelligence agencies across the United States are increasingly viewing artificial intelligence as both a tactical tool for bad actors and a target for extremists. According to internal law enforcement documents, malicious individuals have been utilizing generative AI tools to bypass safety filters, obtain dangerous instructions for weapons, and plan attacks.

Intelligence reports reveal that built-in safety safeguards are frequently circumvented by threat actors using jailbreaking techniques. A notable incident involving a deadly car bombing in California underscored law enforcement's growing fears that generative AI could be leveraged to facilitate violent crimes without triggering timely police alerts.

At the same time, intelligence bureaus are closely monitoring rising public backlash against artificial intelligence and its infrastructure. Extremist groups targeting datacenters and critical tech facilities have prompted law enforcement agencies to step up monitoring and adapt their counterterrorism strategies for the AI era.
