The winning entry by Ning Xu of Tsinghua University in the Nikon Small World in Motion video competition has been disqualified following an investigation into allegations of unauthorized artificial intelligence use. As a result of the ruling, Nguyen Nam Nhat of Vietnam was named the new first-place winner.

Concerns had been raised by researchers who noticed structural abnormalities inconsistent with actual cellular biology and potential AI watermarks in the source files. While the original entrant stated that AI was only used to assist in visualizing features from reconstructed grayscale images, the competition committee concluded the rules were breached.

With this disqualification, other finalists moved up in the rankings, including Benedikt Pleyer from Germany taking second place and Andrew Moore from the United States taking third.
