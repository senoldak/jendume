Cybersecurity teams have long struggled to close the gap between discovering vulnerabilities and remediating them. Traditional penetration testing remains a snapshot in time, often becoming outdated as soon as the assessment is complete. As organizations accelerate software development and integrate AI into their operations, digital infrastructures are evolving faster than conventional security methods can track.

Terra Security addresses this challenge with its Terra Platform™, which combines agentic artificial intelligence with skilled offensive security researchers. The technology continuously tests network infrastructure, web applications, and AI systems themselves for vulnerabilities. By utilizing autonomous agents, the company can reduce testing timelines that typically take four to six weeks down to a matter of hours, representing an improvement of up to 250 times in speed.

One of the platform's standout features is its ability to distinguish technical findings from genuine business risks. The AI agents analyze the organization's code and business logic to prioritize which attack paths pose the most significant consequences. This allows security teams to focus on critical issues rather than sifting through high volumes of low-impact alerts generated by automated tools.

To manage the risks of autonomous systems in production environments, Terra Security employs a "Human-on-the-Loop" model. Through the Terra Offensive Research Collaboration Hub (TORCH), security professionals can interact with agents, guide investigations, and maintain oversight at critical decision points. The company claims this unified approach can deliver a fourfold return on investment without requiring increases in headcount or security budgets.
