StitchStudio has secured $8 million in a new funding round to expand its AI-powered autonomous agents designed specifically for insurance carriers and brokers. Led by Voyager Capital, the round also saw participation from Lookout Ventures and Grit Capital Partners, alongside existing investors ManchesterStory and BrokerTech Fund. Following a $3 million seed round in 2025, the company plans to use this fresh capital to broaden its product offerings and scale its customer support operations.

The insurance industry often relies on a mix of legacy and modern systems, forcing employees to manually input redundant data across multiple platforms. StitchStudio’s AI agents are designed to integrate with these existing systems rather than replace them. By handling repetitive tasks such as quote comparisons, billing checks, and sales agent onboarding, the agents allow human staff to focus on more complex, strategic decision-making.

The impact on operational efficiency is significant. One customer reported reducing proposal turnaround times from 90 minutes to just 15 minutes. Major industry players, including M3 Insurance, Heffernan Insurance Brokers, and Fidelity Life, are already utilizing these agents to streamline their workflows. Crucially, StitchStudio ensures that every action taken by its AI agents is traceable and auditable, meeting the strict compliance and security standards required in the insurance sector.

The adoption of AI agents in insurance is accelerating, with numerous startups securing funding to address similar challenges. Large insurance carriers are also increasingly integrating these tools into their core operations. With this latest investment, StitchStudio intends to develop new products for employee benefits, life insurance, and commercial property coverage, while also enhancing its self-service tools. This development highlights how AI is transforming efficiency in sectors burdened by heavy administrative processes, moving beyond simple automation to become a core component of enterprise infrastructure.
