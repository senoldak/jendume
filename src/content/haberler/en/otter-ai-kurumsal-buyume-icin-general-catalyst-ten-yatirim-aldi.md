AI-powered meeting assistant Otter.ai has announced that it has secured growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund. The company stated that the capital will be used to drive enterprise growth, expand its go-to-market strategy, accelerate international expansion, and further develop its product suite. Otter.ai integrates into popular video conferencing platforms such as Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet to transcribe conversations in real time, identify speakers, and generate concise summaries and action items. Chief Executive Sam Liang described the financing as a powerful vote of confidence, noting that it will enable the company to build enterprise agentic workflows that bring advanced intelligence to teams everywhere. Otter.ai reports that tens of millions of people use its product, including teams across a vast majority of Fortune 500 companies. The platform has transcribed over a billion meetings and generated massive time-saving value for its users. Sales teams have emerged as key early adopters of the technology, while the company continues to face competition from peers in the fast-growing meeting intelligence space. Otter.ai is backed by several prominent investors, including Spectrum Equity, Horizon Ventures, and Draper Associates. Co-founded by Sam Liang and Yun Fu, the company continues to scale its operations globally. This funding round marks another significant milestone in the rapid evolution and enterprise adoption of workplace artificial intelligence solutions.
