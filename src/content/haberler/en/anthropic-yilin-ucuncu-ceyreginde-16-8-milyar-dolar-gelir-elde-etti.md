Operating in the field of artificial intelligence, Anthropic shared its third-quarter financial results during a private meeting ahead of its upcoming initial public offering. According to reports, the company generated 16.8 billion dollars in revenue during this period, marking a remarkable growth and achieving profitability on an adjusted basis for the first time.

Continuing preparations for an IPO expected in November, the company is reportedly targeting a valuation of up to 2 trillion dollars. This potential valuation points to a trajectory that could position it among the most valuable enterprises on the planet.

Industry analysts note that these figures serve as a bellwether for the health of the broader artificial intelligence market. The company has achieved strong enterprise adoption, particularly driven by its Claude Code tool that generates software code based on natural language instructions.
