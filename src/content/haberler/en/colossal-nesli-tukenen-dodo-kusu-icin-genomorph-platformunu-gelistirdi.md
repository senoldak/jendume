Colossal Biosciences, renowned for its efforts to bring extinct species back to life, has announced GenoMorph, a new artificial intelligence platform designed to speed up this complex process. The company is initially applying the software to its ongoing mission to resurrect the dodo bird.

The platform utilizes comparative genomics, developmental biology, and high-resolution three-dimensional morphology data to determine which DNA regions might play a role in shaping the physical traits of extinct species that lack extensive genetic records. This step is particularly vital for the dodo, given that researchers possess only a single dodo genome, whereas mammoth projects benefit from roughly one hundred genomes.

The creation of the software relied on developmental data gathered from chickens, quails, ducks, and pigeons. These records were combined with the genomes and skull measurements of more than seventy bird species, including the Nicobar pigeon, which shares the closest lineage with the dodo. Through these evaluations, the system identified numerous genetic modification targets related to the beak and skull structure of the target species.
