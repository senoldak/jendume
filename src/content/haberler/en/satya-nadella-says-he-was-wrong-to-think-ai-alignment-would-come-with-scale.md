Microsoft Chairman and CEO Satya Nadella shared new insights regarding artificial intelligence development during an appearance. Nadella stated that he previously believed AI alignment would naturally follow the scaling of models, a view he has now changed. Reflecting on the early days of scaling laws in 2018 and 2019, Nadella noted that while scaling models with more data and compute power worked as expected, ensuring that AI acts in line with human values did not come with it.

Alignment is a harder, different problem that needs to happen during training and potentially pretraining. Nadella noted that reinforcement learning alone is not enough to achieve alignment. He emphasized the need for robust security around autonomous agents, advocating for a zero-trust approach where every action is checked instead of trusting anything by default.

He pointed to Microsoft tools like Agent 365 and Perception, which assign identities to agents and log their calls to ensure tamper-proof auditing. Companies must set policies and monitor whether agents follow these rules. He also addressed the unfaithfulness of models' chains of thought and the importance of transparency, noting that hidden chains of thought can create bigger issues.

Regarding open-weight models, Nadella stated that Microsoft will not release its own MAI models as open-weight, choosing licensed weights sold as software instead. He concluded that unaligned AI poses risks to all nations universally, including China, the US, and the UK, noting that an unaligned open-weight model could potentially attack its country of origin.
