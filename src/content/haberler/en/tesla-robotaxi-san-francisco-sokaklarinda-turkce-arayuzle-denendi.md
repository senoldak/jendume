A recent test ride conducted on the streets of San Francisco has once again highlighted the intense competition in autonomous transportation. Tesla's Robotaxi service, operated by its advanced autonomous driving software, was tested with Turkish interface support. Summoned via a dedicated application using a standard Tesla account, the vehicle allows users to seamlessly pay using their existing credit card information stored on the platform.

The test journey resulted in a total fare of 13 dollars, proving to be cost-effective compared to traditional alternatives on similar routes. Although a safety driver was present in the front seat due to local regulatory requirements in California, the vehicle's navigation and driving were entirely handled by the autonomous system. The infotainment system successfully integrated with personal music streaming accounts, demonstrating the growing focus on personalized experiences in autonomous vehicles.

While competing companies like Alphabet's Waymo operate entirely driverless fleets using expensive sensor hardware, Tesla pursues a camera-based approach utilizing its own proprietary chips. These electric and environmentally friendly fleets contribute to carbon reduction goals while aiming to make urban transportation more accessible. In the future, the expansion of autonomous technology into logistics and freight is expected to bring profound transformations to global commerce and daily life.
