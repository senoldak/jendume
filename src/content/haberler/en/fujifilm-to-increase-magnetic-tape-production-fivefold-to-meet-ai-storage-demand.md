Fujifilm has decided to increase its magnetic tape production capacity fivefold to address the growing storage demand driven by artificial intelligence data centers. The Japanese company will invest ¥10 billion (€56 million) into its facility in Odawara, Kanagawa. Spanning between fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2028, the investment will expand production lines and increase headcount, measured against the volume of tape shipped in fiscal 2025.

Most of the additional capacity will be dedicated to LTO Ultrium 10 cartridges, which businesses utilize for data backup and archiving. Fujifilm noted that this represents its largest investment yet in facilities dedicated to LTO tape. Although tape might seem like an unexpected technology to benefit from the AI boom, it offers distinct advantages over alternative storage options by holding massive amounts of data for years at a relatively low cost. Furthermore, keeping cartridges offline protects them from ransomware and network-based attacks.

Cloud providers and data center operators increasingly rely on tape for long-term archiving. Toshinari Fujii, a top executive leading the advanced materials sector at Fujifilm Europe, emphasized that resilient data infrastructure must support secure storage, preservation, and recovery as AI and digital services continue to accelerate. Fujifilm's latest LTO cartridge stores up to 40TB of data, with a long-term roadmap aiming to reach 365TB per cartridge. The company initially rolled out its premier batch of LTO Ultrium 10 cartridges during 2025, deploying a 40TB edition subsequently in January.

Demand for tape has experienced fluctuations, with shipments across the LTO format falling 9% in 2025 to 160.3 exabytes from a record 176.5 exabytes in 2024. However, shipments rebounded in early 2026, rising 57% in the first quarter according to industry tracking. Fujifilm's capacity expansion arrives as AI data centers strain supplies of memory and storage components. Having developed magnetic tape since the 1950s, Fujifilm currently maintains a dominant position across the worldwide magnetic tape sector based on internal corporate estimates.
