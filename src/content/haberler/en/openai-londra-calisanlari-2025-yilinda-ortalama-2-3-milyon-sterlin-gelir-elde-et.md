OpenAI's 110 staff members in London received an average of about £2.3m each in 2025, combining both salaries and shares. The financial figures emerged from the accounts of the company's UK branch, showing that employees shared £87m in salaries and £168m in shares overall. This represented a significant increase from the previous year's average package of £1.1m.

The 2025 average figure stands significantly higher than typical full-time earnings in the region based on official data. Compensation varies widely among employees, with the largest packages directed toward the artificial intelligence researchers responsible for building the company's models. The London office remains OpenAI's largest international research hub and also houses government affairs, sales, and marketing teams.

Financial accounts also revealed that OpenAI UK Ltd more than trebled its turnover in 2025, reaching £293m while posting a profit of £20.3m. The company operates an office in Westminster and is currently opening a new headquarters in King's Cross. Former political figures have also joined the organization as part of its expanding regional leadership structure.

These financial disclosures coincide with a period of fierce competition for specialized artificial intelligence talent globally. As major technology firms vie for top researchers with substantial compensation offers, OpenAI continues to expand its presence, pursue new funding, and prepare for future market plans while balancing its core mission.
