Palo Alto Networks has reached a definitive agreement to acquire artificial intelligence infrastructure company Applied Compute for 4.5 billion dollars. The transaction, which will be settled entirely in stock, marks a significant strategic expansion for the cybersecurity giant into the broader AI infrastructure and development ecosystem. Applied Compute’s platform is designed to enable enterprises to adapt open-weight AI models to their specific proprietary data and deploy them effectively in production environments. By integrating this technology, Palo Alto aims to move beyond its traditional role of securing AI deployments to actively participating in how businesses develop and launch their AI technologies.

Palo Alto Networks CEO Nikesh Arora anticipates that enterprises will increasingly rely on a hybrid approach, utilizing both open-weight and proprietary models. This acquisition complements Palo Alto’s previous move in September to acquire Console, an AI-based platform that helps organizations build agentic workflows using natural language and automate operational tasks. Applied Compute’s platform offers flexible deployment options, including public clouds and sovereign environments, which aligns with Palo Alto’s goal of providing secure, versatile infrastructure.

Following the completion of the deal, Applied Compute co-founder and CEO Yash Patil will join Palo Alto Networks as co-chief AI officer. The startup’s research team will also join the company to assist in training proprietary AI models for Palo Alto’s product suite. The acquisition is expected to close in the third quarter of the company's fiscal 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and standard closing conditions.

Alongside the acquisition announcement, Palo Alto reported its financial results for the first fiscal quarter ending October 31. The company’s revenue grew by 39 percent year-over-year to 3.44 billion dollars, while its next-generation security annual recurring revenue rose by 66 percent to 9.73 billion dollars. GAAP net income increased from 334 million dollars to 482 million dollars. Looking ahead, the company raised its fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to a range of 14.52 billion to 14.57 billion dollars.
