Sequel has secured $15 million in a new funding round to enhance its platform, which allows companies to host webinars directly on their own websites. Led by Sorenson Capital, this investment brings the company's total funding to $26 million. The capital injection marks a significant shift as the platform integrates AI agents designed to streamline video programming and content repurposing.

While traditional webinar tools often redirect viewers away from a company's site, Sequel focuses on keeping engagement within the brand's own digital environment. The newly introduced AI agents are capable of planning video programs, transforming talks into various content formats, and converting viewers into sales leads. The company emphasizes that these agents are not merely standalone tools but are powered by the specific data gathered during webinars, making them context-aware and highly functional.

In an era where building an AI agent has become accessible to almost anyone, Sequel’s strategy centers on the importance of context. The company leadership notes that an agent without access to relevant data is largely ineffective. By leveraging insights from what viewers watch and the questions they ask, these agents provide actionable intelligence that helps companies refine their marketing strategies. This data-driven approach allows businesses to better understand their audience and improve conversion rates.

Sequel reports that it has tripled its revenue for two consecutive years and currently serves over 400 companies, including notable names like ElevenLabs and Datadog. Having previously raised an $8 million Series A round in 2022, the startup is now doubling down on the impact of AI in the marketing sector. As the ease of generating content increases and the prevalence of synthetic media grows, the demand for reliable, data-backed interaction tools becomes increasingly critical. This technology empowers marketing teams to strengthen their connection with audiences, transforming digital events into more efficient and results-oriented experiences.
