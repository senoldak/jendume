Sysdig has introduced Sysdig AI Defense, a runtime security and governance solution built specifically for artificial intelligence agents. The platform monitors agents during live operations, allowing risky actions to be sent for human review or blocked entirely before completion.

According to Sysdig founder and CTO Loris Degioanni, AI has transitioned from simply responding to users to actively performing tasks on behalf of humans. AI agents deploy code, modify infrastructure, and access production systems. However, this high level of autonomy and speed means that unmonitored agents carry the serious risk of going out of control as their complexity grows over time.

The system tracks every step an agent takes from start to finish. Because agents can quickly acquire new permissions and run multiple background processes that only the operating system kernel can see, Sysdig emphasizes that intervention decisions must occur in real time while the action is still underway in the live environment.

Data from an IBM Institute for Business Value report highlights the growing scale of the challenge, projecting that companies will deploy an average of 1,661 AI agents by 2027. This represents a 38 percent increase compared to baseline figures, driven by hundreds of thousands of autonomous decisions made daily. As organizations expand their automated workflows, real-time monitoring and governance frameworks become increasingly critical for maintaining operational safety and preventing unintended system modifications.
