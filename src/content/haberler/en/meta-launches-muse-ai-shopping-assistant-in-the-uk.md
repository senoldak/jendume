Meta has introduced its Muse AI shopping assistant to the United Kingdom market. The company, which owns Facebook and Instagram, has partnered with major retailers such as Marks & Spencer, Sainsbury’s, JD Sports, and Currys to reshape the shopping experience. The tool is designed to assist users in finding gifts, organizing daily tasks, and searching for the best deals.

For retailers, this technology presents both a new sales channel and significant uncertainty. Brands that have transitioned from physical stores to online platforms are concerned that AI agents might bypass their websites, potentially reducing impulse purchases and complicating return processes for items like apparel. Industry analysts emphasize that retailers must prioritize their own channels to maintain direct relationships with consumers.

Consumer trends highlight the adoption rate of this technology. According to recent polls, approximately one-third of people planning to shop during Black Friday intend to use AI assistance, a notable increase from 19% in the previous year. Conversely, research indicates that 12% of those planning to shop during Black Friday intend to use AI tools specifically to find deals, slightly down from 15% the previous year.

Trust and data privacy remain significant concerns for users. Nearly half of shoppers (48%) are worried about biased or sponsored recommendations from AI. Research findings show that only 23% of consumers trust the recommendations provided by chatbots.

Competition within the sector is intensifying. Amazon has blocked AI agents from accessing its store while reportedly engaging in discussions with tech firms. A critical question remains regarding liability: if an AI agent makes an incorrect purchase or recommendation, who is responsible? With approximately 70% of shopping still occurring in physical stores, the complete displacement of traditional retail by AI agents remains a distant prospect. Retailers are currently focused on balancing the convenience of these tools with the need to protect their brand identity and customer data.
