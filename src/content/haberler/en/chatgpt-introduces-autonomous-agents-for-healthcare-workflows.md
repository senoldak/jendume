The ChatGPT for Healthcare platform has received a significant update aimed at digitizing and accelerating healthcare workflows. The newly announced features include AI agents capable of performing autonomous actions across healthcare software, improved clinical search capabilities, and more than 20 new healthcare plugins. These innovations aim to eliminate the need for healthcare teams to manually transfer data between disconnected systems such as electronic health records (EHR), hospital policies, and payer portals.

The autonomous agents utilize computer-use capabilities—such as reading screens, clicking, and switching between applications—to carry out multi-step tasks in both clinical and administrative workflows. For instance, when a clinician uses voice commands to follow up on an MRI request, the agent can review the chart and draft the necessary orders. These processes are conducted under the organization's security controls and human oversight, in compliance with HIPAA regulations. OpenAI emphasizes that content shared within ChatGPT for Healthcare is not used to train its models.

The platform's clinical search capabilities have also been enhanced. ChatGPT can now more effectively find, evaluate, and cite medical evidence, providing clinicians with verifiable sources. In blind evaluations involving over 1300 reviews, physicians rated the updated experience higher across eight dimensions, including factual correctness, safety, and actionability.

More than 20 new plugins have been introduced through collaborations with organizations such as Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare, and CVS Health. These plugins allow teams to manage tasks ranging from patient outreach and insurance eligibility checks to prescription preparation within a single conversation interface. This integration enhances operational efficiency in healthcare while ensuring that clinical decisions are grounded in current medical evidence.
