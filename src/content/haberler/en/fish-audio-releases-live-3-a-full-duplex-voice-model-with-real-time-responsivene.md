Fish Audio has introduced Live 3, a full-duplex voice model built specifically for real-time voice agents. The model keeps listening while speaking, allowing callers to pause or change directions without breaking the conversation flow. Unlike traditional voice assistants that split tasks across separate pipeline stages, Live 3 integrates conversational timing directly into the speech model. Fish Audio states that the model can decide to respond in as little as 186 milliseconds for short reactions like acknowledgments.

The core architecture shifts conversational timing away from hand-tuned pipeline rules and thresholds, allowing the model to learn directly from real human conversation data. While a separate reasoning layer handles the agent instructions and tools, Live 3 manages acknowledgments, wait times, and interruptions smoothly. This capability allows the system to process corrections and pauses without disrupting the ongoing dialogue.

Developers can deploy Live 3 through the Fish Agent Platform, the Fish Audio API, or on-premises enterprise setups. The agent platform supports phone number integration, WebRTC connections, and website widget deployments, while featuring a large library of available voices including cloned options. Public beta documentation also outlines specific streaming configurations for audio engines.

Regarding pricing, Live 3 is available at a promotional rate per minute, down significantly from its standard list rate. The platform also details additional costs for phone numbers and provides trial balances for new accounts. Currently supporting only English, the model operates without published benchmark scores or third-party evaluation metrics.
