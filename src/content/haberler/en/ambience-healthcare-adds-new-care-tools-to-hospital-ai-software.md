Ambience Healthcare has announced the addition of two care-orchestration features to its inpatient AI suite, designed to help hospital teams prioritize tasks and identify obstacles affecting patient discharge earlier in their hospital stay. Named Rounding Assistant and Discharge Readiness, these new tools integrate into software that already supports clinical documentation and coding. The Rounding Assistant compiles overnight events, patient condition changes, and suggested tasks for each shift, while Discharge Readiness flags medical, practical, and social hurdles that might cause delays for patients returning home prematurely. The company highlights that these additions aim to improve operational efficiency and financial metrics in hospitals. For instance, Onvida Health observed a 6.5% increase in its case mix index when utilizing the technology. Mike Lewis, chief financial officer of Onvida Medical Group, stated that to justify the investment they needed roughly a 1.5% improvement in the case mix index. Ambience also notes that admission notes generated through its application successfully identified 59 percent more of the diagnostic codes utilized for quality rankings during a single healthcare system review covering 1,100 patient stays. As healthcare providers increasingly adopt artificial intelligence to manage complex inpatient workflows, scrutiny regarding accuracy and reliability remains a key focus across the industry. Meanwhile, competitors continue to launch competing healthcare solutions, reflecting rapid growth in the medical AI sector.
