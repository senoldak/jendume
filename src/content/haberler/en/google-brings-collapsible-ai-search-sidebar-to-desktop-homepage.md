Google is rolling out a new collapsible sidebar on its desktop search homepage designed to display recent AI mode chats and searches. Announced via social media updates by company executives, the feature allows users to easily jump back into previous AI interactions, search through past threads, or start new conversations directly from the main search interface.

According to company testing, this sidebar improves the user experience by facilitating continuous exploration and progress during web research. The update reflects Google's ongoing commitment to expanding its Gemini-powered search capabilities amidst growing competition from rivals like ChatGPT and Claude. Company leadership continues to view AI integration as a long-term strategic focus rather than a short-term financial metric, emphasizing a long-range planning process.

The new sidebar appears specifically within Google Search's dedicated AI mode and can be easily collapsed or expanded as needed by clicking on the left side of the search window. Users can view both AI and organic recent searches, connect a Gemini Notebook, or close the sidebar at any time using the close icon at the top right. While some users and publishers have expressed concerns about the prominent placement of Gemini within Google Search, the company maintains its focus on maintaining its search lead and helping users manage their ongoing digital queries without opening numerous tabs.
