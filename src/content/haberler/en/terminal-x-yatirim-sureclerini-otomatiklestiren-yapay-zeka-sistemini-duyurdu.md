Terminal X has introduced its Capital Memory operating system designed to automate decision-making processes for investment firms. The system aims to pay down what the company calls memory debt by encoding the expertise and reasoning of senior investors into dedicated AI agents. Engineers work alongside finance professionals to build customized agentic architectures tailored to each firm's investment strategy.

The agents operate using OpenAI models, including GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna. GPT-6.1 Sol handles complex reasoning tasks such as synthesizing investment theses, while GPT-6 Luna manages high-volume steps requiring token efficiency. Operating within secure enterprise data rooms, the agents process documents in multiple languages and store insights as a unified firm memory.

To ensure quality control, Terminal X built an Eval Agent powered by GPT-6.1 Sol to review outputs against a 54-criterion rubric. In testing, the GPT-6.1 Sol model matched senior human reviewer judgment 88.6 percent of the time. Additionally, one private equity customer reported 2.4 million dollars in annualized savings from automated due diligence and investment workflows.

To date, Terminal X has built 1,700 firm memories, run over 1 million custom workflows, and maintained weekly active users equal to 81 percent of monthly active users. The company's next objective is to extend firm memories from desks down to individual investment professionals.
