Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei once held the view that immersive video games could act as a political solution to the widespread unrest that might occur when artificial intelligence automates global labor. According to a published profile, the research scientist-turned-tech executive defended this idea during debates with fellow researchers before leaving OpenAI in 2020.

While it remains uncertain whether Amodei still maintains this perspective, he has since emerged as a prominent advocate for universal basic income as a social remedy for mass joblessness. In an essay published earlier, Amodei suggested that future universal basic income programs could be supported through corporate taxes or increased capital gains taxes, arguing that broad economic growth should help establish a tax base for shared prosperity despite economic displacement.

The report also draws parallels to how modern cultural events, such as major video game releases, capture the attention of millions of people who reorganize their daily routines around them. Observers note that such phenomena reflect how modern populations spend their time, prompting discussions about digital pacification and societal engagement in an era shaped by technological shifts.

Although experts agree that society remains far from a total mass automation scenario, the early philosophical musings of key AI figures show the broader implications of technological change. Amodei's past statements reflect the complex intersection between rapid automation, future labor markets, and societal stability.
