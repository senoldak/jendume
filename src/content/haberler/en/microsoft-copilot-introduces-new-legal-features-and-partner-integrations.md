Microsoft has rolled out its Copilot for Legal tools for general availability, bringing contract review and document preparation capabilities directly into everyday office applications. The new package includes over 20 specific legal skills and partner integrations designed to streamline document workflows and tracking within applications like Word. The system goes beyond single documents by utilizing Cowork to analyze multiple files, emails, and agreements simultaneously, extracting key obligations and deadlines. Powered by Work IQ, the tool incorporates internal organizational context into its responses. Furthermore, various third-party partner integrations spanning legal research, document management, and electronic signatures are now accessible through the platform. According to data shared by Microsoft, since the release of the version in April 2026, over 1,800 users have generated approximately 100,000 edits. Users accepted roughly seven out of every ten editing suggestions provided by the system. In internal evaluations, the edits scored above 90 percent for fluency, 95 percent for conciseness, and 85 percent for relevance. While these tools aim to accelerate document processing for legal teams, the final review and decision-making responsibilities remain with human experts. Microsoft also plans to introduce additional Word features in upcoming previews to further assist users in managing defined terms within complex agreements. These tools are designed to handle complex administrative burdens in legal environments.
