Satlyt, a startup co-founded by former Google and SpaceX product manager Rama Afullo, has raised $8 million in a seed funding round. The company develops software designed to run AI models on satellites, allowing spacecraft to handle complex computing tasks directly in space.

Rather than building physical spacecraft, Satlyt focuses on creating an open software ecosystem compatible with various satellites, drawing comparisons to foundational platform models. The startup previously deployed Google DeepMind's Gemma AI model on a spacecraft, successfully reducing transmission errors and cutting down operational costs.

The company's software is slated for upcoming rocket launches in October 2026, featuring collaborations with NASA and other aerospace startups to test cloud computing protocols and image processing workloads in orbit.
