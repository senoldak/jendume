Children's relationship with screens and artificial intelligence is a constant source of anxiety for families. However, pediatrician Megan Moreno argues that the greatest threat may not be technology itself, but our increasing unwillingness to have curious and open conversations about it.

Parents often approach their teenagers' screen time with criticism, leading to defensiveness and frustration. Instead, starting a dialogue by asking what children enjoy online and sharing mutual concerns can ease tension and foster healthier habits. Moving past moral judgments allows families to address the real issues of wellbeing and digital safety.

Ultimately, preserving human connection in the age of AI requires more than just restricting device use. It demands that we talk honestly with one another about how technology shapes our daily lives.
