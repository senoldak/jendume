Workflow automation platform Asana has successfully optimized its browser-based AI agents to operate significantly faster and at a fraction of the previous cost. Using advanced coding assistants to investigate inefficiencies, the engineering team revamped how the agents handle browsing history and data caching.

Initial investigations revealed that while the agent cached fixed instructions, the growing history of page text and screenshots was repeatedly resent with every request, driving up costs and slowing down execution. By refining caching policies, expanding history budgets, and batching screenshot removals, the team streamlined the workflow.

In benchmark tests, the optimized setup powered by the GPT-6.1 Sol model reduced average run costs significantly and cut execution time down to just a few minutes. Compared to the original production setup, the new workflow achieved a 76x reduction in cost and a 5x increase in speed while successfully completing tasks without errors.

Asana has already integrated these improvements into its browser automation platform, paving the way for more scalable and sustainable AI deployments across business applications.
