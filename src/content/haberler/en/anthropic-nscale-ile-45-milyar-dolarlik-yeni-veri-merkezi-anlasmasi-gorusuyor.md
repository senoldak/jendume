Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company behind the Claude model, is holding talks with Nscale to secure additional computing capacity. If the parties finalize the agreement, a second facility will be established at the Monarch data center site in West Virginia, United States, providing an additional 45 billion dollars in computing capacity. This new round of talks follows previous massive agreements between the two firms. According to documents submitted by Nscale to the US Securities and Exchange Commission, the companies signed contracts on August 25, 2026, outlining payments of up to 44.6 billion dollars. With this new addition, the total value of the contracts could reach approximately 90 billion dollars. However, these figures depend on Nscale fulfilling specific delivery and service conditions. Therefore, the amounts represent the potential total value of the contracts rather than guaranteed cash flow. Meanwhile, both companies are preparing to offer their shares to the public through upcoming initial public offerings. Reports indicate that Anthropic aims to go public before the end of 2026, while Nscale is also pursuing an IPO process. Nscale's target valuation of roughly 30 billion dollars has faced skepticism from some investors, while broader concerns persist over whether massive AI spending will yield adequate financial returns. Investors closely monitor the balance and financial returns of massive infrastructure investments made by technology companies.
