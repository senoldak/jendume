Dinesh Rai serves as a clinical AI engineer at Boston Children’s Hospital, helping navigate the intersection of medicine and technology. Trained as an emergency physician who taught himself to code before entering medical school, Rai completed a fellowship in clinical informatics before transitioning into health technology. Initially brought on board in August 2023 for prompt engineering, Rai watched the focus of the job shift as underlying technology improved.

With better models now available, elaborate prompting has become less critical, making the heavier lift the actual integration of artificial intelligence into healthcare workflows and ensuring its outputs can be trusted. Roughly half of Rai’s time is spent working with developers on technical execution, while the other half involves collaborating with clinicians to understand practical medical needs. Among the projects developed at the hospital is a system designed to help doctors treating complex patients find similar historical cases within the institution's data.

Such initiatives require far more than knowing how to query a language model; they demand a deep understanding of hospital data, clinical utility, and model limitations. As artificial intelligence adoption surges in medicine, healthcare professionals continue to view these tools as supportive aids rather than replacements for clinical judgment. Rai emphasizes that rather than mastering specific tools, individuals entering the field should focus on fundamental programming skills and a deep understanding of healthcare workflows.
