Fanatics offers more than 1.5 million unique items for sports fans, manufacturing over 45 million items each year. However, this massive growth created a challenge for customers trying to browse through the vast catalog. Shopping sessions routinely ran over 30 minutes on average as the company released thousands of new products each week.

To address this, Fanatics created 'Shop the Look' with the help of OpenAI technology. Traditional curation strategies could not always keep pace with the rapidly increasing product pool, and random suggestions do not work for sports fans who require strict team and player alignment.

The new feature parses product and catalog data to find compatible items, suggesting coordinated outfits such as hats and socks to go with a jersey. According to Fanatics Commerce Chief Product Officer Justin Tsai, rolling out the feature resulted in a 10% increase in views of recommended items, helping shoppers gain more conviction about their purchases.

OpenAI representatives noted that personalization in retail is an age-old problem. While e-commerce previously relied on past purchase history and browsing data, AI allows for more deterministic matching. This integration demonstrates how artificial intelligence can help narrow down massive product selections and enhance the existing retail experience.
