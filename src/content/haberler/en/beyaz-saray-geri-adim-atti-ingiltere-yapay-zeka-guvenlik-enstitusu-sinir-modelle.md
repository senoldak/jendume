The White House has reversed its September decision that blocked the United Kingdom’s AI Security Institute (AISI) from accessing new frontier AI models for pre-release safety evaluations. Under the restored arrangement, British researchers will once again receive early testing access to advanced systems from Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and OpenAI.

In September, the US administration had advised domestic AI developers against sharing unreleased models with the British agency, prompting Anthropic to restrict early access to its Claude Mythos 5.1 model. Following diplomatic engagement led by UK AI Minister Kanishka Narayan, the restriction has now been lifted.

## Stronger Incident Reporting for Critical Infrastructure

The reversal comes amid heightened transatlantic debate over international AI governance. While UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham has advocated for coordinated international safety standards at the United Nations General Assembly, intelligence agencies have warned that frontier capabilities could be misused by hostile actors.

Alongside restored model testing, Narayan is preparing stricter reporting obligations for AI-related security incidents affecting critical public infrastructure.
