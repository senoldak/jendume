US-based artificial intelligence company Applied Compute announced on November 2, 2026, that it has completed a new funding round of 350 million dollars. Led by Elad Gil, the round pushed the company's total valuation to 3.25 billion dollars. According to the official announcement, the startup surpassed an annualized revenue run rate of 100 million dollars in less than 18 months since its inception.

The funding round saw participation from Kleiner Perkins, Lux Capital, Hanabi Capital, NVIDIA, Blackstone, Shopify Ventures, HubSpot Ventures, and various investment funds. The company stated it will use the new capital to expand its compute capacity, grow its team, and scale enterprise deployments. Company data shows that its headcount has tripled since January, with researchers and engineers making up 85 percent of the workforce.

Applied Compute focuses on helping businesses train and run AI models using their own proprietary data, a process known as post-training. This approach fine-tunes pre-existing models for specific enterprise tasks to make them more cost-effective and accurate. The company's Agent Cloud platform, introduced three months ago, has hosted over 1,700 training runs since July, while its AC Inference service processes more than 1 trillion tokens daily.

Serving enterprise clients such as Microsoft, Shopify, Harvey, DoorDash, Cognition, Clay, and Decagon, the startup delivers measurable cost reductions. For instance, a model trained for Harvey improved quality at less than half the inference cost of a leading alternative, while a router developed for Clay cut inference costs by up to 47 percent. As competitors like Trajectory also secure funding, these developments underscore the rising enterprise demand for specialized AI adaptation solutions.
