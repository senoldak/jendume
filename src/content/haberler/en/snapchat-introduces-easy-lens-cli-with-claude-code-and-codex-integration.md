Snapchat has introduced a new software designed to streamline the creation of augmented reality filters and games leveraging artificial intelligence technologies. Named Easy Lens CLI, this software allows developers to design custom Lens contents for the Snapchat platform with the assistance of AI helpers such as Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex. The Lens items that users can add to their photos and videos consist of facial filters, animated elements, and visual effects that place virtual objects into the environment. Through this system, developers can simply describe the designs they want to create in everyday language instead of writing complex code, while the AI assistant processes these descriptions to build the technical foundation independently.

The new software does more than just generate content; it also handles stages such as testing prepared projects, debugging potential errors, and publishing the final content directly on Snapchat. Developers can utilize this functionality to design camera effects, beauty filters, and interactive games. The platform features over one hundred augmented reality tools and fifty AI-powered content creation functions. According to shared information, the time required to take a game from its initial concept stage to publication on the platform can be reduced to just twenty-four hours. This innovative infrastructure appeals not only to professional programmers but also to commercial brands and independent creators operating across various sectors.

Game development studios can adapt their existing web-based games to the Snapchat environment using this system, while advertising agencies can transform visual materials from their promotional campaigns into interactive experiences. Additionally, Snapchat opens up supplementary revenue streams for individuals who create Lenses for the platform. Developers participating in the Lens+ Payouts support program can earn income through the projects they build. According to data shared by the company, a total of 1.8 million dollars in payments was distributed to creators under this specific program. Compatible with Mac, Windows, and Linux computers, Easy Lens CLI aims to bring augmented reality technologies to a significantly broader audience.
