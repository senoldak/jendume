Amazon Web Services has released a broad update for its AI workspace, Quick, adding chat-built business workflows, multi-agent orchestration, and a redesigned web application. The new features aim to make AI-assisted processes repeatable across teams and help organizations handle everyday work more efficiently.

Users can now describe a business process in chat and have Quick construct a flow that combines agents, skills, and tools. With multi-agent orchestration, several specialized agents can work on a request at the same time, combining their findings into a single response. The update also brings new data connectors for platforms like Meta ads and Amplitude, alongside expanded language support including Japanese, German, Chinese, and Vietnamese.

Real-world customers are already leveraging these capabilities to streamline complex operations. For instance, Latin American fintech provider Pomelo reported significant time savings and a cultural shift toward broader data exploration without expanding their data team. The updated web interface also introduces improved daily briefings and allows longer-running tasks to continue processing after closing the browser.

These enhancements reflect a growing focus on moving AI assistants from individual productivity tools to collaborative enterprise solutions. By standardizing recurring workflows and maintaining governance controls, companies can integrate AI deeper into their daily operations while keeping processes verifiable and consistent.
