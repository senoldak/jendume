OpenAI has expanded the capabilities of its flagship platform by rolling out ChatGPT Space globally to Plus subscribers. Previously limited to Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, the dedicated workspace is now accessible across web, desktop, and mobile for subscribers paying twenty dollars a month. The feature allows users to organize projects, create spreadsheets, and build documents that automatically update themselves using information from connected applications such as Gmail and Slack.

At the core of ChatGPT Space is a feature called Pages, which functions as an interactive document supporting text, images, charts, and tables. Users can edit these documents independently, collaborate with others, or ask the AI to implement changes. Existing conversation threads can also be transformed directly into interactive pages without starting from scratch. This setup combines elements of traditional digital workspaces with the core intelligence of ChatGPT.

A primary challenge in AI productivity has been the quick obsolescence of generated information. ChatGPT Space addresses this by allowing users to configure automated document updates. Individuals can dictate which information to refresh, select which connected apps to consult, and determine the frequency of checks. Practical applications include automated weekly planners, daily task lists pulled from emails, vacation itineraries reflecting new bookings, and project dashboards updated via Slack conversations.

Furthermore, OpenAI has integrated ChatGPT Slides into the workspace, powered in part by Imagegen 2.6. This addition lets users create polished presentations, collaborate on feedback, and generate editable charts that can be exported to PowerPoint and modified in Excel. While competition among AI models often centers on raw intelligence, ChatGPT Space represents a practical shift toward integrating AI more usefully into everyday personal and professional organization.
