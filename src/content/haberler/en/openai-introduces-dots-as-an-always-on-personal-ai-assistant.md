OpenAI has officially entered the personal AI assistant market with the launch of dots, unveiled during its developer day event. Designed to operate continuously in the background, the assistant integrates with users' calendars, emails, and desktop tools to streamline workflows without requiring constant manual prompts. The company has repackaged the core functionality of its earlier coding tools into a more intuitive and accessible consumer experience.

The core philosophy behind dots is proactivity. Instead of waiting for a prompt, the assistant anticipates needs, such as flagging schedule conflicts or upcoming deadlines. According to OpenAI's Alexander Embiricos, the system utilizes a feature called proactive research, periodically evaluating what it knows about the user to offer timely assistance and suggestions.

The assistant is currently available to subscribers on the Pro tier or higher within the ChatGPT desktop and mobile apps, with pricing starting at 100 dollars a month. While OpenAI provides standard privacy controls—such as allowing users to opt out of model training—the balance between personal data utility and privacy remains a central topic of discussion.

As AI agents become more prevalent, questions regarding infrastructure scale, business integration, and user boundaries continue to shape the industry landscape. The technology points toward a future where autonomous software acts as an ongoing digital partner.
