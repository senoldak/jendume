Box is upgrading its enterprise content management platform with a new AI-powered search feature. By integrating Nvidia’s Nemotron embedding system into the core of its product, the company is launching a tool called "Box Content Retrieval." This system is designed to allow AI agents to locate and process information within documents far more effectively than previous methods.

This development has the potential to fundamentally change how AI agents operate in corporate environments. While traditional search methods often rely on simple keyword matching, this new system based on Nemotron can analyze the underlying concepts and meanings within documents. Consequently, tasks such as a lawyer finding relevant clauses in a complex contract or an employee querying a vast data pool become significantly faster and more accurate.

The efficiency gains provided by the system are notable. According to performance metrics, queries are answered four times faster with this new implementation. Furthermore, the error rate is reduced by 20 percent, while the number of tokens used drops by 90 percent. This combination not only lowers operational costs but also enables AI agents to perform more complex tasks with fewer resources.

Box plans to roll out this feature to its "Enterprise Advance" customers early next year. This integration serves as a concrete example of how AI models can move beyond simple text generation to develop a deep, semantic understanding of corporate data. By packing complex search technologies into a single, simple API, the company aims to optimize the way employees access and utilize information across their organizations.
