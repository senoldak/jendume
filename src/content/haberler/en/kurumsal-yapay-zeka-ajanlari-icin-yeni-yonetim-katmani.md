The increasing reliance on autonomous agents in enterprise workflows brings new challenges regarding system governance, security, and operational control. Simply accessing powerful foundational models is no longer enough; organizations need dedicated layers to govern how these agents operate.

Mick McNeil, founder and CEO of Deliverance AI, emphasizes that true business continuity requires strict control over the environment where models run. Through a partnership with HPE, the company is deploying a full-stack agentic operating system directly into customer data centers, integrating storage, networking, and computing resources.

The new control plane observes agent activities, enforces governance policies, maintains audit trails, and includes a kill switch to safeguard sensitive enterprise data. This ensures that organizations retain ownership and oversight of their automated workflows.

Additionally, the system features routing technology that allows enterprises to fall back on open-source alternatives if primary frontier models face restrictions, ensuring mission-critical operations remain uninterrupted.
