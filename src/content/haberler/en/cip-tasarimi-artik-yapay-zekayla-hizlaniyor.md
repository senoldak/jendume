To address the anticipated surge in compute demand over the coming years, startup Ricursive Intelligence is focusing on AI-designed chips. Founders Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini explain that their technology enables artificial intelligence to design microchips, learn from each iteration, and continuously improve subsequent layouts.

Traditional chip design can take months or even years, limiting innovation. By letting artificial intelligence optimize entire chips rather than segmented blocks, the technology discovers unconventional arrangements—such as circular or wave-like patterns—that often outperform human designs while drastically cutting development time down to about a week.

The founders emphasize that this shift allows human engineers to focus on high-level applications and creative problem-solving. Ultimately, faster chip design makes it financially and energetically viable to create customized processors for specialized fields like space exploration, augmented reality, and biological computing.
