OpenAI has introduced ChatGPT Wallet, a system that enables artificial intelligence agents to securely shop and make payments on behalf of users. Following the discontinuation of its earlier Instant Checkout feature due to limited flexibility, this new system was built entirely from scratch. The feature is initially available exclusively to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the United States.

According to the system's workflow, users link their payment card once. The agent scans the open web, APIs, or merchant product data feeds to present options. Once a user selects a product, the purchase is approved through biometric verification methods such as Face ID or fingerprint recognition. Thanks to proxy card numbers generated by card networks like American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, neither the agent nor the online merchant ever accesses the actual card details.

To ensure smooth operations, OpenAI has also published a Commerce Partner Playbook for merchants. Since many websites and security firewalls flag AI agents as bots and block them, this guide aims to help sellers configure their systems to accommodate agent transactions. Integrations with payment providers such as Stripe, Adyen, and Riskified are also incorporated into the process.

Similar payment infrastructures have previously been introduced by other industry players. Companies such as Anthropic, Meta, xAI, Google, and Perplexity already feature payment and shopping tools within their own ecosystems. The next phase planned for ChatGPT Wallet is to fully delegate shopping to the AI agent within pre-established spending limits and categories.
