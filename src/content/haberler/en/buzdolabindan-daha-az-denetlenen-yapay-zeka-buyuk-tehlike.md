The rapid and unchecked development of artificial intelligence presents existential risks of an unprecedented scale. Legal experts point out the alarming reality that everyday household appliances face stricter safety regulations than the advanced labs building frontier AI systems.

Without immediate international coordination, power over this transformative technology concentrates in the hands of a few nations and tech conglomerates. This trajectory threatens to undermine human agency, economic stability, and global justice.

Experts stress that humanity must demand a meaningful voice in shaping AI. Establishing rigorous international legal frameworks is crucial to ensure that technological advancements benefit society as a whole rather than a select few.
