Advanced artificial intelligence is reshaping cybersecurity on both sides of the digital front line. While the spread of capable models gives attackers new ways to probe for software vulnerabilities, defenders are deploying specialized AI agents to narrow the response window.

Cybersecurity provider Sophos, which protects more than 625,000 organizations across industries and regions, reported that integrating OpenAI Daybreak agents into its security operations reduced threat investigation times by 96%.

## Keeping Human Judgment at the Center

According to Chief Technology Officer John Peterson, security teams face an immense variety of daily attack patterns where speed determines whether a breach is contained. Autonomous agents now gather logs, correlate suspicious signals, and synthesize case evidence in moments rather than hours.

Crucially, the deployment keeps human judgment at the center: while AI agents accelerate investigation and surface structured evidence, human analysts review the findings and make the final operational decisions.
