Tesla brand aims to commence sales of its autonomous driving model, the Cybercab, by the close of the calendar year. The newly developed autonomous vehicle lacks conventional driving components such as a steering wheel or pedals. Designed in a gold color with a two-seater capacity to offer a fully autonomous travel experience, the vehicle is positioned as a transportation service rather than personal ownership.

Lars Moravy, who manages the engineering division, stated that currently manufactured Cybercab vehicles will initially join the brand's own robotaxi fleet. As the fleet expands, the delivery of these fully autonomous designs to customers is anticipated. Executive Elon Musk emphasized that the model will be utilized with a service-oriented transportation approach rather than as a private vehicle.

Meanwhile, the brand's autonomous transportation initiatives have encountered a federal investigation. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration launched a probe under rules requiring manual control mechanisms like brake pedals and rearview mirrors in vehicles. The regulatory agency has begun reviewing the technical data underlying the certification process of the autonomous vehicle in question.

As the company gains experience in autonomous vehicle delivery, ownership, and fleet management, it aims to achieve growth. Although current regulations do not yet fully accommodate vehicles lacking manual controls, the brand continues preparations to realize its autonomous transportation goals.
