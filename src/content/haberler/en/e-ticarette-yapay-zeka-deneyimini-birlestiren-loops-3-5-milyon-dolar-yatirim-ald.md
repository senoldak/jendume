Online retailers have increasingly adopted artificial intelligence, but these tools often operate in silos—ranging from search engines to customer support chatbots and virtual try-ons. To solve this fragmentation, e-commerce startup Loops Smart AI has raised $3.5 million in seed funding to build a unified AI layer for online shopping.

The funding round was led by Andreessen Horowitz’s a16z speedrun accelerator, with participation from NTT Docomo, Connecticut Innovations, Alumni Ventures, and Monday Capital. Co-founders Ari Nazir and İlker Selim Zorluoğlu noted that retailers lack the infrastructure to operate natively in an AI-driven world, often relying on disconnected features.

Loops integrates five core components into a single system, including natural language product discovery, real-time shopping assistance, product catalog enrichment, and virtual try-ons. Major retailers have already utilized the platform to significantly boost customer conversion rates and average order values.

With the new capital, Loops plans to scale its platform globally and eventually expand beyond e-commerce into industries such as financial services and energy, helping businesses transition into truly AI-native operations.
