Google has introduced a significant update regarding data privacy for users of its Gemini AI chatbot. The company is rolling out new settings that allow users to retain their chat history while preventing that data from being used to train Google's generative AI models. Prior to this update, the only way for users to opt out of contributing their chat contents, including attachments, to model training was to disable their chat history entirely. This approach made it impossible for users to revisit or continue past conversations.

With the new settings, Google is providing separate toggles for model training and chat history. Users can now keep their chat history enabled while opting out of having their interactions used to improve Google's AI models. This change ensures that users can continue to benefit from core AI functionality without feeling that they are sacrificing control over their personal data.

The new controls are located under the 'Activity' section within the Gemini app settings. Users can also access these options through their Google account by navigating to 'Data and Privacy,' then 'My Activity,' followed by 'Other activity' and finally 'Gemini Apps Activity.' This move comes at a time when transparency regarding the data used to train AI models and the necessity of user consent have become central topics in the tech industry.

By implementing these changes, Google is addressing growing user concerns regarding privacy as AI tools become increasingly integrated into daily life. This update represents a shift toward more flexible and user-centric data management policies, allowing individuals to customize their AI experience while deciding whether or not their data contributes to future model development.
