The issue of foreign technology dependence in artificial intelligence was addressed at the AI Journey 2026 conference held in Russia. During the meetings, it was emphasized that merely having advanced AI models is not enough, and foreign systems could influence national values.

Officials pointed out that models trained with foreign values could shape the mindset of societies. It was stated that this is not only a matter of technological independence but also of cultural and mental sovereignty. The discussions stressed that nations must avoid depending entirely on foreign technologies to prevent situations where systems could be shut down remotely.

Furthermore, the discussions called for new legal regulations to increase the use of domestic models. It was proposed to update the national artificial intelligence strategy extending to 2036 and to offer various artificial intelligence options within mobile operating systems, allowing users to choose among different models including local ones.

Assessments regarding the labor market indicated that artificial intelligence could help solve demographic problems and workforce shortages while simultaneously transforming workers' positions. Autonomous systems and AI agents were noted to replace lower and middle-tier workers in the foreseeable future. Energy infrastructure, financing risks, and electricity generation shortages were among the other key topics addressed.
