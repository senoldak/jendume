Global law firm Ropes & Gray has entered into a strategic partnership with OpenAI to fundamentally change the diligence process in complex commercial transactions such as mergers and acquisitions. The developed proprietary AI tool automates the comprehensive legal investigations known as 'due diligence' required before any acquisition. While this process normally requires dozens of lawyers to scan thousands of documents for days, the new system significantly alleviates this burden.

The AI tool scans thousands of pages in a data room, automatically grouping relevant documents based on themes and potential risks. The system does more than just find documents; it also produces summaries and draft reports in the firm's signature format. This technology performs at a level comparable to a mid-level associate, completing the first-pass review. Consequently, lawyers can spend more time providing strategic advice to clients based on these findings rather than just gathering data.

Officials at Ropes & Gray state that during the development phase, they optimized the system by comparing AI outputs with actual reports previously authored by humans. The firm's ambition is to produce detailed, issue-level diligence reports in hours rather than weeks. This increase in speed provides a significant competitive advantage for the firm and its clients, especially in high-stakes commercial bids and acquisition processes where time is of the essence.

To ensure security and accuracy, lawyers continue to review all interim summaries and source documents. The firm emphasizes that AI is not just saving time and money, but also creating direct value by making collective institutional knowledge accessible. In the future, this technology is planned to serve as a corporate memory, bringing together the past experiences and expertise of more than 1,500 lawyers.
