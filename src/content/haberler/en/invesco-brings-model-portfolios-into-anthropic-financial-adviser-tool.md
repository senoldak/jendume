Asset management firm Invesco has initiated a collaboration with Anthropic to integrate its model portfolios into the Claude for Financial Advisors environment. According to the company announcement, the newly developed connector enables financial advisers to access portfolio data directly within the artificial intelligence platform. Anthropic originally introduced its specialized adviser tool as part of a broader financial sector expansion that began earlier in the year.

The integration also provides advisers with research materials focused on business management and growth strategies, accompanied by a referral scheme featuring standard scripts. Professionals can utilize Invesco's pre-built portfolios or develop customized alternatives alongside the firm. Invesco manages 2.5 trillion dollars in client assets globally, with Clint Harris overseeing the solutions and custom strategies division.

The firm emphasized that the platform is intended exclusively for educational purposes and does not constitute formal investment advice. It cautioned that responses generated by artificial intelligence may occasionally contain inaccuracies or outdated information and require verification before reliance. Other software providers in the sector are expanding their platform integrations accordingly.

CAIS, Conquest, and FINNY integrated their respective connectors following Anthropic's initial public offering filing. Meanwhile, artificial intelligence competition within the financial sector remains active as alternative platforms continue to secure funding and expand operations.
