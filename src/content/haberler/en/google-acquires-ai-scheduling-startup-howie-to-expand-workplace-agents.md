Google Labs has acquired Howie, an AI scheduling tool designed to organize meetings directly inside email threads. Announced on Monday, the terms of the acquisition were not disclosed, though the startup was valued at $14 million in 2024.

Howie operates similarly to a human administrative assistant. When users CC the AI tool into an email thread, it analyzes the conversation, finds an appropriate time slot, and books the meeting. Austin Petersmith, co-founder and CEO of Howie, confirmed on LinkedIn that the tool has always run on Gmail and Google Calendar, and the team will continue developing the service at Google.

The acquisition underlines a broader industry transition toward autonomous AI agents capable of performing multi-step tasks rather than simply answering queries. Google previously introduced Gemini Spark, an always-on assistant that processes tasks via email, and recently assigned unique email addresses to workplace agents. Google Labs stated it plans to build intuitive AI tools tailored for small businesses alongside the Howie team.

Competition in the autonomous agent market is intensifying. Rival tech companies including OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and xAI are actively building performing agents, with OpenAI recently acquiring travel agent Odessia. Google Labs has also been expanding its portfolio, having recently launched group planning tool Tabletop and acquired music app ProducerAI.
