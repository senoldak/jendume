Solink has announced that it has raised over $100 million in new funding to expand its AI-powered video intelligence platform. The Ottawa-based company plans to use this capital to accelerate its roadmap for what it describes as "AI vision intelligence." By leveraging existing security cameras in retail stores, restaurants, and warehouses, the company’s software transforms standard surveillance hardware into powerful tools for gathering business insights and operational data.

This development marks a significant shift in how physical businesses manage their security and operational workflows. The technology is designed to help businesses reduce loss and uncover new revenue opportunities by analyzing footage automatically. In February, the company introduced AI agents specifically for perimeter security and loss prevention. Shortly thereafter, it integrated vision language models into its VerifEye command center, allowing the system to filter out false alarms before they ever reach a human operator.

Currently, the platform processes data from approximately 500,000 cameras across more than 35,000 customer locations in 65 countries. The latest funding round was led by private equity firm Vertu Capital, with participation from Goldman Sachs, Golub Growth, and Comcast Ventures. With this injection of capital, the total investment in the company has now surpassed $198 million.

Founded in 2009, the company aims to eliminate the need for manual video monitoring, arguing that businesses should not have to watch footage to understand what is happening on their premises. By utilizing existing infrastructure, the company enables businesses to make data-driven decisions without the need for proprietary hardware. Industry experts view AI vision intelligence as one of the most significant software opportunities, and this funding positions the company to further define and lead this emerging category.
