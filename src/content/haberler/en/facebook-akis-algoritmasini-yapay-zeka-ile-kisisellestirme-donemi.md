Meta is introducing a new feature called 'Your Algorithm' to give Facebook users direct control over the topics they see in their feeds and Reels. The tool allows individuals to manage their content preferences and receive immediate responses from recommendation systems.

By interacting with dedicated interest buttons on posts or through the options menu, users can fine-tune what appears on their screens. Meta previously introduced similar algorithmic control features on Instagram and Threads earlier this year.

This update aims to shift more control into the hands of users, allowing them to actively curate their social media environment rather than passively accepting automated recommendations.
