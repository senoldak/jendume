Oracle has launched a new orchestration layer called Fusion Companion within Fusion Cloud Applications to make enterprise business processes more efficient and coordinate AI agents in a centralized structure. Announced at the end of October 2026, this technology eliminates the need for employees to manually switch between complex enterprise applications, allowing them to initiate tasks directly through external AI assistants like ChatGPT. This new layer is positioned as a comprehensive management tool that interprets user goals and automatically triggers necessary approval processes and workflows.

One of the biggest obstacles to AI adoption in the business world is the constant need to switch between different applications. Experts state that Fusion Companion reduces this friction and increases efficiency without disrupting employees' daily workflows. Instead of entering separate applications when preparing an email draft or triggering an approval mechanism, employees can now use AI assistants as a command center. For CIOs, this provides a strategic solution in striking a balance between employees' interest in modern AI tools and the organization's security policies.

Along with the advantages brought by the new technology, experts also warn against certain critical risks. Especially data privacy and the business context transferred to external systems are among the primary agenda items for CIOs. When working with external tools like ChatGPT, which data leaves the organization and how it is preserved is of great importance in terms of legal compliance. Another concern is that AI might misinterpret user intent. It is stated that commands given with ambiguous expressions can trigger faulty transactions, leading to difficult-to-reverse problems in automated processes.

Oracle also brings along the Fusion Agentic Home solution for institutions that do not want to depend on external systems. This built-in interface keeps the powerful orchestration capabilities offered by Fusion Companion within in-house systems, providing employees with a safer environment to work with AI support. Thus, institutions can both benefit from modern technology and maintain control of their processes internally.
