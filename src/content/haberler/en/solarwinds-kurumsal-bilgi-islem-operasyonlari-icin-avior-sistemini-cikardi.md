Enterprise software and management provider SolarWinds has introduced Avior, an autonomous system created to handle initial operational duties for technology departments. The platform focuses on resolving reliability concerns often encountered when automated tools interact with production environments.

Leadership notes that the architecture pairs predictive models with strict operational boundaries and mandatory approval steps, ensuring that every workflow remains fully traceable. The software operates independently within the company ecosystem while connecting to external tools securely.

At release, the platform can consolidate related alerts, identify unexpected system modifications, and remove outdated inventory records. Autonomy levels adjust dynamically based on risk assessment, requiring human verification for critical procedures.
