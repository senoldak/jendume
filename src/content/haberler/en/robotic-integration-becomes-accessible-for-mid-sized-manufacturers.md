Robotic integration is moving precision manufacturing processes, which were long the exclusive domain of massive industrial corporations, into the reach of mid-sized manufacturers. Solutions provided by industry leaders such as SCHUNK, Universal Robots, and FANUC allow businesses to modernize specific workflows without the need to overhaul their entire production lines. This transformation is gaining momentum, particularly as collaborative robots (cobots) can now work safely alongside humans without the need for extensive safety cages.

Italy-based Torneria Novoletto has utilized SCHUNK’s R-C2 automation concept to reduce setup times while achieving 24/7 unmanned production capabilities. Similarly, Telesis has integrated Universal Robots systems with laser marking technology to establish a high-precision traceability process without complex safety barriers. Northern Kentucky Machine (NKM) has increased its production volume using FANUC M-20 series industrial robots, allowing operators to focus on more value-added tasks.

Small-scale metal parts manufacturer PrecisionForm has implemented Universal Robots’ ActiNav system to automate the process of picking complex parts from bins and loading them into CNC machines. This technology not only extends the lifespan of older CNC equipment but also significantly improves the return on investment. These examples demonstrate that high accuracy and efficiency are no longer exclusive to large-scale factories; with flexible robotic solutions, manufacturers of all sizes can benefit from these technologies. These systems provide a concrete solution to fundamental industry challenges such as skilled labor shortages and rising cost pressures.
