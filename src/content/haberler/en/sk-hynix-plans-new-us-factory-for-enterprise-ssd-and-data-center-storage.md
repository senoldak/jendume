The rapid global expansion of artificial intelligence systems has significantly increased the demand for advanced hardware and storage solutions in data centers. In response to this growing pressure, SK hynix announced plans to build a new manufacturing facility in the US. The upcoming plant will support the production of 3D QLC NAND, which is heavily utilized for Solidigm's high-capacity enterprise SSD solutions tailored for data centers.

QLC NAND technology stores four bits of data in each memory cell, while the 3D NAND architecture achieves higher storage density through the vertical layering of cells. The massive influx of AI workloads has strained Solidigm's existing production capacity, making the new facility a critical step in addressing market demand.

Meanwhile, SK hynix has decided to suspend its previous plans for an initial public offering and pre-IPO financing for Solidigm. The company cited fluctuations in semiconductor stocks and market uncertainties, concluding that Solidigm might be undervalued under current market conditions.

To finance the new factory without an IPO, SK hynix is considering funding the project through its own financial resources, debt, or a combination of both. SK hynix and Solidigm currently control 21.1 percent of the global enterprise SSD market, and the new US facility is expected to further expand the company's semiconductor footprint in the region.
