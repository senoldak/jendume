Scientists have redesigned natural enzymes that break down protein chains with the help of artificial intelligence. By manipulating the catalytic core of two protease types using AI tools, researchers bypassed the slow diversification process that normally takes place in nature over extended periods.

These newly engineered AI-designed enzymes can be precisely tuned to target and cleave specific proteins. During laboratory tests, researchers successfully demonstrated that these synthetic enzymes can effectively cut proteins associated with neurological diseases in half.

This approach short-circuits traditional biological evolution, enabling highly targeted interventions against disease-causing proteins. Expected to revolutionize targeted therapies in medicine, this technology opens the door to a new era in molecular biology.
