Voxel51 has released version 3.0 of its multimodal data platform, aimed at teams training models on sensor data from robots, vehicles, and factories. The new update brings search, curation, annotation, and evaluation together into a single unified system, allowing development teams to manage their complete data workflows from one central location. The platform is designed to handle diverse data types ranging from images and videos to 3D point clouds and audio streams.

The release introduces time-series multimodal support, unified annotation, and agentic labeling features. Teams can seamlessly query large datasets and track data provenance across deployments and sample histories. The platform supports multiple sensor streams on a single timeline and leverages vision-language models to generate labels from natural language instructions. Under the hood, vector retrieval is powered by LanceDB, supporting vector, scalar, and full-text indexes while separating storage from compute.

The free tier of the platform includes 30,000 inference requests and 30 compute hours per month, capped at 3 datasets with 10,000 samples each and 20 gigabytes of storage. Teams can deploy the software in hosted web applications, cloud environments, hybrid setups, or air-gapped systems without requiring local GPU disclosures.

The platform remains model-agnostic, allowing development teams to bring their own custom models for embeddings, auto-labeling, and evaluation workflows. Built-in provenance layers track deployment activities and dataset versions, ensuring full reproducibility for machine learning pipelines.
