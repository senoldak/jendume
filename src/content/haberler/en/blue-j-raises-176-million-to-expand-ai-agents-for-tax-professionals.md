Toronto-based technology firm Blue J has successfully closed a $176 million funding round to advance its AI solutions designed to automate workflows for tax professionals. This Series E round, led by JMI Equity, has pushed the company’s valuation to over $700 million, or more than C$1 billion. The company plans to utilize this new capital primarily to expand its "Workflows" suite, a set of AI agents specifically engineered to handle routine tax tasks.

Starting as a tool to help tax professionals find answers to complex questions, Blue J is now moving beyond research to manage operational processes. The newly developed AI agents are capable of checking tax returns before filing, drafting responses to tax authority letters, and reviewing partnership and trust agreements. The system ensures transparency by documenting the sources used and the steps taken by the AI, allowing tax professionals to audit the work before making final decisions.

The company’s growth trajectory is significant. Since its $122 million Series D funding in August 2025, Blue J has increased its annual recurring revenue by 165%. Currently, its software is utilized by more than 6,500 organizations, including major accounting firms and large-scale corporations. With this new capital, the company aims not only to broaden its product offerings but also to expand its presence into the UK and other international markets.

Blue J’s strategy is positioned around augmenting tax professionals rather than replacing them, providing them with a team of AI agents to handle repetitive tasks. This allows human experts to focus on judgment, strategy, and client relationships. Having doubled its workforce over the past year, the company expects to employ more than 200 people by the end of 2026. This development highlights how AI is transforming professional services, particularly in sectors like accounting and tax that require high levels of accuracy and compliance.
