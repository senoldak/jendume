A leading technology company specializing in artificial intelligence and chip architecture has introduced a 120-parameter open multimodal model designed for specialized enterprise agents. Capable of processing text, images, documents, and videos continuously in the background, the model utilizes a Mixture-of-Experts architecture where only 12 billion of its total parameters are active per token. The system was developed in collaboration with several prominent artificial intelligence labs and industry partners.

According to technical evaluations, the new model achieves high accuracy on multi-step business processes, instruction following, and visual analysis while using only a fraction of the parameters found in larger reasoning models. Independent benchmarks indicate superior processing speed and throughput compared to similar open-source alternatives, making it highly effective for handling large volumes of tasks over extended periods.

Targeting businesses that wish to fine-tune models using proprietary data and run them on private servers, the release offers an alternative to closed frontier models. Organizations across insurance, financial services, cybersecurity, and media are already adapting the model for tasks ranging from automated fraud screening and document processing to threat analysis and vulnerability detection. Infrastructure optimizations help maximize concurrent user capacity and drastically reduce initial response latency.

Alongside the enterprise-grade release, the company introduced two compact models tailored for local hardware and personal workstations. One version is built for running multi-step agent tasks on high-end local machines, while a smaller upcoming variant targets fast voice assistants and interactive device interfaces. By releasing the model weights, training datasets, and recipes openly, the initiative provides developers and enterprises with greater flexibility for local and custom deployments.
