Leading artificial intelligence lab Anthropic has shared its third-quarter financial figures with potential investors during a meeting in San Francisco, reporting a quarterly revenue of $16.8 billion. This figure marks a roughly 45 percent increase from the previous quarter and exceeds prior annual totals.

However, growth has noticeably slowed down compared to earlier periods when developer tools drove massive surges. Seasonal slowdowns and aggressive competitor launches played a role, yet the company continues on its path toward profitability. Anthropic reported an adjusted operating margin of 11 percent for the year through the third quarter, marking its continued progress in financial efficiency.

The upcoming public offering planned for November will serve as a major test for investor appetite during the ongoing market expansion. While high inflation, rising borrowing costs, and intense market competition create a challenging backdrop, Anthropic's financial results provide critical insights into whether foundational models can build sustainable businesses.
