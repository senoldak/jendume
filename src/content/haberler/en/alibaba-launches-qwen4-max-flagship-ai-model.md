Alibaba's Qwen team has officially unveiled Qwen4-Max, marking the debut of the Qwen4 generation. Built as a sparse mixture-of-experts model, it features a massive 2.4-trillion parameter backbone with 71 billion parameters active per token. The model accepts text, images, and video inputs, supporting an extensive 1-million token context window designed for long-horizon professional workflows. Architectural updates including advanced attention mechanisms and N-gram embeddings allow the system to compress history efficiently and maintain stability during large-scale pre-training. Significant efficiency gains were noted, reporting a 62% drop in average task cost during internal tests, where task expenses fell from $4.43 to $1.67. API pricing has also been adjusted downwards, with input tokens priced at $1.50 per million and output tokens at $4.50 per million. Unlike many previous releases in the Qwen family, Qwen4-Max is offered exclusively via cloud APIs and proprietary platforms without open weights, reflecting a tightened approach to distribution. Safety training was a key focus during development, incorporating safeguards against biological and chemical risks while omitting data that could enhance offensive cyber capabilities. Additionally, reports noted the model spent time independently designing a new coolant material for chips, which was later manufactured and tested in a laboratory setting. The team continues to optimize the infrastructure to provide robust agentic capabilities for enterprise users.
