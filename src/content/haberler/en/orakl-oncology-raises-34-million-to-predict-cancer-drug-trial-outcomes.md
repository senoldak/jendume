Paris-based Orakl Oncology has secured $34 million in Series A funding to advance its AI-driven platform designed to predict the efficacy of cancer drugs before they reach clinical trials. This latest round of financing brings the company's total funding to $50 million. Developing new cancer treatments is notoriously expensive, time-consuming, and fraught with high failure rates. Orakl Oncology aims to transform this landscape by growing miniature copies of patients' tumors—known as organoids—in a laboratory setting. These organoids serve as a testing ground for various drugs, and the resulting data is used to train sophisticated AI models.

The core objective of this technology is to forecast how a drug will perform in clinical trials with high precision before it is ever administered to a patient. According to the company's data, the system has demonstrated 90% accuracy in retrospective tests on past clinical trials. This capability could significantly accelerate the development of more effective and personalized cancer therapies, ultimately benefiting patients who are waiting for life-saving treatments.

Spun out of a French cancer center in 2023, the startup is now engaging with the majority of the world's largest pharmaceutical companies to integrate its predictive models into their research pipelines. The new capital will be deployed to increase lab data generation fivefold, expand research efforts into lung and gastric cancers, and establish operational teams in the United States and the United Kingdom. This development serves as a concrete example of how artificial intelligence is reshaping biotechnology and drug discovery. By combining biological samples with digital predictive power, the company is working to reduce the inherent risks and costs associated with bringing new cancer drugs to market.
