Artificial intelligence startup Anthropic has disclosed the details of its large-scale investment agreements with Amazon and Google as part of its preparations for a public offering. According to the released data, Amazon will hold a 14.7 percent stake in the company once the public offering process is completed. Meanwhile, Google's share will stand at 13.7 percent. This brings the combined partnership stake of these two technology giants in Anthropic to 28.4 percent.

The disclosures highlight the current financial value of the capital injections made by Amazon during 2023 and 2024. As Anthropic positions itself as a key player in the competitive artificial intelligence market, the adoption of enterprise solutions like Claude Code has significantly bolstered the company's market influence. Amazon's early multi-billion-dollar investments have allowed the company to benefit directly from the growth in Anthropic's valuation.

Despite this, Amazon has chosen to retain a portion of its investment instruments rather than converting all of them into equity immediately. A segment of the financing provided by Amazon consists of debt securities that can be converted into company shares under specific conditions. Depending on the year the investment was made, the conversion price for these securities is set at approximately 2.09 dollars or 5.05 dollars. The investment instruments held by Amazon that have not yet been converted into shares provide the company with the potential to further increase its stake in Anthropic in the future. This structure serves as a significant example of how tech giants support artificial intelligence startups and manage long-term partnership models.
