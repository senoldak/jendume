Apple initiated a virtual research project in the United States in collaboration with healthcare-focused artificial intelligence company OpenEvidence. The initiative aims to understand how individuals interact with conversational health systems, examining response quality, comprehension, and everyday health actions.

Participants utilize a specialized software utility allowing them to ask medical-related inquiries. Responses are supported by clinical information originating from publications including The New England Journal of Medicine and the Journal of the American Medical Association. Users can rate the simplified answers, provide feedback, and participate in scheduled surveys. HealthKit metrics such as resting heart rate, activity, and sleep can also be shared for more personalized suggestions.

The study is open to United States users over twenty-one using an iPhone fifteen or later running iOS twenty-six or later with Apple Intelligence enabled. Apple aims to run the study with up to 200,000 participants for more than twelve months to help guide responsible health artificial intelligence design.

Apple specified that all user data will be anonymized before being transmitted to a secure third-party cloud provider and will not be utilized to train any artificial intelligence models. OpenEvidence automatically deletes query and response data after twenty-four hours, ensuring user privacy throughout the research process.
