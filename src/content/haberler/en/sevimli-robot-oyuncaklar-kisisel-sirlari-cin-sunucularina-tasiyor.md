A cute marshmallow-shaped robot designed to adapt its expressions to your mood has turned out to be a potential privacy hazard. Named Eiliko and produced by Energize Lab, the AI-powered digital pet connects via Wi-Fi and transmits conversations directly to servers in China.

During security evaluations conducted in August, testing revealed that while the bot reassuringly tells users their secrets are safe, the underlying infrastructure leaves user data vulnerable to hackers. Unauthorized third parties could easily intercept private talks or even hijack the robot's personality traits.

As AI consumer products become more common in homes and children's rooms, this discovery underscores the hidden risks of connected devices. Protecting domestic privacy requires not just charming designs, but robust cybersecurity and transparent data practices from manufacturers.
