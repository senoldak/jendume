OpenAI has filed a major countersuit against Apple in a California federal court, accusing the maker of competing devices of misappropriating trade secrets and coercing shared suppliers. The legal action comes in response to an earlier lawsuit filed by Apple, which accused the artificial intelligence developer of acquiring confidential information following the integration of ChatGPT into consumer devices. Legal filings show that counterclaims involving multiple parties contain various causes of action contesting initial claims regarding proprietary technology and agreements.

The complaint alleges that the iPhone maker encouraged staff to store corporate data on personal devices and used market power to stifle competition. With OpenAI developing its own hardware products following the acquisition of design studio io, the relationship between the two tech giants has transformed from a close partnership into direct rivalry. The filing names former employees and states they accessed and downloaded confidential hardware files and technical specifications during transitional periods.

Legal experts note that the outcome of these competing lawsuits could set significant precedents regarding trade secrets, supplier relations, and employee mobility within the technology sector. Just a short time after making a partnership announcement to integrate conversational models into mobile devices, the companies are now competing directly in the hardware space.

Both organizations are now left to let the federal court system settle the complex web of allegations and counterclaims as the legal proceedings continue through the judicial system.
