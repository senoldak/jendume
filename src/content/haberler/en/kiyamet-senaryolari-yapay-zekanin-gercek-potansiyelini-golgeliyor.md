Speaking at a major industry gathering, chipmaker executive Andrew Feldman pushed back against the growing trend of apocalyptic warnings surrounding advanced computing. Feldman suggested that some prominent voices in the ecosystem should tone down the public panic about existential threats, arguing that such extreme negativity undermines the incredible promise the technology holds.

While acknowledging that powerful technologies come with inherent risks and require thoughtful regulation, Feldman emphasized that the focus should also remain on what the technology can achieve for humanity, such as helping eradicate major diseases in the future.

He also addressed the infrastructure challenges facing the industry, noting that data center builders need to engage more openly and effectively with local communities. According to the executive, balancing risk with reward requires both sensible oversight and a clearer vision of the positive impact the technology can bring to everyday life.
