Boston-based artificial intelligence company ASI has raised 325 million dollars in a Series C funding round, bringing its valuation to 3 billion dollars. The financing was led by Spark Capital, with participation from investment partners and venture firms including Citadel Securities backers, Meritech Capital, Craft Ventures, Energy Impact Partners, and Andreessen Horowitz. The organization previously operated under a different name. Company leadership noted that while the broader technology sector focused heavily on text generation, their primary goal remains the management of physical assets.

The enterprise software creates advanced models for flight routing, electrical distribution networks, and field operations well in advance, allowing system operators to intervene before disruptions occur. The Federal Aviation Administration awarded the firm a substantial contract valued at 875 million dollars to oversee national airspace operations using automated systems. The platform integrates meteorological data, flight trajectories, and personnel schedules to mitigate congestion and flight delays.

Initial deployment of the technology began around the Washington region with plans for nationwide expansion. Additionally, the United States Army awarded a 99 million dollar multi-year agreement for advanced command and control applications. Major commercial carriers such as Delta Air Lines, Alaska Airlines, United Airlines, and utility provider National Grid utilize these operational tools.

The newly secured capital will support broader deployment across energy and defense logistics while expanding research and engineering operations in Boston. ASI continues to participate in a growing sector of aerospace and defense technology companies drawing significant venture capital backing.
