OpenAI continues to expand its footprint in the artificial intelligence market, posting notable financial results in the third quarter. The company's revenue exceeded 10 billion dollars for the first time during this period. Having reported 6.7 billion dollars in the previous quarter, the company achieved an increase of nearly 50 percent. This growth was driven by intense demand for new GPT models and a stronger focus on enterprise-oriented products. The latest figures indicate an acceleration compared to the first half of the year.

Although the company's expenses still exceed its revenues, an improvement was recorded in its loss margin. The loss rate, which stood at minus 150 percent in the second quarter, dropped to minus 109 percent in the third quarter. OpenAI is working to reach a broader customer base by reorganizing its pricing, offering discounts, and focusing on artificial intelligence tools that help users complete complex tasks. These steps are part of the company's efforts to balance its financial structure.

Meanwhile, market competition remains fierce. Anthropic, a key rival to Sam Altman's team, maintains a strong presence in the enterprise market and reported 17 billion dollars in revenue for the third quarter. If OpenAI maintains its current revenue trajectory, it could reach an annualized run rate of 50 billion dollars, while the reported figure for Anthropic stands at 81 billion dollars. However, it is noted that these companies utilize different calculation methods, meaning the direct gap does not entirely determine the final outcome of the competition.
