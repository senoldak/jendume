Anthropic has set its share price at $83 for its upcoming debut on the US stock market. The company is scheduled to begin trading on the Nasdaq exchange under the ticker symbol ANT. This move marks a significant milestone, positioning the artificial intelligence firm for one of the largest initial public offerings in history and serving as a major test for the sector's financial viability.

Founded in 2021, the company had previously set a preliminary price range between $70 and $78. The decision to price shares at $83 reflects strong investor interest and confidence in the company's trajectory. CEO Dario Amodei and other co-founders are also planning to sell a portion of their stakes as part of this public offering.

A key focus in the company's investor prospectus is the substantial expenditure required for computing power. Anthropic has committed to purchase agreements and lease commitments for data center capacity exceeding $70 billion over the next 12 months. Looking further ahead, these commitments scale to over $450 billion by 2030, with an additional $450 billion due by 2045, highlighting the immense capital requirements of modern AI development.

This IPO is particularly significant as it represents the first major generative AI company to go public. With competitors like OpenAI reportedly delaying their own public offerings due to safety concerns, Anthropic's performance on the stock market will provide a crucial indicator of investor sentiment toward the AI industry. Despite recent market volatility and broader industry concerns, this debut is widely viewed as a defining moment for the future of AI-driven enterprises in the public markets.
