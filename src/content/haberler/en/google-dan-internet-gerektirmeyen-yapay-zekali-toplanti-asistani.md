Google has announced AI Edge Foresight, a new meeting assistant optimized specifically for Apple Silicon processors. Capable of working entirely offline, the application can transcribe meetings and extract notes even during travel when an internet connection is unavailable.

Powered by the on-device 740-million-parameter EmbeddingGemma 2 model, the system allows users to upload documents such as PDFs, Google Docs, and Microsoft Office files to build a personal knowledge base. Users can then ask questions regarding these documents during meetings and receive instant answers.

Competition in the meeting assistant market has been intensifying recently. While companies like Wispr and Calendly have introduced their own tools, this industry shift highlights how artificial intelligence is becoming an essential part of our daily workflows.
