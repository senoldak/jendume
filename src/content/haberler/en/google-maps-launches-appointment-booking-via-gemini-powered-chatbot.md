Google Maps has introduced significant updates to its Gemini-powered chatbot, "Ask Maps," transforming the platform into a more proactive personal assistant. Users can now utilize the chatbot to find and book appointments for services such as beauty treatments, pet grooming, and home or auto repairs. The system can check for available slots and even place calls to local businesses to secure bookings on the user's behalf, effectively automating a common daily chore.

The update also enhances travel planning capabilities. Users can generate detailed road trip itineraries based on personal interests, which can be modified, shared, or exported directly to Google Docs. Before setting off, travelers can access "know before you go" information, including real-time weather updates, electric vehicle charging station locations, toll information, and potential traffic delays. The system also allows for on-the-fly itinerary adjustments during the journey.

The service is expanding its reach significantly, becoming available in over 30 additional countries across Europe, including the UK, Germany, and Spain. This brings the total availability of the chatbot to more than 200 countries. While features like appointment booking and route advice are initially launching in the US, the company plans to roll out these capabilities to more regions in the near future. Available on both Android and iOS, these updates reinforce the role of mapping software as a comprehensive personal assistant rather than just a navigation tool. This shift highlights how artificial intelligence is increasingly being integrated into the fabric of daily logistics and personal organization.
