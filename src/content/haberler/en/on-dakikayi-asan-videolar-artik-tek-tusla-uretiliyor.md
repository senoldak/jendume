A major milestone has been reached in AI-driven video production. Higgsfield AI has updated its creative suite to allow users to generate videos exceeding ten minutes, a massive leap from the previous one-minute ceiling. According to CEO and co-founder Alex Mashrabov, this capability enables a single creator to execute high-end campaigns from start to finish.

The new models tackle one of creative AI’s toughest challenges: maintaining visual consistency across longer narratives. Autonomous agents handle repetitive production tasks and complex 3D modeling work, while advanced image models ensure precise text and infographics for smaller businesses.

Looking ahead, the company is focusing on voice-to-video technologies that translate spoken ideas into real-time visual storytelling, bringing the dream of voice filmmaking closer to reality.
