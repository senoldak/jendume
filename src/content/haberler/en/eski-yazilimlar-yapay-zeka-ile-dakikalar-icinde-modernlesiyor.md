One of the software industry's biggest burdens, legacy code structures, is undergoing a radical transformation with the rise of artificial intelligence coding agents. The ability to convert decades-old programs into modern infrastructure within minutes heralds a new era in software engineering.

This breakthrough demonstrates that complex systems, which have accumulated over the years and become nearly impossible to manage, can now be redesigned in weeks. Instead of manually parsing thousands of lines of old code, developers are utilizing artificial intelligence to quickly transition to more modular and sustainable architectures.

Long-standing industry rules dictating that working legacy code should never be rewritten from scratch are now being re-evaluated thanks to the speed and efficiency provided by artificial intelligence tools.
