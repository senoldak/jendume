General-purpose artificial intelligence models are becoming a growing challenge for security teams, as safety guardrails designed to stop hackers often end up blocking defenders as well. To close this gap, US startup Artemis Security has launched Orion-1, a model built to investigate and respond to corporate network attacks on its own.

Orion-1 processes log data from cloud setups, authentication services, and network gear, connecting individual signals to identify a single attacker's footprint. In benchmark tests developed by the company, Orion-1 significantly outperformed other models, particularly in attack reconstruction tasks.

The model is currently live in a private preview for select customers, with high-impact actions requiring human approval by default. As major AI providers continue to restrict the cybersecurity capabilities of broad frontier models, specialized defense tools like Orion-1 are taking on a more critical role in protecting digital infrastructure.
