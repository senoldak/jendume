Following the termination of its acquisition agreement with Meta, AI startup Manus has returned to independent operations with a major financial boost. Its parent company, Butterfly Effect, has raised more than $500 million in a funding round led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital, with participation from Tencent, HSG, and ZhenFund.

Although the company did not disclose its exact valuation for this round, reports previously indicated it was discussing a $4 billion valuation. The newly secured funds will support hiring initiatives both in China and internationally, as well as fuel the company's global expansion.

Manus develops AI tools and agents designed to help users conduct research, build digital applications, and generate content. With recent innovations like the Manus 2.0 release and the Cue application—which allows AI agents to interact with digital services and handle payments within set limits—the company continues to expand its footprint. Meanwhile, reports suggest Manus is also considering a potential IPO in Hong Kong.
