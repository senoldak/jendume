The integration of autonomous artificial intelligence agents into business workflows is ushering in a new era that forces security and data resilience disciplines to converge. Traditionally treated as separate functions, cybersecurity and data recovery are now merging because AI agents operate at machine speed and can modify data in unpredictable ways. During its virtual global launch event, Veeam Software announced Intelligent ResOps, a new product designed to discover AI agents, map sensitive data access, and allow teams to undo agent-driven modifications file by file.

Company executives point out that unlike traditional ransomware or data exfiltration, unauthorized data modification by an AI agent cannot simply be disclosed; it must be precisely identified and reversed. According to company data, while 88 percent of organizations are using or piloting AI agents, only 7 percent qualify as truly AI-ready, and a vast majority harbor unknown agents in their environments. The platform's first generally available workload supports Microsoft 365, with private previews for Okta, GitHub, and Azure DevOps slated for the fourth quarter, aiming to eliminate fragmented tooling and provide a unified view of data resilience.

Experts emphasize that modern recovery requires detailed data maps rather than simple recovery points, allowing organizations to know exactly what changed and what needs to be restored first. Preview customers discovered numerous active agents modifying production data and significant amounts of redundant or obsolete files that posed compliance risks. Merging identity management, data security posture, and recovery operations ensures that companies can effectively clean up after unexpected AI-driven modifications without disrupting their entire business infrastructure.
