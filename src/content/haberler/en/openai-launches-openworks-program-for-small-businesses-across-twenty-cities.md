OpenAI has rolled out a pilot initiative named OpenWorks, connecting small enterprise owners across twenty US locations with regional experts proficient in artificial intelligence. Structured to involve no costs for business operators, the program supplies participating organizations with a three-month complimentary subscription to ChatGPT Business. Regional specialists taking part in the initiative can earn compensation reaching up to eight thousand dollars per month.

The pilot scheme specifically targets independent eating establishments, retail shops, manufacturing facilities, and service-oriented enterprises. According to the eligibility criteria established by OpenAI, software development firms, venture-backed startups, and businesses controlled by private equity funds are excluded from participation. Out of the twenty included hubs, fifteen are situated in the Midwest and Southern regions of the country, while the roster also features major metropolitan areas such as Austin, Seattle, and New York.

Individuals seeking to apply as partners are expected to dedicate four full days per month to the assignment and host a community gathering. During the application procedure, candidates are requested to describe a previously resolved artificial intelligence challenge and present their portfolios. Meanwhile, OpenAI has not yet disclosed specifics regarding how the monthly payment cap of eight thousand dollars is calculated or the duration for which these payments will persist.

The three-month complimentary trial phase grants businesses the chance to examine the time-saving and efficiency gains of artificial intelligence without facing subscription charges. This framework establishes a compensated consulting avenue for regionally based artificial intelligence talents while simultaneously carrying the potential to secure new enterprise subscribers for OpenAI's platform once the trial concludes.
