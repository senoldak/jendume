Healthcare reforms and spending cuts in the United States are expected to increase medical debt, prompting a shift in how hospitals and debt collectors operate. Artificial intelligence is increasingly being utilized to listen in on exam room conversations, code visits, send billing messages, and engage with patients through automated platforms.

Companies are leveraging machine learning models to predict a patient's propensity to pay and to match individuals with customized payment plans or discounts. These technologies help cash-strapped hospitals streamline their billing operations and reduce labor costs associated with manual debt collection.

At the same time, the growing reliance on automated coding and billing tools has sparked debate between insurers and healthcare providers regarding transparency and overall spending. As both sides adopt algorithmic systems to manage care authorizations and collections, the financial landscape of healthcare continues to evolve rapidly.
