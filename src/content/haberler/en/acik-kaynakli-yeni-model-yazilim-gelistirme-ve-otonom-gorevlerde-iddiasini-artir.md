Meta Platforms Inc. has released open weights for its Muse Spark artificial intelligence model, shifting the system from proprietary access to an open format. Named Muse Spark Open, the model is specifically engineered for software coding and long-running autonomous agent operations. Meta describes it as the most capable open-weight model to emerge from a U.S. lab, featuring 816 billion total parameters while activating only 42 billion parameters per token to optimize performance. The system supports multimodal inputs including text, images, audio, and video, backed by a context window of up to one million tokens. Training processes relied heavily on real developer workflows, encompassing multi-file refactoring and extensive debugging sessions.

The model is available under a new license that permits free commercial adaptation for developers, though companies surpassing specific revenue thresholds require separate agreements, and military deployments remain strictly prohibited. In benchmark evaluations, the model achieved top scores in tool-use tasks while showing competitive performance in coding challenges against international rivals. Beyond cloud infrastructure, Meta tested the model on desktop workstation setups linked via specialized frameworks, demonstrating its capability in practical software debugging and video editing tasks. Inference access is currently supported by major cloud catalogs, hosting providers, and independent platforms worldwide to ensure broad developer reach.
