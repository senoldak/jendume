Thinking Machines Lab has introduced Inkling 2 Small, marking the first model in its second-generation lineup. The new system is built as a sparse Mixture-of-Experts model featuring 594 billion total parameters and 17 billion active parameters. It accepts both text and image inputs while supporting variable reasoning effort, aiming to improve performance in agentic coding, vision, and knowledge work. The model is currently live on the Tinker platform for fine-tuning and inference.

The training process involved a multi-phase pretraining pipeline processing massive amounts of tokens, followed by a joint reinforcement learning run. Coding made up a significant portion of the reinforcement learning tokens. Special attention was paid to alignment and safety during development, successfully reducing early misbehavior tendencies and improving internal safety metrics compared to the previous generation model.

In benchmark evaluations, the model achieved strong results in tasks such as SWE-Bench Pro v2, showing significant progress on the intelligence index compared to its predecessor. While it excels in perception and enterprise tasks, it trails behind certain competitors in terminal-based evaluations. Pricing for the model is structured around input and output token costs for developers.

Regarding availability, the model weights are currently closed at launch. Thinking Machines Lab plans to release the weights under an Apache 2.0 license next week following external safety testing and red-teaming, allowing for broader self-hosting and deployment options in the near future.
