As businesses shift from debating artificial intelligence adoption to active deployment, governance has emerged as a critical hurdle. Autonomous agents now operate around the clock across various platforms, bypassing control mechanisms that were originally designed for traditional, deterministic software.

During a technology leadership symposium held in October 2026, industry experts discussed the mounting challenges of managing costs and maintaining operational control. Shiv Agarwal, co-founder and chief executive officer of Singulr AI, noted that bridging the gap between corporate intent and actual runtime states is becoming increasingly difficult due to the rapid pace of adoption.

Experts point out that monitoring hidden actions—such as agents fetching external data or sending private messages—requires continuous behavioral assessment during execution. To address these security concerns, many chief information officers are now restricting large language models and autonomous agents to secure, virtual private cloud environments.
