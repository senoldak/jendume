Elon Musk announced on X that the Grok Bot AI assistant is now capable of making phone calls on behalf of users. According to the updates shared regarding the system, Grok Bot can call individuals or businesses, convey requests, take notes on responses, and send a summary of the conversation once the call is completed, eliminating the need to wait on hold or make calls manually for reservations.

Reports detailing the features in version 0.77 indicate that the bot can dial numbers independently, wait on hold for extended periods, navigate automated phone menus by pressing keys, and be transferred to other departments if necessary. Users can follow the transcribed conversation live on their screens while the call is taking place in real time.

Grok Bot is developed through a collaboration between SpaceX Super Intelligence and Cursor, operating on its own dedicated virtual machine in the cloud. The application, which expanded to Mac and iPhone earlier, is now available across Windows, Linux, and Android platforms with interface support in multiple languages. Access to the bot is provided through subscription tiers starting with the monthly 30-dollar Premium plan.

While similar automated calling concepts have been explored in the past, such as previous industry demonstrations, Grok Bot differentiates itself by providing real-time transcription and concise summaries directly to the user. If rolled out broadly across different regions, this capability aims to streamline time-consuming phone interactions such as booking appointments and dealing with customer support lines, requiring users to manage decisions carefully.
