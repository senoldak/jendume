Data integration and automation provider Boomi has announced an increase in its internal artificial intelligence adoption. Chief Executive Steve Lucas stated that approximately half of the company accounts payable are now reviewed and executed by AI, with human oversight remaining in the loop. Lucas emphasized that AI must be integrated by targeting specific business problems rather than generic use cases. Providing integration, automation, and AI tools to more than thirty thousand companies, Boomi helps various corporate applications, databases, and APIs communicate smoothly with one another. The integration firm is approaching one billion dollars in recurring revenue, having tripled its earnings over a four-year period since the current leadership took charge.

Arguing against the idea that AI will quickly wipe out jobs, Lucas noted that transformative technologies historically create new types of work rather than eliminating them entirely. He also highlighted that data accessibility, quality, and security are fundamental prerequisites for successful AI deployment, warning against meaningless agent deployments. Employing between two thousand two hundred and two thousand three hundred people, Boomi operates through a direct sales force and a vast network of partners to scale internal productivity through targeted automation and robust governance. Lucas additionally noted that curiosity will remain the primary skill set emerging from universities as the software landscape continues to expand.
