While discussions surrounding the risks of artificial intelligence have intensified, experts point out that a crucial requirement for safety is often overlooked. Preventing cyberattacks or rogue behavior is essential, but the political context in which these systems operate matters deeply.

The exact same powerful AI model that advances science and medicine in a healthy democracy can become an oppressive tool under authoritarian rule. Experts warn that AI-driven economic insecurity and national security challenges could further strain democratic institutions and checks and balances.

To counter these risks, legal frameworks must be updated to limit executive overreach and ensure robust oversight. Furthermore, tech industry leaders are urged to look beyond commercial interests and actively defend basic constitutional principles to ensure a secure digital future.
