RapidCanvas has successfully raised 29 million dollars in Series B funding to expand its enterprise artificial intelligence application services. The funding round was led by Vertex Ventures SEA & India, alongside participation from Hitachi Ventures, with returning investments from Accel, Peak XV Partners, Valley Capital Partners, and Titanium Ventures. This announcement brings the startup's total raised capital to 52.5 million dollars following its previous 16 million dollar Series A funding milestone secured in December 2024.

The Austin-based enterprise combines a specialized artificial intelligence agent platform with dedicated field engineers who build and manage customized business applications for clients. These custom tools integrate seamlessly with existing enterprise software systems such as CRM and ERP platforms to streamline automated business operations. Rahul Pangam, co-founder and chief executive of the startup, noted that artificial intelligence can serve as a great equalizer, though its biggest advantages have historically favored the largest corporations. Company leadership reported significant growth, noting that annual recurring revenue multiplied eightfold over the past six quarters with more than seventy-five companies utilizing their technology.

The newly acquired capital will be deployed to expand sales operations, strengthen strategic partnerships with private equity firms and advisers, and hire additional engineering talent across the United States, Brazil, and India. Furthermore, the startup intends to direct resources toward its Enterprise Context Engine to store client business rules and definitions, enhancing how artificial intelligence applications retain and utilize specific business logic for corporate clients.
