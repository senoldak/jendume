YouTube has introduced an artificial intelligence-powered update to its Replace Song tool, offering a practical solution for creators facing copyright claims. With the newly added Create button, users can generate alternative instrumental tracks using advanced audio technology to substitute problematic audio in their videos, providing an additional option for those dealing with copyright issues.

The feature operates directly within the Suggestions tab of the Replace Song tool in YouTube Studio. Creators who receive a sound or song copyright claim can instantly generate four different royalty-free instrumental tracks using this button. These pieces can be downloaded and seamlessly integrated into videos, reducing the hassle of searching for alternative background music while starting directly from the suggestions tab.

The tool also provides flexible editing options based on video length. While each generated track has a three-minute limit, loop options help accommodate longer videos, and volume controls allow creators to fine-tune audio balance. Additionally, adjusting volume levels helps determine the proper mix of the music within the video, making it easier to use a portion of a track in a short video or apply looping for longer content.

One of the most significant aspects of the process is the automated removal of copyright claims. Once the copyrighted audio is fully replaced with the new AI-generated track, the Content ID claim is automatically lifted, sparing creators from a lengthy dispute process and eliminating the need to conduct any separate appeal procedures.

The feature is currently available in most countries outside the European Economic Area, while details regarding its availability in the EEA remain unannounced, meaning creators in that region must wait for a future announcement.
