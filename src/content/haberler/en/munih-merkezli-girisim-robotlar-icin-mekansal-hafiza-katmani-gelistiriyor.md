One of the primary challenges in physical artificial intelligence is enabling machines to adapt dynamically to changing environments rather than following a strict set of pre-programmed instructions. To operate freely, robots need robust spatial context that mirrors how humans navigate the physical world.

Spranic, a startup based in Munich, is addressing this challenge by building a spatial memory layer that records physical spaces and their modifications, integrating them with knowledge graphs. According to founder Jordi Spranger, robots require advanced navigational context to transition from statically planned tasks to multi-purpose operations.

The system utilizes knowledge graphs to securely connect relevant data sources while preserving the origin of information. This approach aims to provide robots with the flexibility and auditable safety needed to operate across a wide variety of real-world scenarios.
