NiCE Ltd. shared its comprehensive artificial intelligence strategy and organizational transformation following the Cognigy acquisition at its annual industry analyst summit. One year after closing the Cognigy deal, the company has transitioned from a contact center provider with bolted-on AI to a core AI platform.

Chief Executive Scott Russell noted during his keynote that the acquisition fundamentally changed where the company builds from and who it is as an organization. Philipp Heltewig, Cognigy co-founder and NiCE's chief AI officer, explained that Cognigy now serves as the underlying substrate for conversational and agentic AI across the entire platform. NiCE continues to offer Cognigy not only to its own customer base but also as a standalone solution on competing platforms.

NiCE's primary competitive advantage lies in its decades of compliance interactions, analytics, and screen recordings. During demonstrations, executives showed how multimodal models can observe human agents and automatically build AI agents, including self-inferred guardrails.

Data shared at the event showed that prospects receiving automated insights based on their own data book nine times more AI than those that do not, while platform API volume has grown 7.5 times over two years. Russell emphasized that year-over-year annual recurring revenue growth stands at 52%, reflecting durable long-term demand for enterprise AI workloads.
