Shopify is advancing its agentic commerce ambitions by allowing a wider variety of AI agents to browse and purchase directly from its listings. The company announced that shoppers will soon be able to configure their preferred AI assistants to buy products on their behalf, opening up access to a catalog containing over a billion items.

Previously, Shopify partnered with Meta to enable specific agents like Muse to perform purchases, but the newly introduced framework removes the need for custom integration partnerships. This means customers can now connect virtually any AI agent of their choice to handle their shopping needs seamlessly.

This shift signals a broader trend where e-commerce is increasingly mediated by autonomous software agents, fundamentally changing how consumers interact with online storefronts and complete purchases.
