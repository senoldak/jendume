OpenAI will extend access to its artificial intelligence models for software creators utilizing Google's cloud infrastructure, following a history of market competition between the two organizations. According to the announcement, OpenAI's GPT products will arrive in Google's cloud model garden and Antigravity coding tool in the upcoming weeks. This effort reflects a growing push by the ChatGPT creator to expand the distribution of its technology to enterprise developers beyond its consumer roots.

Less than six months ago, OpenAI noted that Microsoft would no longer hold an exclusive license on its intellectual property. Following that change, cloud leader Amazon announced plans to host OpenAI models, and now Google is following suit. With this latest integration, OpenAI's models will be hosted on all three of the top cloud infrastructure providers in the industry.

Google has experienced strong momentum in cloud computing, with annualized revenue growth accelerating in the second quarter, driven largely by robust demand for artificial intelligence models and custom chips. While corporate spending on artificial intelligence heavily favors OpenAI and Anthropic, various enterprise IT departments also utilize Google's Gemini systems.

Founded in 2015 as a counterweight to the concentration of artificial intelligence research at companies like Google, OpenAI is now targeting an initial public offering while courting investors at a valuation of 1.4 trillion dollars. The two organizations previously collaborated when the artificial intelligence laboratory utilized Google's computing services to power ChatGPT and associated application interfaces.
