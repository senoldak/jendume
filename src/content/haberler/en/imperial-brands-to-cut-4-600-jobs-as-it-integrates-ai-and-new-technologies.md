Imperial Brands has announced plans to cut up to 4,600 jobs as part of a significant cost-cutting initiative driven by the integration of artificial intelligence and new technologies. The company aims to become more agile and data-led, shifting its operational focus toward automation and software-driven processes to navigate a changing market landscape.

This move highlights the profound impact of AI adoption on the labor market within traditional industries. As corporations prioritize automation to streamline operations, the nature of employment is undergoing a rapid transformation, with AI serving as a primary catalyst for restructuring workforce requirements. The shift reflects a broader corporate trend where technology is utilized to optimize efficiency in established sectors.

The company plans to eliminate 2,100 roles globally this year, with a further reduction of between 2,100 and 2,500 jobs expected by the end of 2030. Through this transformation, the firm targets at least £320 million in annual savings by 2030. With a global workforce of approximately 25,000, the company is also closing a factory in Germany and selling a plant in Taiwan as part of this restructuring.

Facing declining volumes in traditional cigarette products, the group is pivoting toward newer categories like vapes and oral nicotine. This shift is mirrored across the industry, as other major tobacco manufacturers have also announced significant workforce reductions to become more "technology-enabled." These developments underscore a broader trend where AI is not merely an auxiliary tool but a central driver of corporate strategy and labor force planning in established manufacturing sectors.
