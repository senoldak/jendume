Cloudflare has rolled out a new beta feature that allows AI agents to search websites directly, transforming sites into searchable engines for automated visitors. The newly launched Search for Agents tool is available to all customers, including those on free plans, and automatically indexes a site to create a dedicated search endpoint on its own domain.

Instead of generating AI-written answers, the search returns matching text directly from the site. Cloudflare notes that since visiting agents already operate their own AI models, they only require clean and direct source material. Website owners retain their existing firewall rules and bot defense settings, while visiting agents discover the search capability through hidden page tags.

The launch comes as automated bot traffic on Cloudflare's network experiences massive growth, with daily requests from AI agents surging significantly over the past year. Executives have pointed out that bot traffic could eventually reach scales far exceeding human activity. Billing for the new service begins under updated pricing tiers, with introductory allowances included for free users.

This release follows Cloudflare's previous initiatives in the agentic web space, such as the release of the Kitesurf browser for AI agents. By providing standardized endpoints for automated search, the company aims to reshape how AI systems interact with web infrastructure.
