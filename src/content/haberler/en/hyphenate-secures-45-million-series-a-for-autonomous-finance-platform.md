Hyphenate, an autonomous finance platform for corporate finance offices, has announced a $45 million Series A funding round led by Nexus Venture Partners. The round included participation from B Capital, Jefferies, and existing investors such as Foundation Capital, bringing the company's total funding to $54 million. The new capital will be used to deploy its AI agents deeper across corporate finance functions.

The platform deploys autonomous agents to handle cash reconciliation, sales order processing, invoicing, and maintaining subledgers. Finance teams step in only when human judgment is required. Customer reports indicate a 90 percent reduction in repetitive finance work and a 75 percent drop in audit exceptions, as systems handle complex workflows to auditor standards.

Enterprise customers such as Mini Melts USA, HiBid, and Nasdaq-listed Groupon are replacing legacy systems with Hyphenate to streamline treasury and order-to-cash processes. Co-founder and CEO Ramnandan Krishnamurthy noted that customers steadily expand the system's responsibilities once it proves reliable in production environments.

Finance has traditionally been one of the most challenging sectors for autonomous AI due to strict accuracy and auditability requirements. By training on historical spreadsheets and system logs while maintaining verifiable evidence for every automated action, Hyphenate provides a secure path toward autonomous financial operations.
