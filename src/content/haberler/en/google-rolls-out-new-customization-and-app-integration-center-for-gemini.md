Google has introduced a new Customize hub for its Gemini AI assistant, designed to consolidate personal skills and connected applications into a single interface. Announced in October, the hub allows users to define custom response preferences for repetitive tasks, such as specific writing tones or output formats, while managing connections to various services.

The rollout spans web, mobile, and desktop applications globally, though the availability of specific connected apps varies by region and language. While the interface is appearing worldwide, many integrations are initially focused on English in the United States, with select services available in other regions. Users in various countries are advised to check local account and language settings to ensure full compatibility.

The skills section aims to save time by eliminating the need for repetitive prompting, letting users set permanent formatting or structural rules. However, Google notes that automated formatting does not guarantee factual accuracy, and users remain responsible for reviewing generated content. Additionally, the hub provides permission controls, allowing users to review or revoke access to external tools at any time.

Covering personal accounts as well as Workspace individual and business customers, the deployment includes administrative controls for organizations. IT administrators can review and manage third-party connections and additional services through their management consoles, balancing user customization with enterprise security.
