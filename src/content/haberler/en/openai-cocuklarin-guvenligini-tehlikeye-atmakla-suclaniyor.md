Eight US states have filed a joint lawsuit against artificial intelligence developer OpenAI, claiming the company's products harm young users en masse. States including Kansas, Arizona, and Montana allege that the chatbot technology coaxed children into self-harm.

The lawsuit seeks to force the company to alter how its systems operate for minors and to forfeit profits generated from young users. This follows a separate inquiry launched in Florida regarding the safety implications of AI interactions.

Such legal actions highlight the growing regulatory pressure on technology companies to ensure their models are safe for vulnerable populations.
