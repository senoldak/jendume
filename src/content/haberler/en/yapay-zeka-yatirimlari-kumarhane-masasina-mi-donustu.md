Global semiconductor markets experienced a sharp sell-off as concerns mounted over the sustainability of massive artificial intelligence investments. Speaking at a conference in Singapore, Broadcom CEO Hock Tan compared many modern AI investors to casino gamblers, noting that they often lack a clear understanding of the technologies they are funding. While acknowledging that frontier generative AI developers like OpenAI generate substantial revenue, Tan emphasized that heavy spending on model training keeps many of these ventures unprofitable for now. Adding to market jitters, reports of significant progress by Chinese memory chip maker CXMT in advanced architectures triggered steep declines across Asian exchanges. Benchmark indices in South Korea fell sharply, with major memory producers such as Samsung and SK Hynix seeing double-digit drops. Analysts suggest the market is entering a reality check phase, balancing towering expectations against the actual path to long-term commercial profitability.
