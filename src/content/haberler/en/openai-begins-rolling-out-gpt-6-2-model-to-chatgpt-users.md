OpenAI has begun rolling out the GPT-6.2 model within the chat tab of ChatGPT on a global scale. According to the company's release notes dated November 23, this new version is based on GPT-6.2 Sol and is specifically optimized for everyday conversations. The distribution process is rolling out gradually across Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscription tiers. As a result, not all users on the same plan will see the model immediately, and enterprise access may also depend on workspace administrator settings.

Integrated into ChatGPT, GPT-6.2 allows users to follow more detailed instructions. Examples shared by OpenAI include complex requests such as weekend hotel comparisons. When multiple criteria like price and location are specified, the model can discuss options found on the internet and make recommendations based on those priorities. The company also states that when asked if a product has gone on sale, the model can suggest notifying the user when the price drops. In this example, the initial response addresses the current question, while the follow-up suggestion is presented taking into account that the price may change later.

Significant improvements have also been achieved in the model's visual understanding capabilities. Answering questions about uploaded images with better accuracy, the model outperformed its predecessor in OpenAI's internal evaluations. In the visual comprehension testing conducted by the company for the chat tab, GPT-6.2 scored 71.7 percent, while GPT-6 scored 47.4 percent. The difference between the two scores was recorded as 24.3 percentage points, reflecting results obtained from individual screenshots, photographs, or documents containing text.
