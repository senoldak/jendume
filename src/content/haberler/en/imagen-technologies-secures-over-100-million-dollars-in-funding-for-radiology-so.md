US-based radiology services provider Imagen Technologies announced that it has raised more than 100 million dollars in growth capital from current and new investors. The funding announcement revealed that the new capital will be used to bolster clinical infrastructure and expand its radiology team. The company aims to strengthen its operational capacity and infrastructure through this financial backing.

Alongside the investment, the company gained access to up to 150 million additional imaging studies to train its artificial intelligence models. This access encompasses X-rays, computed tomography scans, magnetic resonance imaging, and ultrasound studies. Because a single imaging study can include multiple images depending on the examination method, the stated figure does not represent the total count of individual images. This dataset expansion enables more comprehensive training for the artificial intelligence models.

This extensive dataset will be utilized to train new models and test them against diverse clinical case types encountered in daily medical practice. Imagen aims to integrate these models into the existing workflows used by radiologists. When evaluating patient scans, attending physicians will use the software to review intermediary images and prior examinations on their workspace displays. These AI-driven tools are specifically designed to assist physicians during their decision-making processes.

The company's primary objective is to make image evaluations more consistent and reliable through advanced software integration. A portion of the funding is specifically allocated to clinical infrastructure development, hiring additional radiologists, and supporting the current team. Imagen also plans to present its ongoing work at the RSNA meeting in Chicago, reinforcing its position within the sector while broadening its clinical service scope.
