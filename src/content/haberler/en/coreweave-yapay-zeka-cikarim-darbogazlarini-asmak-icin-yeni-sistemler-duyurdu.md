As the artificial intelligence boom matures, inference workloads are rapidly becoming the primary driver of infrastructure economics. While the initial wave focused heavily on model training, serving models faster and more affordably is now the defining challenge for the industry.

Specialized cloud providers are moving beyond raw GPU capacity to offer comprehensive storage, networking, and software solutions. CoreWeave has introduced managed services spanning training, post-training, and inference to support developers in their quest for high performance and scalability.

Reinforcement learning and agentic model iteration place intense pressure on inference pipelines, often creating bottlenecks during rollouts. By leveraging optimized frameworks, the company has demonstrated significant improvements in model reload latencies, allowing training and inference to scale concurrently.

To broaden accessibility, the company also launched Forge, a new platform connecting serving, observability, and evaluation tools. By offering free tiers alongside advanced features, the platform aims to make leading-edge AI development tools available to individual developers without compromising reliability.
