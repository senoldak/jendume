SpaceX has initiated a massive investment move at its Starbase facility in Texas to bolster its artificial intelligence operations. The company plans to build both a large-scale supercomputer facility and a natural gas-fired power plant to provide the necessary electricity. The projected investment for the initial phases of the project is $24 billion, marking it as one of the largest private sector investments in the region.

The decision to build an independent power plant stems from the high energy requirements needed to train and run advanced artificial intelligence systems. By generating its own electricity, SpaceX aims to bypass the long wait times associated with connecting to the main Texas power grid, allowing for faster deployment of its facilities. This strategy ensures that the computing capacity required for AI processing remains uninterrupted and under direct control.

The economic scope of the project will be further clarified through discussions with local authorities. SpaceX's request for property tax abatements and an economic development agreement will be reviewed by the Starbase City Commission in December 2026. While specific technical details and the exact amount of tax incentives have not been disclosed, the investment is expected to expand across multiple phases in the coming years.

In line with Elon Musk's broader vision, this investment will also enhance SpaceX's operational integration. The company intends to use natural gas not only for electricity but also to produce liquid methane fuel for Starship rockets. To support this, a 12.9-kilometer pipeline named 'Starpipe' is planned to be operational by January 2027. This create a vertically integrated model spanning from energy supply to high-level AI computations.
