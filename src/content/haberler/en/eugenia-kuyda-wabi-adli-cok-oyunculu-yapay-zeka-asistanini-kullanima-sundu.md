Replika founder Eugenia Kuyda has officially released Wabi to the public, realizing a personal assistant vision she initially presented over a decade ago. Twelve years after showcasing her early lifestyle concierge chatbot, Kuyda stated that modern technology has finally caught up with that foundational concept. Wabi operates as a messaging application that enables users to maintain multiple agentic threads focused on separate tasks and long-term objectives, automatically generating customized mini applications upon connecting to user data streams like email.

The public release marks a strategic transition from Wabi's initial positioning as a manual social platform into an autonomous utility provider. Diverging from conventional personal assistants constrained by linear, single-thread conversations, Wabi integrates a multi-threaded and multiplayer framework. Consequently, participants can share specific threads with family members or romantic partners, establishing collaborative group chats supported by live activity tracking, calendar coordination, and shared lists.

Platform architecture prioritizes stringent data privacy and security measures. Wabi maintains SOC 2 compliance, reflecting independent audits of its internal security controls, while preserving the majority of user information within cloud infrastructure located in the United States. Within shared chat environments, all participants retain full visibility whenever the AI requests sensitive personal data, such as calendar events or bank details.

Distributed without charge during its initial rollout phase, Wabi has not yet established a definitive monetization strategy as corporate leadership pursues additional venture financing. The platform exemplifies a broader industry movement toward collaborative artificial intelligence tools designed to facilitate real-world interpersonal organization.
