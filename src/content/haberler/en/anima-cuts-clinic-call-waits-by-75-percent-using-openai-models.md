Anima has expanded its healthcare platform by integrating OpenAI models to help care teams respond to patients more efficiently and improve information flow across the system. According to CEO and co-founder Shun Pang, the company currently serves over 14 million patients across more than 1,000 clinics primarily in the UK while expanding its operations to the US, Australia, and beyond.

The platform's AI receptionist, Andi, uses conversational AI and clinical intake workflows to take patient symptoms over the phone and structure them into medical histories. Clinics using this system have successfully reduced phone wait times from 40 to 50 minutes down to 10 minutes, representing a reduction of at least 75 percent. Patient surveys indicate that 86 percent of respondents would use Andi again, with elderly users reporting high satisfaction rates with the voice agent.

For document workflows, Anima employs GPT-6 models to extract information from incoming clinical paperwork, match documents to the correct patient, and suggest appropriate clinical codes and next steps. Practices that previously processed about 15 documents per hour can now handle 30 to 45, while top-performing clinics process up to 60 documents per hour, clearing backlogs of over 100,000 documents within days.

Additionally, the Scribe product turns consultation transcripts into structured clinical notes in seconds, allowing doctors to maintain eye contact and attention with patients rather than typing throughout the appointment. This automated documentation also helps draft referral letters and follow-up messages quickly.

With most platform cases resolved within 24 hours and a median resolution time of approximately 3.5 hours, Anima aims to eliminate administrative bottlenecks in healthcare systems and provide personalized healthcare for everyone.
