OpenAI is expanding access to Dots, the proactive and always-on AI agents capable of handling tasks without direct supervision. Following an initial launch for higher-tier plans, the company announced it is testing the feature with a small number of users on the $20 per month ChatGPT Plus plan. The engineering team noted positive feedback regarding the agents' proactivity following recent improvements.

Thanks to OpenAI's plugin ecosystem, Dots can interact with over 4,000 applications. The agents can operate autonomously, making progress even without user interaction, and can even connect to a PC to work alongside the user or control it on their behalf. Users can check in on the agent's progress at any time through a virtual computer interface.

In addition to the limited rollout for Plus users, Dots are also being made available to all ChatGPT Business Standard users. For higher-tier Pro and Business Premium subscribers, conversations with the agents do not count toward regular usage limits as long as they avoid complex tasks, though it remains unclear whether Plus and Business Standard users will receive the same benefit.

Originally powered by GPT-6 Astra, Dots can now also run on GPT-6.1 Terra. OpenAI has simultaneously rolled out several new features for the agents, including iOS widgets, Shortcuts integration, iMessage and RCS support, and iOS notification replies. This deeper integration into everyday digital environments demonstrates how AI assistants are evolving into active workplace companions.
