Microsoft announced plans to raise 25 billion dollars in bonds, utilizing its solid financial standing to support its extensive infrastructure expansion. Management is targeting 170 billion dollars in 2026 capital expenditures as the enterprise looks to satisfy intense demand originating from artificial intelligence laboratories Anthropic and OpenAI, alongside a broader base of clients requiring advanced computational power.

According to an official corporate statement, the software maker intends to allocate net proceeds from the offering toward general corporate requirements. These allocations may encompass working capital financing, capital investments, stock repurchases, business acquisitions, and the retirement of existing debt obligations. The bond offerings are scheduled to close on November 4.

Industry peers including Amazon and Alphabet have similarly accessed the debt capital markets to finance infrastructure projects during the year, while specialized cloud provider CoreWeave has leaned on borrowing to expand its physical data center footprint. Meanwhile, financial markets have shown increased selectivity as monetary authorities maintain elevated interest rates to address macroeconomic pressures.

Amy Hood, who serves as Microsoft finance chief, explained during an investor earnings call that the corporation will leverage supplementary funding avenues to augment its organic cash generation whenever market conditions prove favorable. She emphasized that the organization maintains strong capabilities within debt markets due to its top-tier credit ratings.
