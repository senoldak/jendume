StepSecurity has raised $20 million in a Series A funding round to bolster the security of software development pipelines and AI agents. The startup focuses on protecting developers' laptops, code repositories, and build systems from malicious code often hidden within open-source software. As the software industry evolves, the tools and libraries developers rely on have become prime targets for sophisticated cyberattacks.

As AI agents become more integrated into software development, the toolkits and frameworks they rely on have become new targets for attackers. A recent incident involving poisoned versions of Microsoft’s Python toolkit for AI agents highlighted the severity of this threat. StepSecurity aims to mitigate these risks, ensuring that the environments where AI agents operate remain secure. The company is now prioritizing the protection of the sandboxes where AI agents execute code, a critical step in securing the AI development lifecycle.

Led by Elephant, this latest funding round brings the company’s total capital raised to $23 million. Founded in 2021, the firm already supports over 20,000 open-source projects through its free security tool. In an era where AI coding tools are increasingly targeted by cyber threats, StepSecurity emphasizes that security must extend beyond traditional coding environments to the very systems powering autonomous AI agents. Moving forward, the company plans to scale its efforts to safeguard the infrastructure that enables AI agents to function safely and reliably.
