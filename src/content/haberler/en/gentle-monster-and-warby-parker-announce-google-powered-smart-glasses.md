Eyewear brand Gentle Monster has announced that its upcoming smart glasses, built on Google's Android XR platform, will launch in December. According to the company's website, pre-orders will begin on November 12, a date that was also seemingly shared by fellow partner Warby Parker. Google has yet to officially confirm either release date.

Both eyewear brands, alongside Samsung, are collaborating with Google on the project, which aims to compete with Meta's smart glasses while acting as a spiritual successor to Google Glass. The upcoming devices will offer a display-free, audio-first experience that provides seamless access to Google's Gemini AI assistant.

Alongside the announcement, Gentle Monster shared a teaser for a short film directed by Jonathan Glazer and starring Joaquin Phoenix. The full version of the project is scheduled for release in mid-November, signaling an artistic and experimental marketing approach rather than a traditional commercial campaign.

Google's Android XR platform is engineered to power everything from mixed reality headsets like the Samsung Galaxy XR to augmented reality glasses like the Xreal Aura. The Gentle Monster and Warby Parker models will focus on an audio-first approach with AI integration. Industry observers are watching closely to see how consumers respond to this branded hardware strategy against existing market competitors.
