# Surface: jendume site (all public pages)

Mode: Read. Audience: TR/EN readers who want to understand world events and why they matter. Job: find today's stories, read one comfortably, switch language without losing the story.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Finding your way through the world's news like through an international airport: every frame element is a bilingual wayfinding sign (primary language bold, the other language light beneath), while the reading column stays calm. Refuses the broadsheet hairline-and-serif newspaper default and the dark tech-lab look.

OWN-WORLD: Sign black panels (#16181D) with signal yellow (#FFC72C) owning a full-width band on every screen; cool off-white reading ground (#F3F4F1), white column. Signika for every sign (masthead, nav, headings, labels); Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading. Six sections are permanent wayfinding codes 1–6 in black-on-yellow / yellow-on-black squares, with drawn SVG direction arrows. Square corners, 2–4px rules, no shadows. Light only.

STORY: The reader sees where they are (language, section), what leads today and why it matters, and moves to a story or section in one step; on an article, region + perspective sign precede the prose.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black overhead sign full width: JEN DUME wordmark left with motto in yellow beneath (other language muted), two-state language switch right (both shown, active struck forward in yellow). Under it the yellow section band: six gate plates, number square + TR bold / EN light + arrow. Then lead story large in Signika at left (meta as a sign line under the title, no eyebrow), black "Neden Önemli? / Why it matters" sign block below it; right column "Son Haberler / Latest" list with section code squares.

FORM: Bilingual transit wayfinding signage, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key 0be24791. Raises: yellow band at page scale (cutting bench), both languages present with active struck forward (cathode), fact vs interpretation typographically separate (provenance), fixed label grid per story card: code · region · date (sneaker boxes).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
